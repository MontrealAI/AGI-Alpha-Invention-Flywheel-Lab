# Compensated orbital separation: construction and limits

This is a computational design experiment. It creates no mission approval, real contract, insurance, operator consent, measured industrial saving or flight-qualified safety system. The business hypothesis concerns independently checkable maneuver studies and integration support, with voluntary cost allocation. It does not charge a royalty merely because collision losses are large.

The selected capability has broad possible consequence, but the public CREAM evidence defeats any claim that automated cooperative avoidance itself is a missing means. Whether bounded compensation/cash guarantees fill an externally valuable residual is unresolved. An unrestricted conventional entrepreneur is given the same terms and may match it exactly. This is a falsifier of superiority, not a reason to hide useful classical code.

## Actual construction and purpose bridge

`orbital.py` implements the standard circular-reference Hill-Clohessy-Wiltshire state transition and an independent matrix-exponential reconstruction. It does not invent orbital mechanics. A two-dimensional Gaussian model at a specified epoch produces a conservative overlap upper bound by combining the triangle inequality, the covariance maximum eigenvalue and the standard two-dimensional normal radial tail. Numerical disk integration supplies an independent calculation. This bound is **not** time-integrated collision probability. Unobserved encounter times, non-Gaussian tails, covariance correlation, drag, J2, finite burns, missed commands and unknown objects remain unqualified.

The finite contract chooses quoted impulses and transfers only if both modeled operators weakly prefer them to their strongest enumerated original-game pure-equilibrium option, current modeled cash covers fuel/transfers/fees, consent persists and the modeled overlap criterion passes. No pure outside equilibrium causes abstention. Quotes and private-type scenarios are supplied; truthful revelation is not proved. The finite optimality gap covers only the enumerated individually rational quote grid. A default-known case is refused; no legal recovery from unknown default is certified.

K2 changes the function from separating a pair at an epoch to reaching a stand-off inspection position **and velocity** through two burns. It constructs a new six-dimensional boundary-value solve using frozen dynamics. Direct numerical ODE propagation checks endpoints. A one-burn ablation exposes the new endpoint obligation. Continuous flight-corridor clearance is not delivered. The ordinary equal-content arm receives the same primitive; a fresh conventional specialist reconstructs it with a matrix exponential. These controls can establish mathematical reuse and cost boundaries, not a better discovery method.

The finite maneuver game separately supplies per-burn private costs and modeled asset-loss valuations. Those private costs are neither venture revenue nor complete venture expense; internal compensation is a transfer, never new aggregate surplus. Current cash is tested before selection. Principal reserved for obligations is protected cash rather than another expense. Side payments and risk limits do not establish legal enforceability or real preference. The strongest free same-capability competitor can remove fees entirely; that region defeats scalable value capture even if the mathematics passes.

Actual local resources are CPU/wall seconds recorded by the runner, zero incremental spending and zero human intervention after assignment. Token totals and actual host/model bills are unknown. A deployable human-baseline productivity gain is unresolved: no humans were observed, and giving conventional software the same classical mathematics is expected to tie computational quality. A lower hypothetical wage bill must not be inferred from the numerical test.
