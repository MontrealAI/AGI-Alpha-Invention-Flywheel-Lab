## Constructed K1 and different K2

K1 removes a common illumination-gain nuisance through simultaneous open-beam reference and dark correction, then solves for aluminum/copper mass areal densities at three nominal photon energies. Its deterministic component intervals follow from a supplied bounded error on dark-corrected signals. It receives observations and calibration coefficients, never mass truth.

K2 adds a ray/cell geometry constructor and spatial inverse, reusing the frozen spectral interface. The output is a two-material spatial density map and the position of a strongly depleted interior cell. Locating a defect is a different function from integrated composition. Its relevance to real structural inspection is prospective; the 4×4 authored phantom is not a measured industrial component or validated porosity instrument.

## Negative evidence defines the operating envelope

Nonpositive corrected signals, a large unmodeled iron contribution, and rank-deficient spatial geometry were rejected. A coherent sample/reference gain mismatch equivalent to an extra `[0.2, 0.03]` g/cm² of Al/Cu passed the residual check and returned exactly that erroneous extra material. This is an explicit undetectable violation of the common-gain contract, not a repaired score or a successful universal fault detector. It proves why an internally consistent residual cannot establish physical truth.

The bounded-noise parameter is authored (`1e-4` relative error on each corrected signal), not experimentally calibrated and not a Poisson counting model. Stored photon-like signals are floating point ideal intensities. Polychromatic spectra, detector response, scatter, unknown composition, reference-path mismatch, finite resolution, physical geometry errors and reference-coefficient uncertainties require external characterization. No clinical use or structural safety certification is supplied.

The 208 authored computations are one finite protocol with one model family, not 208 independent physical measurements or discovery campaigns. A separate portfolio verifier specifies additional cases; its acceptance is reported separately rather than assumed here. The developer knew the public fixture family and seeds; this is not an externally blinded trial.
