# Retained photonic design foundry: facts and limits

The instrument is an independently implemented, bounded planar-wave calculation. It does not invent the transfer-matrix method, demonstrate a new coating, or establish that thin-film design was previously uneconomic. The strongest complete conventional route is allowed the same public material data, robust objective, geometry, search budget and construction opportunities. Public source inspection already defeats a generic-optimizer novelty claim.

## Three domains compared before construction

| Domain and useful outcome | Credible complete route | Executable first construction | Main unavailable evidence | Selection |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Optical multilayers for acceptance-qualified photon routing | Classical multilayer optics; Byrnes `tmm`; current differentiable and verifier-first engines; conventional thin-film engineer | Material-aware transfer/scattering calculation, antireflection coating, fresh two-band separator | Deposited-film metrology, fabricated device, buyer validation, comparative human lifecycle time | Selected for a sharply falsifiable computational test, not presumed commercial superiority |
| Compliant structures with joint force/displacement requirements | DTU topology optimization, manufacturing-robust formulations and nonlinear finite elements | Finite-element synthesis with an independently checked mechanical response | Fabrication, fatigue, contact/model discrepancy, unmet complete alternative | Deferred: credible consequence but a slower decisive material-validation loop |
| Catalyst-discovery instrument for new reaction outcomes | Established self-driving laboratories and specialist Bayesian experimental design | Public-data selection followed by experimental synthesis/characterization | Authorized lab, measurements for new candidates, safety/rights and deployment costs | Deferred: a surrogate-only score cannot stand in for a new catalytic capability |

These are resource-conditional choices, not a global ranking of undiscovered industries. The photonic computation can reveal whether retained code beats equally informed reuse; it cannot itself establish market creation.

## Evidence and rights boundary

The compact material files are CC0 data with retained provenance. All constructed outputs are computational blueprints. No deposition, fabrication, optical measurement, physical installation, lifetime test, customer procurement, legal freedom-to-operate opinion or real payment occurs. Absorption and exact Maxwell boundary agreement are useful checks of the specified model; neither establishes missing real-world scattering, film anisotropy or temperature-dependent response. Tool/code licenses do not imply patent exclusivity or customer demand. The private assignment text and private account material are excluded.
