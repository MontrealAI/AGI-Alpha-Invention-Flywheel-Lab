## The actual missing information instrument

The official [CMS 2024 provider-and-service public dataset](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/medicare-physician-other-practitioners-by-provider-and-service-2024-12-01) was updated **21 May 2026**, and the [published data dictionary](https://data.cms.gov/data-api/v1/dataset/335e5f35-eca6-482d-87b3-f99883e213e3/dictionary) exposes count and average payment columns, but not claim identifiers or clinical source notes. Its HCPCS/CPT descriptions can have separate AMA licensing obligations; **no such code descriptions are imported here**.

**Disclosure about release suppression:** Official CMS public files suppress group rows involving fewer than eleven beneficiaries. The following one-beneficiary synthetic construction therefore does **not** represent a row actually visible in the published CMS download. It deliberately grants the hypothetical public-only auditor *more* aggregate information than the real suppression policy permits. Even under that more-informative projection, the claimed duplicate-ID property is not identifiable; withholding the row cannot restore identifiability. This is an information-order argument, not an observed CMS record or validation of CMS release counts.\n\nThe small executable construction in `proof.py` builds two *synthetic-only* underlying micro-claim worlds: two dated services for one fictitious beneficiary/provider/code at identical amounts. One has two different claim IDs; the other deliberately reuses an ID. The worlds project to precisely the same named CMS-style public aggregate fields (counts and four monetary averages). But an authorized ordinary claim-ID uniqueness check distinguishes their truth. Therefore a deterministic rule receiving only those aggregates **cannot decide both cases correctly**. This is a mathematical information-barrier witness for this example, **not** evidence of real duplicate claims, fraud, clinical coding errors, a complete interpretation of all CMS files, or a proof that no different information channel could work.

A credible successor construction would require a **genuine new rights-cleared information channel** to the required claim-level fields, eligibility, safety and verified buyer acceptance, and must beat a competent authorized human-plus-conventional-software audit with full costs. Neither rights nor acceptance is present. Do not infer buyer consent or an invoice from this RFI. The observed source does not authorize outreach, health-data access, paid services or commercial execution.
