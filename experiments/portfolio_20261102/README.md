# Portfolio 071: five construction routes, executed and challenged

This portfolio tests five ways of constructing consequential productive capabilities. It produced runnable instruments, independent scientific checks, a measured-data rejection, and a native economic experiment. **No candidate has earned industry-creation admission, no physical deployment or actual revenue is established, and no USD 1B or USD 3B profit milestone has been established.** Ordinary complete methods remain strong: they tie the accepted finite-model constructions and outperform the proposed measured sensor interface.

The economic experiment models licenses for two independently accepted **computational thermal designs**. The sold unit is design access and support; physical implementation remains unqualified. Other routes' research and failed attempts are included in the declared shared cost hypothesis; their unqualified scientific results do not become revenue-producing services.

## Why these opportunities

Three leading businesses differ materially: persistent chemical measurement, integrated industrial heat, and adaptive network resilience. Chemical measurement supplied actual chronological observations capable of rejecting a proposed interface. Industrial heat supplied a consequential engineering purpose with conservation and temperature constraints. Network resilience supplied a distinct identification-to-intervention sequence and unusually strong conventional alternatives. Each route also considered three alternatives before selecting its bounded construction; the [source ledger](SOURCES_AND_SELECTION.md) records their substitutes, rights, qualification burdens and unknown capital requirements.

The candidates were selected for what the available experiments could decide. There is no claimed ranking over all possible businesses, no observed market-size estimate and no proof that these are the most profitable opportunities. The heat design is the only accepted artifact family admitted to the prospective native service schema. It remains a numerical research candidate whose strongest conventional complete competitor ties.

### R1: useful decisions from incomplete information

The constructor retains the entire declared feasible concentration set. It certifies safety only when the worst permitted value of every requested quantity meets the original limit, certifies failure only where the bounds warrant it, and otherwise abstains. It rejects an inconsistent observation set and does not answer questions about unmeasured species by assuming their absence.

The independent reference uses vertex enumeration and linear equations rather than the constructor's SciPy optimizer. The 38 outcomes are **5 safe, 14 unsafe, 18 abstentions and 1 inconsistent**. Of 24 independently generated successor blending tasks, **6 are feasible and 18 infeasible**. Eight controls cover changed purpose, new analytes, stale or altered certificates, invalid inputs and false reassurance from point estimates. The factorial does not force a two-change success story: changing the purpose query alone suffices in the registered example. Full reconstruction was an unnecessary intermediate requirement there.

These are certificates about a declared linear uncertainty model. They do not measure real water chemistry, sampling cost, contamination risk or willingness to buy. A competent conventional planner receives the same information and solves the same problem. See [the independent cases and outputs](results/R1_INDEPENDENT.json).

### R2: preserving information did not solve drift

The pinned UCI archive contains 13,910 observations from six gases, 16 sensors and ten chronological batches. Fitting uses batches 1–4, tuning uses batch 5, K1 confirmation uses batch 6, and K2 confirmation uses batches 7–10. Confirmation denominators are 2,300 and 7,977. Only measured features enter prediction; true gas identity is not supplied to the concentration predictor. No exact fit/tune-to-confirmation feature duplicates were found.

K1 macro-F1 counts abstentions as classification errors. K2 absolute errors are normalized by each gas's concentration range in the fitting data and averaged over all confirmation rows, including rows the interface declines. Coverage and per-gas errors are reported separately; abstention cannot remove difficult rows from the headline MAE.

| Proposed shape/amplitude frame | 0.497028 | 95.65% | 0.309543 | 80.57% |
| Raw features | 0.619118 | 99.78% | 0.227679 | 95.59% |

K1 required macro-F1 at least 0.80, every gas recall at least 0.60, and coverage at least 90%. The proposed interface has zero recall on gas 6, which is present in 467 confirmation examples. K2 required pooled normalized MAE at most 0.10, worst-gas error at most 0.20 and coverage at least 80%. Neither contract is relaxed after observing failure. The recorded reconstruction check has maximum relative error approximately 1.06e−15, so the frame's information-preservation bridge passes. That bridge does not imply useful generalization.

All 20 separately registered recalibration fits fail their relevant contracts. Every zero-filled missing-sensor example in the numerical challenge is accepted, exposing a failure-detection limitation. The tested 1,000-fold gain perturbation and malformed/nonfinite inputs are refused; this does not establish detection of arbitrary physical gain changes. Equal-content reuse and fresh reconstruction tie. K2 remains a diagnostic after K1's failure.

Raw observations and fitted support-vector binaries are excluded from the public repository. ### R3: thermal complementarity within a declared model

K1 delivers 80 kW of useful heat for six hourly intervals, raising a 45°C source toward a 90°C sink. The assumed temperature/approach model gives COP 3.012136, with 159.355335 kWh of electricity used across the six hours. Direct heat recovery alone cannot meet the temperature requirement; electric resistance alone cannot meet the registered electrical limit. Recovery and the heat pump together satisfy the declared demand and conservation constraints.

K2 changes the function to continuity: source heat disappears for two hours, but useful heat must continue and terminal stored energy must be at least 25 kWh. Its separate contract allows 70 kW of electrical input, compared with K1's 40 kW limit, and 150 kW of source heat in the four available hours. Storage capacity is 220 kWh, with 100 kW charge and discharge limits. These changed resources are explicit successor assumptions. The accepted design uses 178.389795 kWh of electricity and ends at the required 25 kWh. Removing storage under that same K2 contract makes the instance infeasible. Ordinary complete co-design may add the same storage and ties.

The checker independently evaluates source consumption, heat/electricity balances, temperature eligibility, COP, equipment limits, storage dynamics, losses and terminal obligation. The original implementation used the wrong numeric tolerance; it was corrected to the **preregistered formula**, `1e-6 + 1e-8 × scale`, which is stricter for the two retained six-hour artifacts. Original artifacts and decisions did not change. The correction is a disclosed recheck of known cases, not new blind confirmation. Original evidence is preserved under `evidence/r3_initial_tolerance/`.

The model has fixed COP, hourly resolution, supplied equipment limits and an LP relaxation that can permit simultaneous charging/discharging. It contains no calibrated equipment map, pressure/transient model, measured plant validation, installation design or industrial safety qualification. The actual artifacts are [K1](evidence/r3/k1.json) and [K2](evidence/r3/k2.json), with the [independent report](evidence/r3/RESULTS.json).

### R5: a retained instrument, with an ordinary explanation

The independent verifier supplies 32 finite, nonnegative, mass-conserving or decaying compartment systems of dimensions 3–6. The constructor observes a callable one-step measurement interface rather than the hidden transition matrix. It acquires the transition behavior with coordinate and mixture experiments, then chooses the least-cost sensor layout in the finite catalogue that meets the source-separation requirement. The successor asks a different question: construct a robust mitigation plan for new protected targets while accounting for removed and decayed mass.

The verifier uses separate loops and finite enumeration to check all 32 identifications and sensor designs and the 25 feasible successor plans. Seven successor cases are infeasible. The no-inheritance arm repeats 240 one-step response queries; retention avoids those queries. Giving an ordinary constructor identical retained observations removes the advantage. Therefore the evidence supports useful retained content, not a superior discovery procedure.

The original [confirmation record](evidence/r5/RESULTS.json) contains model values, sensor and mitigation outputs, and acquisition receipt identities. A separate [retention replay](evidence/r5/retained/INDEX.json) builds 32 runnable instrument objects from those disclosed same systems, matches the original matrices and receipt hashes, and revalidates the original 25 accepted K2 plans. It consumes **another 240 response queries**. It does not add 32 new capabilities or constitute fresh confirmation. This abstraction is not an EPANET hydraulic model or a validated physical water network.

### What the financial experiment does

Each trajectory starts a distinct `JournalAccount`, which owns the mandatory adaptive `Account`. It uses the existing signed native operation path for costs, terms, offers, buyer consent, prefunding, artifact preparation, release/refund, maturity and protection. The new service schema is opt-in and accepts only source-bound thermal artifacts that pass the independent checker. The existing financial engines, authentication, earlier accounts and historical results are unchanged; shared code has only three additive schema dispatch hooks.

Twelve synthetic coalitions of three or four identified members can decide once per annual contract period. Their choices include the offer, a strategic conventional rival, self-service, delay and refusal. Failed or unfunded prior contact does not earn a discounted renewal. Collections, buyer wallets, company cash, profit, reserves and paid costs remain different quantities. Unsupported follow-on financing is refused without adding cash. No account is pooled with another, and hypothetical opening equity is not represented as secured funding.

The native controller chooses the highest-contribution **affordable** offer in the finite menu. Its advance cash test and pending-gross-tax memo are deliberately conservative; the tested capital frontier does not prove a universal minimum capitalization. The tax memo is a spending constraint under the same writer, not a second account or native reserve. Every realized scenario tax is paid immediately after its collection, including post-horizon drainage. Native protections are not charged again as expenses.

The 1,825-day horizon contains five prospectively registered annual decision periods. Daily observations are either native event snapshots or explicitly inferred unchanged spans. A 390-day matched control checked every daily financial field, complete native histories, taxes, collections, cancellations, maturity, annual overhead, successor funding and renewal against explicit daily processing. All 19 frozen-source regression tests passed. The final year is exactly native hours `[35040,43800)`; later settlement drainage is reported separately.

The two accepted thermal artifacts already existed before modeled company time began. Day-180 K2 spending is an acquisition/requalification assumption. Even where its native funding label is earned, it does not prove that real future profits financed the earlier scientific construction. Licensed units reuse two artifacts; summing catalog counts across 24 counterfactual accounts would wrongly turn two scientific artifacts into 48.

## Reproduce and inspect

Use Python 3.12 and a fresh output directory. The numerical and native dependencies are pinned; OS, BLAS, hardware and Python patch versions still affect measured runtime. The full repository checkout is required. The staged `native-source` in workflow artifacts supports financial audit, not the entire five-route distribution.

```bash
python -m pip install --only-binary=:all: -r requirements-portfolio071.txt
python -m unittest discover -s tests -p 'test_portfolio071*.py' -v
python -m portfolio071.reproduce --output /tmp/portfolio071-replay --download-sensor
python -m portfolio071.verify_economics \
  --root /tmp/portfolio071-replay/native-source \
  --campaign /tmp/portfolio071-replay/economics \
  --output /tmp/portfolio071-replay/INDEPENDENT_ECONOMIC_AUDIT.json
```

Alternatively pass `--sensor-archive /absolute/path/uci270.zip` to reuse the exact pinned research archive. Its SHA-256 must be `98fe3a30981a222dd4518fbcc3dddd45d5c0ce9b03ef6dc6fe5cf7a04cfbff5e`. Omitting both sensor flags skips that replay and labels it `NOT_RERUN`; it does not convert historical failure into a pass. `--skip-economics` explicitly skips the native campaign. A source-matched replay of disclosed cases is reproducibility evidence, not another blinded scientific confirmation.

The sequential command above remains valid. To schedule independent native accounts concurrently after scientific reproduction, use a separate fresh output directory and the continuation driver used by CI:

```bash
python -m portfolio071.reproduce \
  --output /tmp/portfolio071-parallel --download-sensor --skip-economics
python -m scripts.replay_portfolio071_parallel \
  --reproduction-output /tmp/portfolio071-parallel
python -m portfolio071.verify_economics \

  --campaign /tmp/portfolio071-parallel/economics \
  --output /tmp/portfolio071-parallel/INDEPENDENT_ECONOMIC_AUDIT.json
```

This changes account scheduling while retaining the frozen financial controller and separate account histories. The scientific `SUMMARY.json` from `--skip-economics` continues to record that skipped stage; financial completion is established by the campaign, orchestration receipt and independent economic audit. The optional `--resume-completed` flag accepts only fully completed, independently audited existing accounts with matching recipes and manifests. It refuses an incomplete account and does not reset or retry one.

For plots, install `matplotlib==3.10.8` in addition to the runtime requirements, then use `python -m portfolio071.plot_results --campaign /tmp/portfolio071-replay/economics --output /tmp/portfolio071-figures`.
