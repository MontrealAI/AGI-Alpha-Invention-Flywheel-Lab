## Route 2 frozen construction

**K1.** Transform a recorded 16-sensor, eight-feature-per-sensor observation into eight directions and eight retained log-amplitudes. The original 128-feature measurement must be reconstructible. Fit a gas-identity predictor without access to future batches.

**K2.** Freeze that representation and fit a concentration predictor. Gas identity is not supplied at prediction time. Identity labels are available only to the independent evaluator for stratification. This is a different computational function on the same historical apparatus; it is not a second physical instrument or independent industrial discovery.

**Purpose bridge.** Keep the amplitude information needed by later concentration questions. A ratio-only transformation is an ablation, not a silently acceptable substitute. In a response model proportional to concentration times unknown gain, different concentration/gain pairs give identical observations. No reference-free software can resolve that ambiguity without additional assumptions or measurements.

**Strong alternatives.** Raw scaled features and fit-only signed-log features receive the same finite SVC/SVR search and tuning batches. Equal-content ordinary reuse gets exactly the same frame; a fresh constructor may reconstruct it. A tie prevents a retained-discovery superiority claim. This finite comparator set is strong enough to challenge the proposed representation, but it does not exhaust published chemometrics or certified hardware alternatives.

**Data separation.** A verifier retrieves the original archive and exposes batches 1–4 for fitting and batch 5 for tuning. Batch 6 confirms K1; batches 7–10 confirm K2. First-twenty-row recalibration is a separately registered, label-costed adaptation arm, scored only on the remaining rows. No confirmation outcomes may alter the frozen model search or acceptance.

**Commercial boundary.** No concentration estimates from this research pipeline authorize a physical safety decision. A future offer would be a qualified calibration or instrument integration package with explicit accuracy, scope, downtime, remedies and maintenance. The research dataset's rights conflict and the unmeasured physical operating envelope prevent an unconditional commercial deployment claim.

## Applied manuscript contracts

The constructor read the full **Transformational Intelligence** and **The World as an Output** manuscripts at the repository's preserved editions. Transformational Intelligence, sections 2, 5–7 and appendix D, motivates the original-purpose bridge, unchanged-authority baseline, frozen comparison and scoped claim record. The World as an Output, sections 2, 4, 6–8 and 12, motivates preservation of later-use information, repeated drift/adaptation, changed-successor scope and explicit unknown physical bounds. Its supplied support and disturbance assumptions are not inferred to hold for chemical sensors.

The organization follows the foundation's bounded-job, explicit-ownership and independent-acceptance principles and the repository's research/OUT guides. The implementation does not reproduce either manuscript's experiments, implement the full organizational architecture, or establish autonomous scientific invention. A new representation with exact reconstruction establishes only that it did not discard measured features; useful drift robustness still has to survive the held-out experiment.

## What changes the investment decision

Route 2 must first survive its frozen engineering thresholds, per-gas failures, coverage requirements, chronological shift and ordinary controls. A failure is a reason to keep the accepted code and measured boundary while rejecting that commercialization premise. A pass is a reason to test an independent instrument and acquire lawful calibration rights, not to assign market share or licensing revenue. A constructive next step must address the particular failed condition—such as missing reference information—without quietly tuning on confirmation data. No raw market-size estimate or illustrative billion-dollar multiplication qualifies a route.
