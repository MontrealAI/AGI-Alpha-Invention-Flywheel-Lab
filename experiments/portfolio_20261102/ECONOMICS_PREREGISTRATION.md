# Portfolio 071: economic construction preregistration

Status: prospective specification, written before the route's confirmatory outcomes.

## Question and scope

Can an accepted shared industrial heat-network design and a voluntary commissioning coalition make a useful project affordable when a single buyer will not commission it? A distinct successor adds thermal storage and peak-shaving analysis. The software artifacts are computational designs. No physical plant, industrial qualification, customer contract, or actual revenue is represented.

The three arrangements considered are single-site purchase, a performance contractor, and a pooled commissioning/licensing consortium. The consortium is selected for a bounded test because individual participation, shared setup, competing offers and prefunding can all be challenged without operating equipment. Performance contracting needs unobserved operational savings and enforceable guarantees; it is retained as an outside option, not presumed available on favorable terms. All industrial capital, construction, safety, permitting and physical verification remain the buyers' additional project costs and unresolved external qualification. They do not become free company-owned assets.

The firm in the native account sells a bounded computational design and support license. Research and development, failed attempts, public-data acquisition, computational production, independent checking, sales, onboarding, rights review, support and taxes are expensed under a declared cost envelope. The USD 15,000 shared research/failure hypothesis includes portfolio selection and R1/R2/R5 work, followed by separately priced K1 construction, independent qualification and K2. It is an assumed complete-campaign deployment allocation, not a token bill or measured labor wage; a tenfold shared-research-cost stress is registered. No capitalization or depreciation is claimed. The included research environment has zero authorized incremental spending; its complete economic resource cost is unknown.

## Scientific prerequisites and counted units

K1 and K2 must be accepted by the independent thermal checker from their source-bound inputs and outputs. The native service adapter accepts only those independently checked artifacts. Their code and checks must be frozen before accounts are opened. The number of actual scientific artifacts is counted separately from modeled licenses of an already accepted design. Repeating a native prepare event is not a new physical experiment, a fabricated installation, or an independent discovery. K2 cannot inherit a physically validated capability from K1 because none is claimed.

## Finite game

The company chooses a technical scope, contract form, coalition, price, and an affordable action. Identified synthetic buyers choose among the offer, a conventional engineering offer, self-service, delay and refusal. A rival can choose from a registered price menu subject to its own cost floor. A supplier accepts only compensation meeting its modeled cost and opportunity hurdle. A financier may refuse proposed capital whose modeled return fails its explicit hurdle. Refusal and non-entry are feasible actions. Price, buyer value, adoption cost, rival cost and supplier quotes are authored scenario inputs, not elicited willingness to pay.

The selection objective is company after-complete-cost expected contribution within the finite supplied action set, subject to voluntary buyer/supplier participation and prefunding. Buyer values of USD32,000–38,000 and prices of USD3,000–18,000 per member are authored design-license hypotheses, not industrial savings or verified buyer budgets. A complete conventional co-design rival may use the same heat pump, recovery and storage construction. An equal-capability/equal-unit-cost rival stress is registered; no residual is established by comparison to resistance heating alone. Company profit, customer benefit, investor return and unrestricted cash are reported separately. A conventional entrepreneur is allowed exactly the same technical, coalition and financing actions. Its optimal value should tie the supplied enumeration; any tie limits a discovery-superiority claim. A finite-search regret certificate does not validate the utilities or establish global game-theoretic optimality. No private-information incentive compatibility is claimed; value understatement is an explicit stress. The operating policy is explicitly myopic finite offer selection, not a proven five-year optimum.

The factorial compares original technical means/original single-buyer commissioning, technical change alone, pooling alone, and both. A zero-valued non-entry decision does not erase the cost of a performed unsuccessful attempt.

The finite one-coalition game uses a separate USD18,000 construction/setup hypothesis. It is a static counterfactual about shared setup, not the full campaign accounting horizon. The native trajectory separately expenses USD15,000 portfolio research/failures, USD16,000 K1 construction, USD6,000 validation, USD3,000 rights review, USD2,000 retirement, annual overhead and any K2 acquisition. Its profit must not be substituted with the static game's value. In the equal-capability rival control, rival integration, expert review, support, acquisition/renewal and the 2% payment fee use exactly the company's line-item formula; it receives no artificially higher complete contract cost.

## Capital and native accounting

Fresh, separately identified initial-equity counterfactuals are USD 25,000; USD 100,000; USD 1 million; USD 10 million; and USD 1 billion. Non-entry is also evaluated. These are assumed received opening capital for conditional simulation, not secured commitments or pooled accounts. Investor-return feasibility is a separate diagnostic and may refuse the larger cases. A promise, receivable or buyer wallet never funds company operations. Follow-on debt and equity operations are unsupported and must be refused; no financing cash is injected through another operation.

Every accepted native action uses `connected_enterprise.finance.JournalAccount`, which owns the mandatory `adaptive_enterprise.account.Account`. Existing engines, authentication, reservation rules, source identity, older accounts and historical outcomes remain unchanged. A prospective opt-in static scientific service schema may add source-bound loader/checker dispatches for fresh accounts only. It may not turn an unaccepted artifact into qualified revenue. If that extension does not independently qualify, retain debit-only research accounts and label commercial arithmetic as projections.

Protected custody, refund and support exposure restricts available cash and is not a duplicate expense. All support charges and losses are expensed once. Retirement/support archive is prepaid at opening, avoiding an unfunded terminal bill or duplicate cost. A conservative controller memo withholds the maximum scenario income tax on pending gross collections from discretionary spending under the mandatory single writer. This memo is not another cash account or a native reserve; native source-bound cash and obligations remain authoritative. On each release, including post-horizon drainage, incurred scenario tax is charged immediately before discretionary actions. Advance commitments are not company collections. Principal is not revenue. Research expenses are financed when incurred. Seed remains usable throughout. An earned-only K2 control uses native unrestricted-earned funds and refuses when insufficient; it must not silently fall back to seed. The actual scientific artifacts predate the financial run; simulated day180 K2 spending models acquisition/requalification and does not claim real earnings funded their earlier creation.

## Horizon, population, controls and stopping

The operating horizon is 1,825 days, exactly 43,800 native hours from 2026-11-02T00:00:00Z. The `day` field in native actions means integer hours. The final rolling-year interval is `[35,040,43,800)`, with exact calendar dates reported by the observer. Never annualize an exit period. All 365 daily observations in that window must be evaluated or explicitly inferred from a complete unchanged-state span; terminal settlement drainage does not extend the operating horizon.

There are twelve uniquely named synthetic buyer coalitions, each with three or four identified members. Each modeled annual contract has one renewal decision, with fresh comparison to competing offers. First contact, onboarding, support, renewal review, rights and customer-specific integration have explicit costs. The finite population is the registered small discrimination test, not a market-size claim or universal business cap. No buyer, cohort or license is counted twice. An uncontacted buyer is not available for free acquisition. No real organization is represented by an identifier.

The capital frontier holds the base world fixed. Adversaries use USD100,000 and include no demand, low willingness to pay, rival improvement after1,6,12,18 and36 months, a stable cost gap, failed scientific qualification, lower usable yield, costly maintenance, collection delay, escrow cancellation/refund, refused external finance, supplier refusal, rights refusal, the equal-capability rival, and10×shared research costs. Escrow cancellation is not unsecured receivable default. Include a technically successful but unprofitable case and a positive-margin contract that cannot be funded before payment. Compare a working-capital-aware controller against a stylized prepaid reach/capacity policy in the same world. The latter buys affordable identified first-year capacity at the same contact/integration/review/support/fee quotes before collateral checks; subsequent use does not charge it again, and unused prepayments are lost. It is not represented as an exact replay of a historical controller. The aware controller chooses the highest-contribution offer among presently affordable prices, withholding pending tax commitments. Discounted renewal applies only after the previous year's collected license; failed/unfunded prior contacts need paid reacquisition. No posterior seed selection is allowed.

The registered native denominator is 24 fresh trajectories: five central capital comparisons, seventeen additional adverse/control worlds at USD 100,000, one earned-only successor control, and one prepaid-capacity control. The capital comparison's financier return test is an ex-post diagnostic, not a demonstrated prior funding commitment. The non-entry alternative is evaluated in the finite game. A separate stationary frontier reports the number of annual contracts arithmetically needed for USD 1B and USD 3B under each price, collection and variable-cost hypothesis; it includes setup, fixed overhead and scenario taxes, assumes no market share, and claims neither a financed ramp nor native revenue. It is not pooled with any executed account.

Stop a trajectory on native insolvency, investment-stop state, exhausted admitted demand, or completion of the registered horizon. Preserve accrued obligations and expense tails. A recoverable implementation error is recorded and corrected without altering the acceptance contract or selecting a favorable world. No result authorizes a physical installation, outreach, fundraising or spending.
