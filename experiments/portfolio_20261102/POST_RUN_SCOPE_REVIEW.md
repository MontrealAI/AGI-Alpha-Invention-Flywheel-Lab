The one-project factorial's USD 18,000 setup allowance and the native trajectory's USD 42,000 initial cost envelope concern different accounting scopes. They are not interchangeable profitability estimates. Similarly, a game-theoretic opportunity with positive modeled surplus can fail the native prefunding requirement. The five-year controller is explicitly myopic; exhaustive choice at each offer is not a proof of globally optimal five-year strategy.

The static factorial is also a restricted participation comparison: its supplied outside surplus is zero. The native commercial choice additionally evaluates self-service and a profit-maximizing rival. Static joint feasibility therefore does not imply that participants prefer the offer once those stronger outside options are admitted. The controller's prefunding calculation is a conservative sufficient budget test. Refusal under that policy is not a proof of the minimum capital required under every permissible price, financing, collateral or operating arrangement.

The frozen denominator is five central capital comparisons, seventeen additional adverse/control worlds at USD 100,000, one earned-only K2 control, and one prepaid-capacity control: 24 fresh trajectories. Each has 1,825 scheduled days, or 43,800 native hours. Idle-day observations may be inferred only where the single writer has no native event; the reported 390-day explicit-versus-inferred development comparison is distinct from the final frozen-source qualification. The economic freeze records 19 development cases reported as passed and a separate concurrent-edit refusal. It requires an exact frozen-source rerun; this review does not substitute those reported development results for that rerun.

The stationary market frontier is an additional conditional calculation, not another native account. It reports annual contract counts needed for the two profit targets under price, collection and variable-cost hypotheses, while assuming no market share or funded expansion. Its USD 62,000 setup-and-annual-fixed allowance includes one annual overhead charge and all scheduled research/K1/checking/rights/K2/retirement costs. Neither that frontier nor the finite population demonstrates qualified demand. Source-bound account records must establish realized costs, collections, profit, available cash, support restrictions and post-horizon obligations.

The conservative pending-tax memo constrains discretionary spending; it is not an additional native cash balance or reserve. Escrow cancellation/refund is distinct from unsecured receivable default. Discounted renewal requires a previously collected license. Finally, K2's scientific artifact existed before the economic campaign: modeled day-180 acquisition/requalification cannot establish that later real earnings financed its earlier creation.
