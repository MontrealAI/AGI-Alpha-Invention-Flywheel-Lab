# Reliable computational fragments: constructed, but no Super Alpha earned

The prototype now produces exact linear matrix products and quadratic Gram products despite the loss of any one of three declared worker domains. The decisive comparison rejects the proposed extraordinary advantage: established coded computation matches the construction, and ordinary replication requires fewer workers for the nonlinear successor. The result is a reusable, bounded computational instrument and a reason to preserve capital, not a new industry or commercial success.

## What actually runs

`fabric.py` constructs a placement using the task's polynomial degree and declared domain capacity, encodes three source blocks over F65537, executes matrix operations, and decodes returned results. It emits executable plans by bounded enumeration, rather than selecting a stored answer. This is developer-written implementation of established algebra and ordinary optimization, not autonomous scientific discovery.

`reference.py` independently implements direct arithmetic and an exhaustive conventional placement optimizer. Replication executes actual worker computations and the same complete result is checked. The main demonstration uses three 16x16 blocks. The two exhaustive erasure comparisons use3x3 blocks and all 128 subsets of seven returned workers. No hosted distributed workers, actual deadlines, floating-point training, secrecy, Byzantine correction or physical properties were tested. Returned corruption is caught by full local recomputation; its work is counted. The encoding is unmasked and provides no confidentiality.

| Same one-domain-loss requirement | Constructed coded workers | Conventional coded workers | Replicated workers |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Linear products | 5 | 5 | 6 |
| Quadratic Gram products | 8 | 8 | 6 |

The linear coded construction uses 16.7% fewer worker tasks than replication, before encoding, decoding, transfer, verification and all business costs. For the quadratic successor it uses 33.3% more. The conventional coded route ties exactly. Neither percentage is measured money, network speedup, end-to-end latency or an AI-versus-human productivity result.

Across all 256 erasure subsets, the linear task has 99 correctly decoded subsets and 29 refusals; the quadratic task has 29 correctly decoded subsets and 99 refusals. These exhaustive counts describe a finite possibility set, **not reliability probabilities**. Domain-aware plans pass all three single-domain outages for both tasks; ordinary replication also passes. Two lost domains make the quadratic coded plan infeasible within the registered three-by-three supplier slots. This is a finite supplier constraint, not a universal scale cap.

Eleven focused tests cover these outcomes, job/cache/field binding, changed operations, duplicates, insufficient returns, malformed elements and corruption. Self-consistent cache hashes alone do not authenticate data: a forged cache passes the cache identity check but fails independent computation. This limits the trust claim and demonstrates why verification cannot be omitted from costs.

## Preserved failed evaluation and successor evidence

The first test run had 9 passes and 1 failure: `SUCCESSOR_COUNTEREXAMPLE_MISSING`. The initial generated blocks varied affinely across the three source indices, making their actual Gram-output polynomial degree low enough for three returns. The negative witness was therefore invalid for that input family. That failure is retained here and the original affine fixture remains an explicit favorable-region regression. A second-degree source-block term was added prospectively for the full-degree case; the protocol's outcome, comparison and acceptance thresholds were unchanged. Revised evaluation passed 11/11. This is a disclosed fixture repair, not an untouched holdout, independent external replication or retroactive pass.

On the full-degree case, inheriting the predecessor's three-return threshold produces a wrong quadratic result. The current degree-aware contract refuses that invalid inheritance. Retaining the predecessor's encoded data has a separate, useful effect:

| Successor arm | New encoded shards | Encoding scalar multiplications | New outbound field elements |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| No retained data | 8 | 6,144 | 2,048 |
| Predecessor data retained | 3 | 2,304 | 768 |
| Equal-content ordinary reuse | 3 | 2,304 | 768 |

This is a 62.5% saving in **successor encoding**, excluding predecessor acquisition/construction, storage retention, computation, decoding, verification, network performance and other costs. Total initial-plus-successor encoding is 3,840+2,304=6,144 multiplications. All three arms deliver identical results. The causal effect is retained content/capability; better discovery is not established. The stronger replicated successor was also executed successfully. No earnings finance this transition: it is research funded by the original registered capital.

## Complete offer and financial boundary

The proposed buyer receives a dataset-bound exact finite-field result, named operation, deadline, declared failure-domain coverage, independent acceptance, delivery/support obligations and refund/default terms. A buyer may instead choose specialist coded computation, competent human conventional implementation, human-plus-AI, another autonomous provider, reliable self-service or no purchase. Those human and provider services were not run; no superiority over them is claimed. The strongest actually executed controls are conventional coded placement/direct arithmetic and replication.

No price, real supplier quote, probability of timely delivery, buyer value, acquisition reach, retention, tax jurisdiction, enforceable guarantee or workload demand has been observed. No buyer reservation values are passed to the constructor. There are no simulated qualified orders because the service has neither a demonstrated complete advantage nor native sale admission. Existing native fixture services initialize the accounting engine only; none is sold or relabeled as coded work.

A cost frontier makes the missing economic evidence explicit. Let `w` be a quoted complete worker-task fee, `s` a local scalar-multiplication charge, `t` a transferred field-element charge, `V` independent verification cost, and `H` acquisition, onboarding, orchestration, delivery, support, maintenance, default/refund expense, tax, financing and allocated construction cost. These are symbolic prices, not estimates. Field-operation counts exclude interpolation coefficient generation, additions, memory, scheduling and transport overhead; include those separately in H before making a price claim.

- Linear coding: `5w + 6144s + 2560t + V + H`; replication: `6w + 3072t + V + H`. If the remaining burdens are equal, coding wins only when `w + 512t > 6144s`.
- Retained quadratic coding: `8w + 6144s + 2816t + V + H + retained-storage-cost`; fresh replication: `6w + 3072t + V + H`. The network saving must exceed `2w + 6144s + retained-storage-cost`, assuming equal other burdens. Ordinary cached replication could further improve its comparator and must be allowed in a deployment study.
- Reliable local production uses 12,288 scalar multiplications before its own validation and complete service costs. Coding's direct checker itself uses 12,288. This tiny local demonstration does not establish a rational buyer need for outsourced execution.

Buyer choice applies to the whole offer. A feasible price must cover all costs and leave the buyer at least as well off as its complete outside option including switching friction. All suppliers must prefer their exact terms to their outside options. Collection delay, refunds, supplier prepayment and reserves determine cash affordability independently of accounting profit. Missing price or demand evidence stays missing; the correct current action is no entry. Commerce-only, capability-only and joint expansion have not been compared as full commercial policies, so no strategy-optimality claim is made. Rival access to the same public algorithm defeats any assumed algorithmic moat.

## Decision executed and next discriminating step

The ordinary replicated successor has been executed and checked against all registered domain losses. Retain conventional coded computation only where its complete measured costs beat replication; retain the stronger ordinary alternative elsewhere. The current primary retains USD97,500 and has no qualified offer or earned-funded expansion.

Reopen this route only for a source-grounded workload where data rights, independent acceptance, real domain correlation, bandwidth/storage, task pricing and complete buyer outside options expose a residual the incumbent cannot already serve. That needs a permitted public trace or benchmark with those relevant measurements; a larger synthetic sweep cannot supply them. This probe does not close the search over Super Alpha. It establishes which construction works, which apparent advantage does not survive, and what evidence is necessary before risking commercial expansion.
