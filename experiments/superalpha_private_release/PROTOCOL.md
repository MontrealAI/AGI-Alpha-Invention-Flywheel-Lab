## Three different possibilities

1. **Self-building laboratory cooperatives.** We could make new experiments accessible to laboratories without dedicated automation engineers by constructing transferable instrument skills and a jointly funded validation service, overcoming repeated integration. The strongest inspected alternative is [Self-Building Labs](https://self-building-labs.github.io/), with physical trials, reusable skills and human oversight. Proposed advantage: lower complete integration and revalidation burden on a new lab. Falsifier: competent ordinary skill reuse delivers the same accepted experiment within the same resources. Capture: a share of independently accepted integration savings. Physical transfer evidence and willingness to pay are missing; a simulator alone cannot establish the gap. Defer, do not claim incumbents already solve every laboratory.

2. **Private rare-case discovery consortium (selected discovery step).** We could make cross-institution rare-case screening accessible to institutions unwilling to expose one family's contribution by constructing encrypted computation plus a release arrangement, overcoming output disclosure as well as input access. Strong alternatives: [PRISM](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btaf468), ordinary secure computation with consent/query governance, and differential privacy. Proposed advantage: useful jointly released findings under a recipient-specific family privacy promise. Falsifier: the promised utility conflicts with that privacy contract, or a standard release mechanism matches it. Capture: a fixed fee for an accepted complete screen, retaining most value for participants. Buyer privacy requirements and demand are **authored hypotheses**, not observations.

3. **Legacy equipment continuation through reconstructed software.** We could keep valuable discontinued equipment usable by constructing executable behavioral models and verified replacements, overcoming missing source/specifications. The strongest inspected research alternative combines control-flow reconstruction and guided symbolic execution in an [industrial automotive study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.22673v1). Proposed advantage: complete accepted replacement under missing specifications, beyond function documentation. Falsifier: conventional engineering plus existing symbolic tools meets the complete acceptance contract, or hidden hardware behavior invalidates equivalence. Capture: acceptance-contingent engineering fee. Neither a binary fixture nor physical acceptance authority is available; defer. This would replace our present service model entirely.

These are candidate statements, not novelty, demand or superiority findings. The previous coded-compute experiment tied ordinary codes and ordinary reuse; do not reopen it. Recent rack-codec PR184 independently recommends stopping that direction. No winner is manufactured.

## Decisive contract and construction

Test a necessary condition before constructing encryption infrastructure. All other families are fixed. One family's possible contribution changes a screening answer between two states; the successor changes two indicators jointly between four states. Every pair is a permitted replacement of this one family. Each state has a distinct correct complete answer. This synthetic subproblem is deliberately harder than hiding data only from a server: **the recipient's released answer** must obey pure differential privacy, `P(y|i) <= lambda * P(y|j)` for all adjacent states, including refusal. It is not a claim that PRISM promises this contract or is cryptographically insecure.

Generate an entire stochastic output channel by linear programming, including a refusal output, maximizing worst-state probability of the correct complete answer. Execute/verify the resulting distribution exactly through probabilities, not sampled success counts. Compare exact release (including encrypted exact release's output semantics), no release, consent-authorized exact release (different privacy contract), and ordinary generalized randomized response. Use public state/adjacency/utility definitions only; no actual patient data, buyer draws or hidden labels enter a policy. Classification: developer-written construction, **ordinary optimization**, not autonomous scientific discovery.

Predeclared likelihood-ratio bounds: 1.1, 2, 10, 19, 57. No single epsilon is declared clinically acceptable. An illustrative whole-offer requirement is 95% correct on every state; this is an authored screening SLA, not medical validation. Also report the complete continuous analytic frontier, so the decision does not depend on that example. Exact zero-error release is a separate condition. Check LP results against a separately implemented closed-form upper bound and achievable ordinary channel; tolerate numerical errors of at most 1e-8. If a bound and optimizer disagree, stop as inconclusive and repair before interpreting results.

Successor: release the pair as one package; compare joint optimization, independent randomized response with evenly divided privacy budget, ordinary equal-content joint reuse, and direct construction without the predecessor. Separate numerical gains from a weaker factorized comparator from discovery-method gains. No elapsed CPU time becomes human labor. No unavailable human, human-plus-AI, specialist or frontier-model comparator is claimed beaten.

Advance only if there is an accepted complete contract with a residual advantage over the strongest ordinary method and a plausible voluntary arrangement. Stop this strict offer if a necessary condition fails. If ordinary release ties, retain it; do not run a larger sweep. A consent/governance pivot changes the contract and requires separate evidence of participating institutions' acceptance, unavailable within current no-outreach permissions. It cannot retroactively pass this test.

## Complete offer and funding boundary

The hypothesized order comprises input rights/onboarding, encrypted computation, authorized release, independent checking, corrections, delivery, support, refund/default terms and collection after acceptance. Privacy and accuracy apply to the **whole package**, not separately attractive features. No price is supported before acceptance; no orders or collections will be invented. Known methods have ordinary acquisition, integration, governance and operating burdens; their actual complete prices are unknown. No entry remains feasible. Failed acceptance ends commercialization in this experiment; growth, rival response, retention and a US$3 billion path are therefore unqualified rather than populated with invented demand.

Prepaid authored simulation envelope: research $300; construction $300; verification $150; failed attempts/corrections $50; inference/tools/host/maintenance $100 = **$900**. It includes this finite investigation, not a production service. Book at simulation hours 5–9 through existing JournalAccount and adaptive Account, preserving the original contract and source binding. These are assumptions, not measured bills, staff wages or free compute. Actual economic cost is unknown. No sale, capital purchase, debt, tax credit or future obligation is created; no positive taxable profit. Stop after the registered finite cases and analytic check; do not expand resources to manufacture an advantage.

Keep only source, small deterministic evidence, one native public continuation record and this concise decision. Preserve prior evidence privately/publicly where it already exists. Require original journal/action prefixes unchanged, read-only independent audit, source-bound restart and no duplicate costs. Native observer must report insufficient 365-day history, never annualize these hours. Reuse OUT-LEARN's evidence-calibration principle ([practical paper map](../../docs/harness/OUT_PAPERS.md)): its concrete dependency here is deciding whether the promised output is feasible; the executable probability constraints, not the paper name, supply the test.
