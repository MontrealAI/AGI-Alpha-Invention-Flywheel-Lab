# Verified interoperation: the retained constructor did not beat ordinary reuse

**Decision: prefer the ordinary constructor within this tested synthetic domain.**
The retained constructor completed 56 of 192 K2 schedule challenges; the
equal-content ordinary constructor completed 60. Both produced zero harmful scored
outcomes. The preregistered retained-method advantage criterion failed. A positive
demand simulation was profitable for both methods, but the weak and adverse
regions lost money. No newly enabling industry or broader Blue Sky breakthrough
is admitted by this result.

## The valuable outcome being tested

The proposed arrangement supplies a complete digital outcome through several
independent providers. Its consequential hypothesis is that autonomous construction
and qualification could make temporary production arrangements worthwhile when
bespoke integration would otherwise consume their value. An isolated successful
call does not meet a purchaser's requirement for the whole bundle.

Three materially different arrangements were compared before selection: verified
interoperation construction, confidential joint computation, and verified
opportunistic computation. The first offered a directly executable acceptance
test using permitted tools. The others lacked a sufficiently specific, testable
residual beyond strong existing alternatives. No deployment was a separate
reference. [CANDIDATES.md](CANDIDATES.md) records the beneficiaries, alternatives,
constraints, costs and primary research sources. Its hypotheses are not evidence
of actual willingness to pay.

## K1: evidence changed the executable construction

The initial explanations were a request lost before an effect and an effect
completed without an acknowledgment. Actual development probes exposed conditions
that those two explanations alone could not represent.

| Inspected K1 observation | Consequence for construction |
| --- | --- |
| A timeout followed by `ABSENT` coexisted with one committed effect; replaying the same token created a second effect. | Treat stale measurement and non-idempotent replay as additional mechanisms. A response-only retry can cause harm. |
| A fence reported `ABSENT` after a genuinely lost request, `DONE` after an effect, and `ABSENT` after effective compensation. | Construct and qualify a reconciliation branch that distinguishes these states before another effect. |
| Waiting eight ticks changed a stale query into `DONE`, while cancellation of the effect was refused. | Bounded delayed observation can be useful within its tested timing contract; compensation cannot be assumed. |
| Cancellation on an empty namespace succeeded even where an existing effect could not be undone. | Qualify compensation after an effect. An empty-state check is insufficient. |

These are synthetic sandbox observations with independently exposed effect and
hold counts. Production receives responses only. The evidence is retained in
[K1_DEVELOPMENT.json](K1_DEVELOPMENT.json) and
[K1_ADDITIONAL_PROBES.json](K1_ADDITIONAL_PROBES.json).

An included model agent read the observations, requested discriminating probes,
and generated an executable constructor. Its frozen call path is
`treatment.construct → costed probes → profile expansion → graph construction → independent replay`.
The retained artifact creates request-identity, reconciliation, compensation and
reservation branches, with explicit unresolved obligations. This is actual
one-time model-produced code followed by deterministic reuse. It is not an online
model generator, a change to the assistant's weights, or general internal model
self-improvement. [DEVELOPMENT_TRACE.json](DEVELOPMENT_TRACE.json) separates the
observations, proposed change, executable path and development costs.

## K2: a different acceptance problem and a strong comparison

K1 requires exactly one effect at one provider. K2 requires exactly one effect at
**every complementary provider**, while global time advances across the whole
bundle. Mixed semantics, expiry, delays, observation gaps and partial completion
make a successful K1 adapter insufficient by itself.

Confirmation contained exactly 48 paired K2 worlds: six authored composition
families, eight frozen seeds each, and four precommitted challenges per world.
The challenges covered healthy operation, lost productive requests, lost
acknowledgments, and a hidden seed-bound provider refusing new work. The target of
refusal was corrected before freeze so that provider position could not reveal it.
No failed case was replaced and no treatment was changed after these outcomes.

The ordinary constructor was independently authored using the same K1 observations,
substantive retained knowledge, operation rights and resource envelope. It uses
conformance tests, conventional reconciliation, compensation and prepared commits,
finite role assignment, and qualification of replay at reachable global-clock
phases. It is the equal-content ablation of the retained procedure. It is a strong
bounded conventional implementation; it is not a run of full LearnLib or cvc5 and
does not establish superiority over every feasible engineering method.

Both methods had a $24 probe allowance per world, at most 48 production calls and
256 graph nodes. Different probe histories were allowed. Construction contexts and
model token costs were not matched or metered, so the estimand is the difference
between these frozen executable procedures, not a causal claim about general
model learning or measured AI-versus-human productivity. The exact and normalized
duplicate checks found zero duplicates and zero normalized K1/K2 overlap. The
cases still share one authored simulator. Separation between source authors and
the evaluator was procedural on a shared host, not external experimental blinding.

| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Complete delivered schedules | 56 / 192 | 60 / 192 |
| Safe abort schedules | 136 / 192 | 132 / 192 |
| Harmful schedules | 0 | 0 |
| Worlds with at least one accepted delivery | 20 / 48 | 20 / 48 |
| Worlds safe on all four challenges | 48 / 48 | 48 / 48 |
| Valid constructed programs | 48 / 48 | 48 / 48 |
| Total modeled probe cost over 48 worlds | $759.60 | $669.75 |
| Total modeled operation cost over all 192 challenges | $92.80 | $116.70 |

Safe abort earns no delivery credit. Equal counts of worlds with some delivery do
not imply equal delivered outcomes. Probe cost is charged once per request in the
primary metric; operation cost is averaged over its four challenge schedules.
The operation totals in the table therefore should not be added to probe totals
as though they were a single realized enterprise account.

The primary metric valued a safely accepted complete delivery at a hypothetical
$600 and charged the declared probe, operation and residual-loss costs. The mean
**retained minus ordinary contribution was −$14.2473958333 per K2 request**.
The descriptive 95% family-cluster bootstrap interval was
**[−$40.7989583333, $0.3828125]**. The preregistered requirement was a lower endpoint
above **+$3**, with no increase in harmful schedules. It was not met.

The $3 threshold amortizes an authored $300 incremental deployment burden over
100 non-guaranteed future opportunities. The interval uses 4,096 deterministic
resamples of six family means. It describes sensitivity to the authored families;
it is not a calibrated market probability or evidence from independent providers.
Because the interval includes zero, this is not a general statistical proof that
the ordinary method is superior. Its observed result supports preferring it within
this finite comparison and rejecting the treatment's claimed material advantage.

### What the offer, costs and outside options mean

Every simulated offer specified an accepted complete outcome, correction/refund
handling, collection timing and 48-hour support. Buyer budgets and benefits,
supplier costs and outside surplus, competing quotes, delayed collection and
voluntary refusal were authored separately. The seller used public quotes and
observed accept/refuse results, not hidden values or future outcomes. Human with
conventional software, human-plus-AI, autonomous and self-service alternatives were
separately represented. Their prices are scenario inputs, not measured labor
productivity or actual willingness to pay.

All incurred costs for research and construction, qualification, acquisition,
onboarding, probes, supplier capacity, production, independent verification,
support, maintenance, payment charges and declared tax provisions are charged.
Refunded orders earn no revenue; refunds and failed work remain in the records
without counting returned deposit principal as an additional expense. The common $1,200 opening research envelope includes all development
attempts and shared evidence; $100 covers economic-model construction. Actual
measured development probe/operation units are recorded inside that envelope,
not charged twice. The retained method additionally pays the hypothetical $300
K2 deployment charge, or $900 under the adverse multiplier. This premium is a
sensitivity assumption, not a measured difference in model effort.

Production purchases a nonrefundable 48-call lot per provider before customer
funding. Unused capacity remains paid. The 20% positive-profit tax provision is an
authored accounting scenario. No capitalized research asset, uncharged amortization,
debt or cash interest is used. Equity opportunity cost is a separate noncash
sensitivity. Actual model/host bills and human delivery hours are unknown, and
CPU seconds are not converted into invented human labor.

At three times the reported expenses with revenue held fixed, positive-region
profit becomes **−$4,023.60 retained / −$3,262.68 ordinary**. This is a static cost
stress, not a forecast that buyers and competitors would remain unchanged.
Weak and adverse operation was worse than retaining cash after common research.
Those losses remain in the result.

### What K1 actually financed

The positive retained account had $2,881.45 in unrestricted earned funds after K1
settlement, before an actual native **EARNED** reservation of $300 for K2 deployment.
It subsequently accepted three K2 deliveries. This establishes an executed
conditional financing link and use of retained code within the same account.

It does **not** establish that the retained procedure was necessary: equal-content
ordinary reuse accepted the same three K2 orders without that incremental charge
and earned more profit. Weak and adverse deployment used seed-backed funds, earned
no accepted revenue and lost money. Those cases are not earned-financed expansion.

## Adoption and the next consequential decision

**Adopt ordinary reuse in the registered positive demand case; choose no deployment
in the weak and adverse cases.** Withhold the retained-method superiority claim and
further scale claims. Preserve the useful reconciliation and obligation-handling
tools and their applicability limits. [ADOPTION.json](evidence/ADOPTION.json) records
this post-confirmation choice among the unchanged registered alternatives.

The next worthwhile observation is whether a materially valuable complete outcome
exists that the strongest existing route cannot already supply at an acceptable
complete cost, and whether beneficiaries would voluntarily accept its terms.
Another favorable synthetic sweep cannot settle that fact. Any new construction
must use a separately frozen fresh comparison; these exposed cases remain
development evidence for subsequent work.

The 141-day finite cohort is not a trailing-365-day business, and no short-run
annualization or market multiplier is used. The US$3 billion trailing-year profit
objective remains unachieved. The broader Blue Sky opportunity remains open and
unresolved; this bounded experiment closes one retained-method superiority claim.
