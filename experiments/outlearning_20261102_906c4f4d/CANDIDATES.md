# Candidate selection: verified interoperation construction

Campaign: **OUTLEARN-20261102-906c4f4d**. Selection recorded before fresh confirmation.
This document is a prospective research judgment, not a claim of discovered demand,
novel industry, delivered profit or retained-method superiority. All operation is
simulated. Existing campaign source, accounts and holdouts are outside this scope.

## Consequential possibility and reversible decision

The selected hypothesis is that autonomous intelligence can make short-lived,
cross-provider production arrangements worthwhile when constructing and qualifying
each bespoke interface would otherwise consume the arrangement's value. A buyer
wants a complete useful outcome; individual successful calls are intermediate work.
The means being tested is a procedure that acquires service semantics, constructs
an executable interoperation policy, checks its limits, and retains useful
construction knowledge for a different successor outcome.

The decision is whether to fund qualification and offer the registered complete
outcome using this retained procedure, use the competent ordinary procedure, revise
the arrangement, or withhold deployment. The experiment can reject the proposed
discovery advantage without rejecting ordinary profitable commerce. The finite
prototype tests a necessary enabling component of the wider possibility. It does
not establish an unattended supply network or a commercially new industry.

The ranking uses enabling importance, credibility and whether an available test
changes the stated decision. It uses no fabricated market size, willingness to pay
or numerical success probability. The selected arrangement replaces the inherited
service catalogue for this campaign; the catalogue is not a boundary on discovery.

### No-deploy reference

Preserve available cash, incur actual inspection and research costs, and make no
sales or delivery claims. The beneficiary uses its best existing alternative or
foregoes the outcome. Failed-search costs remain incurred. This is a fourth
reference, not one of the three productive arrangements. Missing actual customers
does not force no-deploy in all explicitly conditional simulated demand regions.

## Selected construction and the strongest objection

K1 starts with competing explanations for an ambiguous observation: a request may
have failed before an effect, or an effect may have occurred without a reliable
acknowledgment. Neither explanation may be adequate. A persistent identity, epoch,
reservation rule or another mechanism can require an expanded representation.
Costed observations may resolve the ambiguity, be too expensive or remain noisy.
An unsafe intervention can create duplicate effects or unreconciled obligations.

A generated adapter cannot grant a provider idempotency, visibility or compensation
that the provider does not possess. When the registered environment lacks a usable
means, decline, defer or construct a permitted alternative arrangement. The
independent evaluator owns the hidden transitions and acceptance definition; the
candidate cannot rewrite them. All interface changes have the same costs and
rights in both methods.

The strongest objection is that existing engineering already supplies the
essential mechanisms. LearnLib and ALEX infer stateful service behavior; synthesis
systems construct implementations from specifications and counterexamples; mature
transaction patterns handle retries and partial execution. The comparison therefore
cannot be a sophisticated treatment against blind retries. A conventional tie
rejects superiority over that comparator. It can still leave a useful tool or a
conditional business, provided complete economics and acceptance support them.

## Ordinary baseline and knowledge boundary

`baseline.py` is an ordinary, model-agent-authored implementation of established
system-identification and protocol-engineering ideas. Its author inspected the
public interface, development observations and the same substantive retained K1
content made available to the treatment. The author must not inspect hidden
confirmation state or evaluator source. The baseline may actively gather evidence,
construct a competent protocol, use permitted extensions, adapt under its frozen
rule, and decline unsafe or uneconomic work.

This small implementation is **not** a run of the full LearnLib, ALEX or cvc5
systems. Its precise finite search, probe and action coverage is specified by its
source and protocol. Any superiority conclusion is limited to this comparator,
information set, action space and charged resource envelope. The primary human
productivity reference remains competent humans using conventional software;
writing this baseline with a model does not measure human labor. Human-plus-AI,
autonomous competitors and self-service remain separately labeled alternatives.

The baseline and treatment receive the same K1 facts, validated artifacts, initial
observations, rights and execution envelope. The distinctive retained procedure is
the treatment. Different observations caused by adaptive action selection are
permitted. A no-procedure ablation preserves information and removes the retained
executable procedure. An arm must not obtain free development, extra test feedback,
unreported human intervention or access to a hidden scenario label.

## Economics and scale boundaries

The complete offer and account protocol must precede production. Charge research,
probes, failed construction, acquisition, provider onboarding, production,
verification, correction, maintenance, support, collection friction and all
remaining obligations. Record measured command resources separately from assumed
economic cost envelopes and unknown actual billing. CPU duration is not human
labor. Included assistant access is not evidence of zero economic cost.

Use the canonical native account for actual simulated commitments and settlement;
this candidate document introduces no balance or financial authority. Delayed
collections are not available funds. A liability surviving technical timeout
survives the economic report. Capability transfer and earned financing are separate
claims: K1 is not said to finance K2 unless the native record shows that link.

Positive, weak and adverse demand regions must permit voluntary no-purchase,
competent outside options, finite reach, lead times and changing competitive terms.
Their parameters are scenario assumptions, not measured demand. Report first-use
construction economics and any amortized result over a stated non-guaranteed
volume. Do not multiply a short cohort into a billion-dollar profit claim. The
US$3 billion trailing-year milestone requires dated complete-cost operating events
and remains unestablished by this selection.

## Public primary sources for alternatives

- [LearnLib](https://learnlib.de/) and [ALEX](https://learnlib.de/pages/alex): active
  automata learning, conformance testing and learning of web/API behavior.
- [Syntax-Guided Synthesis, Microsoft Research](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/syntax-guided-synthesis/)
  and [cvc5](https://cvc5.github.io/): existing specification-, grammar- and
  counterexample-based executable construction.
- [AWS Builders' Library: idempotent APIs](https://aws.amazon.com/builders-library/making-retries-safe-with-idempotent-APIs/):
  ambiguous acknowledgments, caller request identities and atomic recording of
  identity with effects. These mechanisms are conventional prior art.
- [Microsoft: compensating transactions](https://github.com/MicrosoftDocs/architecture-center/blob/main/docs/patterns/compensating-transaction.md):
  domain-specific compensation, retained recovery state, and cases where retry
  suffices or compensation cannot restore a valid state.
- [MP-SPDZ](https://mp-spdz.readthedocs.io/en/latest/) and its
  [protocol guide](https://mp-spdz.readthedocs.io/en/latest/readme.html): implemented
  MPC protocols with different threat models and preprocessing costs. Its guide
  cautions that implementation is not production security review.
- [BOINC replication](https://github.com/BOINC/boinc/wiki/Job-replication) and
  [scientist guidance](https://boinc.berkeley.edu/central/scientist.php): established
  redundant validation and the distinction between throughput and turnaround.

These sources motivate mechanisms and strong alternatives. They are not evidence
that this campaign has met acceptance, improved discovery, acquired customers or
earned profit.
