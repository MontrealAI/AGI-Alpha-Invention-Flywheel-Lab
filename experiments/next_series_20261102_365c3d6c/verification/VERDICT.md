All six compact spatial witnesses matched the exact rational stress reference within `2.132e-14 MPa`. With the declared eigenstrain equal to minus the elastic compliance applied to stress and zero displacement, the construction satisfies the stated constitutive and equilibrium model. It is an explicit smooth, finite-energy model witness, not an experimentally observed stress field or a fitted NIST specimen.

The three offset controls show that a mean-corrected score measures variation while discarding uniform offsets. This is the original benchmark's declared comparison purpose; the public raw strain estimates themselves are **not** claimed to be mean-erased or incorrectly measured.

### Why physical admission remains HOLD

[N3_MEASUREMENT_SCOPE.md](N3_MEASUREMENT_SCOPE.md) independently examines NIST's benchmark description, detailed primary methods, dataset metadata, and README. Public EDD observations are diffraction strains along slightly tilted directions, with finite and orientation-dependent gauge support. Diffraction elastic constants and stress-free spacing are material/measurement-specific; the generic isotropic `E = 200000 MPa`, `nu = 0.3` model is not an installed calibration for IN718. Matching nominal coordinates does not establish common measurement kernels. The neutron three-axis measurements concern a different specimen, and references to contour work do not by themselves provide matched raw independent truth.

Consequently, the source admission is **one dataset-level prerequisite HOLD with zero raw EDD rows opened**. The published count of 2,248 locations is metadata, not 2,248 independently executed rejection cases. No local truth, measured maximum stress, or physical defect threshold has been validated. A third matched directional measurement can resolve a normal-component ambiguity under the model; it does not automatically resolve spatial averaging, unknown shears, diffraction calibration, or the transfer to a pointwise target. Pointwise admission needs support-appropriate independent truth or additional justified spatial restrictions.
