## What the source screen changes

The [measurement-scope review](../verification/N3_MEASUREMENT_SCOPE.md) found that the checked EDD filenames are same-hash aliases, not separate references; the inspected inventory did not expose independent numerical contour/neutron truth. Neutron measurements describe a different specimen. This is a bounded inventory finding, not proof that no relevant public source exists.

The actual directional/gauge/calibration semantics also matter. Conditional normal-stress algebra is not automatically applicable to tilted effective diffraction strains with orientation-dependent finite gauge support. A third matched direction can resolve a declared model ambiguity without recovering pointwise stress. The compact spatial witnesses show how averages can agree while a local target differs. Mean-corrected scoring legitimately tests spatial variation; it does not establish absolute-level accuracy, and this result does not allege that the original benchmark performed its intended task incorrectly.

Consequently, **the proposed substitution is rejected for use on the present evidence; physical admission remains HOLD**.
