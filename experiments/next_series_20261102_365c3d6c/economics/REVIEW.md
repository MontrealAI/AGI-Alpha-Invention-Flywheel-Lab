## Strong incumbent and claim boundary

The March 2026 CM4QC strategy treats validated model reuse/transfer as an unresolved issue. It distinguishes bulk residual-stress prediction from the local gradient and uncertainty requirements of damage-tolerance/life prediction. Its discussion also recognizes efficient commercial inherent-strain methods and the cost/input burden of more detailed models. This supports a narrow transfer test, not a claim that computational qualification or stress modeling is absent. [S1, printed pp. 15, 135–136]

NIST's Phase II already proposes realistic component builds, extensive measurements and criteria for computational-materials use in qualification. This is an active strong reference route, not a finished substitute for every proposed local functional. [S2] Both facts must survive reporting: the obstruction is consequential, and competent alternatives already do substantial relevant work.

The primary productivity comparator is competent **100% human specialists with conventional software**, including its genuine automation and permission to construct the same instrument. Their research, decisions, alignment, verification and support time must be measured or supported before valuing a labor difference. The separate commercial outside options include hybrid providers, autonomous rivals, self-service and no purchase. A technical tie and equal-content ordinary reuse may both be the correct results. Neither establishes or defeats a commercial margin by itself.

For an immediately expensed analysis setup, no capitalized equipment or debt, and `N` scope-matched accepted **and collected** artifacts, the elementary pre-tax relation is

`profit = N * (net_price - complete_variable_cost) - complete_setup_cost`.

Here `net_price` is sales revenue after returns/contra-revenue, while transaction fees belong in cost exactly once. This does not identify any missing commercial input. Break-even requires a positive variable margin and `N * margin >= setup`; buyer participation must separately be worthwhile. Later taxes, capitalized assets, losses or financing require their own recognition policy rather than disappearing from this simplified relation.

For a dated prospective event tape, let `O(t)` be cumulative cash outflows, `R(t)` current protected commitments/obligations, `C(t)` cleared operating cash received, and `F(t)` external financing actually received in addition to opening capital. A read-only shortfall condition is

`available(t) = opening_capital + C(t) + F(t) - O(t) - R(t)`

`required_opening_capital = max_t max(0, O(t) + R(t) - C(t) - F(t))`.

Use a consistent event order and avoid counting an already-paid obligation in `R(t)` again. Deposits may enter cash only with their required custody/reserve; recognition and available earnings remain separate. Depreciation is not another cash payment. A promise of future funding does not enter `F(t)`. These formulas do not maintain a balance, mutate state, implement a second ledger or qualify a native trajectory.

**Independent algebraic cash control:** let `L` be required protection, `C` committed fulfillment cash, `G > 0` optional reach/capacity spending, and `K` current available funding before these commitments. Whenever `L + C <= K < L + C + G`, accepted work is fundable before `G` but not after spending `G` first. Both policies can have identical hypothetical eventual revenues; only their chronology changes feasibility. This is a general witness for reserving obligations before discretionary expansion, not a measured performance result for this candidate. Once a priced tape exists, an independent direct prefix-sum calculation should agree with the native available-cash series and report the maximum deficit and its dates.

No executable finance module is added: there are no sourced numerical quotes or dated commercial commitments to run, and the repository already supplies canonical `JournalAccount`/`Account` operations. A parallel ledger would add machinery without resolving the present decision.
