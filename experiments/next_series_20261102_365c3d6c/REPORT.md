## N3: what the new instrument establishes

### Local observation identifiability

The frozen local model is isotropic, linear elastic and infinitesimal. Stress is in MPa and strain is dimensionless. For a unit direction `q`, the observation is

`e_q = ((1 + nu) q^T sigma q - nu trace(sigma)) / E`.

The instrument checks whether the requested linear stress functional lies in the observation operator's row space. It returns either a model-only estimate with a deterministic uncertainty interval, or a null-direction witness that changes the target without changing the observations. This is ordinary linear algebra available to the conventional comparator.

With `E=200000 MPa` and `nu=0.3`, exact X/Z strains identify `sigma_xx - sigma_zz`, but leave `sigma_xx` ambiguous. A third matched exact-axis strain identifies the normal stresses; six directions with independent tensor observation rows can identify the full symmetric local tensor. The zero-Poisson-ratio control correctly identifies `sigma_xx` from its axial strain. Thus a missing component is not a universal impossibility without specifying the material law and target.

The tilted-direction control loses the simple stress-difference invariant when shears remain unknown. In its registered witness, the target changes by **32.783 MPa** while the observation change is at numerical precision. An approximate axis label cannot silently authorize an exact-axis formula.

### Spatial ambiguity with mechanical constraints

The second construction supplies a smooth, compact stress field from a potential, `sigma = I laplacian(phi) - Hessian(phi)`. It satisfies symmetry, equilibrium, zero exterior traction and finite elastic energy. The declared residual-eigenstrain construction makes total strain compatible with zero displacement; this is not a purely elastic, zero-eigenstrain model.

Across the three registered interior box shapes, two fields have center `sigma_xx` of **-100 and +100 MPa**, while all full-support uniform through-Y averages vanish. The independent exact reference and adaptive quadrature reproduced those properties: maximum stress implementation difference **2.132e-14 MPa**, maximum integrated average magnitude **5.773e-15 MPa**. An independently calibrated observation of `sigma_xx` at the center can distinguish that pair; another copy of the same average cannot. This describes the necessary information, not an acquired or qualified physical point sensor.

This is a physical-law-constrained **authored model witness**. The uniform averaging kernel and generic elastic constants are not the actual NIST gauge or a calibrated NIST specimen. The witness establishes the declared information limitation, not an observed 200 MPa error in the published measurements.

The detailed methods report effective multi-peak diffraction strains, tilted measurement directions, a finite rhomboidal gauge and systematic-reference limitations. Their original benchmark compares spatial strain variations. Its mean-corrected score is appropriate to that declared purpose; it does not validate an absolute stress claim. The raw published strain estimates are not claimed to have been mean-erased.

For our proposed local-stress use, the inspected evidence lacks a qualified diffraction-to-bulk constitutive conversion, direction/shear closure, known matching spatial kernels, absolute reference uncertainty, independent same-specimen local truth and a supported beneficiary release criterion. The source therefore receives **one prerequisite HOLD**. No raw-row accuracy test was conducted.
