# Independent review

## Verdict

**All 15 registered finite-data decisions pass independent source reconstruction, complete displayed-output checks and exact minimum-cardinality certificate verification. The same optimal cardinalities are obtained by capable conventional methods.**

The first generic capability was frozen before successor execution and transferred unchanged to a different decision problem. This establishes a working portable instrument and ordinary artifact transfer. The experiment does **not** establish a previously unattainable scientific or economic outcome, a better invention method, prospective chemistry performance, realized assay savings, revenue or economic compounding.

## What was checked

The verifier was independently authored without inspecting or importing the producer's solver. It reconstructs each problem directly from hash-pinned source CSV bytes using exact decimal arithmetic. It checks every displayed recipe and substrate assignment in the first outcome, including selected membership, source line, recorded yield, recorded maximum and required relative threshold. For the successor it checks the entire condition-pair denominator, the selected substrate panel, every displayed discrimination witness and all omitted pairs with their maximum-gap source rows.

It also independently validates the finite optimality argument. A feasible size-k selection is accompanied by an infeasibility proof for k−1. Packing leaves, capacity leaves, branch completeness, exact dominance replacements and shared-DAG contexts are recomputed. No producer-supplied residual state or lower bound is trusted.

The original protocol is preserved in `PROTOCOL.json`. The proof-encoding amendment in `CERTIFICATE_SCHEMA_AMENDMENT.json` accepts the communicated DAG encoding and sound capacity/dominance reductions; it changes no outcome, acceptance threshold or comparison. It was created before successor outcomes were reviewed. The earlier narrower `FIRST_CERTIFICATE_VERDICT.json` is superseded for complete-output acceptance by `FIRST_FULL_VERDICT.json`.

## Controls and interpretation

The first independent exact control uses dynamic programming over at most 1,024 nucleophile-coverage masks. The successor control uses a separately authored memoized exact search. SciPy/HiGHS receives the identical finite covering problem with binary variables and zero requested MIP gap, and is accepted only with optimal termination and exact independent feasibility checking. Different equally optimal selections qualify.

The retained method improves primary successor cardinality over greedy, but a capable conventional exact route also returns six. Greedy is therefore a useful heuristic comparison, not evidence of an invention advantage.

Five repeated warm-library runs per method and case record source reading, preparation, solving and acceptance checking. These timings concern the conventional decision route; they do not include serialization of the producer's larger evidence package. They must not be compared as though both methods emitted identical complete deliverables. Process startup, imports, construction time and money are separate. Detailed measurements are in `FIRST_BASELINES.json` and `SUCCESSOR_BASELINES.json`.

Naive successor subset enumeration was interrupted after excessive combinatorial runtime, without a complete result. The protocol permitted exact DFS; the correction retained every source, cohort and threshold. `INTERRUPTED_BASELINE.json` preserves that failure, and `REPRODUCTION_HISTORY.json` records the final-source first-suite reproduction. This was an engineering correction on exposed data, not fresh scientific confirmation.

## Concrete integrity challenges

All **36 targeted mutations** are rejected while all 15 valid source-bound decisions and a valid shared-DAG example are accepted. They include incorrect displayed recipes, assignments, yields, thresholds, source lines, omitted cases, selected panels, witness gaps, omitted-pair maxima, source provenance, false capacity/packing bounds, false dominance, omitted branches and invalid shared-node contexts. See `MUTATION_VERDICT.json`.

This is meaningful regression coverage for demonstrated risks. It is not an exhaustive security or chemistry-validity claim.

## What remains open

The useful technical deliverable is an inspectable, independently checkable recommendation from existing published data. No new laboratory measurement was performed, and the full public matrices were already available. Their existing measurements cannot be counted as assays saved by this software.

A prospective scientific or economic enabling claim still needs a real beneficiary decision, adequate experimental uncertainty, a matched complete incumbent workflow, and evidence that using the retained capability changes the accepted outcome or its complete economics. The current experiment does not establish a causal construction advantage beyond ordinary reuse. It provides a reusable instrument and the evidence needed to evaluate that next claim honestly.

## Conclusion française

Les **15 décisions sur données publiées** sont vérifiées indépendamment, y compris les recommandations affichées, leurs lignes sources et les certificats d'optimalité. Les méthodes conventionnelles exactes obtiennent les mêmes cardinalités optimales. La capacité conservée a été transférée sans modification à un objectif différent; cela démontre un instrument fonctionnel et une réutilisation ordinaire. Une nouvelle possibilité scientifique ou économique, des économies réelles d'expériences, des revenus et un avantage causal sur les meilleures méthodes existantes restent à démontrer.
