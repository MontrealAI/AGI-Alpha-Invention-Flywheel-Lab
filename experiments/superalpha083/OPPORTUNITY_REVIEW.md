# Super Alpha opportunity review

**Review date: 3 November 2026. No candidate is admitted as the best Super Alpha.** This comparison distinguishes consequential ambitions from demonstrated new productive abilities. The codec experiment supplies a negative selection result. The other directions below are research possibilities, not selected experiments, achieved inventions, physical validation, or business cases. This was a bounded review, not an exhaustive search of all opportunities.

## Executed codec rejected as the flagship

The codec produces and recovers actual bytes under the specified eight-data, fourteen-shard, three-rack contract. However, ordinary locally recoverable codes already provide the complete recovery service. The local comparison includes a conventional pair-local code with fewer average minimum helper reads than the constructed XOR code; the candidate's smaller worst-case helper count comes with other reliability tradeoffs. See [the experiment record](README.md), [original results](RESULT.json), and the supplementary evidence referenced there.

This is not a new productive activity or evidence of enormous complete-cost advantage. Reusing linear coding for distributed binary computation does not establish a successor capability unavailable through ordinary reuse. Production throughput, commercial demand, revenue, and profit remain unestablished.

Relevant primary alternatives include [Azure LRC](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2016/12/LRC12-cheng-webpage.pdf), [Google's practical wide-LRC study](https://www.usenix.org/system/files/fast23-kadekodi.pdf), and [UniLRC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.06819). The experiment did not implement every optimized or adapted version of those systems. The unmodified UniLRC parameter family does not directly supply this n=14, k=8 instance; that is a comparison limit, not grounds for claiming superiority.

## Research lead portable process invention

**Desired outcome:** discover a useful manufacturing process and reproduce its qualified output on unfamiliar equipment, without restarting discovery from scratch. This would be consequential if it unlocked production that a competent team with current software could not achieve within the same resource budget.

**Proposed missing means:** identify which target-equipment interventions distinguish source-equivalent explanations of kinetics, transport, and measurement. A recipe that works on one reactor need not identify a transferable mechanism or work on another. This is a hypothesis about a possible bottleneck, not an established unsolved frontier.

The strongest comparison must include ordinary system identification, optimal experimental design, scratch Bayesian optimization, and transfer or multitask Bayesian optimization. [RoboChem-Flex](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44160-026-01053-0) already demonstrates flexible autonomous reaction optimization and transfer-learning methods; its [public implementation](https://github.com/Noel-Research-Group/Robochem_Flex) is an important incumbent. Adding search, memory, or a transfer model is not enough to establish novelty.

**Possible first test, not selected or executed:** freeze independently held-out simulated reactor mechanisms that fit the same source observations but respond differently after equipment changes. Give every method the same target-intervention and total resource budgets. Require an executable target recipe to satisfy preregistered yield, purity, and throughput constraints, rather than merely predict them. Reject the proposed advantage if recipes fail those constraints, consume more than the allowed resources, or the strongest complete conventional alternative achieves the same productive regime within budget.

Even a positive result would establish only a result in that simulator. No suitable matched cross-equipment physical dataset was verified in this review. No physical process, manufacturing transfer, or qualified product has been executed or demonstrated. This direction remains a research lead, not the replacement flagship.

## Alternative autonomous useful algorithm invention

**Desired outcome:** repeatedly construct exact, useful algorithms that enable a valuable workload beyond the strongest practical alternatives, with independently checkable correctness and full implementation costs.

A concrete mathematical target considered was a rational 4-by-4 matrix-multiplication algorithm using at most 47 scalar multiplications. Existing work provides [48 non-complex multiplications](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.13242), with [subsequent practical recursive implementations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12027). [The published open-problem specification](https://epoch.ai/frontiermath/open-problems/matrix-multiplication) supplies an exact-certificate target. An [existing public search campaign](https://github.com/matyasstoch/cloudroom-math) reports no 4-by-4 rank-47 result, so repeating its search without a different mechanism is a weak proposal.

**Status:** no rank-47 search or result was launched here. An exact rational tensor identity would be a mathematical construction; an approximate numerical fit or an identity only in a different field would not satisfy that target. Failure to find it would not prove impossibility.

Moving from 48 to 47 saves only about 2.08% of base scalar multiplications. Additions, memory movement, coefficient growth, numerical behavior, and conversion costs can erase any practical benefit. A giant end-to-end advantage, a business, or a generally effective invention engine does not follow. Practical admission would separately require comparisons against the best applicable implementations, including optimized BLAS, and a genuinely useful successor task. This is a high-risk scientific possibility, not an established commercial opportunity.

## Alternative design to qualified physical manufacture

**Desired outcome:** generate a functional component from requirements, manufacture it, and independently qualify repeatable performance. This could connect design invention to actual productive capacity.

The gap cannot be closed by CAD generation or a favorable simulation alone. Qualification requires evidence about material variation, fabrication errors, measurement uncertainty, and repeated physical output. Existing autonomous manufacturing is a serious alternative: [autonomous control of plastic forming](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlepdf/2025/mh/d4mh00051j) already combines in-situ characterization and active learning in a physical process.

No specific residual capability beyond those alternatives was established here, and no physical qualification was performed. A local design-and-simulation exercise would test only an initial computational component. It must not be described as autonomous qualified manufacture.

## Selection decision

Retain the codec as a documented rejection, not as a Blue Sky success. Keep portable process invention as the leading research question among these possibilities, without selecting or executing it on the strength of this review. None currently has the combined evidence of a consequential unmet productive outcome, a demonstrated missing means beyond strong alternatives, an independently supported successor, and a credible complete-cost economic path.
