# Opportunity comparison and primary evidence

Evidence reviewed on 2 November 2026. This is prospective selection evidence for a bounded construction experiment. It does not establish novelty, a purchaser, qualified physical deployment, financing, or profit.

## Selection decision

Select **reusable battery characterization that enables predictions of heterogeneous parallel modules** for the first discriminating experiment, conditional on obtaining and auditing the public measurement files. It offers a particularly direct causal test: characterize individual cells, freeze those assets, then predict independently measured behavior of a connected system without using its branch-current labels. The outcome matters to battery integration and can be evaluated against physical observations rather than a constructor-authored world.

This is a choice of where to resolve uncertainty first, not a finding that a new industry has been created. Cell-to-module modeling already exists. The important residual would be a demonstrable reduction in the complete burden of producing sufficiently accurate, transferable module assessments, while preserving uncertainty and qualification. If a competent conventional implementation achieves the same accepted outcome within the same resources, the claim of a newly enabled capability fails even if the artifact is useful.

### Why the battery test comes first

All three possibilities have consequential applications. Battery characterization provides the clearest available separation between the inherited object and the successor function: a single-cell instrument becomes an input to a networked module model, and measured branch currents supply a distinct acceptance target. Manufacturing may have larger process leverage, but testing a genuinely new design requires physical production beyond this environment. Historical water sensing has clear public validation data and a recent strong comparator; useful coverage and commercial differentiation both remain uncertain, and the initial ScienceBase route was not retrievable during this research pass.

The battery choice therefore has high immediate information value and a clear negative result if transfer or the incumbent comparison fails. It is not selected because capital-intensive alternatives are forbidden. No architecture has been assigned secured capital, market share, a royalty, or a profit margin.

## Battery experiment and incumbent obligations

The selected [Piombo et al. dataset version 2](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/zh58byr53c/2) contains single-cell characterization and 54 module conditions varying temperature, interconnection resistance, chemistry and aging. Individual branch currents and temperatures were measured. The release is dated 19 April 2024 and declares **CC BY 4.0**. Attribute the authors and version; preserve raw-file identities and transformations. The descriptor does not guarantee that all intended files can be retrieved or that the records are mutually independent.

The strongest closely matched published comparator found is [Lone et al., parallel-pack dynamics with interconnection resistances](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.14454v1). It provides dynamic current expressions, nonlinear cell-voltage compatibility, interconnection effects and generalized matching conditions. Its experimental validation uses the same Piombo measurement family, including four LG M50T cells at 25 degrees Celsius and 1/3 milliohm interconnections. This already establishes the general cell-to-module route. The [accepted publication record](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/243946/) dates the IEEE article to 22 July 2026, DOI **10.1109/TEC.2026.3716248**. Treat uniform current sharing and a fixed resistor split as diagnostic ablations, not the sole competent baseline. Independently implement the published equations or establish the code license before redistributing source: the [authors' public MATLAB repository](https://github.com/jaffarlone07/Parallel-Pack-Paper-MATLAB-Code) was accessible, but a root `LICENSE` file was not found in this check. Recompute numerical errors: the paper's Table II labels values as MSE, while the following prose describes a current error inconsistently; do not inherit that unit conversion.

Required interpretations are narrower than pack certification. Predicting branch-current behavior does not establish lifetime, fault detection, thermal-runaway protection, acceptable remanufacturing, or safe field operation. K1-to-K2 usefulness must be compared with fresh construction and ordinary reuse of the same public information. Charge characterization and setup when incurred; inherited parameters are an acquired asset, not a free physical measurement.

An independent later test is available in principle: [Zhou, Stephens and Sun's 2026 dataset](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/ssrgfmb8vw/1) covers 78 three-cell module configurations with paired cell/module characterization. Its records were not downloaded or accepted in this selection stage. It is a candidate for external-laboratory transfer, not current evidence of generality.

## Commercial interpretation and capital

There is primary evidence that rapid characterization is a practical development problem. The [2025 NREL and Smartville CRADA report](https://doi.org/10.2172/2583494) describes work on rapid EV state-of-health characterization and second-use lifetime forecasting. This identifies an existing activity and a capable incumbent collaboration; it does not make those organizations customers or prove demand for this construction.

[RIT's published 2026 battery-testing schedule](https://www.rit.edu/batterydevelopment/testing-pricing) supplies a concrete, limited cost reference: a 5 V/50 A cycler channel is listed at $200 per month, a small cell in a shared temperature chamber at $175 per month, and test-engineer labor at $180 per hour. These are one laboratory's rates, with setup and other charges separate. They may bound an avoided-test calculation only when the test is genuinely displaced. They are not our selling price, a market average, or evidence that a physical test can be omitted.

[UL 1974's public scope](https://www.shopulstandards.com/ProductDetail.aspx?productId=UL1974) covers sorting and grading for battery repurposing/remanufacturing, and [UL's facility-service description](https://www.ul.com/services/second-life-electric-vehicle-battery-repurposing-facility-certification) describes assessment of those processes. An accurate model is one potential input to an industrial process; certification, acceptance, liability allocation and required physical tests remain separate burdens.

Model buyer choice against in-house use of open methods, conventional engineering/testing, another provider, purchase of new components, recycling, delay and refusal. A fee is feasible only where demonstrated avoided complete costs and decision value exceed the fee plus integration, uncertainty and switching costs. A model license must include maintenance and support; an outcomes contract also requires a verifiable counterfactual, dispute terms and reserves.

Capital and lead time are unresolved parameters, not measured facts. A software-only public-data experiment, an externally validated engineering service, and a physical repurposing facility are different financing strategies. Compare the $100,000 and $1 billion capital cases as specified in the experiment registration, but allow the latter to remain unused or unattractive when the bottleneck is evidence or demand. More cash does not supply certification, a proprietary right or customers. Physical deployment scenarios require priced equipment, integration, supply, specialist review and obligations before a funded scale claim.

## Claims at selection

**Supported:** public measured evaluation opportunities exist; close established comparators exist; the selected battery dataset declares a reuse license; several relevant qualification and testing activities have observable institutional counterparts.

**Unresolved:** actual artifact acceptance, retained-asset advantage, previously uneconomic outcome, customer willingness to pay, safe industrial deployment, financing availability, and any sustained profit. The next registered measurement test must resolve a technical claim before commercial scale is represented as earned or scientifically enabled.
