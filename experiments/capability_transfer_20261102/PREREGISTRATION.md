# Independent cell to pack capability experiment

Registered 2 November 2026 before raw measurement evaluation. Parent source: `88ddcfa3745dd5ae3022b9224d151943c9489b10`. This is a new independent campaign, not continuation or adoption of another account or experiment. Existing campaigns retain their original mandates and results.

## Decision and scope

Compare materials laboratory infrastructure, historical water measurement, and heterogeneous battery qualification. The first battery test asks whether a reduced characterization instrument can retain enough cell-specific information to construct useful predictions of previously untested parallel packs. This investigates a potentially valuable qualification bottleneck. Cell equivalent circuits and pack composition are established methods; their implementation is not claimed as our invention.

The proposed missing means is a cheaper **qualified** characterization and transfer procedure. The strong conventional route is full per-cell characterization followed by a nonlinear open-circuit-voltage, one-RC equivalent circuit and a ladder interconnect network. Ordinary reuse of that model is a required control. The published analytical pack model of Lone et al., arXiv:2508.14454, and the underlying Piombo et al. data are mandatory comparators and provenance references.

## K1 contract

Input: cell identity and chemistry, individual-cell OCV/capacity characterization, and isolated-cell pulse excitation at 23 C. Candidate: reduced pulse-anchor characterization with a retained chemistry-conditioned response representation. The reduced anchor set is the closest available states to 20, 40, 60 and 80 percent SOC. Full characterization is a strong cost/quality frontier comparator; conventional interpolation using the same reduced anchors is the equal-information comparator. The representation and fitting rules must be fixed before evaluation.

Output: versioned cell response parameters, validity envelope, source hashes, acquisition burden and prediction interface. Test on separately held-out multisine excitation or whole non-training pulse groups, never a random split of adjacent rows. Provisional engineering acceptance is voltage RMSE <= 30 mV and no more than 10 percent degradation versus the full conventional model, with no unsupported interpolation/extrapolation silently accepted. These thresholds are experiment choices, not safety or regulatory standards.

A cost advantage requires >= 50 percent reduction in complete characterization burden at accepted quality versus the best feasible route. Count SOC preparation, OCV/capacity testing, rests, calibration, failed identification, checking and acquisition of a shared prior; fewer recorded rows alone are not a shorter physical experiment. Report exact recorded duration separately from hypothetical redesigned protocol duration. If the source cannot identify these costs, the economic advantage is unresolved.

## K2 contract

Construct an ex ante predictor of individual branch current through a four-cell ladder-connected pack using frozen cell models, known topology/interconnection resistance, imposed total current, and predeclared initialization. No measured future pack voltage or individual branch current may enter the predictor. This is a different functional construction from cell voltage characterization, but different function alone does not establish new industry creation.

Use independent Hall-sensor branch-current measurements as truth. Nominal transfer is 25 C, adjacent to the 23 C characterization. Report 10 C and 40 C as out-of-temperature challenges, not covered nominal conditions. Separate chemistry mixture, aged-cell presence and interconnect resistance strata. The nominal engineering target is per-condition normalized branch-current RMSE <= 10 percent of mean absolute per-cell applied current, and peak branch-current magnitude error <= 10 percent; retain all conditions and failures. Do not infer safe operating limits, aging lifetime, arbitrary load performance, thermal-runaway prevention or certification from 0.75 C discharge data.

Controls: full conventional nonlinear 1RC model with interconnects; same reduced anchors with ordinary interpolation; fresh construction with full costs; retained full conventional components; no cell-specific parameters; no interconnection resistance; and analytic KCL/KVL checks. Report candidate-vs-comparator differences without changing information, current inputs or truth. Unimplemented strong comparisons leave the residual-advantage claim unresolved.
