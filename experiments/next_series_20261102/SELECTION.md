## Source-bound flagship rejection and the smaller C2 decision

The selected test observes a decision consequence—which whole experiments are selected and which measured targets are missed—rather than substituting reconstruction error for utility. Its source panel supports independent labels and a precise finite denominator without measuring new cells or buying a model. The constructor cannot tune on masked outcomes, and the ordinary specialist may use exactly the same method. The public inputs do not establish a new industrial capability or demand; this stage can reject the proposed assay-budget crossing at low additional construction and verification burden.

The contemporary complete prior art changes the choice before confirmation. NAIAD already combines single-perturbation effects, learned gene representations and active selection. BATCHIE reports a prospective physical drug-screening loop, including batch constraints and later duplicate-well checks after corrupted observations. Rubbi et al. explicitly target many threshold hits rather than a single optimum. We therefore reject the generic claim that joining a predictor to a reveal loop creates newly missing productive means. The economic admission decision is non-entry absent an evidenced integration, qualification or complete-cost advantage beyond those options.

The remaining small test is still discriminating: it can reject this inexpensive six-feature representation under a counted discovery contract, or show that it merits a separately defined prospective qualification study. The 2026 method changed the unpublished draft from UCB to purpose-matched Probability-of-Hit before public scientific freeze and before data retrieval. UCB remains a control; static Probability-of-Hit isolates feedback. A favorable alternative will not replace a failed primary. This is not a head-to-head reproduction of full NAIAD or BATCHIE, and no superiority over them is inferred.

The masking restriction is a benchmark device, not current customer information scarcity. All outcomes can already be downloaded from the public workbook and analyzed by a competent specialist or self-service user. That complete-data route is a separate strong outside option in the economic model. Any profile-count reduction is conditional evidence for a future unseen campaign; no physical assays are saved by this replay. Failure of the declared discovery boundary cannot be converted into qualification of that service by relabeling the negative report.

C1 was initially plausible because its physical metrology is unusually clear. The complete contemporary comparator and the distinction between interface motion and temperature truth reversed that provisional preference before confirmation. C3 remains a consequential alternative if a matched intervention truth set and rights become available. No invented numerical probabilities, market values or universal ranking decide this choice.

## Complete economics and capital decision

The scientific resource unit is a counted reference/profile query. It is not a constant-cost assay, a bill, a specialist hour or a revenue unit. A laboratory may pool assays, differ in cells/read depth, need technical/biological replication, or incur a substantial setup cost. All single profiles, control, calibration pairs and selected double pairs are retained in the count. Actual research, construction, failed retrieval, verification and support costs remain part of a later complete service calculation even if included tools issue no new bill.

The immediate investment choice is finite research versus non-entry. Staged and smaller/larger physical investments, including the $100,000 reference and a relevant $1 billion financing hypothesis, remain unqualified because buyer acceptance, assay-specific costs, supplier obligations and investor terms are absent. No fictitious price, demand elasticity, financing availability, return, tax jurisdiction or complete specialist time is inserted to make a game solvable. Native accounts and existing cash are untouched. A successful replay would justify a next evidence stage, not an unearned collection or acquisition budget.

The accompanying `next072/economic_check.py` implements a finite, three-valued voluntary-choice and cash-admission model. Its named model roles are a research buyer, assay supplier, specialist/rival provider and financier, with opt-out and non-entry preserved. It tests the information and terms needed for participation, complete service costs and protected cash; missing monetary inputs remain UNKNOWN and produce HOLD rather than favorable invented numbers. Its independent protocol and adversarial fixtures are in `ECONOMIC_ADVERSARY.md`. Execution against the scientific verdict follows confirmation; no native account is created by this analytical model.

Rival adoption and 6/12/18-month cost-gap compression are prospective hypothesis cases, not observed forecasts. With the current unknown buyer losses, prices, assay costs, financing terms and timing, strategic regret, equilibrium price, optimal capital and maximum financed cash shortfall remain **UNKNOWN**. These are explicit admission failures in the finite model. There is no qualified profitable trajectory to extend and no claim to the $3 billion trailing-365-day complete-cost-profit milestone.
