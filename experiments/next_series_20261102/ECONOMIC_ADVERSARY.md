The provider considers a source-bound report or a separately qualified prospective study; the latter is currently refused for lack of that qualification. Payment terms are acceptance payment or a refundable deposit followed by acceptance payment. Capital alternatives are zero, $100,000 and $1 billion. The price is a symbolic scenario input, not a fitted optimum. The external offer faces the internal same-algorithm specialist and full-measurement routes at their full costs.

The solution concept is **finite-menu best-response and joint voluntary-feasibility screening**. A route is available only if its acceptance condition holds. The laboratory voluntarily selects it only if its net utility is at least each eligible outside option's utility. Operator, supplier, investor and cash constraints must all hold simultaneously. A known violated necessary condition gives REFUSE; any unresolved necessary condition gives HOLD. Fully specified artificial payoffs can yield `CONDITIONAL_SCENARIO_ONLY`, which still gives no native financial admission.

No calibrated Nash equilibrium, regret, approximation ratio or global optimum is claimed. ## Explicit participation and break-even conditions

Write `V` for the laboratory's decision value, `L` for missed-target/incorrect-use loss, `A` for its own attention and switching burden, `P` for the report price and `Uoutside` for its best eligible outside utility. The buyer constraint is:

`V - L - A - P >= Uoutside`.

The corresponding maximum voluntary price is `Pmax = V - L - A - Uoutside`. These quantities are not inferred from retrospective recall. A laboratory may rationally reject a report that passes the authored recall threshold, or value a well-supported negative report differently from an assay-allocation service.

Let `Ccomplete` include research and failed attempts, setup, compute/inference, review/qualification, support/remedies, acquisition, channel, license, depreciation, financing fees/interest, expected default loss and explicit taxes. The operator's threshold is:

`P >= Ccomplete + required_operator_surplus = Pmin`.

The joint price interval is nonempty only if `Pmax >= Pmin`. For an equally acceptable same-algorithm internal specialist route, the external price plus the buyer's extra switching burden must also not exceed that route's complete cost. Human attention and loaded wages cannot be set to zero or charged to an AI operation by analogy; both are separate unmeasured inputs.

The complete public-source route has utility `Vpublic - Cretrieval - Cinterpretation - Cverification`, with every effort term unobserved. It carries no new-assay expense for these already published outcomes. The report buyer must prefer the offered net utility to this explicit option; a conveniently low supplied aggregate `Uoutside` cannot overrule a better explicit public option.

Supplier participation requires `supplier_payment >= supplier_complete_incremental_cost + supplier_outside_surplus`. Applicable supplier terms and obligations must be explicitly accepted. The current public-data replay commits no new assay work; zero new assay purchases is not a measured zero complete cost for a future laboratory service.

Investor participation requires `discounted_investor_distributions >= capital_supplied + required_investor_return`, together with accepted fees, funding dates, downside, repayment and dilution/control terms. This is a necessary conditional participation test, not evidence that those distributions are attainable. The equity fraction is bounded between zero and one when specified. Unsupported financing operations remain scenarios; no promise enters an operating account as current money.

## Cash timing and obligations

Each capital/payment-term comparison exposes four events:

| Event | Cash treatment | Protected obligations |
| --- | --- | --- |
| t0 | Only capital assumed actually received in that scenario can pay setup and initial financing fees. | All existing committed refund, collateral and warranty reserves remain protected before setup is funded. |
| t1 | A genuinely received customer deposit may increase cash; supplier deposits, fulfillment and acquisition consume cash before acceptance. | The refundable customer deposit, collateral, warranty reserve and prior refund commitments remain protected. |
| t2 | The remaining report payment may enter only after scope-matched acceptance and a voluntary funded order. | Existing refund, collateral and warranty obligations remain protected. Only this customer's refundable deposit is released at the expressly conditional accepted-report event. Total deposit plus collection cannot exceed the contract price. |
| t3 | Maintenance/remedies, tax cash, principal repayment and interest repayment must be paid. | All existing refund, collateral and warranty protection remains. No release milestone is evidenced, so none is silently removed. |

At every event, cash must cover protected obligations. The model reports the maximum shortfall and the event labels needing funding evidence; when inputs are unknown, these remain UNKNOWN instead of becoming zero. A profitable final endpoint cannot fund earlier setup. Refundable deposits do not become unrestricted working cash while the associated liability remains.

This conservative four-event screen retains collateral and warranty reserves throughout because no separately accepted release timing is supplied. A later cash model may release them only through a declared, evidenced milestone that also preserves every remaining obligation. Paying a maintenance expense does not by itself prove a warranty has expired.

The model deliberately distinguishes cash from profit. Principal repayment and restricted reserves are not expenses. Depreciation is an expense without being a same-event cash payment. Financing receipts are neither revenue nor earned profit. The cost model and cash inputs need a properly sourced reconciliation before a native trajectory is admissible; this small screen does not pretend that every accrual and financing transition is already qualified.

## What profile counts can and cannot establish

`unique_single_profiles + 1 control + R + B`.

`unique_single_profiles + 1 control + N`.
