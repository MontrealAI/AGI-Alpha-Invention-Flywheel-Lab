# A calibration lesson that did not transfer

**Result: reject the proposed structural transfer premise.** This compact payload records an executed, model-generated procedure that detects a thermal calibration scaling ambiguity but does not find the corresponding target-affecting multiplicative scale freedom in a fixed-source two-bus AC adapter. It is a useful negative development result, not a discovery-method advantage or commercial success.

## What actually happened

1. Six ordinary thermal readings and two calibrated reference readings were generated from two explicitly authored development systems. Ordinary readings coincide; reference readings differ. These are simulator outputs, not measurements from hardware. `K1_OBSERVATIONS.json` records the eight outputs and their public costs.
2. The included assistant generated `retained_symmetry.py`, an exact-rational scaling-symmetry check and finite, costed reference-selection procedure, after the K1 observations. `K1_FREEZE.json` fixes the source and its K1 inputs by SHA-256.
3. The retained procedure ran unchanged on `k2_card.json`. Its constant-source electrical model has no target-affecting multiplicative scaling freedom in the supplied generic representation. A source-unanchored counterfactual has one, but is excluded from the actual fixed-source family. `TRANSFER_RESULT.json` records the rejection.
4. Independent algebra and physical checks confirmed the result. No blind confirmation was run and no ordinary comparator was completed or scored. No superiority, tie or inferiority to ordinary adaptive identification is established.

## Exact conclusion and limits

The independent ranks/nullities are K1: 6/1 of 7 coordinates; fixed-source K2: 12/0 of 12; unanchored-source counterfactual: 11/1 of 12. An absence of multiplicative scaling does not establish global or practical identifiability, good conditioning, safe decisions, or that reference measurements are useless. Affine/nonlinear/discrete ambiguities, noise, special probes, low-voltage branches, uncertain taps and more realistic networks need separate analysis.

The K1 `C*theta/time` monomial represents the scaling weight of `C*dtheta/dt`, not the literal transient equation. Its card assumes offsets have been removed and a constant-loss regime. K2 is a balanced steady-state one-branch series circuit without shunts. The source magnitude is fixed. Common complex-phase rotation does not change magnitude safety and is not the target ambiguity.

A physical K1 witness has identical ordinary readings but different acceptance for the same heating cap 2.5 over time 120: temperature rises about 10.808 versus 7.206 against a limit 10. A K2 counterfactual with source scaled 1.02 preserves measured voltage while changing a cap 0.5 decision; keeping the source fixed destroys that invariance. This confirms why the counterfactual cannot rescue the frozen task.
