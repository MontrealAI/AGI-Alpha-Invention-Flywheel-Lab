# Complementary OUT-LEARN development experiment

Status: local development only. Campaign identity `outlearning-grid074` is provisional,
not an externally acknowledged reservation. Other-team nonoverlap is not fully verified.
No public write, account operation, physical control, API call or external spend occurred.

## Outcome-first comparison (3 November 2026)

1. **Make useful distributed clean generation connectable sooner.** Beneficiaries are
   generators, electricity users and distribution operators. The scarce deliverable is a
   model-qualified operating permission, not a prediction or application document. A
   developer or operator could buy a commissioning/verification service if its accepted
   result improves their project decision. Binding constraints include observability,
   accurate network models, utility acceptance, protection and network capacity. Existing
   strong routes include specialist engineering, active inverter probing, robust dynamic
   operating envelopes, self-service studies, physical metering and network upgrades.
   Candidate missing means: constructing the cheapest admissible observation programme
   that breaks decision-relevant calibration ambiguity, with conservative abstention.
   K1 thermal-instrument qualification would retain an executable identification procedure;
   K2 would use it for a different AC operating-permission task. The cheap experiment can
   reject the incremental claim without claiming real interconnection approval.

2. **Produce accepted fit-for-purpose reused water.** Beneficiaries are water utilities,
   industry and water-constrained communities. Acceptance is delivered volume meeting its
   specified chemical/microbial quality and monitoring contract, not a feasible flow
   schedule. Utilities or industrial users might voluntarily pay where supply reliability
   and alternatives justify full lifecycle cost. The missing-means hypothesis is affordable
   validation of unobserved treatment failures rather than mere sensors or scheduling.
   Strong alternatives include established treatment trains, laboratory validation,
   centralized reuse and fresh-water sourcing. K1 could validate a selective contaminant
   measurement; K2 could qualify a different feedstock/treatment chain. Not selected now:
   available computation cannot validate the proposed biological/chemical acceptance
   instrument, and an authored quality model would risk hiding the central uncertainty.
   This is a task-specific evidence limit, not a claim that water reuse is impossible.

3. **Replace fossil process heat with accepted useful heat as a service.** Beneficiaries
   are industrial heat users. The unit is specified thermal energy/temperature and uptime,
   with correction and downtime liabilities. Buyers can choose boilers, direct electrification,
   conventional heat-pump integration, or no change. A proposed self-commissioning,
   risk-sharing heat-recovery arrangement would have to beat those complete routes.
   K1 could construct a thermal-response instrument; K2 could qualify a new plant-integration
   service. Not selected now: physical heat-pump integration and heat-as-a-service already
   exist. No residual beyond incumbents has been established. A cheap RC exercise is not
   evidence for superior industrial integration or profitability.

**No-deploy reference:** keep resources uncommitted and perform no commissioning or
production. It avoids the experimental costs and project liabilities but earns no delivery
revenue and creates no new capacity. It is not counted among the three arrangements.

## Selection and falsification

Select candidate 1 for a cheap *rejection-capable* development test, not because its market
is presumed most profitable. Public evidence establishes importance and strong incumbents;
it does not establish missing capability or willingness to pay. A two-bus test can reject a
weak construction claim. A positive result cannot establish feeder deployment, regulatory
acceptance, utility demand or superiority to full-scale engineering. If a competent ordinary
adaptive identification/robust-analysis route gets the same accepted result at comparable
complete cost, reject the retained-method and newly-enabling claims for this construction.
Do not manufacture a weak static-cap baseline to obtain a win.

Capital, procurement, acquisition, qualification, project acceptance and residual liability
would govern real ramp. All prices, costs and buyer behavior in any later economic track
must be explicit conditional assumptions. Net social value is not capturable seller profit.
No opening equity, earned financing, accounts, commercial result or $3B/365-day progress is
claimed by this development test. Do not build a substitute ledger.

## Inspected primary evidence

- DOE, Distributed Energy Resource Interconnection Roadmap (16 January 2025): distribution
  interconnection needs include data, automated studies, qualified models and physical
  reliability; a software improvement is only one component.
  https://www.energy.gov/cmei/i2x/doe-distributed-energy-resource-interconnection-roadmap
- Bhela, Kekatos and Veeramachaneni, Smart Inverter Grid Probing for Learning Loads,
  identifiability analysis, v2 (2019): active inverter probing is already a conventional
  candidate with explicit observability conditions. https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.08834v2
- Liu, Braslavsky and Mahdavi, Robust Dynamic Operating Envelopes, v5 (2023): uncertainty-
  robust DOE and two-bus demonstrations already exist. https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.13472v5
- EPA, Potable Water Reuse and Drinking Water: reuse is an established portfolio option
  with treatment/quality obligations.
  https://www.epa.gov/ground-water-and-drinking-water/potable-water-reuse-and-drinking-water
- WHO, Potable Reuse guidance (2017): source protection, treatment, monitoring and public
  acceptance are distinct requirements. https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241512770
- ARENA, Hardwick heat-pump final report page (1 August 2025): describes actual industrial
  integration and operating experience; not a modeled willingness-to-pay estimate.
  https://arena.gov.au/knowledge-bank/hardwick-meatworks-heat-pump-installation-and-power-upgrade-demonstration-final-report/
- DOE heat demonstration selections include a process-heat-as-a-service proposal. A
  selected demonstration is not proof of a new autonomous business.
  https://www.energy.gov/cmei/oced/industrial-demonstrations-program-selections-award-negotiations-heat
