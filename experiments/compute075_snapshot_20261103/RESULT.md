# Compute075: accepted mathematical delivery and conditional native commerce

## What executed

A complete local simulated service accepted a buyer-owned mathematical request, prepaid
its declared resources, computed a new field, independently certified the result, and
collected only through the canonical native account. Thirteen fixed financial arms ran
once. They produced six actual solutions: five were accepted and released; one was
mathematically valid but late, so the order was refunded. The fixed same-service
continuations used native earned-funding receipts, not copied balances.

These are independent authored counterfactual accounts, not one pooled enterprise.
The technical outcome is a source-bound discrete diffusion solution with absolute l2
error at most 10^-8. It is not physical validation or an observed customer outcome.

The $2 price was posted before buyer responses. The policy did not optimize against
private reservation values. Checked refusals retained the two $0.02 prospect expenses.
A zero-capital, insufficient-initial-liquidity or known negative-margin arm did not buy
unnecessary checks. Failed submissions retained their prepaid package costs. Repeating
release added no revenue. The $1 support claim removed unrestricted earned funds and
blocked the second package despite substantial remaining seed cash.

Every account ended with no unpaid liability or outstanding modeled reserve. This means
only that the declared finite obligations were settled, not that real-world liability or
support tails disappear. A native scope-limited guarantee is not a provider/legal guarantee.

## What the positive region assumes

The optimistic branch assumes $1 total one-time construction, $0.18 per started request,
$0.02 per prospect check, buyers who prefer the $2 service plus friction to their complete
outside options, and a willing supplier at the declared quote. These are sensitivity
inputs, not measured engineering bills, demand or supplier prices. Actual model/host and
labor billing is unknown. The unfavorable $100 construction point is also a conditional
input, not a measured bill.

For this exact two-order horizon, the maximum recoverable construction is $3.60 before
extra claims. This is not a business ceiling. Reusable means may rationally be built for
more costed, uncertain repeat demand, but future earnings are not current cash. The
separate conditional threshold calculation binds its simple aggregation to the native
honest arm, varies conversion/delivery and construction costs, and makes no demand,
capacity, annualization or $3 billion trailing-year claim.

The strong self-service and rival branches are genuine alternatives within the authored
world. They can use the same mature solvers, exact checker and source. Known algorithms
are not a business veto, and their availability is not withheld to manufacture a win.

## Full resource boundary

The complete financial subprocess used 139.145 seconds wall time and 139.051 seconds
of child CPU time, including startup/imports, requests, all native calls, actual solves,
checking, reporting and teardown. The six mathematical production/serialization/check
segments totaled 2.026 seconds. Most execution effort therefore belonged to this
research/accounting/replay apparatus rather than the numerical kernel. These observations
are not a causal performance comparison or a price for actual business operations.
Earlier construction, prior failed exploration, review, included-model access, and later
independent audits are outside that subprocess. Their dollar costs remain unknown and
must not be silently set to zero. Installed native dependencies matched the isolated
pin for this run; no released or full-repository qualification is claimed.

## A preserved provenance defect

The post-run independent audit found that the runner stored its initial public-input
dictionary by reference. A later update after paid prospect checks also changed the saved
initial snapshot. Nine original before-check snapshots therefore cannot be treated as authentic
historical state. The frozen source still calls the first policy before adding buyer or
supplier responses. The auditors separately reconstructed earlier inputs from the protocol, opening cash
and source call order; that reconstruction is not the original log. There is no separate
trusted solve-start timestamp, so ordering is source-and-native-chronology evidence.

No original source, result or protocol was rewritten, and no scenario was rerun. A separate
future-only deep-copy helper now reproduces the original defect and passes its correction
regression. This is ordinary post-outcome engineering repair, not a learned advantage.

## Claims and next decision

The supported classification is conditional simulated commercial success without an
established discovery-method advantage in the optimistic region. The adverse/refusal
regions do not establish commercial success. Discovery-method advantage was not tested;
it should not be called either a success or a measured failure in this service experiment.
Different sizes and layouts of diffusion remain the same service, not a genuinely different K2.

Keep the reusable mathematical acceptance component and preserve the negative grid074
route and all financial failures. The next substantive test should target a different
accepted outcome whose complete formulation, integration or qualification cost is actually
binding. It should test whether retained construction helps against competent ordinary
reuse, not spend on a faster subsecond kernel merely to make a discovery narrative.
A justified repeat-volume/retention test can establish an economic region; it cannot
invent guaranteed buyers, channels or capacity. The larger mission remains open.
