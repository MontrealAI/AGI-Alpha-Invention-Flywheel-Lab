## What worked

The system produced new solutions to specified discrete diffusion problems, independently certified their numerical error, and exercised prepaid production, acceptance, collection, refusals, repair, refunds, claims and earned-funds restrictions through the canonical account implementation.

Thirteen separate conditional accounts produced five accepted paid outputs. A sixth mathematically valid output arrived late and was refunded. These accounts must not be pooled into one compounding enterprise.

The optimistic honest and repaired arms each earned **$2.60 over two orders**, assuming a **$1 one-time construction cost** and a posted **$2 price**. Those prices, costs and buyer preferences are authored sensitivity assumptions, not measured development bills, observed demand or real commerce. Strong self-service and rival options can prevent purchase. Actual model and host billing remains unknown. No annualized or $3 billion claim is made.
