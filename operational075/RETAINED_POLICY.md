K1's typed slot assignment and b-matching do not implement temporal directed
routing. The missing executable means is `Oracle.check`: it starts a whole
route at the current absolute departure seconds, sums the directed matrix
legs, waits until each order's opening, requires service **start** no later
than that order's closing, adds that order's service duration, enforces total
load at most 1000, and returns to depot zero by its closing. Driving cost
includes the directed depot legs and excludes waiting and service. It assumes
neither symmetry nor triangle inequality. Multiple orders at one customer
retain their separate order-row demand, service and windows; customer defaults
never substitute for those fields. K1 slot labels never become clock times.

`decide(window, state)` uses only its current public arguments. It keeps
dispatched IDs by service day in its own JSON state, checks cumulative current
orders against that history, and returns the exact round envelope. No validity,
score, dispatch confirmation or executed-order feedback is consumed. Every
round's routes are independent departures; there is no invented fleet limit.

The conventional construction is distinct from the ordinary arm's registered
four-start insertion search:

1. Revalidate the previous own next-round whole-route witnesses at the actual
   current departure. Construct current feasible singleton routes for other
   orders. For a small singleton-infeasible nonmetric instance, bounded subset
   route enumeration and whole-order set partitioning may recover a cover.
2. Use a directed savings heap to merge feasible routes. Test forward and
   reversed concatenations through the temporal oracle. Raw directed arc
   proximity shortlists candidates; it never replaces route checking.
3. Apply adaptive, strictly improving route descent using order/chain
   relocation, suffix exchange, within-route reversal/relocation, and swaps.
4. Try two deterministic route-fragment rebuilds. Check depot-anchored
   fragments separately because a feasible nonmetric whole route need not
   have feasible fragments. Merge and improve the fragments, adopting only a
   strictly cheaper feasible complete partition.
5. If an entire route is feasible at the next public one-hour departure, defer
   it with that explicit witness. If the bundle expires but each singleton
   remains feasible next round, dispatch it when it preserves a strictly
   cheaper known consolidation, otherwise defer with singleton witnesses.
   A missing next witness means dispatch now. Round six dispatches all pending
   orders. The next invocation revalidates and retains whole-route witnesses.

The search preserves a checked feasible incumbent; it claims no global
optimality. It can report unresolved if it fails to construct an initial
complete incumbent within its finite allowance. In particular, new nonmetric
singleton failures with more than eleven pending orders are not solved by the
small exact fallback. Such failure is not a proof of infeasibility. Preserving
the previous witness avoids discarding a previously established feasible
nonmetric cover.

Executable K1 reuse is real and limited. `decide -> _input ->
wave.canonical_identity` preserves exact original identity. `_input -> _cover`,
the final history/dispatch/deferred cover, and deferred-witness coverage call
`wave.exact_cover` with typed identities. These functions reject coverage or
identity violations; they supply no temporal or routing guarantee. Fixture
instrumentation counted their actual invocations, rather than inferring reuse
from an import. The optional K1 JSON is not an interpreted learner and is not
read by this policy. Ordinary receives the same substantive K1 facts, code and
primitives plus competent conventional routing tools. Tie or loss is allowed;
no causal necessity follows from retaining these checks.

There is one hard counter of **50,000 complete-route feasibility/objective
checks per round**. Every `Oracle.check` call increments it before evaluation,
including failed and empty routes, current/next singleton checks, bounded
nonmetric enumeration, merging, every local candidate, fragment safety,
next-round whole-route witnesses and fresh final safety. Search reserves up to
two checks per pending order for final decisions. When even that reserve would
not fit, optional next checks stop and final safety can reuse an immutable
already checked current-departure `Route`; that reuse performs no fresh
complete-route evaluation. Summing stored route objectives, raw-arc shortlists,
partial-path pruning and exact-cover checks are not hidden full-route checks.
No search-budget termination discards an established feasible incumbent.
Broker process CPU/wall measurements remain separate from this counter.
Actual host/model billing and total included-session resource cost are unknown,
not zero.

`K2_GENERATION.json` records every authored fixture attempt, recipes, outcomes,
instrumented call counts, scoped CPU/wall measurements, corrections and source
reviews. There was one initial implementation and two corrections before
freeze:

* Source review found that a large pending count could spend its safety
  reserve and then throw away a feasible incumbent. Optional next checks now
  stop at the cap, and final safety reuses a proven current route if needed.
* An independent authored twelve-order directed counterexample demonstrated
  that safe whole-route deferral could be followed by a singleton failure on
  the next invocation. That failed attempt is retained. Revalidating and
  retaining the own whole-route witness corrected the failure; both the
  author's and independent reviewer's reruns passed.
