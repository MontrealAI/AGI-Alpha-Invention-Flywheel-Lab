# Minimal experiment protocol

## 1. Discover before building

Record beneficiary, consequential productive outcome, present complete alternative, constraint, public evidence (URL, access date, relevant observation), uncertainty, permission boundary, and the cheapest decisive next test. Screen materially different opportunities before admitting one; do not select the bootstrap environment because it already runs.

An opportunity remains UNADMITTED until its constraint survives a competent unrestricted alternative, and the proposed outcome has measurable relevance. Important aggregate statistics alone do not establish a customer, causal bottleneck, or willingness to pay.

## 2. Map and construct

Record source mechanism and domain; target relationships; mapping of roles, constraints and invariants; changed assumptions; executable adaptation; failure conditions; and a falsifiable transfer prediction. Superficial analogy is insufficient. Initial candidates use trusted registered experiments with bounded declarative parameters. Do not execute model-generated Python or shell. A later extension needs a real effect/resource sandbox, not a prompt claiming isolation.

## 3. Freeze before independent confirmation

Seal candidate bytes, evaluator, baselines, outcome bridge, thresholds, cohort, permitted adaptation, complete resource caps, and claim allocation. A verifier controlled separately from the builder holds fresh cases. Shared models and infrastructure are disclosed; procedural separation is not external certification. Developer-visible bootstrap cases are never fresh confirmation.

Compare the strongest tuned within-frame method, a competent unrestricted alternative with equivalent permissions, and targeted mechanism ablation. A builder may propose new measurements but cannot change its own judge. Look first for a decisive reproducible counterexample; preserve it even when inconvenient.

Use one campaign-wide error budget across all trials, renamed projects and branches. Predeclare statistical assumptions and allocation before confirmation; no reset after failure, no repeated peeking and relabeling as fresh. The bootstrap makes no statistical claim and spends no confirmation alpha. A production ledger and protected holdout service are intentionally not implemented here: absent those controls, research confirmation stays UNKNOWN.

## 4. Retain, then challenge the successor

Keep candidate, output, unsuccessful search, counterexamples, scope, complete costs, and original verdict. Store runs in new exclusive locations. Successful verification of one mechanism does not validate a composition or a transfer. Record negative evidence with its conditions rather than erasing it.

For a new target, compare inheritance against no inheritance, ordinary reuse, and mechanism ablation at matched total budgets. Include acquisition, search failures, construction, independent verification, integration, maintenance, human work, infrastructure, risk and overhead—not only deployment runtime. Unknown amounts stay null/UNKNOWN. Charge reuse according to a declared horizon and reconcile allocations to campaign totals.

A four-arm campaign can isolate contributions: strategy invention alone; plus structural transfer; plus adversarial verification; plus prior experience. All receive comparable complete resources. Fresh whole campaigns are replication units for discovery-policy claims. Inert storage or synthetic successor scores do not demonstrate recursive improvement.

## 5. Stop or promote honestly

Record three verdicts independently: technical scope, economic viability, successor causality. Report rejected, unknown and invalid evidence, not only wins. Stop when the cheapest decisive test rejects the mechanism, the bounded budget expires, or authorization is needed. Promote only scoped, supported results through reviewed integration; never activate a self-modified evaluator to approve itself.
