# AGI ALPHA — Transformational intelligence and open-ended productive expansion

**SUPER IMPORTANT: BLUE SKY. IMPORTANT: Beyond-Human Foresight.**

Build an enterprise that can repeatedly create accepted value, retain unrestricted earnings, and construct new means of discovery and production. Pursue improvements large enough to change what science, industry and civilization can accomplish. Do not impose an arbitrary limit on service categories, revenue, geography or the ambition of a useful investigation. Actual resources, authority, evidence and physical constraints remain real.

This is an engineering direction and a bounded implementation increment, not an attained intelligence level, a wealth guarantee, or evidence of large-scale physical deployment.

## One mission, three independently evaluated engines

| Engine | Objective | What establishes progress |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Autonomous commerce | Repeat profitable accepted service at fixed capabilities; optimize price, packaging, acquisition and capacity | Actual service artifacts, scoped acceptance, collected real or explicitly simulated revenue, full costs, liabilities and source-traceable earned-funded continuation |
| Transformational invention | Discover and construct better representations, strategies, experiments, tools and production methods | Executed alternatives, decisive counterexamples, stronger fresh successor performance and full lifecycle comparisons |
| Productive expansion | Apply qualified capabilities to consequential scientific and industrial constraints | Independent measurements of useful physical/scientific output, economics, reliability and deployment effects at the stated scale |

Commercial success does not require improvement in intelligence. Invention success does not require an immediate standalone sale. Neither software revenue nor a simulated engineering gain proves physical productive expansion. Keep each conclusion independent; do not create a global AND condition or turn a conditional simulation success into observed commerce.

## Transform the constraint, not just the answer

At each limiting step ask what the authorized outcome actually requires and which assumptions belong only to the current method. Test replacing a representation, information source, dependency, measurement, workflow or productive environment. A process can improve by removing unnecessary reasoning, coordination or work.

The four working mechanisms remain structural discovery, executable strategy invention, adversarial verification, and retention of verified mechanisms and failed transfers. Preserve the wider nine-mechanism repertoire in the mechanisms reference and its source history; it is not a mandatory committee. Every invocation should change a construction, test or decision. Machine-proposed discoveries and developer-supplied insights must remain distinguishable.

Foresight must generate a useful intervention hypothesis and a discriminating present-day test. Include rival futures, competitor response, a dated information set, uncertainty and resolution conditions. Being surprising is not proof of superior foresight. Do not manufacture numerical certainty or select only favorable stories.

## Capital chooses what to do next

The commercial owner remains responsible for the ledger, liabilities, reserves, collection, funding provenance and execution authority. This change does not create another financial system.

The portfolio owner should compare several uses of available earned resources: repeat current delivery, acquire another customer, add permitted capacity, reuse a capability, construct a complementary instrument, or preserve cash. Optional research uses a declared bounded allocation; it does not automatically take a share of every profitable job. Research with unknown cash returns may be worth an experiment, but it must not be given fictitious revenue to win a financial ranking.

Compare incremental **collected cash**, not market size, annualized price arithmetic or invoiced revenue. Include all failed acquisition, attempts, agent-operated onboarding, tools, compute, independent verification, support, maintenance and obligations. No hypothetical human staff or reviewer is inserted into the autonomous operation. Real intervention is recorded only when used or required.

Pricing maximizes risk-adjusted expected collected contribution under explicit buyer and rival behavior, capacity, payment timing and demand. Optimize the chance of a profitable win rather than maximize win rate. Premium service positioning remains the starting hypothesis; neither an arbitrary discount nor an assumed cheap competitor establishes the real optimal price. Test differentiated bundles, outside options, repeat behavior and correlated price/trust/cost shocks. No-bid and retain-cash remain valid decisions.

### The executable addition

`portfolio_scale/transformational.py` is a small, read-only planning extension to PR #18's existing service universe. It does not replace that price model or PR #16's commercial execution. It:

- reads existing catalog service IDs and candidate delivery, instrument and research actions;
- constrains upfront commitments by the minimum of supplied available cash, unrestricted earned funds and the authorized planning allocation;
- evaluates dependencies, mutually exclusive offers and explicitly stated joint benefits;
- compares every subset within a declared computational budget using weighted terminal net cash, a downside penalty and a worst-loss limit;
- preserves unknown-return research as a separate unresolved opportunity, not zero-cost work or invented profit;
- returns a candidate allocation or retains cash without invoking a provider, paying anyone or granting authority.

The supplied capital envelope is **not independently audited by this planner**. Its fields mirror `commercial.ledger.TapeExchange`/`audit` output (`available`, `unrestrictedEarned`) inspected at PR #16 source `a255ec5794bfdd5926a678b51ed349a636fbcc28`. The production integration must call the existing native audit, bind the exact receipt/action digest and current source, then use the native reservation under a fresh-state check. A plan does not reserve funds, prevent another concurrent plan spending them, or certify that a caller-supplied authorization flag is genuine. Recheck at commitment to avoid stale-state overspending.

`cost` is the upfront cash commitment. Per-scenario `cash_returns` are modeled horizon-end cash receipts **net of later variable costs, refunds and obligations**, excluding that upfront commitment. Joint benefits are strictly incremental to every component, counted once. A reviewer must establish that distinct benefit IDs do not describe the same economic benefit; schema validity cannot establish semantic non-overlap. All scenarios use one horizon and the same units. Future returns cannot fund today's initial commitment. Multi-period financing remains the native commercial ledger's responsibility.

The optimizer is exact only for its finite shortlist and supplied assumptions. It is not a market equilibrium, a forecast, a calibrated optimal strategy or a guarantee of profit. Its bounded search budget is a resource control, not a cap on the total service universe. Exceeding it produces an explicit decomposition/budget decision, not a fabricated optimum.

## Build a capability network, not a collection of unrelated demonstrations

Record executable implementations and their prerequisites, applicability and failure boundaries. Relationships such as requires, enables, substitutes-for and funded-by have their own evidence. Evaluate useful combinations jointly; the planner can choose a pair that is unattractive individually but valuable together under its declared scenario.

A paid deployment can reveal a bottleneck. A verified criticism can turn that bottleneck into an instrument specification. A transferred mechanism can supply an adaptation. A fresh successor must then establish whether retaining the instrument actually helped. The strongest ordinary method and equal-content ordinary reuse remain available controls.

Improve the discovery procedure itself on fresh campaigns, separating improved starting tools from improved search. Cross old/new discovery policy with old/new starting capabilities, count failed searches and maintenance, and preserve contrary evidence. Proposed evaluator improvements have a separate validation path; a candidate cannot replace its judge to qualify itself.

## Connect digital earnings to consequential productive capacity

Explore energy, materials, manufacturing, scientific measurement, computation, infrastructure and coordination as possible expansion domains, not predetermined investments. A credible pathway identifies its real bottleneck, a feasible enabling experiment, the physical validation needed, resource requirements and the conditions under which it is worth scaling.

| Domain | Useful outcome to measure rather than infer from revenue |
| --- | --- |
| Energy systems | Net useful energy, delivered cost, reliability, capital requirements and lifecycle resource impact |
| Materials and manufacturing | Yield, throughput, durability, precision, embodied resource use and qualified unit cost |
| Scientific instruments | Cost and time per independently validated result; previously distinguishable versus indistinguishable hypotheses |
| Compute and automation | Accepted useful work per resource unit, error rates, reproducibility and service capacity |
| Infrastructure and coordination | Reduced delays, achieved service levels and independently measured operating outcomes |

A theoretical improvement, synthetic world or digital twin requires a documented bridge to physical measurement before a real-world claim. Add laboratory, pilot and scale trials only under their actual permissions, independent checks and resource envelopes. Positive cash flow permits investment decisions; it does not validate physics or grant control of external infrastructure.

Aim for large, measurable expansions in useful production rather than a financial number alone. Revenue, profit, available capital, created customer value, qualified scientific capability and physical output are separate quantities. Report net benefits, total resource costs and adverse effects; do not double count customer savings as enterprise earnings.

## Delivery discipline and operating boundaries

Build and test the smallest consequential increment; retain one canonical implementation owner per subsystem. No duplicate runtime, ledger or publication pipeline. Reuse pinned dependencies and preserve license/provenance and original failures. Scope PRs, check the exact candidate, run the applicable tests, review the actual diff, and reproduce distributed artifacts before claiming release readiness. Maintain cancellation, rollback, bounded tools, source integrity and least-privilege effects. No new spending, customer contact, live transactions, physical action or release authority is added here.

The risk-management reference is the NIST AI RMF's Govern/Map/Measure/Manage structure, used as voluntary guidance for explicit responsibilities, contextual risk, reproducible measurement and monitoring. This is not a certification or a claim of full framework conformity. See https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/5-sec-core/ and https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework .
