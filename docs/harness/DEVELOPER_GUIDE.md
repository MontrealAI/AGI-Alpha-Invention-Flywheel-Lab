## Adopt established mechanisms without redundant proof

Start from the strongest relevant current primary sources and proven methods; research the actual missing capability, not already-solved basics. Bound the search by the decision it must improve rather than making literature review a launch gate.

A sound theorem, established engineering/economic result or successful historical practice can justify adoption. Cite that basis, verify the relevant assumptions and applicability, and test this implementation. Do not require a new discovery experiment or fresh proof of a known theorem merely to use it. “Game-theoretically optimal” is conditional on the specified game, information and incentives; historical success is informative, not universal. Concentrate novel research proof on what goes beyond established knowledge and expected human foresight. Routine implementation correctness, security, cash conservation and evidence for reported profit remain necessary; these are validation, not demands to reinvent known theory. New claims about our realized profit, scaling or invented-method advantage still need appropriate evidence.

## Run the right comparison

1. Start from a useful accepted outcome and a consequential constraint, not a committee of mechanisms. Select a baseline and a small discriminating experiment; a no-bid/no-invest decision is valid.
2. Give policies only permitted observations. Model voluntary buyer choice, competitors/suppliers, payment delays, capacity, channels, repeat demand and adverse regions. No forced sales, hidden future draws or free resources.
3. Compare baseline, commerce-only, capability-only, joint and tuned fixed-split policies when claiming interaction. Compare no memory, acquired reuse and **equal-content ordinary reuse** when claiming successor-method improvement. Commerce alone may win.
4. Keep complete costs and obligation accounting, exact source and compact success/negative outcome summaries. Do not annualize a short cohort or promote an optimistic projection into native validated scale. The [annual observer](../../connected_enterprise/scale.py) is reporting, not proof that a full-year horizon or $3B profit was achieved.
5. Consolidate a few-word lesson, preserve a useful fix/regression, then delete redundant terminal artifacts under [lean policy](../packaging/LEAN_EXPERIMENTS.md). Preserve true dependencies or migrate them safely. A loss can invalidate a policy without invalidating its useful mechanism code.
6. Publish and inspect actual GitHub Actions receipts, qualified release artifacts and a downloaded released-system run. Choose adoption/defaults using established theory with checked applicability or suitable empirical evidence, and correctness tests—not mandatory novelty research. Preserve a stronger incumbent when the relevant comparison shows it wins; labels such as “joint”, “discovery” or “optimal” are not evidence.
