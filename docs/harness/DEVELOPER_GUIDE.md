Invent the **complete productive arrangement**: a valuable accepted outcome, the means to produce and verify it, and terms under which beneficiaries, producers, funders and other participants voluntarily prefer participation to their available alternatives. Discover these together rather than fixing a low-value service and optimizing its transaction rate indefinitely. A missing process, measurement, interface, representation, contract, financing structure or allocation of risk/work can be the enabling invention. Known effective methods remain usable without new novelty proofs.

For a justified fresh selection, compare at least three materially different consequential opportunities, including a new outcome, capability or coordination arrangement. Choose for enabling potential, not just ease of fixing the current service. Poor current economics can justify a different service, market, contract or production means. Preserve active frozen accounts and comparisons as evidence; separately registered independent campaigns may search beyond their catalogue without rewriting those histories. Include ordinary competent commerce, a direct specialist, reuse, acquisition, construction and holding cash where applicable. For the selected proposal, write one compact arrangement card:

- **Outcome and alternatives:** who benefits, what complete outcome matters, what independent observation accepts it, and what the strongest existing complete route costs. Demand/value assumptions remain explicit; a modeled buyer is not a customer.
- **Binding constraint and construction:** what prevents worthwhile cooperation or delivery, which means are missing, what executable change could remove that constraint, and which assumptions could falsify it. Do not claim historical impossibility because our first method failed.
- **Participation and authority:** each participant's contribution, information, outside option, incentive, compensation, exposure and right to decline. Model strategic responses where relevant; neither truthful cooperation nor universal equilibrium is assumed. No fabricated agreements, coercion, concealed costs or unauthorized external coordination.
- **Costs, cash and acceptance:** include search/construction, failed attempts, verification, onboarding, operation, maintenance, financing and remaining obligations. Separate expected value from currently available funds. Bind a request and checker before execution; pay through the canonical account and check the delivered outcome independently.
- **Successor enabled:** name a second useful opportunity and the specific cycle-created asset it needs. Test whether that successor is economically reachable with the asset and not through the strongest feasible matched alternative without it. Match permitted information, actions and full costs; allow the strong ordinary alternative to purchase or build its own means. Do not lock its seed artificially or give the successor free equipment, information or research. Preserve actual cash and liabilities.

Use this card to choose a decisive prototype and delivery for the ambitious construction, not the smallest ambition or another planning framework. Test an actual native profitable first opportunity leading to a better second opportunity; neither assumed demand nor impressive scope establishes that sequence. The intended result is a growing set of sustainably achievable valuable outcomes—not more transactions, mechanism complexity or dependence on the system. A simpler existing service or voluntary withdrawal can still win.

### Flagship first; support must enable it

Select a consequential frontier problem and name the missing means that prevents a valuable activity. Construct and test those means, then pursue profitable complete delivery whose consequence is large. A small decisive test can serve a large ambition; established methods are eligible when they enable that construction.

For each checker, dashboard, routine optimization, paper or infrastructure task, state the concrete flagship dependency it removes and the consequential test or delivery it enables. Do not let tractable support become the substitute objective; reprioritize when it dominates. Keep this connection concise and operational, not a new admission-checker project. Neither ambition nor this guidance guarantees breakthrough/profit, requires novelty in every component, or relaxes evidence and permissions.

### Close one native business episode

Follow one auditable path: **offer → funding → qualified order → useful production → independent acceptance → collection → obligations covered → reinvestment or retained cash**. A generated invoice, passing test or modeled order alone is not commercial success. Bind the checker before delivery and retain source-matched receipts for the complete episode. Conditional simulation may proceed without live buyers; observed-commerce claims require observed commerce, not simulated demand.

Charge actual autonomous resource use and actual human intervention, not fictional human overhead. Label modeled cost envelopes and unknown actual billing separately. Include research and failures, acquisition, verification, maintenance, support, refunds, taxes and capital allocation without hidden losses or double counting. Keep profit, unrestricted cash, protected principal and outstanding obligations distinct. Conservative assumptions are explicit model choices, not universal commercial laws.

For the primary US$100,000-seed simulation, register the native observer’s fixed endpoint and measure US$3 billion trailing-365-day complete-cost profit there. Revenue, valuation, cumulative profit or an annualized short cohort is not a substitute. No pooled accounts, top-ups or resets. A fresh account is not a migration; preserve authenticated histories and obligations on resume, including race and duplicate-action protections. The original signer-dependent P0 remains a separately owned blocked lineage, not a blocker for every fresh authorized simulation.

Record each registered arm’s denominator, outcome, complete costs and actionable failure lesson prospectively. Keep essential active financial/recovery state and published-claim proof, plus high-value reusable artifacts; do not default to bulk archives, hide failed arms or purge history. Private source documents and their quotations, filenames and diagrams stay private. This guidance changes neither account state nor authorization.

### Keep the achievement levels separate

1. **Implemented means:** a working interface, instrument, contract model or constructor, with checked applicability and correctness.
2. **Useful sustainable delivery:** accepted output and collected conditional or actual revenue exceed complete costs with obligations covered; label simulation versus actual commerce.
3. **Connected enabling cycle:** the first cycle makes a specific otherwise-uneconomic successor reachable. Financing and causal capability are measured separately; temporal sequence alone proves neither.
4. **Better discovery/construction:** a retained procedure finds or builds better sequences on fresh tasks against equal-content ordinary reuse and strong conventional methods, with acquisition and verification costs counted. Better tools alone do not establish this.

Report executed business, consequential capability, retained discovery and scientific/physical expansion separately. Current bounded mechanisms are building blocks, not evidence that all four levels, a general breakthrough or civilization-scale capability have been established. A bold mission is not an achieved result. Developer-authored methods, known adaptations and autonomous discoveries must remain labeled separately. New claims need proportionate evidence; established mechanisms do not incur a redundant research-proof gate.

## Adopt established mechanisms without redundant proof

Start from the strongest relevant current primary sources and proven methods; research the actual missing capability, not already-solved basics. Bound the search by the decision it must improve rather than making literature review a launch gate.

A sound theorem, established engineering/economic result or successful historical practice can justify adoption. Cite that basis, verify the relevant assumptions and applicability, and test this implementation. Do not require a new discovery experiment or fresh proof of a known theorem merely to use it. “Game-theoretically optimal” is conditional on the specified game, information and incentives; historical success is informative, not universal. Concentrate novel research proof on what goes beyond established knowledge and expected human foresight. Routine implementation correctness, security, cash conservation and evidence for reported profit remain necessary; these are validation, not demands to reinvent known theory. New claims about our realized profit, scaling or invented-method advantage still need appropriate evidence.

## Run the right comparison

1. Start from a consequential outcome and enabling constraint, not only an incumbent service’s local defect or a committee of mechanisms. Compare opportunities that could open important classes of work before choosing a decisive prototype. Select a baseline and a small discriminating experiment; a no-bid/no-invest decision is valid.
2. Give policies only permitted observations. Model voluntary buyer choice, competitors/suppliers, payment delays, capacity, channels, repeat demand and adverse regions. No forced sales, hidden future draws or free resources.
3. Compare baseline, commerce-only, capability-only, joint and tuned fixed-split policies when claiming interaction. Compare no memory, acquired reuse and **equal-content ordinary reuse** when claiming successor-method improvement. Commerce alone may win.
4. Keep complete costs and obligation accounting, exact source and compact success/negative outcome summaries. Do not annualize a short cohort or promote an optimistic projection into native validated scale. The [annual observer](../../connected_enterprise/scale.py) is reporting, not proof that a full-year horizon or $3B profit was achieved.
5. Consolidate a few-word lesson, preserve a useful fix/regression, then delete redundant terminal artifacts under [lean policy](../packaging/LEAN_EXPERIMENTS.md). Preserve true dependencies or migrate them safely. A loss can invalidate a policy without invalidating its useful mechanism code.
6. Publish and inspect actual GitHub Actions receipts, qualified release artifacts and a downloaded released-system run. Choose adoption/defaults using established theory with checked applicability or suitable empirical evidence, and correctness tests—not mandatory novelty research. Preserve a stronger incumbent when the relevant comparison shows it wins; labels such as “joint”, “discovery” or “optimal” are not evidence.

## Practical recipes

These are ways to choose and build useful work, not a mandatory multi-agent pipeline. Apply established methods directly when their assumptions fit. The extra fresh/crossed comparisons below support **new advantage claims**, not permission to use known engineering. None guarantees commercial or recursive success.

### 1. Find the smallest transformation that matters

**Use when:** a consequential opportunity requires new means, or the incumbent is blocked or expensive. Do not assume the incumbent service, market or business design is the right objective. Write the unchanged beneficiary outcome, then classify constraints as hard facts, uncertain assumptions or choices of the inherited method. Work backward from the outcome and forward from available tools. Compare obtaining the missing resource, substituting/combining existing means, and eliminating its necessity. A typed interface or stronger production constraint can make verification easier; removing every constraint is not the objective.

**Produce:** an executable change, the burden it removes and introduces, and a checkable failure condition. For example, source-change detection plus claim dependencies can replace repeated whole-document inspection with targeted revalidation—but incomplete dependencies, migration and maintenance still cost. `criticism.instrument_spec/response` and `pipeline.archive_enabling` provide bounded construction hooks; a general source-to-claim production system is an implementation opportunity, not an existing demonstrated capability.

### 2. Transfer structure; construct the missing connector

**Use when:** another domain appears to solve the same causal problem. Map entities, relations, invariants, assumptions and outcome semantics with `mapping.discover/compile_mapping`; do not match vocabulary alone. At a mismatch, reject the analogy, construct an adapter that establishes the missing property, or reformulate to avoid that property.

**Produce:** a runnable transfer with explicit applicability and a falsifying case. The current typed mapper is bounded; it does not autonomously discover arbitrary scientific correspondences. A successful adaptation need not be reinvented merely because its underlying mechanism is known.

### 3. Turn criticism into information and construction

**Use when:** a material defect or uncertainty could change the decision. Give a critic the original source and acceptance target, not just another agent's answer. Seek an exact omission, contradiction, counterexample or executable discriminating test. `critique → adjudicate → derive/response` turns established objections into repair constraints or instrument proposals. Reject unsupported objections; allow “no consequential defect established.” Preserve the useful regression, not the full failed transcript.

If evidence fits competing explanations, consider **neither** being right. Choose between a valuable test, expanding hypotheses, constructing a measurement, an acceptable action robust to the supported possibilities, removing the uncertain dependency, and stopping. “No affordable test found” is not impossibility; robustness over an incomplete hypothesis set is not universal safety. General open-world experiment invention remains a gap beyond the bounded current grammar.

### 4. Build evidence into production; challenge the checker

**Use when:** verification or repeated source reconstruction dominates cost. Carry versions, claim conditions, exceptions, dependencies and source support into the deliverable. Independently check both the result and whether the representation omitted something consequential. This may reduce future reasoning work without changing model weights.

For reference disagreement, [`mechanism_harness.evidence.reflect`](../../mechanism_harness/evidence.py) implements **categorical recovery conditional on a declared source-group fault bound**. It is not the broader mathematical Out-Reflect construction or a general truth oracle. Source labels do not prove independence; coherent shared errors and a wrong fault budget defeat assurance. Test reference semantics, calibration and blind spots outside the candidate's self-approval boundary. Do not transplant a polynomial/error-correction result into natural-language work without applicable evidence semantics.

### 5. Invent the arrangement, not only its parts

**Use when:** order, complementary components, missing interfaces or feedback determine feasibility. Represent each mechanism as permitted inputs + state + resources → outputs + changed state + evidence. Check prerequisites, interface meaning, shared-resource conflicts, synchronization and stopping conditions. `construct`, `pipeline.combinations` and `memory.generate_bundle/ordinary_bundle` support bounded executable compositions; `mechanism_harness` also has symbolic contracts and an actual CSV adapter. Symbolic connector proposals are not automatically qualified production services.

Compare a complete bundle even when its isolated parts have no immediate gain. One step may create the information or condition another requires. Count connector construction, setup, execution, verification and maintenance. Package a useful composition with its own contract for later reuse; retain a simpler specialist when it is better. General hypergraph/interface invention and open-ended new primitives are research goals, not claims established by the current finite language or opt-in sequence policy.

### 6. Inherit the right asset—and improve the inventor separately

Retain three distinct things when useful: **working instrument**, **applicability/failure model**, and **constructor of related instruments**. `memory` and `flywheel.retention` provide bounded source/applicability-aware reuse, not unlimited semantic learning. A descendant gets compatible capability/history, not automatic current acceptance or authority. Recheck actual inputs, outputs, environment and permissions; invalidate reuse when relevant identities change.

For a new successor-method claim, compare acquired reuse on fresh successors against no inheritance, removal of the retained artifact and equal-content ordinary reuse. Retained artifacts are neither retained proof nor inherited authority. For a new discovery-policy claim, cross old/new discovery procedure with old/new starting tools on fresh tasks, counting acquisition and running costs. Better equipment alone is not a better inventor. Supplied templates, transfer mappings and developer fixes remain identified. Consolidate “tried → outcome → why → avoid” into compact knowledge, then remove redundant failed files; obsolete machinery can be pruned too.

### 7. Make useful production finance the next useful choice

**Use when:** choosing an offer, production method or investment. Optimize the whole relationship—price, acceptance/collection probability, repeat value, acquisition, onboarding/context reuse, capacity, support and obligations—against competent human-plus-AI, autonomous, self-service and no-purchase alternatives. Human wages are a comparison, not guaranteed willingness to pay or a mandatory discount. A direct specialist, no bid or retaining cash can win.

Use canonical `plan_actions → select/allocate_actions` and funded execution; do not add a second ledger. Consider recurring complete functions, authorized additional services and costed distribution channels without assuming infinite demand or counting bundle components twice. Sequence planning may select only the currently affordable first action, then reobserve and replan. Future enabling value can justify a finite experiment but is not income or spendable cash. No fixed R&D split: ordinary profitable expansion may be best.

Track **financed by** separately from **causally enabled by**. Commercial surplus, inherited capability, improved discovery and physical productive capacity are different outcomes. Software/simulation results do not establish an installed industrial asset; physical work needs its own authorized environment, measurements and lifecycle costs. None of these recipes establishes a trailing-year profit milestone by itself.


## Scale without arbitrary commercial ceilings

There is no arbitrary maximum buyer count, business count, service catalogue, transaction volume or profit. The historical $3B trailing-year complete-cost profit notification milestone is not a cap. Treat $100B annual profit as a required engineering stress case, not an upper bound, forecast or achieved result; support larger cases when justified.

Separate business-scale semantics from finite execution: resumable batches, scalable state/indexes and validated aggregation can support larger workloads while each experiment and job respects actual platform resources, native cash/obligations, permissions and security. Do not disable resource guards, assume infinite demand or replace an artificial ceiling with another magic number. Trace the full path through admission, planning, execution, persistence, restart, audit and reporting; accepting a large number at one interface is not scale support.

Scale tests must exercise meaningful demand/fulfillment/settlement workloads, conservation and duplicate/recovery behavior, with complete cost and explicit horizon. Validate aggregate calculations against tractable native executions and preserve adverse regions. Report measured limits and unresolved bottlenecks honestly; do not change an in-flight cohort or fabricate profit to meet a stress target. Current scale-cap repairs are implementation work requiring their own qualification, not something this guidance itself implements.
