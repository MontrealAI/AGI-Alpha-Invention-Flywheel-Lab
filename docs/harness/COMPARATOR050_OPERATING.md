# Five native policies with development-only fixed-share tuning

The comparator uses the canonical `run_world`, `JournalAccount`, native account, complete-offer production/checking and independent operating/financial auditor. It adds no optimizer, ledger, free customer, copied balance or default policy. Its native category-cap dependency is `e37f78193e0aaf7644b76727676ea8d3c1b825cc`; normal integrated-source qualification/release remains separate.

| Policy | Existing native path | Deliberate difference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strong conventional | Complete public offers, exact quotes, costed channel menu, complementary capacity quantities and composed future window | Ordinary exact risk-adjusted allocator, without the follower-game objective. It sees the same candidate actions and public facts as joint at a matched prefix. |
| Commerce only | Complete public quote/channel/sequence path | Capacity quantity policy is zero; physical supplier prices, initial capacity and maximum capacity stay unchanged. |
| Capability only | Explicit quantity recourse and current complete-offer delivery | Existing `baseline` engine arm suppresses acquisition. No sequence or channel-menu policy; supplier/channel environment and market inputs are unchanged. |
| Composed joint | Complete public sequence, channel, quantity and exact quote path | Existing finite pessimistic-follower/minimax-regret selector. Authored rival forecast responses are not observed rival dynamics. |
| Tuned fixed share | Strong conventional path plus native `category_budgets_bps` | The share is selected only on separate registered development worlds, then frozen before evaluation. |

Fixed shares mean **per-decision upfront acquisition/capacity invoice caps** on current eligible earnings after operating deductions. They are not persistent category buckets or guaranteed cumulative/lifetime spend proportions. Every chosen upfront category invoice counts, including seed-eligible startup acquisition if present; this experiment does not enable startup acquisition. Contingent future invoices retain global cash/earned/liability protection and must replan under future category caps. Unused room stays cash; no investment is forced.

The strong control is not denied the candidate's window, complete offers, prices or allowed investments. A native test reconstructs both choices at every observed prefix and compares their full delivery/investment candidate lists, native observation, risk, current slots and operating deductions; only the final objective differs. Ablations intentionally remove action classes and therefore do not share that same-action claim. Commerce, conventional or holding cash may win.

## Freeze before any tuning account opens

`campaign/comparator-050/PROTOCOL.json` fixes all source, inputs, finite choices, costs, metrics and stopping rules. Six development accounts use acquisition shares 0/5000/10000 basis points crossed with central/no-demand, seed2611010503 and cohort2611010504. The rule maximizes minimum complete modeled profit, then total profit, then proximity to50/50, then lower acquisition share. No evaluation result can enter this selection.

Ten subsequent evaluation accounts cross the five policies with central/no-demand, using distinct seed2611010501 and cohort2611010502. Every account begins with exactly US$100,000, eight authored prospects,21 operating days plus normal drain, the same finite service dimensions and unchanged source-bound market within each case. These are builder-visible engineering cases, not untouched statistical holdouts or empirical demand. Parameter choice and the selection rule are fixed before outcomes; losses, refusals and held cash remain valid results.

The driver freezes both cohorts' exact generated input/artifact/receipt bytes and all development plus four untuned evaluation configurations before operation. Before the fixed-share evaluation is frozen, all six saved native development histories are independently audited and their complete public rows reconstructed. A producer-written “completed” flag cannot authorize tuning or evaluation. Source changes, alternate valid cohorts, modified configs, duplicates, incomplete native history, forged public profits or changed selection fail closed. Completed cells are revalidated before being skipped on continuation.

## Costs and account identities

All native setup, acquisition, qualification, production, checking, support, payment, tax, maintenance and failed-work costs remain charged. Conventional, commerce and joint use the same sequence setup/planning envelope. Capability only does not pay the unused additional US$5 sequence setup or US$0.05 daily sequence-planning envelope.

Tuning is not free. All six development businesses retain their native incurred expenses and financial histories. Their gross expenses are allocated exactly once across the two fixed-share evaluation setup envelopes, with the integer residual assigned deterministically to central. This is a disclosed conservative modeled lifecycle attribution, not a measured external tuning bill or transfer of their money. Do not add the development expenses again to the already-attributed fixed-share evaluation lifecycle score. No pooled enterprise financial total is reported; development and evaluation accounts remain independent counterfactual simulations. Unknown actual host/model/engineering billing remains unknown.

Before evaluation starts, `SELECTED.json` binds the full development outcome, selection rule, selected share and exact cost allocation. `EVALUATION_FROZEN.json` binds the resulting evaluation configs. No losing world is replaced or extended and no account is reseeded. A paused run continues its original signed checkpoint and queue. Completed-account resume must leave native history unchanged; the public financial row is rederived from independently audited native records.

Completed resume can update resource telemetry in `RESULT.json` while native history remains identical. The wrapper therefore reads, audits and hashes the actually persisted post-resume result; it separately checks unchanged native account history. Its regression rejects changed money or a resumed result that differs from saved bytes. A historical wrapper failure that hashed the pre-resume object must keep its original failed receipt; a separately recorded read-only receipt correction can rebind only the stale result hash after complete native re-audit, without changing the account, frozen source or financial values.
