# AGI ALPHA: funded entry into a better complete outcome

## Implemented mechanism

This opt-in experiment uses the existing merged native enterprise. It introduces no new account, financial runtime, buyer simulator, default policy or private authority. The current complete-offer selector compares a narrow reconciliation service with an independently checked three-component receivables packet. Native capacity search compares resource quantities under current liquidity, earned funds, observed requests and explicit supplier terms. Buyers can instead choose human service, an autonomous rival, self-service or no purchase.

The opportunity is concrete: one production slot cannot fulfill a packet requiring three simultaneous capacity units. The machine may continue qualified narrow delivery, use collected earnings to acquire two additional units, and then deliver the complete outcome. Buying capacity is not sufficient: production, independent acceptance, collection, costs, obligations and audit must all succeed. The experiment invokes the actual existing producer/checker and JournalAccount; a report cannot create a settlement.

The retained object is a compact applicability capsule derived from audited matched outcomes. It keeps the simple method on ties, records no supported profitable expansion in a losing region, and admits complementary capacity only where a positive matched result and conservative same-account successor witness both exist. It carries no current proof or authority. This does not establish improvement of the invention procedure itself.

Within each pair the only change is permitted capacity search: one added unit versus a complementary quantity. These are finite experimental settings, not global business ceilings. A favorable seed or new horizon may not silently replace an adverse result.

The customer-facing outcome, acceptance rule, supplier capacity, input workload, posted prices, outside options and obligations stay fixed within each pair. Requests are assigned unique admitted work IDs; one accepted input is not repeatedly sold as fresh work. Complete packets include cash application, source-derived balances and unresolved exceptions. They do not include autonomous collections contact, external ledger writeback, tax advice or full financial assurance.

All demand, valuations, prices, supplier terms and monetary cost envelopes are authored. The buyer is modeled as valuing narrow work at $55 and the complete packet at $180, subject to a $1,500 budget. Posted prices are $30 and $120. Capacity costs $8 per unit. Setup is the native $75 envelope; variable production, qualification, unsuccessful acquisition, support, payment fees, tax provision and maintenance are charged by the unchanged native engine. Permanent protection remains. Real model, host and development bills remain unknown. Access to the existing code is assumed; its historical development is not magically free.

## Evidence levels and conservative accounting

`project` requires a canonical operating audit before reporting results. `witness` then identifies:

1. A prior accepted and collected narrow outcome in the same account.
2. Actually earned-funded capacity, active when the successor qualifies.
3. An independently accepted, source-linked, settled three-unit packet.
4. Every transitive required enabler invoice, each counted once.
5. All linked costs and losses, including unsuccessful/uncollected work.
6. Positive whole-enterprise modeled profit and a conservative positive result after charging **all enterprise expenses** against the linked successor receipts alone.

The last check intentionally over-allocates shared costs; it does not purport to be causal attribution. A matched comparison estimates this particular capacity-policy effect under the fixed authored market, not general superiority or human-expert outperformance. Payment lineage, technological enablement, positive contribution, profitable enterprise, improved discovery and physical capacity are distinct claims.

## Current result interpretation

Initial local engineering execution on the recorded pre-publication source produced no packet or gain in the short central pair; the strong-outside pair lost money and admitted no sales. The larger pair completed four packets only with complementary capacity: $396.408 modeled profit versus $77.368 for one-unit search. A $30 prior collection preceded $16 of earned-funded capacity and then four $120 packet settlements. The exact final-source public run must reproduce or reject those results; earlier source is never relabeled.

A source-bound capability and scoped lesson are useful even when no novel invention is established. This experiment is a practical known-method transformation of an executable constraint, not a breakthrough beyond human foresight, a universal equilibrium, real trade, billion-dollar annual profit or physical productive expansion. The next economic question is whether stronger recurring demand, validated partner terms and evidence-producing services support a fresh successor after their entire acquisition and operating costs. Do not infer that from a favorable 28-day authored case.
