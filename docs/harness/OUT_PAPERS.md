# Use the OUT papers to construct the flagship

These six user-authorized research manuscripts are **tools for the consequential activity**, not six mandatory stages or evidence that their mechanisms are deployed here. Start with the [flagship and arrangement card](DEVELOPER_GUIDE.md#mission-construct-economically-reachable-possibilities); select only mechanisms that remove its missing-means constraint. Passing their examples, publishing papers or building a generic framework does not establish useful delivery, profitable funded expansion or a scientific breakthrough.

The browser readers below preserve the original PDFs. Page references refer to those supplied editions (20–21 October 2026). Their theorems, synthetic companion results and proposed integrations are manuscript claims; this publication does not independently reproduce companions, qualify a physical system or import their guarantees into this repository. For actual implemented paths and limitations, use the [implementation map](DEVELOPER_GUIDE.md#implementation-and-claim-boundaries).

## Choose by the missing means

### 📚 OUT-LEARN — construct a reusable observation instrument

[Read in the browser](https://montrealai.github.io/AGI-Alpha-Invention-Flywheel-Lab/out-learn.html) · pp. 3–10, 13–17

**Use when:** repeated observations are expensive or mix the desired signal with nuisance. Construct timing, polarity and weights that preserve the signal and cancel the qualified nuisance; jointly choose reference calibration and future-use programmes for the intended reuse horizon. Return gain/response intervals, not an unjustified precise answer.

**Execute and retain:** measurement programme, required compact observations (or verified public source references, available hashes and reproducible retrieval), reference/gain evidence, normalization and nuisance-cancellation checks, error bounds and invalidation conditions. Preserve sufficient replay evidence without defaulting to bulk archival; this does not authorize deletion of existing originals. Qualify reversal, gain stability/floor, signal constancy, sensor range and the polynomial-plus-bounded-residual model before relying on the guarantees. Changed gain, signal-shaped nuisance or clipping can invalidate reuse; exact rational demonstrations do not qualify floating-point hardware. Charge construction, calibration, each query and requalification, not just acquisition units. The sparse-reading and minimax results apply to the stated architectures, not all learning.

### 🧠 OUT-THINK — construct an executable reasoning interface

[Read in the browser](https://montrealai.github.io/AGI-Alpha-Invention-Flywheel-Lab/out-think.html) · pp. 3–10, 12–16, 18–21

**Use when:** a summary answers today's questions but cannot safely update, or an obsolete internal representation prevents a simpler realization. Construct an encoder, decoder and operation-update rules from explicit source, context, answer and operation semantics.

**Execute and retain:** check both required-answer preservation and update commutation for every admitted state/context/operation. Keep counterexamples and supported scope. Updates cannot secretly read discarded source or unpriced history. Retire an internal label only when it is not an external output/audit obligation and sufficient source remains for recoding. New questions/operations require requalification. Charge codebooks, context acquisition, compilation, migration, archives and updates; resident-label savings are not total-cost or model-memory gains. Specialized finite minimum formulas require their stated structural assumptions.

### 🎨 OUT-DESIGN — create usable room to transform

[Read in the browser](https://montrealai.github.io/AGI-Alpha-Invention-Flywheel-Lab/out-design.html) · pp. 4–5, 7–13, 18–20

**Use when:** a valuable upgrade has no legal first move in occupied resources. Preserve the service purpose while constructing an equivalent realization that releases capacity **where the next transition can actually use it**.

**Execute and retain:** service contracts, independent-input equivalence, preparation and operating resource vectors, access topology, and a checked prepare → qualify → promote → retire/restore route. Check every intermediate state, not just the desired endpoint. Isolation, failure domains, state and in-flight obligations belong in the contract when required. A freed slot cannot simultaneously count as new production, rollback space and reserve. Compare buying spare capacity and strong conventional consolidation. The paper's finite atomic service model does not establish safe native-account migration, distributed uptime or physical performance; existing authentication/recovery safeguards remain binding.

### ♟️ OUT-STRATEGISE — construct a sufficient route despite uncertainty

[Read in the browser](https://montrealai.github.io/AGI-Alpha-Invention-Flywheel-Lab/out-strategise.html) · pp. 3–8, 12–14, 17–19

**Use when:** further diagnosis may cost more than a robust construction. Compare direct completion, information gathering and changed means. Construct an observation tree with **one common executable completion per leaf that works in every residual world**, rather than secretly choosing a different action using the true world.

**Execute and retain:** purpose/checkpoints, permitted observations, world/test/module semantics, residual worlds, branch costs and the same leaf route checked across all compatible worlds. Recompute after changes to purpose, costs, access or permissions. Deferred construction and the shortest-path specialization need their stated independence assumptions; interacting modules, construction-enabled tests or shared setup require a richer state/solver. Search-incomplete is not impossible, and a feasible plan is not a proven optimum. The finite research adapter is not production integration or a source of authority to act.

### ⚡ OUT-EXECUTE — retain the right boundary, refresh the actual work

[Read in the browser](https://montrealai.github.io/AGI-Alpha-Invention-Flywheel-Lab/out-execute.html) · pp. 4–10, 11–18

**Use when:** changing interiors interact through a manageable interface. Construct a complete boundary value profile with current attaining witnesses and infeasibility evidence; compose compatible profiles, not isolated scalar optima.

**Execute and retain:** full domains/scopes, single ownership of each factor, input identity, all profile rows and minimum/infeasibility justification. No factor may secretly couple private groups. After a local change, refresh its witness and validate the full current artifact. Only exact profile equality with unchanged admitted semantics/outside factors justifies stopping external reoptimization. Changed boundaries require reconstruction or qualified fallback. Prefer simpler exact-input/fixed-proof reuse when sufficient; dense interfaces and qualification cost may erase savings. Current revision, authority and durable receipt must accompany acceptance; controlled local-datastore retry results do not prove exactly-once remote effects.

### ♾️ OUT-EVOLVE — build and reuse the means of further construction

[Read in the browser](https://montrealai.github.io/AGI-Alpha-Invention-Flywheel-Lab/out-evolve.html) · pp. 3–8, 12–18, 23–27

**Use when:** a valuable activity requires a new instrument, production architecture or access arrangement. Construct a purpose-preserving system that **earns** useful structure, rather than assuming an easy model fits reality. Retain executable means, its envelope, qualification, full resource account and legitimate adaptation rights.

**Execute and retain:** freeze the asset before fresh work; demonstrate transfer and a different accepted successor. For new causal advantage claims, compare asset removal, equal-content ordinary reuse and strong unrestricted specialists with matched access and full campaign costs. Sparse calibration does not certify absent interactions; plant/loading/readout changes can invalidate extension rules. Recompute outside alternatives after invention: cost recovery is neither profit nor voluntary consent, and acceptance-time payment cannot fund earlier construction. The paper's separate finite studies and proposed industrial programme are not one executed commercial flywheel.

## Use one small record, then deliver

Add the following to the existing arrangement card or execution receipt—not a new compulsory framework:

1. **Dependency:** flagship activity, missing means and next accepted delivery/successor this mechanism enables.
2. **Construction:** paper/page, applicable assumptions, executable source entry point and concrete output. If not implemented, say so; a citation is not a call path.
3. **Qualification:** independent acceptance check, invalidating observation, strongest applicable complete alternative, and actual execution receipt/costs. Hashes bind bytes, not semantic truth.
4. **Continuation:** what useful capability survives, its scope/requalification rule, and the native cash/obligations permitting the next action.

Reuse the existing mapping/construction/criticism/retention hooks only where their contracts fit; they do not automatically implement the six constructions. Known methods need applicability and implementation checks, not redundant novelty proofs. Charge search, failed attempts, construction, verification, ingestion, production, maintenance, remedy and financing; separate unknown billing from modeled envelopes. Preserve negative outcomes and distinguish simulation from commerce.

The objective remains an important previously uneconomic activity made possible, then accepted profitable delivery and earned-funded expansion. Keep the primary US$100,000-seed → US$3 billion trailing-365-day complete-cost profit track, authenticated account continuity and no-pooling/no-top-up rules unchanged. Publicly retain compact reusable evidence and lessons; publish no unapproved private material. These papers confer no spending, deployment or external-action authority.
