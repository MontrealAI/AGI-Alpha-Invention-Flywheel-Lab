## What now runs autonomously

The new driver reads the source-pinned inert applicability capsule from that executed experiment, checks its identity and empty proof/authority, and constructs a new mission's **search configuration**, not a purchase instruction. Current packet resource requirements come from `packet_contract`, not an inherited stale proof. Full native buyer choice, actual current cash, earned-funding, expiry and independent delivery checks remain active.

The fixed program compares:

1. One-unit increments (the original limited search).
2. Strong ordinary complementary search, already knowing the general method.
3. Retained complementary search with a known resource bound derived from current outcome requirements. It pays an additional **$5 modeled adaptation/checking cost**. The prior research is sunk shared history, not transferred cash. Actual host/model/development bills are still unknown.

The current offer menu admits at most two simultaneous quotes, each requiring at most three slots. Retained search therefore need not buy beyond that menu's maximum useful workload. This is ordinary known resource reasoning informed by the retained applicability rule, **not a discovered theorem or improved inventor**. The strong ordinary comparator is never weakened or denied useful information.

Three cases and nine independent native accounts are fixed before execution: 24 prospects/28days with a new seed; 48 prospects/42days with another new seed; and the same larger cohort facing a strong $15 outside rival. Exactly $100,000 starts each account, with no top-ups or pooling. These experiment sizes do not cap the enterprise. Increasing authored prospective customers is a workload test, not evidence of new real distribution or calibrated demand. Failed and tied cases are valid outcomes; no new seeds are searched afterward.

The machine selects a case-local policy from fully audited observed modeled profit with obligations covered. Simpler/ordinary alternatives win ties. A costlier inherited method can correctly lose. `ADOPTION.json` retains that outcome and applicability limits but has `current_proof:null`, `authority:[]`, and no automatic promotion. No operating account is silently switched to a new policy.

## Claim boundary

A source-linked enabling chain is not automatically a causal allocation of profit. The matched intervention supports a finite simulation comparison, not general market optimality. Successful inheritance is not a better discovery procedure; that needs fresh equal-content crossed controls. Native simulated revenue is not real revenue. Forty-two days is not a trailing year. No annualized billion-dollar result, physical asset deployment, actual partner consent or limitless autonomous operation is asserted.

The long-horizon goal is to construct increasingly consequential productive arrangements. Here the concrete advance is an executable, costed transfer from a retained rule into larger accepted native workloads, followed by an evidence-based decision about whether to keep it. Exact-source CI, review, normal integration and release remain separate requirements.
