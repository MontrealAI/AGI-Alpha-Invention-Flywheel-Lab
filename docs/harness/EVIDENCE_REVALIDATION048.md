# Dependency-carrying evidence: finite instrument experiment

This is a developer-authored adaptation of established incremental build/cache invalidation to a small evidence artifact. It is not autonomous discovery, commercial delivery or a better inventor. It leaves the canonical runtime, ledgers, defaults, source inventory, historical battery failure and other owners' work untouched.

## Constructed instrument and applicability

`experiments.evidence_revalidation048.construct` compiles an exact supported CSV claim contract into explicit claim/source dependencies and pins the executing instrument. `produce` recomputes changed claims and can carry unchanged values only from an artifact with an out-of-band trusted hash and the identical plan/contract. `audit` reconstructs every claim from all current declared inputs using a separate parser/evaluator; it does not trust the producer's reuse decision or prior output. This is procedural checker separation on one host, not outside certification.

Supported outcomes are exact integer column totals and unique, nonempty keys. Three authored domains exercise their actual semantics: distribution byte totals change, repeated manufacturing serials invalidate uniqueness, and negative measurement totals remain negative. These are synthetic examples, not financial accounts, real production inspection or physical measurements. A source-to-claim semantic mapping is explicitly supplied by the contract; it is not invented or inferred from natural language.

The analogy breaks when dependencies are undeclared, source membership changes, interpretation is ambiguous, the transformation or checker changes, or trusted predecessor identity is unavailable. The instrument refuses unsupported operations, incomplete plans, extra/missing/unaccounted inputs and scope changes. It cannot prove that a human-supplied contract includes every semantically relevant external source. Full independent current-input reconstruction therefore remains mandatory. Source hashes provide tamper evidence relative to a trusted pin, not signatures or inherited execution authority.

## Frozen comparison and criticism

Before the recorded run, `register` writes the full six-case protocol, input content and exact driver/test SHA256 identities. The six cases are three domains crossed with localized and all-source changes. Each arm receives the same current contract and inputs; ordinary reuse and the retained-method arm receive identical predecessor content, dependency plan and trusted identity. Each gets one production and one full current-input audit; no arm searches for a favorable result. Both reuse arms charge the full predecessor production/audit acquisition, with no amortization. The competent strong baseline simply reconstructs the current answer and pays no needless acquisition.

The primary metric is accepted successor artifacts. The secondary metric counts all CSV row visits in acquisition, production and independent verification. Hashing, parsing, orchestration, maintenance and engineering are not fully priced by this metric. Measured process CPU/wall time is telemetry; actual host/model/engineering USD cost and commercial profit remain unknown/null. There is no new ledger, simulated price or invented economic surplus.

The critic produces concrete executable counterexamples in `tests/test_evidence_revalidation048.py`: omitted dependencies; undeclared/missing sources; unavailable trusted predecessor identity; changed scope; noncanonical integers; malformed rows; duplicate headers; forged carried values; incomplete experimental coverage; and adverse quality/measurement outcomes. In particular, a newly supplied hash for a forged predecessor can fool a cache-only producer; the unchanged full current-input auditor rejects the forged result. This objection prevents treating hash consistency as truth or selective producer work as verified total savings.

All cases and tests are builder-visible engineering cases, distinct successor artifacts but **not untouched holdouts or scientific confirmation**. The deterministic constructor and equal-content ordinary baseline use the same known mechanism. This study can refute an advantage claim based only on wrapping identical content in a retention label; it cannot establish learned discovery-policy improvement.

## Exact local operation

From the reviewed checkout, with a new absolute output directory:

```sh
python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -p test_evidence_revalidation048.py -v
python3 -m experiments.evidence_revalidation048 register --output /absolute/new/evidence048
python3 -m experiments.evidence_revalidation048 run --output /absolute/new/evidence048
python3 -m experiments.evidence_revalidation048 verify --output /absolute/new/evidence048
```

Registration is immutable for this source/input snapshot; a changed driver or test fails verification. Re-running a completed fixed operation refuses. The output is entirely public synthetic evidence and inert data, with `authority: []`; it grants no delivery, account, spending or release permission. A finite research run is separate from native operating enterprise evidence.

## Retained lesson

Tried dependency-carrying selective production → localized producer row work falls, but identical-content ordinary reuse ties and one-successor full recomputation costs fewer lifecycle row visits → acquisition plus full independent reconstruction dominates this small workload → retain the narrow correctness instrument and explicit applicability, use the simpler full recomputation baseline here, and do not claim discovery or economic advantage. Further savings would require justified amortization or a stronger independently checked incremental certificate, with its actual construction and trust costs counted.

## Directly executed local result

The frozen six cases ran once with Python 3.12.14. Ten focused adversarial tests passed. Register, operate and a separate-process full verification each exited zero; verification reconstructed all 18 rows. Source identities, exact commands and resource telemetry are in `campaign/evidence-revalidation-048/RECEIPT.json`. The two public JSON artifacts contain the complete small synthetic inputs/results and can be re-audited directly:

```sh
python3 -m experiments.evidence_revalidation048 verify --output campaign/evidence-revalidation-048
```

| Arm | Accepted successors | Producer CSV row visits | Acquisition + production + current full audit row visits |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Strong full recomputation | 6 / 6 | 36 | 72 |
| Equal-content ordinary reuse | 6 / 6 | 24 | 132 |
| Retained-method wrapper | 6 / 6 | 24 | 132 |

The duplicate-serial manufacturing result is false and the changed measurement sum is −18; acceptance means these adverse values are faithfully reported, not that a quality or measurement objective succeeded. All-source changes recompute every claim. No result is annualized, no account is seeded or resumed, and no commerce or retained-method superiority is established. This is local engineering evidence, pending independent source review and normal repository qualification; it is not proof of a merged/released implementation or a transformed enterprise.
