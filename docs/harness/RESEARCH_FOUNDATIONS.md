## Reading order and authority

1. **Flagship:** [Transformational Intelligence: Learning to Change the Game](../research/transformational-intelligence-202610.pdf). Begin with the authorized purpose, not a frozen problem formulation. Distinguish intended outcomes, inherited means and evaluation instruments. Construct and test a better setup rather than merely renaming the task.
2. **Foundation:** [AGI ALPHA: A Scalable Substrate for Intelligence Organizations](../research/agi-alpha-foundation-20260430.pdf). Use bounded work, explicit agent/job authority, evidence, validator-gated acceptance, auditable settlement and retained capabilities as organizational design requirements. The architecture does not itself certify a deployment or grant authority.
3. Select the relevant construction below; connect it to the [OUT mechanisms](OUT_PAPERS.md), [developer guide](DEVELOPER_GUIDE.md) and current campaign rather than opening a competing implementation.

## Apply the flagship on every consequential candidate

- **Purpose and boundary:** record the authorized useful outcome, genuinely binding constraints, uncertain assumptions and removable design choices. Do not gain a result by changing the goal or silently granting more permission.
- **Constructive proposal:** name the missing means and implement the smallest decisive construction. Consider complementary changes that fail individually. An absent test within available resources is not proof of impossibility.
- **Strong comparison:** give complete alternatives equivalent information, construction/coordination/financing permissions and full resource accounting. Preserve ties and failures. A conventional tie limits an invention-superiority claim; it does not alone disprove commercial profitability.
- **Decisive evidence:** freeze the claim, exact source, task population, observations, acceptance and finite experiment conditions. Independently check outputs and reject invalid transformations. Do not let the candidate approve its own easier evaluator.
- **Economics and governance:** jointly choose modeled starting capital, financing and strategy; retain historical reference scenarios without making them universal constraints. Future earnings and hypothetical financing are not spendable today. Reconcile current cash, costs, commitments and protected obligations through the canonical account.
- **Retained value:** use the resulting instrument or procedure on fresh successor work. Compare no inheritance and ordinary reuse with equal artifact content; distinguish saved content from an improved discovery method.
- **Record the decision:** state what changed, what passed, what failed, full costs/unknowns, the warranted claim and next useful construction or stopping decision. Keep commercial, capability, discovery and physical/scientific achievements separate.

## Evidence boundaries

The flagship's causal illustration is model-conditional and matched by an unrestricted planner. The related manuscripts likewise disclose classical/synthetic models and strong alternatives; do not present supplied algorithms as autonomous invention. The foundation describes a proposed organizational substrate, not achieved AGI/ASI or guaranteed returns. Current project qualification still requires executed source-bound tests, original failures, acceptance, resources and appropriate authority.
