# FLYWHEEL Ω — Finite Economic Game-Maker

**Status:** Executable research instrument; synthetic commercial hypotheses only. It proposes a combined production/participation game and an offer. **It cannot spend money, acquire buyers, create contracts, approve a scientific invention, or write to the canonical financial ledger.**

## Economic construction, not just price selection

The existing mechanism_harness finds composable symbolic mechanisms, and the canonical connected_enterprise handles funded, audited simulated work. The addition mechanism_harness.game_maker generates combinations of explicitly described economic operations and evaluates them as alternative games for the **same fixed buyer outcome**:

1. Compose zero or more costed transformations, enforcing prerequisites, incompatible alternatives and partner participation (payment at least outside option).
2. Set a common seller quote from a declared finite grid; no buyer is forced to participate.
3. For each registered scenario, solve the rival's finite **best response**, including no entry and optional imitation. Buyers choose by complete modeled utility, budget and outside alternatives; ties are conservatively lost by us.
4. Charge declared quote, build, fixed and per-buyer production, partner compensation and scenario cost changes; require enough unrestricted starting cash to deliver before collection. The fixture does **not** independently price experiment-search effort, actual model/host billing, real acquisition, all downstream maintenance or real delivery risk; therefore its profit number is **conditional modeled contribution, not verified complete-lifecycle profit**. If a delivery fails acceptance, revenue is zero; if collection is delayed, recognized profit and spendable cash differ.
5. Maximize **declared risk-adjusted expected complete-cost profit** over the whole enumerated, financeable subset and **non-entry**. Exhaustion of a search limit is explicitly SEARCH_BOUND_EXHAUSTED, not an optimum.

This is a complete-information **Stackelberg game conditional on the authored model** (seller leads, profit-seeking rival follows, buyers choose). It is **not** a proven Nash equilibrium, real buyer model or universal best strategy. Scenario weights and cost effects are authored, not empirically calibrated.

## Run

From repository root with Python 3.11+; no network or paid API is used:

    python3 -m unittest mechanism_harness.test_game_maker -v
    python3 -m mechanism_harness.game_maker mechanism_harness/game_maker_fixture.json --output /tmp/omega-game-NEW.json

The output is source-digest-bound and includes selected scenario accounting, a deterministic rederivation receipt, exact enumerated-state count, and a finite-set optimality gap of zero **only on completed searches**. The rederivation is **not independent empirical verification**. Do not reuse an output path; preserve old evidence.

## The frozen illustrative finding

Four buyers each have value 30 and outside utility 10, with prices 16–20 in **abstract units**. Baseline production costs 50 fixed + 12 per buyer + 1 quote. Neither the evidence-in-production operation nor pooled commissioning alone supports a profitable accepted four-buyer delivery at price 19. Combining their declared effects produces fixed cost 46, unit cost 6 and quote cost 1. Price 19 then yields 76 modeled receipts, 71 declared modeled costs and **+5 central contribution before unpriced discovery/overhead**. Each buyer receives positive modeled benefit over its outside option. The rational incumbent rival declines without the combined capability.

In the **copycat** case, an equally capable rival matches our price, wins the model's conservative buyer tie, and leaves us **−1** quote cost. In the **delayed collection** case, the seller recognizes **+5 accounting profit** but collects nothing; unrestricted cash drops to 19, so a hypothetical 25-unit follow-on investment is unaffordable. Across authored weights of 75% central, 15% imitation and 10% delay, the finite risk-feasible maximum expected profit is **41/10** at price 19 with the combined arrangement.

These are scenario arithmetic outcomes, not measured market demand or a defensible moat. A competent conventional designer allowed the **identical set of operations, costs, contracts and information** obtains the same optimum. This instrument does **not** demonstrate complete-cost commercial profitability or novel invention against that comparator. It demonstrates automatic composition of complementary operations and conditional game-theoretic selection without forced demand or erased adverse evidence.

## Next qualified integration

- **Verify the productive changes:** execute the mechanism with the existing harness, independently check the accepted outcome and measure real full-cycle costs. The cost deltas in this fixture are hypotheses.
- **Learn market beliefs from real evidence:** controlled price/offer tests and genuine buyer choices, with credible outside options and competitor responses. Never feed private simulated buyer values into a real pricing controller.
- **Connect only through native authority:** translate a chosen design into an **unfunded candidate** for the existing connected_enterprise and JournalAccount, then use its owned, auditable source-bound resource and settlement paths. Do not create a second ledger or spend based on this score.
- **Test the proposed advantage:** preregister strong unrestricted conventional and AI-assisted comparators with the same permissions, opportunity costs and financing; independently verify delivered quality and causal fresh-successor benefits.
- **Retest as the game changes:** imitation, buyer refusal, negative demand, collection delays, supplier outage, maintenance and partner withdrawal must be included in fresh bounded evaluations.

**Completion scope:** Working finite research constructor and local regressions. Native operation, independently demonstrated new buyer capability, empirical buyer demand, real transactions, general open-ended game invention, and the US$3B objective remain **unestablished**.
