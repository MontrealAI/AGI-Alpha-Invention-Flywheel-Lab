# Paid public-refusal recourse — engineering scope

This opt-in controller repair reuses already evaluated, publicly observed requests after a selected buyer voluntarily refuses. It does not add buyers, change values, access private budgets, create demand, purchase extra capacity, or authorize a new ledger.

The original allocation's investments and every accepted exact offer remain mandatory. Replanning retains the original cash, earned-funding, outstanding-obligation, weighted-capacity, unique-work and risk restrictions. Previously contacted customers are not contacted twice. Selected offers are processed before final no-bids, so a request cannot be diverted to an outside provider and then reused as if still untouched.

A positive fixed daily finite-recourse planning contract is paid natively before the original planning snapshot. The complete contract is charged even if unused; quote evaluation charges remain incurred for the evaluated menu. This authored service envelope is not measured host/model billing. Native financial authority rechecks every actual execution; forecasts never become spendable cash.

The first edition requires the complete-offer market and strategic allocation. General authored risk interactions are explicitly unsupported rather than silently removing liabilities when a request disappears. Existing strategic-game forecasts retain mandatory commitments as the conditional no-new-action HOLD. Hypothetical rivals remain hypothetical; this is not a full dynamic-game equilibrium. Original sequence/quantity compatibility restrictions remain unchanged.

Tests are exposed engineering comparisons, not fresh commercial confirmation, annual scaling, or Blue Sky transformation. Historical defaults and fixed experiments are preserved. Additional paid deliveries may still leave the entire business unprofitable; results must report all costs and actual outcomes rather than guaranteed gain.
