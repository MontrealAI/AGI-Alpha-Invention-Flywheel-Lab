# Pending battery research service — native admission scope

This opt-in service preregisters request/acceptance contracts, not successful
artifacts. Both initial and successor requests are fixed when a **fresh** account
opens. It does not append arbitrary services to an existing account or migrate
old histories. The normal saved-artifact service path is unchanged.

The mandatory Account/JournalAccount remains the only ledger. A unique complete
native prepayment precedes physics execution. A failed attempt retains its paid
cost and cannot reuse that payment. Native prepare receives the actual generated
artifact plus payment proof; source-bound independent physics replay is required
before delivery acceptance. Release and financial audit retain the normal cash,
reserve, claims and obligation semantics. A payment/job cannot fund two sales.

`construction.mode=provided_policy_evaluation` evaluates a supplied policy and
makes no construction claim. `known_envelope` binds training requests, oracle
current and safety factor before payment, invokes the known interpolation
constructor after payment, and independently reconstructs its provenance/policy
before accepting the report. Full verification retraining is included in the
predeclared complete-attempt envelope; actual production/verification CPU and
wall measurements remain visible. Successful exact-identity cache reuse avoids
repeated solver work during same-process native replay, with source, request,
artifact, dependency and payment validation retained. Fresh-process verification
incurs fresh numerical work and must be accounted separately.

The current constructor is developer-authored known-method adaptation. There is
**no executed critic/adjudicated repair mechanism or novel discovery claim**.
Equal-content reuse and stronger baseline results must not be suppressed.
Numerical model acceptance is not physical battery safety, lifetime validation,
independent market demand or guaranteed improvement.

Tests use declared simulated accounting amounts to exercise prepayment,
collection, unpaid refusal and available-earned successor funding. These values
are **not a calibrated service price/cost forecast or commercial success**.
Realized simulation revenue requires a separate prospectively declared voluntary
buyer/alternative/cost protocol. No live customers, transactions or hardware
control are involved. Dollar envelopes are authored assumptions, not measured
host/model/engineering billing. Funding by previous earnings does not prove that
invention causally enabled that profit.

Every `run_paid` invocation now returns a whole-invocation self CPU, waited-child
CPU and wall receipt; failures expose the same receipt on the exception and save
an independent operational JSON receipt beside the account. This file grants no
financial authority. Measurements include payment/source/audit overhead through
receipt construction, exclude its final write and unawaited children, and never
sum cached historical component verification timings as new work.

Envelope stage labels are conservative authored cost allocations, not receipts
proving each named mechanism executed. In particular positive criticism/repair
allocations in accounting fixtures do not establish a critic or repair call.
Use actual invocation and constructor/checker traces to determine executed work.
