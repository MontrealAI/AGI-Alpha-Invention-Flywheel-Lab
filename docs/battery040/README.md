# Battery research service: useful construction, not a declared breakthrough

## Decision-relevant opportunity screen

Three materially different possibilities were considered before this construction:

1. **Observable battery charging instruments.** A battery laboratory or controller developer needs a replayable charging policy that reaches a requested charge while respecting declared model constraints. The binding gap considered here is unavailable internal anode-potential information, not lack of another CSV/reporting tool. Strong alternatives include conventional CC/CV, staged-current charging and the existing model-state anode-potential controller. Public parameter models and executable comparisons make an initial software-only experiment feasible. A complete offer would include the policy, its applicability, independently replayed constraints, resources and refusal cases—not a physical safety certificate or guaranteed lifetime improvement. Free PyBaMM/self-service, competent engineering and no purchase remain alternatives; buyer value and price are uncalibrated hypotheses.
2. **Commercial-building retrofit and energy-system bundles.** Owners could receive a jointly feasible retrofit/storage/control design rather than isolated measures. ComStock provides public building models and upgrades; EnergyPlus and REopt already provide strong simulation/optimization alternatives. A small decisive construction would combine one public building model with a retrofit/storage constraint that separate decisions cannot satisfy, comparing the existing joint optimizer. Integration alone is not a novel breakthrough. Unknown installation, financing and verification costs cannot be replaced by assumed demand. [ComStock](https://comstock.nlr.gov/page/datasets), [EnergyPlus](https://energyplus.readthedocs.io/en/stable/quick_start/quick_start.html), [REopt](https://github.com/NatLabRockies/REopt.jl).
3. **Satellite deformation to inspection decisions.** Infrastructure operators could prioritize inspections from uncertain displacement evidence. NASA OPERA supplies existing public products and validation material; a useful missing instrument would distinguish motion from atmospheric/noise effects and translate uncertainty into a costed inspection decision. A retrospective region with independent ground truth is required before any operational claim. Existing InSAR tools, professional inspection and abstention are strong alternatives; access/ground-truth limitations make this a less immediate decisive experiment. [NASA OPERA](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/go/opera/products/).

The first route was selected for executable testability, not because a novel scientific result or paying buyer was already established.

## Actual construction and strong controls

`battery_service.physics` uses the public PyBaMM DFN model with lumped thermal physics. Requests specify parameter model, starting/target nominal charge, temperature, time, current, voltage, temperature and anode-potential margin. `construct` derives a finite ambient-temperature/coulomb-counted-SOC current envelope from qualified oracle simulations using established interpolation. It is explicitly a developer-authored known-method constructor, not autonomous scientific discovery.

The fixed development comparison includes current-grid CC/CV, staged-current controls, anode-potential-limited model-state controls, acquired envelope and **equal-content ordinary reuse of exactly that envelope**. Oracle access is privileged and separately labeled. The forecast made before comparison is that the envelope may satisfy constraints within its training domain, the oracle may be faster, and ordinary reuse should tie. No general optimality or retained-method advantage is assumed.

The current primary representation-generation paper is already a stronger starting point than merely claiming that generated policies beat fixed templates: [Lei et al., 2026, *LLMs as Representation Generators for Gradient-Free Scientific Optimization*](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13655265/). Its accelerated aging and voltage-surrogate conventions are not physical lifetime measurements. Its repository has a non-commercial-only license, so this implementation does **not** vendor that software. The independently written adapter instead uses documented PyBaMM APIs and established control methods. [Official custom-control examples](https://docs.pybamm.org/en/v25.6.0/source/examples/notebooks/simulations_and_experiments/custom-experiments.html).

PyBaMM25.10.2 here differs from the paper repository's25.1.1: this is a controlled same-version adaptation, not exact published reproduction. NumPy2.2.6 is pinned because newer NumPy removed a function required by this PyBaMM version. No physical-model patch is used.

## Acceptance and commercial boundary

`independent_check` reruns the actual numerical physics and checks the bound trace, target charge and constraints. The entire invocation counts; post-target charging cannot hide outside the measured prefix. It reports input energy and separate replay resources. This is sampled-trace/same-model replay, **not independent physical validation, continuous-time safety certification or a battery-lifetime claim**. Every changed request must requalify; training-domain membership is not a safety guarantee.

Useful-output identity excludes timing, but acceptance caches must additionally bind source, parameter/dependency identity and request. Actual model/host/engineering billing remains unknown; production, research, retries and verification require explicit complete-cost envelopes rather than free-work assumptions. The native bridge must admit the request before output exists, prepay actual execution and checking, then accept the actual artifact through the same mandatory account. A good simulation is not a sale; conditional buyer choice, collection, obligations and earned-funded successors are separate claims.

The full transformational breakthrough remains **NOT ESTABLISHED** unless a consequential previously inaccessible outcome, appropriate strong-control advantage and the claimed successor/commercial chain are independently supported. A tie or known-method result is retained as useful capability, not relabeled as beyond-human foresight.
