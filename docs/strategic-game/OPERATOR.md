**Status: opt-in research increment on the canonical native enterprise, not a globally optimal market or a new financial runtime.**

## The strategic game that is actually solved

The leader (AGI ALPHA) selects exactly one presently *affordable* action: retain the normal direct method, purchase the existing native `pilot` distribution contract, or purchase one existing native capacity unit. Each action is under the canonical `JournalAccount` and existing adaptive account. No future receipts or asset valuations can expand the present funding envelope.

Nature chooses one declared demand/supplier state from a finite scenario set. A rational rival observes that state and the leader's commitment and chooses a priced response to **maximize the rival's modeled cash payoff**. Tied rival responses break **against** the enterprise, not optimistically. Buyer participation is a declared reduced-form capture prior in the game; **actual** native buyers are still free to select the incumbent, human service, rival or decline, subject to their budget. We do not claim buyer dominant-strategy incentive compatibility or information revelation.

Within each scenario, the seller's incremental modeled contribution equals reachable customers × declared demand fraction × supplier realization fraction × the residual fraction not captured by the rival × past **collected** contribution per qualified job, minus the fully declared incremental channel/capacity + outreach/qualification cost. Seller and rival payoffs use exact integer microUSD arithmetic; no Monte Carlo draws or hidden future buyer information enter the choice.

The solver excludes unreachable/zero-increment actions, unavailable cash, supplier terms that violate the declared outside-option cost floor, and downside breaches. For the remaining choices, it enumerates all rival best responses, then minimizes the maximum regret relative to the best feasible action *in each scenario*. Ties prefer higher authored-weighted net outcome, smaller native invoice, then the explicit deterministic action order. This is an **exact optimal result for this finite pessimistic-follower minimax-regret model**. It is not Pareto efficiency, global Bayesian Nash equilibrium, a verified buyer/partner transaction, 60-month optimal growth, or proof of beyond-human discovery.

The no-invest/no-bid option is always available; uncertainty can rationally produce a hold. The controller does **not** price new outside financing, create novel technology, secretly increase buyer budgets, or reserve future profit. We adopt established game theory under its stated applicability; any alleged invention-method advantage requires its own fresh causal comparison.

## Mechanism-design constraints and failures

- **Incentives:** declared minimum supplier payment must be met, and rational rival responses are endogenized in the supported finite subgame. In the real business, supplier rights/consent, buyer preferences, legal terms, commission eligibility and willingness to participate require independent evidence.
- **Cash conservation:** `game` has no spending authority. Existing account reservations, outstanding obligations, customer deposits, supplier activation and risk protection determine real simulated execution. No release of reserve is counted as revenue.
- **Adverse demand:** zero realized demand, supplier failure, competitor undercutting and price pressure can make buying distribution irrational. Retain the loss/hold result and save a compact lesson, not the raw logs.
- **Causal test:** use matched source/seed and unchanged market assumptions to compare incumbent vs mechanism. Only future preregistered fresh cohorts can support general advantage claims; distinguish reuse from improving the discovery procedure.
- **Next transformation:** construct an independently acceptable, high-value recurring enterprise offer plus a legitimately available customer channel, and introduce costed contingent partner incentives in the native ledger only after real terms and authority exist. That is where the greatest additional upside may lie; it is **not** implemented or empirically demonstrated by this game solver.
