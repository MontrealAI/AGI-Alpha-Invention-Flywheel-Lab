## Strong external foundations

The paper cites official NIST SP 800-160 vol. 2 rev. 1, SP 800-184, SP 800-34 rev. 1 and the Canadian Centre for Cyber Security's Ransomware Playbook. Recovery engineering, dependency analysis, rehearsal, metrics and learning from failure are established practices.

OpenAI's CriticGPT report, Kenton et al.'s scalable-oversight study and Kim et al.'s correlated-errors paper support a conditional role for AI criticism while motivating explicit adjudication and independence boundaries. Official 2026 OpenAI and Anthropic cyber reports establish the described provider-reported technical context, not CONTINUITY Ω capability or independent validation.

Official source titles, authors, DOIs, links and bibliography keys are preserved so the exact references remain retrievable. Their role in the argument is explained in both language versions; retaining an official title does not add a performance claim.

## Distinct contribution

CONTINUITY Ω joins business-commitment semantics, an external completeness boundary, portable qualified production, conserved contingent capacity, maintained evidence, voluntary economics and causally tested succession in one concrete continuity architecture. The pooling inequality applies familiar cost and resource constraints; it is not a novel general economic theorem. Synthetic replays are existing engineering evidence at their own source identities, not an autonomous discovery result.

The earlier SURF THE EXPONENTIAL papers retain their own source, titles, evidence and editions. No results are imported as if they were a new experiment here.
