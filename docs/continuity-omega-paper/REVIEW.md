## Results preserved

PR 277's strong conventional route ties all four authored decisions. PR 278's 1,900 units are synthetic outstanding obligations; 22 hours is a supplied lead time; US$650 is a modeled contribution. No actual manufacture, acceptance, booking, payment or customer revenue occurred. Separate checker implementations remain under common research authorship. Actual source completeness and supplier commitments are not established by local hashes or consent flags.

The pinned handoff checker also has observed type-validation and malformed-input weaknesses, and its `inspect` command can display stale evidence. The [five-probe record](evidence/COUNTEREXAMPLES.json) preserves those findings. A successful replay of a known counterexample must never be presented as the checker passing that counterexample.

## Remaining limits

No measured advantage from two different frontier LLMs is claimed. No external assessor or field trial certifies the proposed service. The source-pinned engineering reproduction does not qualify later branch revisions, native-account migration, or a live deployment. The paper identifies a testable product and a new construction target; it does not claim a successful market or improved inventor.
