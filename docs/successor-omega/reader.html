Own the institution. Replace the models. Preserve the mission.

A breakthrough can leave behind a result—or the means of producing the next one. This paper studies an institution that preserves legitimate methods, evidence, skills and operating memory while requiring current qualification and explicit authority after change.

Knowledge can persist. Proof must be current. Authority is granted.

The stronger component can lose the lifecycle.

In the supplied 16-period model, a portable capability earns less per active period but costs less to renew after supplier changes. Its complete trajectory retains more cash.

Abstract units. Initial cash 100; protected reserve 20. Exact authored model, not measured prices, observed revenue or accounting profit.

Across 162 supplied environments, the additional option helps 19 and ties 143. Forced retention is infeasible in 108, loses in 43 and wins in 11. A conventional planner with the same choices ties the optimum. The 30-unit gain is an option ablation, not superiority over the strongest equally permitted competitor.

Continuity must earn its cost under uncertainty.

The reviewed edition removes advance knowledge of supplier changes and permits renewal failure. The 48 eight-period environments all tie the no-portable alternative. A disclosed extension to the original 16-period horizon finds one positive case and 47 ties.

In that positive case, expected terminal cash rises from 293.633 to 307.544—but minimum terminal cash falls from 188 to 66. Higher expected value is not the same as resilience. These are supplied probability models, not estimated market frequencies.

The review also found and repaired a temporal permission defect: an action before a grant's start could be accepted. The corrected local logic checks both interval endpoints, rejects malformed times and scopes, and withdraws old authorization when requalification begins.

Make the next frontier buildable.

The proposed Industrial Requalification Foundry would construct portable, independently testable industrial capabilities across changing instruments, models and suppliers. The paper specifies the missing means, strong controls, fresh-successor tests and voluntary commercial bridge.

The long horizon connects productive surplus to scientific instruments, industrial capacity and energy systems through distinct funding, construction and qualification records. Stellar-scale ambition is expressed in energy, material and thermal quantities—not inferred from a financial growth curve.

Scope and evidence

Established here: exact conditional planning results, 324 deterministic and 192 stochastic optimizer/oracle comparisons, 62 local tests, and physical scale arithmetic.

Not established: autonomous invention, general ASI, real demand or profit, production security, installed physical capacity, or a civilization-scale timetable. No model receives the Specialist ASI designation in this study.

L’institution qui se renforce au fil des générations.

Conserver les connaissances utiles ne signifie pas conserver une autorisation périmée. SUCCESSOR Ω propose de préserver la mission et les moyens d’invention tout en renouvelant les preuves et les permissions lorsque les modèles ou les conditions changent. Les résultats présentés sont ceux d’un modèle analytique explicite, pas des revenus commerciaux observés.
