# SUCCESSOR OMEGA — Institutional renewal study / protocol 1.0

This is an authored finite model, not a sampled market, autonomous discovery experiment, or production authorization. The primary fixture, grid and stopping rule were recorded locally before numerical evaluation in the authoring session. This is not independently timestamped preregistration.

## Question
When does an explicitly costed provider-portable capability improve a solvent finite operating campaign relative to rebuilding a higher-contribution provider-specific capability and direct conventional service?

## Fixed model
16 periods; supplier epoch length 4; cash 100; protected reserve 20. All units are abstract. Initial legacy qualification is supplied equally. A period can build, requalify, execute one service, idle, or retire P. Production costs precede collections. Construction/requalification takes one period. Supplier changes invalidate qualification, not retained P source.

Legacy: pay 10, collect 22. F: build/rebuild for 40, then pay 12 and collect 42 while qualified in the current epoch. P: build for 60, then pay 10 and collect 38; maintenance 1 each retained period including construction; renewal 10 in each changed epoch. Retirement takes a period, has no salvage and avoids that period's upkeep. Qualification actions include supplied adapter/test/admission costs; this simulation grants no real authority. Deterministic acceptance, known turnover, demand, and every monetary input are authored assumptions.

Maximize terminal cash subject to reserve at every pre-collection state. Net cash surplus is terminal minus initial; it is not accounting profit, physical output, dollar revenue, or welfare. Additional real lifecycle costs are unknown, not zero.

## Comparators
Full option set; no-P ablation (legacy plus F construction); legacy-only; ordinary equal-content construction with the full set. Independent forward reachability checks backward induction without calling its transition function. The same feasible set is expected to tie; neither method is a new optimizer. An always-retain policy builds P immediately, renews at transitions, and runs P otherwise; unaffordable actions return infeasible.

## Frozen grid and diagnostics
H={8,12,16}, epoch={4,8}, opening={40,70,100}, renewal={5,10,25}, maintenance={0,1,3}: 162 cases. Preserve ties and every infeasibility. Declared adverse classes: no turnover, no demand, high maintenance=5, renewal=40, reserve-only opening=20, additional fixed burden, retirement, invalid inputs and cash/obligation distinction. One-epoch and opening=40 traces are explanatory additions; the latter condition already occurs in the grid. Additional fixed burdens are evaluated algebraically, not represented as executed market evidence.

## Separate local state witness
Test complete-context binding, distinct role identifiers, mission/source/supplier/environment/evaluator/policy changes, expiry, explicit authorization, supported scope, revocation, export and restore. Historical records travel; active proof and authority do not. This is logic in one local trust domain, not cryptographic identity, independent organizational validation or a secure deployment.

## Stop
Finish the frozen grid, stated diagnostics and regression suite. No favorable-seed search, paid model API, customer contact, physical operation or canonical financial-runtime modification. Physical scale calculations are separate illustrative arithmetic, not part of the economic grid.
