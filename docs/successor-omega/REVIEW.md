# Adversarial review — SUCCESSOR OMEGA 1.1.0

This review improves the research edition; it does not certify a perfect paper, external peer review, a secure production institution or achieved general ASI. Review, construction and checking were performed within the same authoring workflow. Algorithmic cross-checks are not independent organizational validation.

## Findings and repairs

**A real temporal defect.** Edition 1.0.0 recorded a grant's expiry but not its start. Qualify with expiry 10, authorize at time 5 with expiry 8, then query permission at time 1: the original code returns True. The corrected witness stores both endpoints and rejects time before either warrant begins. It rejects nonintegral, nonfinite and boolean time values, empty context/role identifiers, and malformed action scopes. It rejects backward state transitions. Scope: explicit local logical ticks, not a trusted production clock.

**Failed current requalification.** The old implementation raised an exception but left its previous proof and grant active. The new conservative contract withdraws both as current requalification begins, including when it fails. This behavior is now stated in the manuscript and tested. A malformed request can therefore interrupt availability inside this local prototype; authenticated callers and denial-of-service protection are production work, not established here.

**The strongest comparator ties.** The portable option gives 30 units more nominal surplus than an ablation lacking that option. It does not beat an ordinary planner allowed the same complete construction. The paper now explicitly reports zero measured Alpha against that equal-content competitor. Renewal-adjusted Alpha is an institutional application of lifecycle comparison, not the invention of lifecycle costing or dynamic programming.

**Perfect schedule knowledge was consequential.** The new internally specified eight-period unknown-schedule grid has 48 ties. A declared extension, after that result, repeats the same grid at 16 periods to match the nominal horizon. It has one positive difference and 47 ties. The positive case is cash 100, supplier-change probability 1/4, renewal-failure probability zero: expected terminal cash 10319465955/33554432 = 307.5440512597561 versus 157642917421/536870912 = 293.632815444842; exact gain 7468537859/536870912 = 13.911235814914107. The full policy's worst terminal cash is 66 versus 188 without portability; both respect the 20 reserve on every reachable branch. Raising renewal failure to 1/4 at that same setting removes the gain. Do not interpret supplied grid counts as market probabilities or expectation-maximizing behavior as downside protection.

**Source and positioning.** Added direct citations to the earlier mission-relative revalidation and fault-bounded reference-evidence papers, established lifecycle costing, and explicit temporal validity. Added a one-page thesis and strengthened the distinction among artifact retention, current qualification, authority, financing, inventive inheritance and commissioned capacity. Private working documents remain excluded.

## Validation

62 unit tests; 671 interval assertions; 24 small decision-history-tree comparisons; 324 original deterministic optimizer/oracle comparisons across 162 environments; 192 stochastic optimizer/oracle comparisons across 96 environments. Stochastic calculations use exact rational arithmetic and choose actions before future outcomes, with every branch prefunded. Both transition implementations follow the authored model; agreement does not validate economic inputs.

The original economic contracts and their results are unchanged. An initial additional-test denominator was corrected from a mistaken handwritten 770 to the enumerated 671 (61 valid intervals times 11 query times); no predicate was relaxed. A local command interrupted during the long exact extension was rerun with unchanged input and the full grid completed. These development events are not omitted business losses or fresh statistical replications.

## Remaining limits

No live AI performance, real demand, customer collection, complete real-money profitability, production security, physical qualification, or recursive discovery improvement is established. Failed qualification is deliberately fail-closed; external identity and storage integrity remain outside the prototype. The original release and PDF remain preserved. The new edition is a tested, inspectable research contribution, not evidence of an absolute quality ceiling.
