## Remaining evidence gaps

Blue Sky Flagship and inherited successor advantage remain **not established**. Aggregation is established practice and the complete ordinary comparator ties. Physical telemetry, device response, market qualification, observed buyers and real commercial receipts have not been tested. There is no equivalent scoped human-market quote, so human-market benchmark `H` and `H − C` remain unknown. The four-period competitive scenarios are authored choices and quote responses, not a validated equilibrium or learned autonomous competitor population. Actual full model/host billing remains unknown. A complete capitalization/amortization reporting variant is not implemented.

The US$3 billion goal requires the registered primary US$100,000 simulation and native trailing-365-day complete-cost profit at its fixed endpoint. This four-period experiment is not annualized or substituted for that result.
