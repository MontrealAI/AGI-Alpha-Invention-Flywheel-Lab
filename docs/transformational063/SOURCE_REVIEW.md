## Actual foundation manuscripts inspected

The public PDFs below were downloaded from pinned repository revisions and read as research sources. They remain external references rather than copied files in this edition.

- [Transformational Intelligence](https://github.com/MontrealAI/AGI-Alpha-Agent-v0/blob/9cb90e8c189f146eff7d166e249d9474792c4864/docs/Transformational_Intelligence.pdf) separates the intended purpose, formulation and evaluator. Its finite causal example contrasts a passive 50% ceiling with 8191/8192 accuracy using at most twelve interventions. A standard unrestricted planner matches the latter result. It provides a useful purpose-preserving attribution framework, not proof that the present construction is beyond conventional methods.
- [Out-Evolve](https://github.com/MontrealAI/AGI-Alpha-Agent-v0/blob/9cb90e8c189f146eff7d166e249d9474792c4864/docs/Out_Evolve.pdf) asks for a constructed useful regime in which a simpler model is valid. Its twenty-setting example conditionally changes exact identification from 1,048,576 arbitrary values to 211 coefficients under a qualified second-order interaction bound. The physical/structural guarantee is a prerequisite, not a consequence of a sparse fit. We adopt its explicit capability-frontier and complete-construction-cost questions.
- [Invent the Economy](https://github.com/MontrealAI/AGI-Alpha-Agent-v0/blob/b7f017e91a18adb3a79b34e03dc0158896ef7570/docs/Invent_the_Economy.pdf) joins technical capability, voluntary participation, financing and delivery. Its supplied example has US$120,000 expected contribution under an assumed 80% qualification probability, but US$900,000 prefunding and a route-cost failure control. These are reference-model findings, not actual commitments. We carry forward the separation of participant benefit, profit, liquidity and realized exchange.
- The existing [Transformational Autonomous Enterprises](../research/transformational-autonomous-enterprises-v1.pdf) edition reuses content-bound generic checks while checking new task semantics and granting authority separately. Its direct specialist matches useful quality and wins important resource comparisons. The [Foundry v2](../research/autonomous-enterprises-foundry-v2.pdf) constructs simulated thermal-fluid instruments, but a conventional specialist and centralized alternative remain strong. Neither establishes observed demand or realized commercial profit.

This review supports a narrower engineering decision: build a reproducible complete arrangement using strong established methods, then test its acceptance, economics and inheritance. It does not establish a new scientific discovery or a completed general transformational enterprise.
