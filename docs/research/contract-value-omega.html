Standards of evidence

No fictional profits.
No unverifiable promises.

Market case studies published by existing recovery-audit companies establish that companies sometimes recover large sums from supplier contracts. They do not establish that MONTREAL.AI has earned revenue or will outperform experienced providers. The current software screens structured monthly fixed-rate services only. It does not parse contracts, verify signatures, enforce legal rights, automatically contact vendors, handle taxes or currency conversion, or prove a recoverable claim. Every result must be reviewed by qualified buyer personnel and, where required, professional advisers. Real customer demand, full delivery costs, collections and profits remain unverified.
