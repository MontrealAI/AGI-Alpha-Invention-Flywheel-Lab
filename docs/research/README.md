**Subtitle:** Mission-Relative Revalidation for Capability Inheritance Across Four Execution Systems  
**Author attribution:** MONTREAL.AI - Writer #4 research edition  
**Edition:** Research edition 01, 23 October 2026; 29 PDF pages.

This historical research edition studies mission-relative revalidation: reusing content-bound generic module checks while checking each mission's inputs and outputs afresh and granting authority separately. The paper reports a finite synthetic 48-mission comparison across four execution systems, with a 96% reduction in generic qualification cases for reuse versus full replay. It also reports that the supplied direct specialist matches quality with lower pooled CPU and a better final median wall time.

These are the paper's reported results, not an independent rerun performed for this repository addition. The paper does not establish external demand, real-money profit, complete monetary cost, production-host qualification or a learned-model-provider swap.
