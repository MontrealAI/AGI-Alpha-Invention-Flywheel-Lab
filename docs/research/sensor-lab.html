Beyond an ordinary dashboard

A new question can generate a new sensor.

Autonomous intelligence could search archived evidence, construct a new measurement definition, prove its limits and produce a verification-ready answer that would otherwise be uneconomic to investigate individually.

The binding constraint

No prior joint measurement

The two observations existed, but the question-specific co-occurrence series did not. Data gaps and contradictions impose real epistemic limits.

The missing instrument

Proof-preserving retrospective synthesis

Different thresholds produce different derived histories, and each output retains the source rows that support or limit it.

The business hypothesis

New verification-ready outcomes

Historical diligence, asset underwriting and environmental analysis might buy bespoke, auditable indicators. Demand, novelty and profitable scale are not established.

Technical boundary: this is threshold co-occurrence in user-supplied records, not an independently certified heat-stress index, actual indoor exposure, reconstructed missing observations, causal inference, external sensing or commercial success. SHA-256 fingerprints identify inputs but cannot establish their truth. The web version uses synthetic sample records, no buyers and no transactions.
