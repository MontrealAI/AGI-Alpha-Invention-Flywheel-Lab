What this paper explores

This manuscript constructs moment-shaped audit codes for a declared nonlinear polynomial family. Its designed experiments recover model coefficients despite bounded corruption of entire reference records.

Evidence boundary. The result is conditional on the declared target class, exact arithmetic, and fault assumptions. Source-group and beyond-budget counterexamples limit the assurance. No universal self-certification, neural improvement, or operational deployment is established. This publication is not an independent reproduction.
