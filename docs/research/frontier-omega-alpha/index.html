    Wallet access and privacy

    When access is operational, the service uses your public wallet address, optional Club label and signature to establish a short session. It checks eligibility using its Ethereum provider. Sessions expire after five minutes. The session token stays in page memory and the view clears when the session closes. Authorized readers can still copy content they receive.

    Wallet eligibility grants reader access. Research operations require separate authority.
