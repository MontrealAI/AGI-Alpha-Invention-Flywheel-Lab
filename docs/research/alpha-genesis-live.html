Before entering the laboratory

Questions worth answering.

Is this a traditional webinar?

No. The centerpiece is a structured, interactive construction and investigation with a working result and a separate evidence check.

Do I need to code?

The core learning experience is designed to be accessible without programming. Technical participants can examine deeper source material.

Does the event promise to solve any challenge?

No. Participant questions must fit approved tools, evidence access, scope and time. Unsupported claims are not presented as completed work.
