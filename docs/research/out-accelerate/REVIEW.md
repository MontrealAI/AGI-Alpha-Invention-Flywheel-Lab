# Review of OUT-ACCELERATE edition 1.0

The manuscript and companion received separate AI-assisted technical and editorial review during preparation. This is an internal release review, not external peer review or an empirical replication of a discovery system.

## Technical findings

The reviewers checked the two-stage recurrence, its no-idling and input-availability assumptions, the break-even inequality, all numeric result rows, the horizon curve, the worker-time account, the 24-unit rebuilding comparison, and the conditional serial-floor identity. The liquidity example preserves positive profit while deferring an investment that exceeds funds available after unpaid obligations.

The copied companion was rerun independently in a separate output directory. Its deterministic JSON and CSV matched the publication copies byte for byte. The full verifier is implemented separately from the producer's target helper. The certificate checks reject incompatible coefficients, domain, input, and output.

## Revisions resulting from review

- Added explicit input-availability and work-conserving schedule assumptions.
- Defined speedup for finite positive completion times and explained stochastic completion and non-completion.
- Distinguished asset-conditional effects from acquisition-inclusive programme effects.
- Aligned the complementary-construction hypothesis with its isolated-improvement comparator.
- Rephrased the generative-inheritance hypothesis as a policy effect at fixed retained content.
- Made unrestricted cash and unpaid reserves explicit and prevented double-counting.
- Reduced repeated scope qualifications while retaining the local limits of numerical results.

## Claim boundaries checked

The model's timings and tool effects are supplied assumptions. The affine arithmetic task carries the acceptance contract; it does not demonstrate a measured fast verification algorithm. The prespecified subsequent workload is public. The ordinary equal-content workflow ties. General recursive improvement, physical deployment, and commercial success are proposed research questions, not achievements reported by this release.

The bibliography recognizes evaluator-driven discovery, structural analogy, endogenous growth, complementarity, and adaptive-evaluation precedents. No first-invention, global optimality, empirical frontier-model superiority, or external peer-review claim is made.
