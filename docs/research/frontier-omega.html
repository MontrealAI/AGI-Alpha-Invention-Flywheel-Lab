01 / Begin with what matters

Bring your ambition.

A company, a difficult problem, a business you want to invent, or a possibility you suspect the advancing AI frontier could unlock.

Your inputs are processed locally in this browser. The five opportunities are ranked from a finite research catalogue; they are not freshly generated by a live AI model.
02 / What AI has changed

Five openings. One ambition.

These are research hypotheses based on dated, linked AI advances—not verified claims that a particular product was impossible five weeks ago or will be profitable. The ranking is a disclosed heuristic, not AI inference or a measured probability.
Each candidate includes a credible alternative and an explicit obstacle; a product is admitted only after a real matched test.
03 / Beyond the obvious

Invent the route. Change the game.

Transformational Intelligence: look beyond the proposed tool and construct the complete productive arrangement—valuable outcome, missing means, independent acceptance, willing participants and viable economics. Learning to Build: retain verified methods and limits so the next construction starts stronger. Here, these are decision lenses, not proof of autonomous discovery.
04 / From possibility to executable evidence

Build the economic gate.

Assumptions · Not observed transactions

The instrument calculates when the proposed frontier route beats a competent alternative, using your explicit scenario values. Change the assumptions and see what survives.

Scenario values are illustrative until backed by buyer evidence, scoped pricing and full delivery costs. The alternative should be a genuine feasible option, not a deliberately weak straw man. A negative net alternative is allowed; doing nothing remains available.
