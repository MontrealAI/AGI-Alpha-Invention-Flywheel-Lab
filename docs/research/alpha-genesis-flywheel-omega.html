Do not merely adopt the next advance in AI.

Convert it into a useful capability—and establish whether that capability improves what can be constructed next. GENESIS is a proposed autonomous discovery foundry that constructs missing tools, representations and experiments around consequential objectives.

Frontier Replay

Retain the objective, strongest alternative, unresolved obstacle and evidence from a worthwhile blocked construction. When a relevant, accessible model, scientific tool or verified method appears, test whether it removes that obstacle. Construct a challenger, then qualify it on fresh evidence before changing a working procedure.

External model progress, inherited tools and improved invention procedures are distinct sources of improvement. The proposed comparisons separate all three.

Construct a capability. Test what it enables next.

The proposed GENESIS Discovery Studio begins with computational campaigns: construct tools, representations and experiments, execute decisive comparisons and deliver portable results with independently checkable evidence. Fresh successor tests ask whether the retained means improve what can be constructed next. Physical instrument operation requires separate qualification.

Electrical tomography is a prospective first qualification track, not the product’s permanent boundary or an achieved physical breakthrough.

Scope and evidence

Established in the supplied models: exact observation-construction and selective-replay examples, cash-flow arithmetic and conditional adoption-lag analysis. The analytical companion passes 68 tests in normal and optimized Python; ordinary complete alternatives tie in the specified observation and replay comparisons.

Not established: a deployed GENESIS application, autonomous cross-domain invention, improved successor construction, new physical measurements, actual customer demand or commercial revenue. The adoption-lag calculation is conditional arithmetic, not an empirical law of AI progress.

The paper connects the Alpha → Gym → qualification → SUCCESSOR Ω journey, preserving the foundational distinction between authorized purpose, problem formulation and evaluation instrument.
