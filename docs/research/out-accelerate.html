The central idea

Make the next advance easier to reach.

Some progress comes from doing a task faster. A deeper opportunity is to create an instrument, representation, production process, or voluntary arrangement that changes what the next task requires.

The next advance should help build the one after it.

OUT-ACCELERATE develops that idea as a research framework: diagnose the binding constraint, construct the missing means, qualify the result, and test what becomes possible on fresh successor work. Time saved must survive construction, verification, failures, maintenance, and financing.

When to reach for this paper. Use it when additional effort keeps meeting the same constraint, or when a reusable capability could shorten the route to a consequential accepted outcome.

01 / PURPOSE

Choose the useful outcome

Keep the required outcome and acceptance conditions stable while examining which inherited means can change.

02 / CONSTRUCTION

Build an enabler

Compare direct execution, reuse, and complementary changes. Count the time and resources needed to construct and qualify them.

03 / EVIDENCE

Test the whole route

Use strong complete alternatives, failures, and counterexamples to distinguish a useful capability from a faster isolated step.

04 / SUCCESSOR

Earn the next advance

Carry useful means into fresh work. Check their current scope, available resources, and demonstrated contribution.

Scope and evidence

This research edition presents a framework and an exact analytical worked example. The example illustrates conditional mechanisms under stated assumptions; it does not establish autonomous invention, measured frontier-model acceleration, physical deployment, or a commercial result.

Use the companion to inspect the model, reproduce its calculations, and check the comparison. The lab’s US$3 billion trailing-365-day complete-cost profit objective remains unachieved.
