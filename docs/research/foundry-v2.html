A reusable means of discovery

This paper studies configurable thermal-fluid fixtures, an assembly and calibration station, and a qualified research instrument. It examines how these means can support a new pulse-calorimetry investigation and a distinct simulated successor module.

Evidence boundary. The paper reports executed software and simulated physics, not physical construction or actual customers. EVM execution and installed thermal execution are also marked NOT RUN. Publication here is not an independent reproduction or new commercial qualification.
