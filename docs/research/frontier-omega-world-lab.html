OUT-STRATEGISE · CONSTRUCTING THE CONDITIONS OF SUCCESS

What if the winning move is to stop trying to know everything?

One link has failed somewhere in a network. A conventional plan tries to identify the exact fault. Another protects every group. The constructive strategy gets one coarse observation, builds one reliable bypass, and succeeds without ever identifying the failed link. Choose the hidden world, then challenge the guarantee.
