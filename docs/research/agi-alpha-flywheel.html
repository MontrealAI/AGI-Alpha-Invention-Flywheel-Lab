Abstract

How can useful work improve the means of doing more consequential work? AGI ALPHA FLYWHEEL develops an architecture for an autonomous enterprise that searches jointly over valuable outcomes, productive capabilities, verification, voluntary cooperation and financing. A successful cycle may leave reusable knowledge, a qualified instrument, or unrestricted earned funds, changing what a successor can attempt. These gains travel through three distinct flows and require different evidence.

The paper connects structural transfer, the construction of missing interfaces, evidence-producing instruments and purpose-preserving changes to production. It distinguishes a working capability, a successor that benefits from inheritance, and a better procedure for discovering capabilities. A reproducible finite illustration makes the separation between technical enablement, profit and available cash inspectable. The contribution is an architecture and research synthesis with explicit tests for its central claims. General recursive improvement, actual commercial success and industry creation remain objectives requiring further evidence.

The key idea

Build the next starting point.

The value of an invention includes the useful possibilities it makes reachable next.

An enterprise can change more than its output. It can construct a measurement that answers a new question, an interface that makes a useful combination possible, or a production process that makes reliable delivery affordable. The next cycle should test what those retained means actually change.

This synthesis builds on Transformational Intelligence: Learning to Change the Game, the flagship paper, and AGI ALPHA: A Scalable Substrate for Intelligence Organizations, the foundational paper.

Three flows that can compound

Knowledge, productive capability and financial resources interact. Their benefits and restrictions must remain separately visible.

  1. 01 / Knowledge

    What has been learned

    Supported mechanisms, useful representations, applicability conditions and informative failures.

    A remembered explanation becomes an advantage only when it helps on fresh work.

  2. 02 / Qualified capability

    What can now be done

    Executable instruments, interfaces and procedures that a successor can use or adapt.

    Current acceptance and authority are established for each mission.

  3. 03 / Earned cash

    What can now be funded

    Collected surplus available after complete costs, protected obligations and commitments.

    Positive accounting profit does not automatically make an investment affordable.

Scope and evidence

This edition presents an architecture and research synthesis, published evidence within its original scope, and a finite constructed illustration. The illustration is not a native business account or a new market experiment. A later success does not by itself prove that an earlier invention enabled it; financing, capability inheritance and improved discovery need separate comparisons.

The US$3 billion trailing-365-day complete-cost profit objective remains unachieved. New capital, productive strategy and financing are jointly chosen under explicit assumptions; US$100,000 remains a historical reference. The paper does not establish actual customer revenue, achieved AGI or ASI, physical deployment, general game-theoretic optimality, or an autonomous industry-creation breakthrough.
