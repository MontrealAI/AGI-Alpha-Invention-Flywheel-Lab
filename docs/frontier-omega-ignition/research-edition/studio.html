What was constructed—and what has not been demonstrated?

Executed: a generic answer-signature constructor, separately written raw-inequality checks, separating witnesses, a stale-scope counterexample, and an executable successor. Default domain: nine ordered pairs, four upper-bound questions, then six interval questions.

Supplied: finite domain, question semantics, construction algorithm, and conventional comparators. This is not autonomous discovery of the algorithm, an LLM benchmark, or proof of a newly viable business.

Inheritance boundary: the old maximum cannot recover a discarded minimum. The successor accepts fresh original readings or requires authorized access to the original records. A preserved constructor is not recovered information, current acceptance, or operating authority.

Comparison: ordinary maximum and minimum/maximum routines tie the relevant outcomes and minimum label counts. The mean plus a threshold flag also supports both default families. Label counts do not measure total bytes, runtime, money, or intelligence.
