## Acceptance and economics

Scroll Prize, Inc. has a public November 30 Progress Prize programme. Its reviewers value real usage and significant improvement. One best submission can receive US$20,000. No reviewer has accepted this contribution, and a posted prize does not determine its price. The upstream method already exceeded the reporter's GPU feed rate on its tested system. Wider use depends on a remaining bottleneck in another representative installation. The CPU patch, reusable source-pinned comparator and benchmark design can be retained. The original scans are CC BY-NC 4.0. The recipient contains no CT data. A separate commercial deployment needs an appropriate data-rights basis.
