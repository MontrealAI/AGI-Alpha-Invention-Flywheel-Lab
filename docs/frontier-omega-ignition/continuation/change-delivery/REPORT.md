# Two missing survey measurements

**FRONTIER Ω IGNITION · MONTREAL.AI × QUEBEC.AI · Draft integration**

A transient observer brings a concrete question. A candidate has brightened in two filters. Can the archive show whether the difference light was still brighter at the next eligible visit? For ZTF26acbezdh, an observer using the frozen Fink object response would have to leave both bands unresolved. Two public ZTF alert packets retrieved through ALeRCE answer the question before the original deadline. ## How the decision changed

1. **Select the roster.** A registered Fink search for early SN Ia candidates from October 18 up to October 25 selected four distinct objects. Fink's classifier supplied the input population. Each selection used the measured event time and a dated cutoff. The historical observation times are known. The study did not measure what arrived at a live desk at those times. [Early-classifier study](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202142715) · [Frozen class and object requests](../../../../products/frontier-omega-ignition/invention/early_survey/sources/index.json)
2. **Issue the review.** Nine public-program measurements lay in the observing week. With those data alone the rule issued zero notices. Admitting 28 earlier measurements solely as possible baselines issued two, both on ZTF26acbezdh. A separately written SQL program produced the identical delivery, including reference-image IDs and Fink's own rate estimates. The 40 eligible comparisons in two separately registered November cohorts produced zero alerts. [October rule and worklist](../../../../products/frontier-omega-ignition/invention/early_survey/README.md) · [November results](../../../../products/frontier-omega-ignition/invention/evolution/README.md)
3. **Preserve the gap.** The registered deadline was October 29 at 00:00 UTC. The frozen Fink object response had no eligible later detection in either band. This result remains reproducible. A separate experiment registered the search for a public exposure or archival measurement before inspecting a second source. [Recovery registration](../../../../products/frontier-omega-ignition/invention/early_survey/exposures/REGISTRATION.md)
4. **Recover the witnesses.** The ALeRCE index returned the first eligible later detection in each band. The original Avro packets identify public program 1, field 505, readout channel 58, with unchanged reference identifiers within each band. An independent conventional lookup from the same two issued notices recovered the same detection IDs and verdicts. Fixed-position difference-flux measurements from the same exposures also supported persistence, with changes of −1.0390 ± 0.0219 mag in g and −0.7640 ± 0.0218 mag in r. The r-band forced measurement at the original trigger had a processing flag. The baseline and later forced endpoints used for these two changes had zero flags. The r-band raw-binary and rounded catalog baselines differ by less than one millionth of a magnitude in the resulting displacement. The decision is unchanged. ## Five opportunities and the choice of experiment

| Beneficiary | Proposed capability | Strongest unresolved condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Transient archive observer | Recover an already-taken observation before committing scarce review or telescope time. | A checked archive result exists. Actual observer time saved and the relative cost of a direct broker lookup are unmeasured in practice. |
| Power-flow research lab | Select a learned or conventional solver for a specified batch-throughput and accuracy envelope. | Access to a matched grid distribution, target hardware and equal-quality operating conditions. |
| Microscopy imaging core | Route difficult cell-track links to limited expert review with measurable correction yield. | Unseen sequences, adjudicated identities and a timed expert comparator. |
| Formal mathematics library | Detect when a checked formal proof establishes a different proposition from the intended one. | Independently validated statement equivalence and false-rejection costs. |
| Scientific instrument team | Deliver a verified logic block under explicit functional, timing and resource constraints. | A real specification, trusted reference design and permission for any physical test. |

The first opportunity had a real missing observation, usable public archives and an executable rejection rule. Those conditions justified testing it first. They do not rank its market above the other four. Earlier Fink, ALeRCE and ZTFReST work establishes capable incumbent methods. The November 2026 AI studies inform specific alternatives. The astronomical technique predates this campaign. An independent researcher also proposed a way to test whether headline pass rates justify adopting a method across facilities. We built an executable that takes each method’s pooled passes and per-site trial counts, computes the best and worst differences when sites count equally, and produces a feasible allocation for each extreme. For the proposed example, 8/10 versus 7/10 permits differences in both directions. The sharp lower and upper bounds are −5/16 and +7/16, and both are attainable. SQLite independently agreed on all nine allocations in that example and on 2,025 additional pairs of margins. The inputs are illustrative. A facility can substitute its own counts. This takes an open model proposal through construction and independent acceptance on a different subject. ## Résultat en français

Le 26 octobre, les mesures antérieures permettaient de signaler un éclaircissement de ZTF26acbezdh en g et en r. La réponse Fink figée ne contenait aucune mesure de suivi admissible avant l’échéance du 29 octobre. Deux alertes publiques du ZTF, retrouvées par ALeRCE, portent sur les observations du 28 octobre. Le signal différentiel était toujours plus lumineux que lors de la mesure initiale dans les deux bandes. Les identifiants de détection, les images de référence et les incertitudes accompagnent la décision. La recherche classique a retrouvé les mêmes mesures. Le gain de temps pour un observatoire et la valeur commerciale restent à établir.
