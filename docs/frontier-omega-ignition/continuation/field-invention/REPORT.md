# What can we recover from an observing night?

**MONTREAL.AI × QUEBEC.AI · 17 November 2026, Montréal · Draft continuation of #447**

Six historical Fink/ZTF transient candidates led to two deliverables. The first is a data product. Of 44 survey detections in the later, frozen November 1–8 interval, four targets have a qualifying pair of positive difference-flux measurements in g and r within two hours. Each delivered row records the two original detection IDs, UTC times, magnitudes, uncertainties and separation. Two targets lack a qualifying pair. At ten minutes, only one remains. These are measurements of the changing light relative to the reference image. They do not establish a total-source color, a spectral classification or a new observation made by IGNITION. [Scientific inputs, code and source attribution](../../../../products/frontier-omega-ignition/invention/README.md)

The second deliverable is an editable historical worklist. Six modeled twenty-minute slots cover five candidates once or three twice with the registered separation. A full direct search and a separate mixed-integer solver reproduce all fourteen budget/cadence objectives. An independent Astropy calculation checks darkness and visibility. The science case comes from public alerts and later survey measurements. The site, available slots and observing authority are modeled separately. No telescope was operated. ## Scientific reading and release scope

On the new Terwel et al. table, visual reading, PDF extraction and semantic export agreed on 63 scientific fields, nine target IDs and both conditions. The historical Table Desk correctly withheld on merged headings. A separate NOMAI appendix checks 83 labeled IDs. None of the six worklist targets occurs in that printed cohort. The classifier predates this campaign. The November archive is a fresh input. Timing of reader productivity, commercial newness and general autonomous improvement remain open. [Publications, source identities and reproductions](../../../../products/frontier-omega-ignition/numeric_study/transient_table/README.md)

The public exhibit runs entirely from downloaded bytes. Its charts, worklists and CSV change with the contract. At the local workbench, `/field` serves the public exhibit under an opaque origin and `/follow-up` provides the separately authorized private revision and delivery flow. Public bundles draw from explicit public inputs. The public release, live telescope operation, customer service and permanent external consulting business are outside the achieved scope.

## Reproduce

From `products/frontier-omega-ignition` in a clean checkout with Python 3.12 and curl:

```sh
python3 -B field_trials/qualify.py --expected-head "$(git rev-parse HEAD)" --output /tmp/ignition-new-public
python3 -B -m ignition --state /tmp/ignition-new-private serve --port 8784
```

Open the public HTML or the operator's loopback URL printed by the server. To receive the instrument independently, extract `FRONTIER_INSTRUMENT.zip` into a new directory and run `python3 -B replay.py` there. For browser and independent ephemeris/MILP reproduction, the [source-pinned workflow](../../../../.github/workflows/frontier-ignition-field.yml) specifies the browser driver, scientific dependencies, observation commands and public receipts. Each actual Qwen arm is associated with its own checkout and model identities. The [development record](../../../../products/frontier-omega-ignition/field_trials/DEVELOPMENT.json) retains interrupted observations and the configuration change used for the hosted comparison.

## Résultat

Quatre des six cibles possèdent des mesures g et r admissibles à deux heures d’intervalle au plus. À dix minutes, une seule subsiste. Le fichier emporte les identifiants de détection, les dates et les incertitudes. Un calcul SQL indépendant retrouve les mêmes paires. Séparément, six créneaux simulés permettent de planifier une visite pour cinq cibles ou deux visites pour trois cibles. Aucune observation au télescope n’a été commandée. L’outil livré permet de refaire le calcul et de modifier le critère. Sa valeur pour un observatoire et son coût complet restent à mesurer.
