## What is new in edition 1.1

- **Turn an economic gap into a construction target.** The commercial window specifies how much complete cost must fall, or how much additional voluntary demand is needed, for a proposed activity to pay. A worked example identifies three conditional routes to closing a 20-unit shortfall.
- **Calculate the capital needed to execute the sequence.** An exact result for an ordered cash plan includes collection timing and protected obligations. Three declared schedules require 100, 105 or 225 units of starting capital while yielding the same final profit of 55.
- **Qualify a successor before the advantage closes.** A renewal condition joins available earnings, construction cost and qualification delay. The worked example identifies how an additional 10 units of initial available cash, or one period less qualification time, changes the result.
- **Reproduce directly from the source ZIP.** The flat archive includes the inspected `SUPER_ALPHA.pdf`; build and analytical instructions run from its extracted root.

The paper retains its four distinct compounding processes and three proposed industry families. Its long horizon connects scientific instruments, useful energy, manufacturing and progressively capable physical infrastructure.

The new numerical work is an explicitly authored analytical illustration. Strong conventional construction ties its central result. Reviewed public studies retain their original limits; observed demand, improved discovery, physical industrial expansion and the US$3 billion trailing-year profit objective remain unestablished. This publication preserves prior paper editions and operating histories.

## Reproduce

Extract `SUPER_ALPHA_SOURCE.zip` and open a terminal in the folder containing `companion.py`, `Makefile` and `README.md`. No repository checkout or additional `cd docs/super-alpha` is needed for this flat archive:

```sh
python3 -m unittest discover -s . -p 'test_companion.py' -v
make check
python3 verify.py
```

A standard TeX Live installation with Latin Modern, latexmk, BibTeX and Poppler is required to compile the manuscript. Python-only analytical checks use the standard library.

For a repository checkout, first run `cd docs/super-alpha` from the repository root, then use the same commands. The [browser reader](https://montrealai.github.io/AGI-Alpha-Invention-Flywheel-Lab/super-alpha.html) and research links also work when this README is opened outside the repository.
