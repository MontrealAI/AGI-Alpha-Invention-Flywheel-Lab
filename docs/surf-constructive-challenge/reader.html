Do not merely find the flaw.
Build the possibility it reveals.

A fluent summary can omit the chapter that changes its meaning. A challenger can expose the omission—if it can inspect the book and its criticism is checked. The deeper opportunity is to turn that verified objection into a better way to achieve the original purpose.

Challenge the claim. Change the game. Build what becomes possible.

From a missing chapter to a missing capability

Two customers can each receive a valid-looking backup plan while both plans promise the same capacity. The objection identifies what the next construction must change: compatibility, available capacity, coordination, financing, or another legitimate means of keeping the promise.

01 · Anchor the purpose

Give the challenger the original sources, obligations, assumptions, and evidence—not only the success story.

02 · Challenge the claim

Find a material omission or executable counterexample. Reject false criticism, too. Model agreement is not external evidence.

03 · Construct the missing means

Build and test a different representation, instrument, interface, or voluntary arrangement. Let a simpler existing method win.

04 · Test what becomes possible next

Retain useful means and their limits. Test successor benefit and actual economics separately. Chronology is not causality.
