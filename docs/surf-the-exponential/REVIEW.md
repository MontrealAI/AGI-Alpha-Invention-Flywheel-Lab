# Editorial and methodological review

Research edition 1.2.0 reviewed for publication on 6 November 2026.
Editions 1.0.0 and 1.1.0 retain their original source and release artifacts.

## Scope and disposition

Separate assistant review roles examined the manuscript's source fidelity,
probability model, economic interpretation, comparator design, and claim limits.
The final substantive pass covered the corrected conditional analysis, reference
study, prospective evaluation programme, successor claims, and appendices.
No unresolved blocking methodological issue was identified in that reviewed
scope. This is editorial quality assurance within the publication process, not
independent scientific replication, a controlled multi-model experiment, or
evidence of statistically independent model errors.

## Consequential findings and resolutions

| Finding | Resolution in the manuscript |
| --- | --- |
| An incumbent having a flaw does not make every criticism of it true. | The arithmetic fixes one specified material-defect proposition per episode. The allegation and acceptance events refer to that same proposition; unrelated allegations, mixed batches, and adaptive target selection are outside the model. The protocol and code use the same scope. |
| A verifier can select criticisms with different repair effects. | The accept-all comparison explicitly assumes fixed effects within each truth class. The general case identifies the effects of acting on rejected criticisms instead of substituting accepted-branch effects. |
| A hard requirement was ambiguously described as something to change. | The contract distinguishes a hard constraint to honor, a premise to resolve, and a restriction of means to change. |
| A nonempty comparison set need not have an attained maximum. | The comparative-advantage definition now uses a finite, nonempty set of complete alternatives. |
| Receipts, costs, liabilities, and profit can be conflated. | The economic illustration defines project cash contribution, distinguishes it from accounting profit and date-specific liquidity, and excludes double counting of settled obligations and residual provisions. Edition 1.0 limited the deposit illustration to its successful path. Edition 1.1 executes the full-refund failure branch and checks the maximum required opening cash across the two declared outcomes. |
| Extra search and evidence access can masquerade as an adversarial-method advantage. | The proposed evaluation includes constructive alternatives without adversarial wording, matched independent sampling and selection, equal evidence access, and source-first production, alongside direct execution and conventional controls. |
| Criticism can be precise while missing consequential issues. | Escape probability is distinguished from post-repair defect probability, source coverage, and the defect rate under the selected action. Shared-error calculations do not assume independence from model identities. |
| An arithmetic comparator can be mistaken for an observed workflow. | Scenario rates, utilities, review costs, and conventional controls are explicitly stipulated. The abstract and result discussion describe their modeled comparison scope. |

## The substantive edition 1.1 revision

A further review challenged whether the executable evidence reached the paper's
central constructive promise. The original arithmetic audit tested criticism
but did not construct an instrument or execute a changed-task successor. That
was a material completeness gap, even though the original scope was disclosed.

The revision adds an actual generic finite constructor, artifact outputs and a
separate checker. It finds the mean-only collision, emits a three-label encoder
and decoder for 36 checks, exposes a successor collision, and emits a six-label
successor for 54 checks. A different partition procedure independently checks
all 21,147 partitions. Conventional methods tie. The larger mean-plus-flag
control preserves the important negative lesson about premature compression.

Review required the multi-query purpose to be stated explicitly: choose one
representation before query selection. A single fixed Boolean query does not
have the same three/six-label lower bound. Review also required raw source access
before compression, equal access for conventional alternatives, and a clear
separation between label count and bytes, runtime or economic cost. Construction
cannot recover distinctions discarded from old records.

The economic revision executes a full-refund counterexample. Opening cash 50
funds the deposit-assisted success path; 90 is required when the promised
failure refund is included. Conditional expected contribution, modeled buyer
surplus and construction-cost sensitivity are checked separately. No success
probability, consent or actual transaction is inferred.

The construction checker uses its own raw-state predicates, explicit block
partition enumeration and cash simulation; it imports no producer functions.
Fourteen retained construction tests include material mutations to decoder,
witness, control, refund funding, scope and source identity. The original
fifteen review-audit tests remain. A separate review mutation pass also rejected
thirteen corruptions of the new evidence. These checks establish implementation
consistency under the specified finite contracts, not external scientific
replication or general autonomous discovery.

The revised manuscript, claims and calculations received a final scientific
scope review with no remaining blocking issue identified. The reader now gives
a six-step 'Try one Missing Move' entry point and a filled record. These changes
strengthen both the evidence and the reader's ability to apply the idea.

## Evidence and attribution boundaries

### The substantive edition 1.2 revision

The next review asked what the minimum current representation should leave for
future work. Rather than add another construction laboratory, the revision
turns the existing three/six-label example into a decision among preserving a
sufficient record, preserving a qualified source-access route, and relying on
ordinary reconstruction. Lower reconstruction cost can reverse which asset is
worth preserving; rare opportunities can make ordinary access the best route.

| Consequential challenge | Resolution |
| --- | --- |
| Some future interval questions are already answerable from the maximum. Requiring reconstruction for all of them weakens the conventional route. | The model concerns an optional opportunity requiring an actually missing distinction. It supplies the `(60,80)` / `(80,80)` collision at `[80,80]`; it does not treat every interval request as such an opportunity. |
| Why pay for an access guarantee before arrival if it can be bought identically afterward? | The primary comparison explicitly requires preservation before source loss. A separate changed-timing control permits later procurement and allows it to win. |
| Blind later procurement still pays an unnecessary fee when ordinary access exists. | The stronger conventional route first observes ordinary availability, then buys the guarantee only if needed. Its exact value is 8.75 in the cheap-reconstruction case, exceeding upfront access at 7.5. |
| A source snapshot or arrangement can conceal acquisition, rights, qualification and funding costs. | The protocol fixes the exact historical source, complete remaining incremental outlays and a current resource allowance. Source-state signaling, unsupported guesses and funding from future benefits are excluded explicitly. Common implementation resources are separately provided by the task contract. |
| A claim of optimal retention could conceal an unrestricted search over future inventions. | The future purpose is known and the route menu is finite. Only arrival and ordinary availability are uncertain. Classical information comparison is credited; no new information theorem, model-performance gain or globally optimal archive is claimed. |
| The earlier construction protocol did not contain the design disclosure asserted by the appendix, and its 'no expected return' phrase obscured conditional calculations. | The protocol now explicitly states that computations were examined during design and distinguishes conditional arithmetic from an empirical expected return. Earlier released files remain unchanged. |

The producer uses exact rational formulas. The separate checker enumerates
arrival, availability and permitted fulfillment/decline actions without
importing the producer. It checks three primary cases, the exact reconstruction
cost-4 tie, and the stronger adaptive deferred-access control. Twenty retained
construction tests include the new boundaries and material mutation rejections;
the fifteen original review-audit tests remain. The interactive reader uses
integer-scaled values, displays every tie, and makes the stronger late-access
alternative available as an explicit changed assumption. Five retained reader
tests execute the actual script against separate branch/action enumeration,
covering presets, ties, the adaptive late control, boundaries and static fallback.
The scientific pass found no remaining material blocker within this scope.

### Continuing boundaries

- The three foundational editions were matched byte for byte to the existing
  public repository PDFs. Their concepts and evidence remain attributed to the
  original publications; this edition presents an integration, not the origin
  of model criticism, counterexample-guided synthesis, or market design.
- The reference computations use exact authored inputs. Separate artifact,
  partition, cash-path and review-branch checks verify the declared models; it does not calibrate model accuracy,
  establish demand, or independently replicate an empirical discovery result.
- The strongest empirical claims remain prospective. The paper does not report
  a controlled book-summary experiment, an industrial qualification, accepted
  buyer terms, realized revenue, general discovery superiority, or exponential
  enterprise growth.
- A retained capability, an earnings-funded successor, and a better discovery
  procedure have distinct evidence requirements. Existing methods and retaining
  the incumbent remain legitimate winners.
