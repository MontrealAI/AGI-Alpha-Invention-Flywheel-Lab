## What this edition adds

- **Productive composition as part of the economic specification.** Accepted service identities and composition rights determine the prices and choices actually available to a buyer.
- **An exact constructive witness.** At opening cash six, neither staging nor a connector alone completes the supplied route. Their joint construction does, with cash `6 → 4 → 1 → 7 → 3 → 9`, under the stated analytical costs and participation conditions.
- **Independent finite evidence.** The companion checks 2,187 menus, 279,936 carts and 413,343 buyer/budget/menu cases; a separate 4,096-menu study preserves cases in which composition raises, lowers or leaves seller receipts unchanged.
- **Strong alternatives that can win.** A conventional constructor with the same choices ties; more initial cash permits a better pure-bundle route; ordinary equal-content reuse matches inherited capability; and the second episode can change its contract as its resources change.
- **A public model-boundary reproduction.** A pinned historical menu passes 72 single-report deviations while its joint quote can be undercut by the equivalent component cart by 213 cents. The original source, actual finite scope and historical financial results remain distinct.

## Evidence scope

The new numerical study is an authored analytical verification, not observed demand or an untouched empirical holdout. Additional lifecycle burden is explicit: an upfront `h ≥ 0` changes the joint route's required capital to `5 + h` and surplus to `3 − h`. Unknown actual development, discovery, model and hosting costs are not measured or assumed to be zero.

The publication does not establish autonomous open-world invention superiority, physical production, actual commercial revenue or the unachieved US$3 billion trailing-365-day complete-cost profit objective. Earlier publications and operating histories retain their original results. Private source documents, raw conversations, credentials and account/recovery files are not included.
