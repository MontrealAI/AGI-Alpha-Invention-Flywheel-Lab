# Canonical CSV backend operating qualification 029

Dependencies: PR49 `dc0ee61efcf717ec0f0dd60319060694489750f1` (PR45/47 operators and native manifest seam, common production contract). This is an additive, opt-in source candidate; no running account or cohort is migrated. Channel/repeat policy candidates are not silently included.

## What is implemented

The method policy can declare a source-bound `csv_backend` candidate. Its eligible offers are existing complete-outcome reconciliation contracts, never intake-readiness upgrades. The finite method selector and joint quote selector compare its positive incremental cost against ordinary conventional/generated production. Equal output beliefs yield no benefit: the cheaper conventional option remains selected. `diagnostic_csv_backend` is explicitly diagnostic, not economic optimization.

Canonical operating execution now carries the chosen backend through `method_plan`, the ordinary native positive `production_contract` prepayment, `execute_funded`, current PR47 CSV production, independent native semantic acceptance, customer collection/refund, and separate operating rederivation. A backend is not a new ledger. Its source contract includes exact executing code, operator spec and plan. Source identity is procedural verification, not cryptographic execution attestation.

The backend reuses PR47 direct/equal-content/replanned/inherited operations. All produce the same native service artifact fields. They neither expand effect-label authority nor carry inherited proof. Unsupported service/scope is excluded, and postpayment failure cannot turn into free retries or false acceptance. Independent audit reproduces a paid failure rather than trusting its label. Existing account obligations and protections remain intact.

## Costs and evidence limits

The same $1.50 authored production envelope covers complete ordinary production/acceptance once. Additional adapter/proposal/verification envelopes are $0.06 direct, $0.07 equal-content/inherited, $0.10 replanned. They are not measured bills or conversions of symbolic units. Whole process CPU includes native operating execution and the independent operating audit, but not initial service preparation, engineering, host billing or model billing; those remain unknown. No broad optimality, invention, retained advantage or economic improvement is promised.

The fixed public qualification uses six arms: ordinary conventional, optimized-with-CSV-eligible, and four diagnostic CSV arms. Every arm uses the same exposed service seed, market seed, 14-day horizon, starting capital and source. This is development qualification, not untouched confirmation. Actual customers and collections are zero; any profit is finite-horizon conditional simulation only. Full accounting and unfavorable results are retained in compact summaries, not giant private account archives.
