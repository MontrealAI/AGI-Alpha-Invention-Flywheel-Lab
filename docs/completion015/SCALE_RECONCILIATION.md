## Reconciliation and next bridge

The timing differences follow different initial capital, $400 aggregate unit economics versus smaller checked native offers, purchase menus/ranking, demand/cross-sell/retention assumptions, channel cost/realization/expiry, clocks and reserve contracts. PR22's earlier crossing is not evidence that its channels are empirically better; PR23's cheaper imagined reach does not imply a faster result when the allocation and action space differ. An isolated causal attribution of the cross-model timing gap has **not** been executed.

The remaining bridge is therefore not arithmetic scaling of current receipts. It requires costed executable supply and admitted services at larger overlapping sizes, consistent buyer budgets/identities, accepted and collected work, matched acquisitions/capacity, and native-equivalent cash/protection timing. Measure discrepancies in deliveries, acceptance, costs, failed acquisition, capacity and cash at each overlap before extending the forecast. Retain ordinary methods when their collected contribution wins. The next construction should remove an observed finite-inventory, acquisition or working-capital constraint and verify its incremental profitable output; it should not select whichever historical projection reaches $1B first.
