# AGI ALPHA FLYWHEEL

## Inventing What Becomes Possible

Vincent Boucher · MONTREAL.AI · Research edition 1.0 · 2 November 2026

**Build the next starting point. Then test what it makes possible.**

This publication connects Transformational Intelligence to an enterprise that searches over valuable outcomes and the means of achieving them together. It follows three things across successive projects: reusable knowledge, qualified productive capability, and available earnings.

The paper distinguishes a successor financed by earlier work, one enabled by an inherited instrument, and one discovered more effectively by an improved procedure. It includes an original vector flywheel, a precise reachability model, two conditional propositions, an exact reproducible example, strong conventional controls, and a public evidence review.

### Evidence scope

This is a research architecture and evaluation synthesis with a constructed finite illustration. Its review preserves conventional-method ties, measurement limits, and negative economic results. It does not establish actual commercial revenue, physical industrial expansion, general recursive improvement, or the US$3 billion trailing-year profit objective. All prior experimental claims are pinned to their original public evidence snapshot. This paper release does not qualify or replace an enterprise-runtime release.
