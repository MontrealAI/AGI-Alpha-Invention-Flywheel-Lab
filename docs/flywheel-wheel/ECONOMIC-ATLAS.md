# AGI ALPHA: Transformational Intelligence, Incentives and Productive Assets

**Edition 031 — implemented read-only control and visualization extension, not a new ledger or a completed self-improving enterprise.**

The scientific objective remains: each verified invention becomes a tool for discovering, constructing and economically enabling the next. The extension makes incentive contracts, asset conditions and native funding explicit. It also provides a genuinely circular six-ring SVG/HTML architecture. Detailed geometry is not a capability claim.

## Why these six rings are distinct

| Ring | What circulates | What can accumulate | Main falsifier |
|---|---|---|---|
| Invention | Problems, candidate mechanisms, causal compositions, experiments | Useful tools, abstractions, generative procedures | No improvement on fresh tasks against strong methods |
| Evidence | Observations, counterexamples, scoped checks, revalidation | Applicable evidence and knowledge of failure | Wrong semantics, common-source error, out-of-scope reuse |
| Incentives | Defined work, commitments, review, adjudication and remedies | Accountable cooperation; contractual contribution histories | Pay-for-approval, collusion, false attribution, unfunded promises |
| Assets | Artifacts, legitimate rights, qualified deployment and maintenance | Maintainable productive capabilities | Stale versions, missing rights, unsupported valuation |
| Commerce | Voluntary demand, competitive offers, onboarding, accepted delivery | Customer relationships and reliably fulfilled work | No paid demand, rejected quality, negative complete-cost economics |
| Capital | Collections, obligations, reserves, available earned cash, commitments | Actually funded productive resources | Spending forecasts, pooled independent worlds, duplicate charges |

The hub is an authorized purpose plus Transformational Intelligence. Methods may change; the user's outcome and permissions cannot be silently weakened. The external governor preserves WORLD / POLICY / PROOF / AUTHORITY. Ring arrows are independent loops, not a mandatory chronological pipeline or fixed committee.

## The coupling rule

A working capability can become an asset only in its verified scope and with legitimate reuse rights. Deployment may support an accepted sale. Collection may produce unrestricted earnings after all costs and obligations. The native financial owner may then fund further production or a bounded investigation. A funded experiment may produce another verified capability. Every arrow requires its own evidence.

Causal contribution is not financial provenance. A `funded_by` edge does not establish an `enabled_by` edge. Asset market value is not cash. An artifact hash establishes byte identity, not title, a license, a patent, collateral or customer demand. Permission is not inherited with a program. Claimed future option value is not booked earnings.

## Incentives without corrupting the evaluator

Use separate contractual reasons for payment:

* Builder: a specified independently accepted outcome.
* Verifier: completed, competent checking, whether the candidate passes or fails.
* Challenger: an independently adjudicated counterexample, not unsupported accusations.
* Originator: an authorized share of actually collected incremental surplus where contribution and rights are established.

The implementation validates these declared contract shapes and role separation. It does **not** establish real identity independence, create escrow, pay fees, slash collateral or prove an incentive-compatible market equilibrium. Same-source/model/ownership correlation and strategic collusion require additional evidence. A submitted approval cannot manufacture a payment.

Reward caps are fully provisioned in the proposed budget, once per shared contract. Provisioning in a proposal is not an incurred expense, bank reserve or payable. Actual work fees, conditional bonuses and remedies require the canonical owner's current authorization and accounting. The module cannot release them. Baseline direct production costs already include their existing check; declared extra fees must not silently charge that same operation twice.

## Implemented economic-coupling boundary

`flywheel_wheel.coupling` performs a bounded read-only compilation:

1. Accept one selected world's context from a freshly rederived native audit and scoreboard.
2. Bind source, frozen experiment, report, full scoreboard, native action history and financial summary.
3. Require an exact expected-context digest; changes in state, account or source invalidate the proposal.
4. Filter declared assets by current scope, qualification, expiry and reuse rights. None confers current mission proof.
5. Require a fresh mission check, explicit base-versus-extra cost attribution and separated producer/checker/adjudicator labels.
6. Compile shared maintenance/revalidation/integration and incentive caps as dependencies, charged once across the selected set.
7. Call the **existing** `portfolio_scale.transformational.choose` finite allocator; no replacement optimizer is introduced.
8. Bound planning by the minimum of native available cash, unrestricted earned funds and the proposed planning cap. A native investment stop overrides positive balances.
9. Return a proposal with `execution_authority:false`, `funding_authority:false`, `current_proof:null`, `authority:[]` and a mandatory native-state recheck before any future commitment.

Caller-provided qualifications, reuse rights, role labels and expected returns are declared premises, not authenticated truth. JSON hashes cannot certify them. The CLI's native context route executes explicitly trusted frozen code; it is not a sandbox for untrusted archives. The adapter is simulation-only and does not admit real-money claims. A completed negative result or no-affordable-action result is valid.

## Six rings, fifteen mechanism families

Structural transfer; adversarial discovery; strategic invention; transformational reframing; instrument and experiment invention; open-world hypothesis expansion; self-correcting evidence within scope; executable mechanism synthesis; sequence/topology invention; missing-interface construction; generative abstraction; mission-relative inheritance; improvement of discovery procedures; commercial/incentive design; physically validated productive expansion.

These are accumulated design mechanisms, not fifteen measured autonomous accomplishments. The exact nonlinear Out-Reflect result must not be transferred to prose, provider independence or arbitrary physics by metaphor. Strong conventional specialists stay eligible. Ordinary tool reuse and improved invention procedures require different comparisons.

## First executable causal/economic chain to establish

An accepted order exposes a real bottleneck. An experiment compares a new instrument with the strongest competent alternative. The independent evaluator establishes its supported benefit. Authorized reuse improves a genuinely fresh successor. Actual accepted deployments generate collected surplus. The financial owner admits another funded commitment. A further study tests whether the invention method itself improved.

The current coupling extension establishes none of those gains by itself. It makes the boundary between them checkable and connects the visual architecture to the existing native accounting read path.

## Reproduce

```sh
python -m unittest discover -s tests -p 'test_flywheel*.py' -v
python -m flywheel_wheel atlas --output /absolute/new/atlas
```

The HTML's buttons only highlight rings. It has no account controls, network calls, telemetry or credentials. Its sole script is pinned by a content-security-policy hash. The SVG is accessible and editable.

For a **trusted, completed synthetic native enterprise**, construct a request bound to `live_context` and run:

```sh
python -m flywheel_wheel couple --enterprise /absolute/existing/native \
  --world EXACT_REGISTERED_WORLD_ID --request /absolute/request.json \
  --universe portfolio_scale/universe.json --trust-frozen-source \
  --output /absolute/new/coupling-proposal
```

No stored request grants spending rights. `planning_cap_microUSD` only restricts the calculation; it does not issue an authorization. `verify_proposal` recomputes the proposal under the same inputs; this is not independent scientific or financial certification.

## Native integration qualification

`scripts/qualify_economic_wheel031.py` registers two fresh seven-day synthetic worlds, operates their exact frozen code, reruns canonical audits, renders the authentic scoped snapshot, and derives one world's planning context. It tests a deliberately unknown-return next action with a declared verification cap. The expected decision is **retain cash**, not an invented investment. Exact native account-history bytes must remain unchanged by planning. No active owner account is used.

The workflow uploads only an explicit allowlist of sanitized SVG, HTML, snapshots, proposal and integration receipt. Private keys, account files, registration source copies and raw native state stay inside the disposable runner. Resource observations do not substitute for full host/model/engineering bills. This is a wiring test, not an economic superiority or recursive-discovery experiment.
