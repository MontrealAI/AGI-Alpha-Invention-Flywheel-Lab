# AGI ALPHA Flywheel Harness — Transformational Intelligence
## Six coupled loops, one continuing enterprise

**Mission:** We are building an invention-automation machine in which each verified invention becomes a tool for discovering, constructing and economically enabling the next.

**Edition 032 scope:** a complete circular engineering illustration and three concrete admission repairs within the existing read-only economic coupling. This is not a new financial runtime, a general autonomous inventor, an activated reward market, or physical infrastructure. It extends PR #54 rather than creating a competing harness. Existing native accounts and integration-owner work are untouched.

## 1. The wheel is a causal architecture

The centre preserves the authorized purpose. The surrounding rings represent different things that can improve through repeated work. The spokes represent interfaces that require separate evidence. No ring borrows another ring's success label.

| Ring | Rotation | Retained value | Required separation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Invention | Observe → analogize → reframe → synthesize → compose → test → abstract → evolve | Executable mechanisms and generative methods | Inventing an artifact versus improving the inventor |
| Evidence | Scope → source → probe → challenge → correct → confirm → revalidate → archive | Applicable observations, counterexamples and tests | Consistency versus independently justified truth |
| Incentives | Contract → provision → contribute → review → adjudicate → settle → remedy → realign | Accountable contribution arrangements | Completed checking versus a favorable verdict |
| Assets | Construct → qualify → license → deploy → maintain → measure → retire → adapt | Useful artifacts plus valid reuse rights and upkeep | Capability versus title, permission or cash value |
| Commerce | Need → offer → price → onboard → deliver → accept → collect → renew | Customer relationships and accepted productive work | Authored simulation versus observed commerce |
| Capital | Collect → reconcile → protect → provision → choose → reserve → deploy → recover | Available earned resources and funded means | Accounting profit versus unrestricted cash |

Eight gates join the rings: **purpose, scope, acceptance, settlement, liquidity, rights, lineage and revalidation**. Their radial placement is architectural notation, not a requirement that unrelated processes share one clock. The design supports different speeds: a commercial order, an experiment and an industrial deployment need not finish together.

The outer productive frontier tracks useful energy, science, computation, materials, manufacturing and infrastructure. This is an intended application domain, not a physical achievement inferred from software or revenue.

These are an extensible repertoire, not a compulsory chain of fifteen agents. A direct conventional specialist remains eligible and may be best. The original human-supplied analogies and constructed examples remain labeled as supplied insights, not autonomous discovery.

## 3. The complete causal/economic chain

A verified limitation can motivate a new instrument. A structural transfer can suggest how to construct it. Independent evaluation can qualify it in scope. Valid reuse rights can permit a fresh mission to use it. A buyer can voluntarily accept and pay for the resulting work. After incurred costs, liabilities and protected obligations, the native financial owner can authorize another commitment. That commitment can fund the next productive operation or a bounded invention experiment.

Every link can fail. A failure is retained, not repaired by relabeling its evidence. `funded_by` and `enabled_by` are separate relations: a payment can finance a successor without proving a technological contribution. Likewise, an inherited capability can improve a task without proving that the discovery procedure improved.

An asset contract needs artifact identity, scope, dependencies, applicable evidence, reuse rights, expiry, maintenance and invalidation conditions. An incentive contract needs defined work, a budgeted cap, an adjudication rule, eligibility conditions and remedies. Their declarations do not establish actual rights, independent identities or payable entitlements.

**Verifier fees should not depend on a pass.** Builder acceptance, competent review completion, adjudicated counterexamples and authorized shares of collected incremental surplus are distinct contractual bases. All are still subject to legitimate current authority. Neither a model's self-rating nor an agreeing committee can manufacture payment eligibility.

## 4. Runtime and trust boundaries

The canonical enterprise remains `connected_enterprise`. `Account` through `JournalAccount` remains the single financial authority. The wheel reads one completed, explicitly trusted native output, reruns its canonical audit, obtains its scoreboard and binds the proposed calculation to the exact source, freeze, world, financial summary and action history.

`flywheel_wheel.coupling` filters declared asset conditions, compiles shared costs and reward provisions, then reuses `portfolio_scale.transformational.choose`. The proposal stays simulation-only and non-executable. Its planning budget cannot exceed the minimum of available cash, unrestricted earnings and the supplied planning cap; an investment stop dominates positive balances. A cap supplied to the planner is not an authorization grant.

WORLD records facts and uncertainty. POLICY records purposes and limits. PROOF records current source-bound checks. AUTHORITY records permissions issued separately. Successors do not inherit present approval: `current_proof:null`, `authority:[]`.

The enduring runtime cycle is admission → recovery → selection → bounded execution → assessment → checkpoint → continuation or stop. This display/coupling extension does not install a scheduler or replace the native recovery and release procedures.

## 5. Concrete repairs in this edition

**Productive-benefit boundary.** Previously a request's synergy members were validated after synthetic cost nodes had been inserted. That allowed a caller to attach a declared benefit to bookkeeping provisions. A reproduced zero-cost example could increase modeled expected cash without a corresponding productive action. `preflight.benefit_members` now restricts synergies to original productive action IDs before compilation. It does not certify that any claimed future return is true.

**Typed receipt comparison.** Python treats `False == 0` and `True == 1` as true, including nested dictionaries. The prior `verify_proposal` accepted a numeric replacement of a Boolean authority field. It now compares canonical JSON bytes, preserving Boolean/integer/float distinctions while ignoring irrelevant object-key order. This fixes receipt consistency; it is not authentication and does not grant authority.

**Export isolation before code execution.** New presentation and proposal output must be fresh and outside the native enterprise directory. Invalid or overlapping output is rejected before the CLI invokes trusted frozen source. The canonical auditor retains its own documented audit-receipt behavior; the wheel cannot write exports into native custody.

The earlier source and negative results remain in Git history. Existing financial state, current production policy and earlier experiment results are not rewritten.

## 6. Illustration and reproducible operation

The precision view uses the existing six-ring stage inventory, shows the entire uncropped wheel, and adds eight gate spokes, phase identifiers, directional arcs and a productive-frontier annotation. The SVG has a title and description. The standalone HTML offers keyboard-operable causal-route highlighting, a content-security-policy-pinned script, no telemetry, no external requests and no account controls.

```sh
python -m unittest discover -s tests -p 'test_flywheel*.py' -v
python -m flywheel_wheel precision --output /absolute/new/precision-wheel
```

Earlier `architecture`, `atlas`, `audit-render` and `couple` modes remain. `precision` is an architecture-only renderer. Opening its page never operates the enterprise. A presentation is not proof of the claims it illustrates.

For actual trusted completed output, `audit-render` and `couple` continue using the existing canonical read paths. Choose a fresh sibling output directory, not a descendant of the enterprise. All mutations and native financial commitments remain outside this contribution.

## 7. Qualification and interpretation

Local development reproduced two failures against byte-identical prior `coupling.py` and the existing allocator, then confirmed their rejection after the repair. The local subset also covers malformed graphs, resource bounds, typed receipts, file-system isolation, accessible SVG, CSP and no-authority output. This is engineering regression evidence, not a market experiment or an independent scientific replication.

The existing hosted workflow reruns the full wheel suite, generates both prior views and the precision view, and retains the canonical seven-day synthetic audit-to-planning integration. That integration must preserve negative economics and unknown returns. A valid retain-cash decision is not a commercial failure of the checker; a successful checker is not a profitable business.

A meaningful capability gain still requires a strong direct comparator. Successor benefit requires a fresh no-inheritance and equal-content ordinary-reuse comparison. Discovery improvement requires old/new policy crossed with old/new initial capabilities. Financial success requires accepted collected work at complete cost. Physical productive expansion requires actual relevant-environment measurements and operating qualification. None is inferred from test count or diagram complexity.

## 8. Engineering reference points

The implementation applies bounded inputs, strict types, explicit effects, source-bound receipts, fail-closed admission, independent verifier ownership, least privilege, pinned actions, create-only public output and preserved negative evidence. These are concrete practices, not a blanket certification.

NIST's AI RMF organizes risk work around Govern, Map, Measure and Manage and calls for assessments beyond front-line development: https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/5-sec-core/ . GitHub's secure-use guidance addresses least privilege, untrusted workflow inputs and pinned actions: https://docs.github.com/en/actions/reference/security/secure-use . These references inform the design; this PR is not independently certified against either framework.

**Invention becomes infrastructure for further invention only when evidence establishes the inheritance. The enterprise becomes infrastructure for further production only when actual resources and qualified capabilities support that next operation.**
