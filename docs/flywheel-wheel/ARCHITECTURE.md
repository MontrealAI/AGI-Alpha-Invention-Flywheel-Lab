# 🌌♾️ AGI ALPHA — Transformational Intelligence Flywheel Harness

**Architecture edition:** 31 October 2026. **Diagram:** `AGI_ALPHA_Flywheel_Wheel_Architecture.svg` (scalable vector) and PNG companion.

## 1. Why the architecture is a wheel

A real flywheel accumulates momentum from actual inputs while losing energy to friction; it cannot manufacture energy. The architectural analogy must respect that: useful deliveries, tested knowledge, real resources, and durable productive assets can enable additional work, but costs, failures, depreciation, and obligations subtract from available capacity. A diagram of arrows with no conserved state, carried-forward capability, or independently tested gain is not a flywheel.

The AGI ALPHA design therefore has **one governed hub and three concentric, independently verifiable cycles**. Each cycle can produce useful value without forcing a successful result in another. Cross-cycle transfers happen only at explicit proof, authority, and financial gates. None of the rings is a mandatory global sequence for every task.

## 2. Hub: Transformational Intelligence and governed operating control

The centre maintains an owner-authorized **PURPOSE**, a current **WORLD** of sources and observations, bounded **POLICY** and **AUTHORITY**, and separately checked current **PROOF**. It chooses between direct specialist execution, qualified reuse, mechanism composition, new experiment/instrument construction, abstention, and retain-cash/stop. It can question an inherited *method requirement* but cannot silently change the authorized outcome or grant itself permission. It keeps a single continuing enterprise: source-bound checkpoints, an operating queue, complete histories, protection of liabilities, and crash-safe recovery.

`connected_enterprise/` is the sole canonical commercial runner; `adaptive_enterprise/account.py` through `connected_enterprise/finance.py` owns native money, obligations, and investment authority. The harness is an **additive coordinating layer**, not another financial ledger or general-purpose account owner.

## 3. Inner wheel: autonomous commercial compounding (blue)

Clockwise:

1. **Offer:** Discover real buyer demand, design complete outcomes, price against a competent human alternative, a competitor AI, self-service and no purchase; allow NoBid.
2. **Deliver:** Perform permissioned onboarding, useful complete production, source-bound checks, correction or justified rejection; measure all failed attempts.
3. **Collect:** Establish actual acceptance and collected payment, full costs, refunds, liabilities and reserves. Accounting profit is not automatically investable liquidity. Simulated receipts are not observed commerce.
4. **Reinvest:** Reserve *only genuinely available unrestricted earnings* under the current native account, to repeat delivery, acquire capacity/customers, qualify a useful instrument, or retain cash. Recheck current audited state at commitment time.

**Independent criterion:** additional repeatable accepted deliveries, positive complete-cost profit and real (or explicitly *simulated*) available earned-funded continuation. Invention is **not** required for this wheel to qualify.

## 4. Middle wheel: compositional invention and recursive improvement (violet)

Clockwise:

1. **Discovery:** Analyze consequential limitations; find cross-domain structural equivalence, missing assumptions, contradictory evidence, and opportunities to remove unnecessary steps.
2. **Synthesis:** Invent new mechanisms, ordered sequences, causal feedback arrangements, intermediate interfaces, instruments, abstractions, and methods for constructing families of mechanisms. A mechanism contract defines preconditions, outputs, state changes, effects, source, cost and applicability. Symbolic synthesis by itself is never proof of a real execution effect.
3. **Reflection:** Independently reproduce actual outputs, challenge the candidate and the evaluator's scope, design discriminating probes, and retain justified `UNKNOWN` or contradiction results. Out-Reflect correction is conditional on a justified error model and meaningful source independence.
4. **Inheritance:** Retain qualified executable content and histories, but reset current proof and authority for fresh successors. Compare matched no-inheritance, ordinary reuse, direct-specialist and inherited-capability arms. Evaluate improved **discovery policy** separately from improved starting tools.

**Independent criteria:** (i) a new independently qualified useful capability; (ii) causally demonstrated gain on a fresh successor; (iii) separately demonstrated improvement of the invention procedure itself. These are three different claims.

## 5. Outer wheel: productive physical expansion (green)

Clockwise:

1. **Bottleneck:** Identify a measured restriction in scientific measurement, energy, computation, manufacturing, materials or infrastructure; separate a real hard constraint from a conventional workflow assumption.
2. **Instrument:** Construct or acquire the missing scientific/industrial means; use independently authorized data and explicitly costed trials. Qualified specialists or partners may be better than reinventing every subsystem.
3. **Measurement:** Establish actual useful net output, not only a digital twin, gross energy or monetary forecast. Check reliability, supply dependencies, maintenance, repair, energy and material inputs, waste/heat, reproducibility and operating risks.
4. **Replication:** Extend only independently qualified physical performance to new installations/environments under their own current permissions and validation requirements.

**Independent criterion:** a reproducible *net increase in qualified useful productive capacity* after complete operating and lifecycle requirements. Digital income does not by itself establish this result.

## 6. Bearings, governor, inertia and friction

| Flywheel feature | Operational counterpart |
|---|---|
| **Hub / shaft** | Owner-set mission, Transformational Intelligence selection and state ownership |
| **Rim** | Independent commercial, invention and productive-expansion cycles |
| **Inertia** | Durable financial history, qualified capability network, test history, reusable instruments and retained customer context only within permissions |
| **Drive / torque** | Actual accepted outcomes, available earnings, verified enabling capabilities, measured useful physical productivity |
| **Friction / drag** | Failed offers, compute/tools, development and verification, maintenance, taxes/fees, liabilities, depreciation and lost demand |
| **Governor / brake** | Independent evaluator, secure source identity, lease limits, authority boundary, risk limits, NoBid, abstention and stop |
| **Bearings** | `WORLD / POLICY / PROOF / AUTHORITY` separation; canonical native ledger and recovery integrity |

Every proposed gain must include its acquisition and maintenance costs. The metaphor does not assert guaranteed acceleration, exponential compounding or physical operation.

## 7. Three explicit crossover gates

**From commerce to invention:** A proposed research investment is evaluated against repeat delivery, acquisition, capacity and cash retention. It can commit only under an approved allocation and audited native cash reservation. A forecast is not funding.

**From invention to commerce:** A reusable capability is admitted only after independent qualification for a concrete customer mission. It inherits compatible content and provenance, not current acceptance, authority or a self-declared profit advantage. The strongest competent direct method remains eligible.

**From invention or earnings to physical production:** Require a genuine operating purpose, physical measurements, relevant safety/environment controls, owner/operator authorization, qualified engineering interfaces, complete cost and independently measured useful output before deployment/replication claims.

## 8. Operating loop — the real Flywheel Harness

`ADMIT → RECOVER → SELECT → EXECUTE → EVALUATE → ACCOUNT → CHECKPOINT → DECIDE`

- `ADMIT`: freeze objective, task population, comparisons, authority, source and bounded resource envelope;
- `RECOVER`: preserve original account, action tape, obligations and active queue; acquire exclusive ownership;
- `SELECT`: choose best justified useful work across commerce, invention and physical enabling investigations;
- `EXECUTE`: bounded actions using only already qualified effects;
- `EVALUATE`: independently verify the actual output, record `PASSED_IN_SCOPE`, `REJECTED`, `UNKNOWN` or `INVALID`;
- `ACCOUNT`: use existing authoritative finance for incurred cost, real/simulated collected value and liabilities, with no synthetic capital creation;
- `CHECKPOINT`: retain immutable source identity, full original failures, current state and cumulative budget receipts;
- `DECIDE`: adopt under separate protection, continue existing allocation, retain incumbent, stop or seek a new genuine authorization.

The operating cycle is **not** identical to the invention wheel: every ordinary commercial delivery need not perform invention, and every invention need not involve customers or payments.

## 10. The decisive next experiment

Against a strong unrestricted direct specialist, demonstrate on a freshly frozen useful task that a newly composed **real executable** mechanism improves complete cost, quality or throughput, counting discovery, rejected attempts, proof, integration, maintenance and operating costs. Then test that same qualified capability on a *genuinely different* successor using no-inheritance and ordinary-reuse controls. Separately cross old/new discovery policies with old/new starting tools to test whether the **invention method** improved. Maintain the known successful P0 as champion and do not migrate native accounts to obtain a positive result.

Only subsequent actual voluntary paid orders, accepted deliveries, real collected surplus, and funded continuations establish observed commercial growth. Only independently qualified physical trials establish productive expansion. Retain negative results and honest `UNKNOWN` verdicts.

**Governing rule:** Preserve what works; invent what is missing; prove what changed; inherit only what transfers; spend only what is actually available; measure the next useful outcome.

## 11. Research mechanism inventory — what is composed and why

The mechanism repertoire is not a 15-agent DAG. Each invocation must produce a changed executable construction, test, decision, or justified abstention; otherwise it adds friction without increasing useful value.

| Mechanism | Concrete operational test | Maturity boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Cross-domain structural discovery | Map source/target invariants and execute a discrimination test | Transfer cannot rest on wording alone |
| Adversarial discovery | Produce a reproducible decisive counterexample or support abstention | No manufactured criticism |
| Strategic invention | Compare different routes and strongest conventional alternative | Optimistic policy model ≠ market optimum |
| Transformational reframing | Remove an optional method dependency without changing the authorized outcome | No silent goal or permission change |
| Experiment invention | Construct feasible probes that resolve a relevant unknown | Count instrument and reference costs |
| Open-world hypothesis expansion | Return neither, unknown, or no affordable test within limits | No invented certainty |
| Out-Reflect evidence construction | Challenge admitted evaluator class, source fault domain and reference meaning | Polynomial result not universal oracle |
| Mechanism construction | Execute a proposed new operation in a qualified environment | Symbolic postcondition ≠ actual effect |
| Causal sequence/topology invention | Demonstrate an executable new sequence, branch or feedback cycle | Type-check, terminate, measure |
| Missing-interface synthesis | Construct and validate a connector between otherwise incompatible capabilities | Developer-supplied bridge ≠ autonomous invention |
| Higher-order compositional abstraction | Package a verified composite as a reusable new mechanism | Include acquisition/maintenance costs |
| Mission-relative proof and authority | Reuse valid structure; freshly prove current semantics and grant current permission | Proof and authority default empty |
| Generative inheritance and discovery-policy improvement | Cross old/new policies and starting capabilities on fresh successors | Improved tool ≠ improved inventor |
| Autonomous commercial compounding | Qualified buyer choice, accepted work, collections and earned-funded continuation | Simulated ≠ real receipts |
| Productive expansion | Net physically measured useful output and reproduced operational asset | Digital proof ≠ physical production |

Every claimed enablement edge needs an independent causal experiment. A verified composite may remove work, not only add capabilities. Complete costs and honest stop/no-bid decisions compete with every invention action.
