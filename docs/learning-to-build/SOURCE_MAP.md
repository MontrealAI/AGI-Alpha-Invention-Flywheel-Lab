## Primary external literature checked

The bibliography uses the following primary publications or official programme descriptions, checked on 7 November 2026:

- Margaret A. Boden, “Creativity and artificial intelligence,” *Artificial Intelligence* 103(1–2), 347–356 (1998). [Publisher](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0004-3702(98)00055-1). The publisher abstract supports the distinction among combination, exploration, and transformation. Full publisher HTML access was restricted; no claim depends on inaccessible full-text detail.
- NASA, *Systems Engineering Handbook*, NASA/SP-2016-6105 Rev2. [Handbook](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/systems-engineering-handbook/), [product integration chapter](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/5-2-product-integration/). Used for integration, interfaces, enabling products, operations, and support.
- Ellis and colleagues, *DreamCoder*, arXiv:2006.08381. [Author preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.08381). Used for learned symbolic abstractions and search guidance in bounded program-learning settings.
- Wong and colleagues, “Leveraging Language to Learn Program Abstractions and Search Heuristics,” ICML/PMLR 139, 11193–11204 (2021). [Proceedings](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/wong21a.html).
- Jürgen Schmidhuber, *POWERPLAY*, arXiv:1112.5309 (2011; revised 2012). [Author preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1112.5309). Used as prior work for joint task and solver-modification search.
- Novikov and colleagues, *AlphaEvolve: A coding agent for scientific and algorithmic discovery*, arXiv:2506.13131 (2025). [Author preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.13131). Used as evaluator-guided code-improvement precedent, without transferring its reported gains to our study.
- Paul Milgrom and John Roberts, “The Economics of Modern Manufacturing,” *American Economic Review* 80(3), 511–528 (1990). [Authors’ institutional publication record](https://www.gsb.stanford.edu/faculty-research/publications/economics-modern-manufacturing-technology-strategy-organization).
- Paul M. Romer, “Endogenous Technological Change,” *Journal of Political Economy* 98(5, Part 2), S71–S102 (1990). [Open working-paper record](https://www.nber.org/papers/w3210), [published version](https://doi.org/10.1086/261725).
- NASA, “Lunar Surface Technology.” [Official programme description](https://www.nasa.gov/lunar-surface-technology/). Used narrowly for existing integrated technology directions and described simulant tests, not as evidence of deployed industrial closure or our own accomplishment.
