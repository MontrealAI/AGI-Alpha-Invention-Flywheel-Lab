**Change the game → build what the new game requires → learn to build better.**

### Included evidence

The finite companion emits four standalone measurement programs and checks them over 4,000 declared input cases. It evaluates 288 supplied cash/deadline/consent/maintenance cells across three states, totaling 864 complete-route decisions. The equally informed conventional control ties all 288 corresponding constructor-state decisions. Maintenance can reverse the apparent expansion of reachable outcomes.

The constructor, task specifications, cost parameters and participation conditions are authored. The programs and executions are computational artifacts; they do not establish autonomous discovery, empirical constructor learning, real customer acceptance, actual receipts or physical deployment. Separate checker source remains under common research authorship.
