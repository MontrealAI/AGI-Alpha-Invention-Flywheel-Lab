## Prior art narrows the claim

Retry keys and compensation are established means. LIMBO already studies ambiguous acknowledgements, late commits and contract effects. LearnLib provides active learning and conformance checking. The proposed residual is constructing an accepted *composition* from explicit observations under a complete budget, and then testing a retained construction method against ordinary equal-content use. Merely adding a key or rediscovering an ambiguous timeout does not establish invention or improved discovery.

The three claims remain separate: a conditional commercial result; a useful sandbox capability; and a retained-method advantage. The last requires fresh matched comparisons. The flagship remains unqualified unless a consequential residual survives the complete ordinary alternative.
