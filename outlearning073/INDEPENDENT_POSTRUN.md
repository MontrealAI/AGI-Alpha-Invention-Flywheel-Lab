## Decision and limits

The bounded result belongs to **conditional commercial success without established discovery-method advantage** in the positive-demand region. The other five case types preserve commercial failure, refusal or non-entry; none establishes a discovery advantage. Across real-world claims, neither profitable commerce nor discovery superiority has been established.

Use the ordinary construction as the current technical reference. Retain the generated executable instrument and its explicit applicability/refusal conditions, but do not spend further merely to preserve a favorable outlearning narrative. The source protocol already states that a conventional direct K2 route may fit initial equity; neither this financing tape nor capability transfer establishes that K1 made K2 otherwise unreachable. The comparison does not isolate a new industry's existence or necessity.

Prices, buyer budgets, competitive quotes, monetary envelopes, fees, taxes, finite claim expiry and collection timing are authored assumptions. Raw model/host bills and complete attention costs remain unknown. Finite sandbox acceptance supplies no physical qualification, live service contract, calibrated willingness to pay, human-productivity measurement or scaling path. The two delivered offers cannot be annualized into the $3 billion objective. Source-matched hosted qualification, any later integration and any release remain separate gates.
