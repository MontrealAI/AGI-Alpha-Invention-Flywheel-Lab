# Outlearning073: ordinary construction matches the retained method

An actual included-agent constructor turned K1 tool observations into reusable executable settlement code. On a different K2 operation graph, the retained method and the competent ordinary constructor both delivered **21 of 24** complete sandbox outcomes and refused the same three unsupported cases. Both had zero avoidable side-effect loss. The ordinary method used **51 fewer diagnostic units**. This does not establish a retained discovery-method advantage or a newly attainable productive frontier.

The positive-demand native simulation separately completed a connected pair with **US$1,079.68 profit** after all declared costs. K1's collected earnings financed US$250 of K2 resource reservations; seed equity backed producer collateral. Prices, willingness to buy, resource prices and collection are authored assumptions. Actual customers and actual commercial revenue are zero.

## What actually ran

K1 archived two exact-content objects. The agent observed lost acknowledgements, shared-key suppression, unsupported keys and pending effects, then wrote `constructor.settle_intent`. The retained artifact includes applicability limits and the full development trace. K2 reserved a computation slot and published an independently recomputed integer-data product using an actually acknowledged reservation token. This changes both the operation graph and the accepted outcome; it is not another archival row.

The [public contract](CONTRACT.md), [preregistration](PREREGISTRATION.json) and [69-file source freeze](FROZEN_SOURCE.json) were published at [`599d9dda`](https://github.com/MontrealAI/AGI-Alpha-Invention-Flywheel-Lab/commit/599d9dda6b69a65c7b61366402e87c899cade5f5) before the first K2 execution. The primary comparison has 24 fixed tasks: three data instances across eight authored semantic families. Every artifact was executed against all 16 admitted first-write fault combinations, with pending effects drained after termination. Refusal never counts as delivery.

| Fresh K2 result | Retained constructor | Ordinary equal-content control |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Fully accepted tasks | 21/24 | 21/24 |
| Explicit refusals | 3 | 3 |
| Rejected non-refusal tasks | 0 | 0 |
| Maximum avoidable loss | 0 | 0 |

The paired primary effect is **zero additional accepted tasks**. The ordinary control also serves as the procedure-removal ablation: it has the same substantive K1 information and access to the retained primitive, but uses its conventional construction. Its implementation does not need the retained content to build these outputs. No K1 necessity, general invention advantage or inference about arbitrary live-service contracts follows.

The extra diagnostics total 51 resource units; both methods have the same summed worst-case executor units. This is a finite-grid resource comparison, not a probabilistic expected cost. The authored grid supports exact denominators and family results, not an industrial population confidence interval. K1 construction had already consumed 103 diagnostic units plus 272 verifier operation units; later reproduction costs are separately labeled. Actual model/host billing is unknown. Fixed native research/inference envelopes are assumptions, not measured complete bills or evidence of free intelligence.

## Conditional commerce and its failures

All seven scenarios use separate fresh canonical `JournalAccount`/adaptive `Account` histories. Within each scenario, K1 and K2 share one account. No balance was copied or topped up. The [economic plan](ECONOMIC_PLAN.json) immediately expenses construction, failed attempts, inference, acquisition, production, verification, support and maintenance; it charges explicit payment fees and an authored 20% positive-stage-margin tax. Capital and custody principal are not revenue or expenses.

| Native authored scenario | Opening equity | Collected/recognized revenue | Complete modeled expense | Profit | Result |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Positive demand | $2,000 | $2,200 | $1,120.32 | **$1,079.68** | Both deliveries accepted and collected |
| Weak demand / cheaper alternative | $2,000 | $0 | $500 | −$500 | Voluntary no-purchase |
| Doubled resource costs | $2,000 | $0 | $1,000 | −$1,000 | Collateral and production admission deferred |
| K1 accepted, uncollected | $2,000 | $0 | $600.20 | −$600.20 | K2 earned funding refused; obligation retained |
| No entry after research | $2,000 | $0 | $480 | −$480 | Keeps the sunk research expense |
| Underfunded reference | $1,000 | $0 | $500 | −$500 | Refuses an unfunded order |
| Larger capital reference | $100,000 | $2,200 | $1,120.32 | **$1,079.68** | Extra opening equity adds no profit here |

Positive-case cash and unrestricted available funds finish at **$3,079.68**. Revenue $2,200 minus resource envelopes $850, payment fees $0.40 and modeled tax $269.92 equals profit $1,079.68. The native journal records K1 profit **$319.84** before K2's EARNED reservations of **$110 and $140**. Original equity still backs $1,200 of K2 collateral during fulfillment. This proves earned-funded resources inside an equity-backed simulation, not a successor funded solely by earnings or otherwise impossible without K1.

The uncollected case retains $1,000 of custody protection. Its day-48 EARNED spend attempt returns no receipt and leaves the native history unchanged; the last committed event remains preparation at hour 1. It does not execute later default, refund, collection or recovery dynamics. Completed cases reopen with their original authority and unchanged public records. Automatic continuation of an interrupted native phase is unsupported and refuses rather than reseeding.

Read-only sensitivity illustrates fragility: if both resource envelopes rise proportionally, unchanged prices/tax/fees permit the strict earned-only K2 resource policy only up to approximately **9.57% higher costs**. This is arithmetic sensitivity, not another native experiment. A competent alternative may use seed capital; the strict policy tests provenance and is not an optimal-financing claim.

## Verification and scope

[Independent post-run review](INDEPENDENT_POSTRUN.md) blocked private-directory reads, regenerated constructors/probes, recomputed every artifact and replayed all **56 technical records and seven public native accounts**. [Validation](VALIDATION.json) records 21 focused exact-source tests, 25 legacy finance regressions, key-free replay and unchanged-account reopening. A development test run correctly rejected a concurrent source edit; that failure remains recorded and no guard was relaxed. [Raw compact evidence](evidence/primary/SUMMARY.json) and [replay receipt](evidence/primary/REPLAY.json) support the claims.

The evaluator, constructor, baseline and financial reviewer were separated procedurally on one host. This is not externally blinded experimentation. The numeric data, homogeneous tool contracts and diagnostic effect-count instrument are authored; none qualify a live archive, physical laboratory or arbitrary noisy service. The real included agent generated the code during K1; offline replay uses deterministic frozen code and contains no unattended frontier-model generator or model weight editing. Competent humans may use the same conventional compiler at machine speed; no measured human-only productivity advantage is claimed.

**Decision:** adopt the ordinary constructor as the stronger sandbox reference and stop funding this route as evidence of improved discovery. Retain the qualified primitive and its failure boundaries. Before selecting another flagship, investigate a source-grounded end-to-end outcome whose measured interface or acceptance constraint survives complete existing integration and self-building-lab alternatives. More retry variants or authored-demand multiplication would not establish that missing fact. No new industry, physical commissioning, real profit or US$3 billion trailing-year milestone is established. The larger mission remains open.

## Reproduce

From this repository's frozen source and existing pinned CPU dependencies:

```sh
python3 -m outlearning073.run check-source --root "$PWD"
python3 -m unittest tests.test_outlearning073_world tests.test_outlearning073_native tests.test_outlearning073_runner -v
python3 -m outlearning073.run execute --root "$PWD" --output /absolute/new/private-run
python3 -m outlearning073.run replay --root "$PWD" --output /absolute/new/private-run
```

To replay the published evidence without private account authority, copy `outlearning073/evidence/primary` to a new directory **outside** the source checkout, then call `replay` with that copy as `--output`. The published delivery files remain under `outlearning073/deliveries`. The dedicated read-only standard-runner workflow reproduces this fixed comparison; a reproduction is not a fresh undisclosed confirmation or a formal approval, merge or release.
