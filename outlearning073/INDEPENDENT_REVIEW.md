## Consequential findings and pre-freeze corrections

1. **Producer collateral was omitted from admission.** The initial $1,000 seed could not fund a $1,000 K1 order after $500 preoffer costs. The canonical prospective engine requires producer collateral in addition to remaining production funds. The revised primary uses explicitly hypothetical $2,000 equity and checks the price collateral, remaining production and payment fee before admission. Its K1 peak is $1,600.20; the $1,000 comparison remains a registered underfunded case. This is a prospective capital choice, not a top-up of an executed account.
2. **Settlement exceeded the native deadline.** Initial deadlines at hours 12/60 preceded collection at 24/72. The revised order deadlines match collection hours, which remain before maturity at 25/96. Actual artifact preparation is separately scheduled at hours 1/49. This correction changes no exposed confirmation outcome because none existed at review.
3. **An exposure allowance was mistaken for a paid fee.** The initial tax calculation deducted $0.20 per order without charging it. The revised terms explicitly declare a $0.20 fee, paid by the canonical offer operation. The existing principal-plus-fee exposure remains a protection, rather than an expense or income on release.
4. **A skipped successor was insufficient refusal evidence.** The delayed-collection case now attempts the same $250 K2 resource spend with seed fallback disabled and requires no funding receipt and unchanged account history. This distinguishes an actual funding guard from merely stopping the loop. Its result remains pending execution.

## Voluntary choice and limits

Each fixed offer is chosen only when its price fits the declared buyer budget and is strictly below the public modeled outside quote. A tie or cheaper alternative yields no purchase. These deterministic prices, budgets, quotes and their weak-demand change are hypotheses; they are not independently measured willingness to pay, a price optimum or empirical buyer behavior. No real order, collection, financing, human-only productivity result or physical output is established.

The two stages exhaust this finite operating experiment. No yearly profit, scalable acquisition channel, repeat-demand volume, new industry or $3 billion path follows from multiplying these margins. Resource categories are complete declared envelopes rather than measured complete bills; model/host billing and full human attention remain unknown. The same content and ordinary compiler can have the same terms/cost envelopes. Earned financing does not establish necessity or superiority of the retained discovery method.

## Artifact and source binding

The new schema is opt-in. Legacy routing remains unchanged. A job binds a validated request, frozen profile bytes and a unique declared delivery path. Native preparation binds the actual late-produced artifact digest. Independent financial replay reopens that delivery, checks its digest and reruns acceptance; precommitted request placeholders are not credited as delivered output. The manifest validates executing service-source hashes, while canonical financial source identity adds native/account dependencies. This is the appropriate seam for native composition rather than another monetary ledger.

At review, constructor/baseline/runner files were still being completed. The final freeze must include those executable files, every imported local dependency and request-profile hashes; preflight should demonstrate missing/changed source, request and prepared-artifact rejection. Source-matched native tests and independent public-export replay remain mandatory before a positive result. A final immutable manifest is not supplied by this static review.
