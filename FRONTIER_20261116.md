## Commercial execution gates, not simulated revenue

1. Obtain one voluntary **private-company** design partner per product, with authority to use customer inputs and no third-party IP violations. Do not contact anyone or purchase resources without separate scope/authority.
2. Freeze task, strongest complete alternative (formal methods/security review for PROOF; vendor/migration specialists and existing tools for RESURRECT), acceptance criteria, full costs, timeline and failure conditions **before** the benchmark.
3. Execute and independently reproduce a nontrivial customer-owned task. Require material functional advantage or an accepted capability previously unavailable *within the same real constraints*. Ordinary bounded toy results stay prototypes, not frontier wins.
4. Negotiate and **collect** an actual private-sector invoice for a qualified result. Model invoices and price suggestions are not collections. Calculate gross profit and complete-cost net profit including failed attempts, support, liability and compute, then validate repeated customers.
5. Different new customer workload must benefit from an inherited reusable asset, compared with the strongest readily accessible alternatives inclusive of transfer costs.

**Current verdict:** two functional local prototypes, not production deployments; no qualified real buyer, signed contract, revenue, matched specialist superiority, demonstrated November-1 impossibility, or recursive successor gain.
