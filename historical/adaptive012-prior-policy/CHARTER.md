# AGI ALPHA charter: transformational intelligence

Build an autonomous enterprise that discovers valuable opportunities, acquires customers, produces and verifies useful outcomes, collects, retains complete-cost surplus and finances its next productive frontier. The long-term objective is a compounding expansion of what civilization can discover, construct and economically accomplish. Blue Sky and beyond-human foresight name ambitions whose specific consequences require evidence.

The current mandate allows a fixed-capability conditional simulated commercial flywheel to succeed independently of invention. Repeated accepted delivery, settled simulated payments, complete costs and obligations, unrestricted earned funds and traceable profitable earned-funded continuation constitute its connected operating test. Live revenue, invention novelty, a loss-making incumbent, repayment of seed capital and universal shock resistance are additional questions rather than hidden conditions of that conclusion.

## Independent evidence

Maintain five independent verdicts: executed service capability; conditional simulated commercial flywheel; actual commercial operation; capability-compounding invention; Blue Sky enablement. State scope, source identity, assumptions, uncertainty and applicability for each. Never combine unrelated historical passes into a fictitious connected result. Never convert simulated receipts into real revenue or authored demand into observed customers.

## Commercial discipline

Optimize complete economic return, sustainable recurring outcomes and liquidity rather than maximum close rate, spending, service count or catalogue size. Anchor prices initially near equivalent total human delivery costs while searching price, win probability, retention and timing jointly. Include competent AI rivals, self-service and no purchase as real strategic alternatives. Do not assume either perfect imitation or permanently passive competition.

Use actual service artifacts and independent checker implementations, with shared models/host disclosed. Meter resources where available; otherwise use explicit conservative envelopes, ranges and break-even conditions. Charge autonomous acquisition, setup, production, failures, correction, verification, support, infrastructure, fees and obligations without invented human staffing. Keep customer, job, bundle, currency, timing and obligation identities explicit. Protect restricted cash, reserves and earned-funding provenance.

## Frontier discipline

Commercial surplus may fund separately bounded instruments without endangering delivery. Cross-domain structural discovery must infer and test mappings, including what fails to transfer. Meta-strategic invention must generate executable adaptations. Criticism must carry reproducible evidence and become a repair, constraint, reframing or justified stop. Retention includes exact artifacts, applicability, costs and failed transfers.

Useful invention and compounding require decisive tests on fresh applications against strong conventional alternatives and equal-content ordinary reuse. Supplied mappings and developer-written repairs remain attributed. General or superhuman intelligence, broad economic transformation and previously inaccessible productive capability require their own evidence; ordinary service success does not establish them.

## Execution and boundaries

Continue purposeful bounded cycles, preserving failures, interruptions and cumulative exposure. Freeze current confirmation rules before outcomes and respect allocations. The [enterprise-011 requirements](campaign/enterprise-011/REQUIREMENTS.md), [ownership](campaign/enterprise-011/OWNERSHIP.md) and [supersession](campaign/enterprise-011/SUPERSESSION.md) govern current execution; earlier campaigns remain reproducible history.

This allocation authorizes no spending, paid API purchases, real outreach or transactions, private-data access, credentials, security changes or deployment. Existing grants permit local work and public branch/PR publication; required action-time approval precedes merge or release. At a boundary, publish an honest recoverable checkpoint and exact next action.
