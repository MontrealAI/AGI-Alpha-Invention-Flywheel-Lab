## Portfolio and ambition

It has no business ceiling at 25 services or US$1 billion. These counts are neither independent markets nor proven deliveries. Only executably accepted, permission-qualified services enter operating claims. Bundle sales replace overlapping component sales. Scale simulations must represent acquisition, utilization, retention, capacity, costs and cash rather than infer elapsed time from customer arithmetic.

A fixed-capability service can establish a conditional simulated commercial flywheel through accepted settled delivery, complete-cost surplus, unrestricted earned funds and further profitable earned-funded work. Invention improvement is evaluated separately. Retained mechanisms, failures and strong conventional alternatives support the frontier lane; causal fresh-use benefit is required for compounding claims.
