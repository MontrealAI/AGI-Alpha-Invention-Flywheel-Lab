> **Intelligence that discovers what intelligence should make possible next.**

## Strategic thesis, not an achieved ranking

MONTREAL.AI should compete to become an institution that **discovers consequential new objectives, invents the missing productive and epistemic means, verifies results against the strongest complete alternatives, and inherits the capabilities that improve subsequent discovery**. This is a goal and a research program, not a claim that MONTREAL.AI is currently ranked first, has achieved open-ended autonomous invention, or has established an economic flywheel.

The essential distinction is *direction selection* rather than merely optimizing a fixed task. A direction comprises a beneficiary's valuable outcome, what makes it inaccessible, a transformable set of constraints, a candidate enabling construction, a discriminating experiment, a complete alternative, full resources, and a potential successor capability. The engine must be able to recommend **no new invention** when an incumbent solution wins.

## The eight mechanisms as one institutional engine

| Mechanism | Operating responsibility | Required output |
|---|---|---|
| Structural Transfer | Expose reusable mechanisms in remote fields | Explicit, testable mapping |
| Adversarial Discovery | Detect false premises, missing information and strong outside options | Counterexample or verification status |
| Strategic Invention | Consider materially different routes and institutions | New complete, feasible strategy hypothesis |
| Problem Transformation | Change removable assumptions and dependencies | New attainable problem or no-go certificate |
| Epistemic Invention | Construct missing observations or tests | Verified instrument and information gain |
| Self-Correcting Evidence | Challenge proposed solutions and their verifiers | Source-bound independent replay |
| Generative Capability Inheritance | Keep transferable productive means | Fresh successor test, net of costs |
| Recursive Economic Selection | Finance genuinely valuable progress | Actual accepted outcome, costs, obligations, optionality |

The mechanisms are **not eight mandatory pipeline stages**. They are reusable powers, applied when the problem requires them.

## Why start with epistemic direction?

October–November 2026 frontier research already demonstrates strong hypothesis generation and physical automation. But *Into the Unknown* shows substantial scientific-integrity shortcomings in unconstrained open research; *Sage-Taiyi* already explores metacognitive research-target adaptation. A new institution cannot claim novelty simply for selecting research directions. It must show **the selected direction makes a genuinely new, valuable outcome attainable**, with transparent methods and independent scientific/physical evidence.

The active AGI ALPHA Blue Sky 064 programme has already identified an interior-property observatory hypothesis. That hypothesis is blocked by the possibility that permitted observations do not identify the target property. This v0 kernel tests that **abstract obstruction**, not real-world EIT or elasticity.

## Executed conditional result — exact two-dimensional linear observation model

In a fully disclosed synthetic model, the target is the contrast `(θ₁-θ₂)/√2`, while the installed sensor observes the sum `(θ₁+θ₂)/√2` with Gaussian noise. The target is exactly orthogonal to the installed sensor; **no number of additional readings on the same channel reduces the target's prior variance**. The engine enumerates all within-budget measurement plans and can select an additional *hypothetical* complementary channel. This is a classic inverse-problem information principle, **not a discovered new physical measurement technology**.

Run `python omega_direction/engine.py --output /tmp/omega-direction.json`. Results in `omega_direction/RESULT.json` were produced locally with Python's standard library, with deterministic seed `20261116` and 4000 Monte Carlo draws.

| Authored world | Exact selected action | Posterior variance | Modeled utility |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Only repeat same sensor vs construct complementary sensor | Construct channel, take one reading | 0.038462 | +5 units |
| An existing complete supplier can provide the channel more cheaply | Buy supplier reading, **do not invent** | 0.038462 | +6 units |
| Missing construction financing | Do nothing | 1.0 | 0 |
| Property not valuable enough | Do nothing | 1.0 | 0 |
| Proposed new channel is identical to installed one | Do nothing | 1.0 | 0 |

All costs, budgets, values and acceptance thresholds are **illustrative dimensionless assumptions**; no customer, physical apparatus, revenue, market demand or funding is represented. An exact best plan in this finite game establishes no general optimum. The observation law, including the hypothetical new sensor, is assumed rather than discovered.

`python -m unittest discover -s omega_direction -p 'test_*.py' -v` executes the eight checks. No model API, account, secrets, network, GPU or external Python packages are required.

## Adversarial precursor: why optimizing a fixed measurement rule is not enough

A separate exploratory local active-measurement comparison attempted uncertainty, gradient-enhanced uncertainty, residual/surprise, integrated variance and integrated absolute-error criteria on **one** public synthetic function from Noack et al. and one illustrative step function (3 seeds, two budgets, fixed surrogate hyperparameters). The authors' full gpCAM task, 5 functions, 10 seeds and original SMART/gSMART reproduction were **not** completed. On the smooth field, the simpler uncertainty baseline outperformed several sophisticated heuristics; some alternatives were competitive on the step illustration. These findings are diagnostic and limited, not a new active-learning paper or a declared benchmark win.

That negative evidence motivates a **different testable direction**: perhaps the missing capability is a different *observation channel*, not another adjustment to where the existing channel measures. A toy nullspace certificate is a starting point for instrument design, not evidence that any new real sensor is possible or useful.

## What would constitute a consequential next achievement?

1. Freeze a *real* beneficiary outcome and accepted hidden physical property, including precision, safety, cost and reproducibility requirements.
2. Obtain public or authorized physical measurements; characterize whether the requested property is identifiable with **complete existing** instruments and pipelines.
3. Demonstrate an actual additional measurement operation or a demonstrably better complete arrangement. Compare matched budgets against conventional tomography, experiment design, specialized agents, renting equipment, and no purchase.
4. Reproduce the outcome independently on withheld physical samples, with uncertainty coverage, refusals, failures and complete time/financial accounting.
5. Retain only a rights-cleared verified instrument, then test whether a fresh *different* mission gains a net advantage. Record a negative result when it doesn't.
6. Separate **installed capability**, **inventor superiority**, **economic viability**, **successor advantage**, and **founder independence**; none implies another.

Before these gates, this is an **engineering proof of a decision mechanism** with an open science hypothesis, not a qualified Blue Sky flagship.

## Accountabilities and claim boundaries

This implementation is deliberately auditable, human-governed and narrow. It does not select from the unbounded universe of future goals, generate scientific hypotheses using a frontier model, buy equipment, control instruments, move funds, or connect to customer data. Those capabilities require authorized tools, adequate security, actual measurement access and domain-specific verification. The immediate expansion path is to implement source-grounded opportunity discovery and experimental construction **only** as evidence and infrastructure make it justified.
