## Executed check of the published local schema

Fetched and byte-pinned the Commission semantic archive. Verified the official public-battery conforming example with the supplied Draft 2020-12 schema, a local registry of its exact reference schemas, and format validation. An alternate numeric capacity with no supporting evidence also conformed. Replacing the number with a string failed at `batteryCapacity/value`. These are controlled schema-scope probes on the publisher's own fixture. They say nothing about SHACL logic, signed claims, upstream manufacturer support, another product, or overall correctness. [Executed receipt](DPP-SCHEMA-SCOPE.json)

The narrow hypothesis is that a purchaser of a preflight review values precise, repeatable dispositions over the raw source bytes for named product offers and revisions. Technical validators, linked evidence and commercial supply-chain verification all precede this proposal. Proving one more code path validates a JSON record does not establish that hypothesis. The matched task must preserve the complete alternatives and scope the advance to measured source-matching results or effort.
