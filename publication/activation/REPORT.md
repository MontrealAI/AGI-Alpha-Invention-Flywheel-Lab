## A real verification handoff

The operator-authored exact planner restored eight files from pinned public source and independently reran the prior timing verifier. Full archive extraction reproduced the same 56-case result, 89.0-second trained MAE and rejected model disposition. The selective route read **83,549 body bytes** in eight requests. The full archive read **346,623 body bytes** in one request. This is a **75.9% reduction in transferred body bytes** for one real task. Serial selective transfer took 24.40 seconds against 4.47 seconds for the full archive. With eight concurrent reads, the retrieval times were 4.34 and 3.01 seconds. Network conditions and cache effects limit interpretation. The selective route is usable where bandwidth is decisive. The full archive remains the low-request alternative. This is a conventional engineering result with no inferred revenue or inventive superiority.

A second parallel pair ran the full archive first and timed planning, acquisition, reassembly and execution together. It took 3.780 seconds for the archive and 4.046 seconds for selective recovery. The full and selective verifier outputs matched exactly. The [raw transfer receipts](live-replay.json) preserve all three pairs.
