# De-novo structural elucidation: primary demand and complete-route cost notes

Research observed 30 October 2026. This is the new investigation of identification beyond a spectral library. It does not admit a commercial cohort, extend the previous compatibility receipt, or price a successful chemical identity from the cost of an ordinary LC-MS/MS sample. No quote, sample submission, customer contact, payment or physical experiment was initiated.

The beneficiary is an analytical chemistry team handling unexpected reaction products, manufacturing impurities or natural products without a reference-library identity. The useful outcome is a defensible structural assignment, with its ambiguities and applicability stated, supported by admissible original measurements. Enumerating 89 allowed amine graphs or ranking their MS fragments is a supporting step; neither alone is that delivery. Current evidence establishes that full services are sold and needed. It does not establish that competent complete methods fail or that a software instrument opens an otherwise unavailable class of work.

The strongest complete route must therefore include suitable separation or isolation, physical MS/NMR and any necessary standards, competent interpretation, checks of competing structures, uncertainty and a report. Acquisition-only tariffs, cached snippets and unrelated sample prices cannot substitute for this route's full quote or observed cost. The opened NorrChemica schedule differs from cached search excerpts; the numbers above follow the opened page, not the older snippets. The Singapore Phenome Centre pricing URL returned 404 on direct opening, so its cached large 2D-NMR price is excluded as a usable current bound.

An authentic physical-data continuation is available without a new acquisition: the Oregon State page links a 76.95 MB menthol NMR archive. This may exercise a multimodal interpretation baseline, but it is a different molecule and does not validate the proposed 89-amine population. Data identity, license, purity, structure labels and transferable measurement assumptions still need qualification before use.

For the current construction, absent direct price/participation evidence, AGI ALPHA's capture remains conditional. A candidate may create value by reducing necessary experiments, sample consumption, unresolved structures, turnaround or expert work, but must measure the complete effect against the physical and software combinations above. No arbitrary charge per fragment, graph, collision energy or nominal sample becomes earned income. Failed identifications and residual responsibilities stay in the same prospectively admitted population. No loss-making incumbent cohort, positive complete-cost threshold crossing, captured revenue or earned-funded successor is established by these sources.
