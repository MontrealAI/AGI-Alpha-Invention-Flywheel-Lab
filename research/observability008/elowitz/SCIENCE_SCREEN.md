## Executed construction

`gauge_probe.py` parses the actual SBML initial assignments and sixteen kinetic laws through a fixed MathML operator interpreter. It constructs exact rational homogeneity constraints, derives constant-exponent scaling directions, creates a measurement plan and executes the source model numerically with SciPy LSODA. The Hill exponent stays fixed under scaling; the linear coefficient in that exponent is checked exactly at two distinct values. This is a scaling-symmetry diagnostic, not a general structural-identifiability algorithm.

The source is the PEtab benchmark collection, revision `c44d5ede9bf979db5aa8ae4e94fc31fba9776675`, `problems/Elowitz_Nature2000/v1`. It has eight model states, twenty-one estimated parameters and fifty-eight recorded fluorescence measurements. The custom numerical interpreter is independently checked against the collection's published `simulations.tsv`. Maximum absolute discrepancy is approximately `3.8541e-6` against the prospectively coded `1e-5` numerical-reproduction tolerance. This validates the digital implementation in this scope, not the physical model.

## Reproduce and integrate

```sh
OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 timeout 180 python3 gauge_probe.py --output /tmp/alpha-gauge-NEW
```

The output directory must not exist. The command records raw trajectories, exact source/code hashes, numerical validation, complete witness limits and `model_constraints.json`.
