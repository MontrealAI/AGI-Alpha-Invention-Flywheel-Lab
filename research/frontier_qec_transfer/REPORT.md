# FRONTIER Ω — A qualified two-capability research chain

**21 November 2026 · Computational research experiment · Draft integration**

Two distinct executable capabilities now work: a general sparse recovery instrument for valid quantum codes, and a constructor for measurements that identify available noise information while preserving the logical state. The second capability actually invokes the retained first capability. All six paired second-stage constructions and the ordinary-reuse control passed the common independent qualification.

The construction-time result is mixed: retaining the first source was faster in two pairs; the third was essentially tied and slightly favored construction without it. The average recorded reduction was about **50.6 seconds**. This does **not** repay the first capability's recorded **348.8-second dispatch-to-freeze interval** when acquiring that capability solely to save work on one successor. Research, verification, integration and unknown inference costs remain additional.

This is a useful computational research result with a demonstrated functional dependency and bounded descriptive transfer evidence. It is not a completed acquisition-inclusive economic-compounding proof.

## 1. The outcome made usable

MONTREAL.AI's existing `inventions/phase_codes/runtime_v2.py` explicitly supports an orthogonal-error-sector, zero-moment subclass. Valid quantum codes can fall outside that subclass. Simply removing the refusal would not construct a correct recovery channel.

The new `Recovery` tool constructs that channel. It accepts sparse complex codewords, validates orthonormality and the single-qubit Knill–Laflamme conditions, handles overlapping and degenerate error spaces, and exports executable recovery operators. Its output retains raw probability mass and reports the uncorrectable complement as erasure. It does not hide failure through postselection or normalization.

The independent verifier checked six cohorts: the existing fifteen-qubit controlled-S artifact, a published seven-qubit Pollatsek–Ruskai code, the nine-qubit Shor code, and fresh complex variants. It checked full-span operator certificates, arbitrary tested local channels, logical-state tomography, subnormalized inputs, branch splitting, erasure and invalid witnesses. Both the first candidate and an independently constructed established-method alternative passed **824/824 checks**, without private-feedback repair.

That establishes available executable computational functionality on the evaluated finite domain. The independently frozen cohort reaches fifteen qubits and logical dimensions two and four. The constructor also reported a twenty-qubit development case; it is not part of the original 824-check independent cohort. Supplemental later review remains separately identified.

The independent conventional success matters. Established sparse recovery mathematics already supplies a capable route. This experiment does not show a new quantum-error-correction theorem, a world-first recovery algorithm, or superiority over competent conventional recovery.

## 2. The different successor capability

The second objective is information gathering: construct an executable measurement instrument, its observation model, the maximally identifiable noise-parameter subspace, and an estimator. The input is a finite library of known local CPTP noise hypotheses with unknown mixture weights. Every supported measurement branch must preserve the arbitrary unknown logical state.

The successor uses recovery operators as a syndrome/logical factorization, then constructs an informationally complete measurement on the syndrome. It fits a feasible mixture representative. When physical degeneracy makes some weights indistinguishable, it reports the identifiable quotient rather than inventing a unique answer.

This requires new executable means. Returning another recovered state or evaluating the same calculation on new inputs is insufficient. A source-bound call trace verifies that the selected successor actually called the unchanged first asset's `Recovery.__init__` and `Recovery.kraus` during construction of a measurement experiment.

A concrete descriptive witness shows the difference. For the recorded first asset's Kraus basis, two distinct valid seven-qubit noise mixtures produce indistinguishable original readout probabilities. The selected successor separates them and reconstructs both mixtures from ideal probabilities to within numerical tolerance. Its readout has rank 22, compared with rank 21 for that recorded original readout. Rank includes normalization: all 22 mixture probabilities can be inferred subject to their sum-one constraint; this is not 22 independent free parameters.

The 21-to-22 example is specific to the frozen source, Kraus basis and numerical environment. It is not an impossibility theorem about all conventional recovery measurements. Other recovery bases can behave differently. The general second contract requires all physically available information for its supplied hypotheses, independently of this descriptive witness.

The selected implementation uses **484 registered measurement outcomes** on the public seven- and nine-qubit fixtures. A separate qualified retained implementation uses 946. The selected source had a lower recorded qualification memory footprint and fewer source lines. Both remain available. Fewer outcomes and response-matrix singular values alone do not prove better finite-shot efficiency, optimal experimental design or cheap hardware realization.

## 3. What was frozen before construction

The local comparison protocol, common task, public inputs, source assignments and launch order were frozen before second-stage construction. An independent evaluator froze the hidden inputs and scientific checker before the first submitted second-stage source. A public GitHub checkpoint followed dispatch and preceded the first final second-source freeze; it was not a public preregistration before dispatch.

Three fresh-context pairs used the same configured included-agent route, objective, fixtures, permitted scientific stack, public-research access and acceptance. The only planned information difference was the qualified first source and its API description. The no-asset arms could reconstruct any necessary means themselves. Launch order alternated. All three pairs ran regardless of earlier outcomes, and no private results reached later constructors.

Each constructor was allowed one initial source and at most two public-development repairs. All six paired sources passed on their initial implementation. Shared-host blinding was procedural, not OS-enforced. Exact model weights, inference calls, tokens and sampling parameters were not exposed; identical configured access is not verified deterministic backend identity.

Each source was frozen before qualification. The independent evaluator checked the physical operators directly, compared identifiability with the density operators' available information, and checked ideal inference. A separate execution wrapper bound the entry file, retained dependencies and actually loaded local modules before and after execution. The scientific checker did not inspect candidate source as prose. A later code reviewer separately inspected source and evidence.

## 4. The paired construction result

Each entry below passed **163/163 primary second-stage checks** on the common six-cohort qualification. The time is the constructor-recorded start-to-freeze interval, including its chosen public development and documentation work. It is not exclusive inference time, normalized coding effort or complete economic cost.

| Pair | With retained capability | Without retained capability | Observed difference |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 368.4 s | 479.4 s | 111.0 s lower; 23.15% |
| 2 | 344.0 s | 386.7 s | 42.7 s lower; 11.05% |
| 3 | 349.2 s | 347.3 s | 1.9 s higher; 0.54% |
| Mean | 353.9 s | 404.5 s | 50.6 s lower; 12.51% of the control mean |

The median paired reduction was 42.7 seconds. Two of three paired intervals favored retention. These are repeated constructions of the **same** second objective, not three new inventions or three productive portfolio assets. The sample is small and elapsed intervals include orchestration and scheduling. The results support a bounded observation; they do not establish a reliable general speedup, a population causal effect, or an improved inventor.

The study preserves the third pair and the successful controls. A comparison based only on the favorable first pair would overstate the evidence.

### Ordinary reuse control

Its recorded construction interval was **373.638374 seconds**, with zero source repairs. Its task and access were otherwise unchanged. This is one successful ordinary-reuse run, not a separate multi-run significance test. It demonstrates a capable conventional reusable route; it is functionally comparable, not byte-identical to the selected first source. The frozen protocol's imprecise “equal-content” wording is clarified in a separately dated record without changing the arm.

## 5. Costs and the economic boundary

The first candidate's constructor interval was **320.233835 seconds**. Its earlier recorded dispatch gives a **348.773488-second dispatch-to-freeze interval**, including approximately 28.54 seconds before the constructor's own start record. This remains only a measured acquisition interval, not total acquisition cost. Prior opportunity research and protocol preparation are additional.

The three marginal second-task differences—110.98, 42.72 and minus 1.87 seconds—are each smaller than that first acquisition interval. On these observed intervals, acquiring the first tool solely to save construction time on one successor does not repay its acquisition. Verification, packaging, maintenance and unknown inference costs do not disappear from that comparison.

If both first and second outcomes are independently required, the first output also has productive value, which this timing subtraction does not price. The three-pair ablation helps examine retention after first construction; it does not substitute for valuing the first outcome or comparing complete commercially available routes. No monetary value is assigned to research output without evidence.

The full study also incurred independent conventional construction, repeated experimental constructions, source research, evaluator development, review, integration and reproduction. Those repetitions are research costs. They cannot be counted as three productive successor revenues or used to manufacture portfolio amortization.

Two included-CLI diagnostic attempts failed without a completed result and were preserved; backend work and cost remain unknown. Several fresh constructors independently encountered an unavailable timing utility before scientific Python executed; their successful measurement wrappers and failures remain recorded. A later packaging-copy failure and a reproduction-path correction are separate integration records. No failure was converted into a successful construction by omitting it.

Model-host compute, token usage, actual monetary cost, maintenance obligations and a measured human-only comparator remain unknown or unrun. Included execution does not establish zero economic cost. There are no customer sales, receipts, funded expansion or profit in this experiment.

## 6. Statistical and physical scope

Each qualified successor is also run on 18 fresh seeded million-observation multinomial trials. These are simulated counts. Their finite-sample errors are recorded descriptively and did not change the primary pass criterion. They are not universal error guarantees, experimental hardware shots or evidence of optimal sample complexity.

The package's scientific output is a mathematical measurement instrument. No physical circuit has been compiled, installed or characterized. Classical CPU execution does not demonstrate quantum advantage, physical fault tolerance or deployable quantum-device economics.

The first constructor advertises a bounded sparse numerical domain up to twenty physical qubits; independent original qualification covers its stated six cohorts up to fifteen. The second contract is a finite domain up to nine physical qubits, a two-dimensional logical code, and up to 32 known local channel hypotheses. Double-precision rank decisions assume well-separated relevant spectra. No out-of-model noise-identification guarantee is made.

## 7. What is retained and how to use it

The operator entry points are `recovery.py` and `noise_experiment.py`. The selected successor is the unchanged qualified `pair2_retained` source. All other constructions, their source identities, original timing records, executed dependency bindings and independent qualifications remain under `constructions/`.

`demo.py` executes both capabilities on inspectable examples and reports source hashes. It generates noise observations by applying the exported physical measurement operators, independently of their predicted response matrix. `reproduce.py --all-arms` replays every archived scientific construction under fresh processes, one BLAS thread and a 10 GiB address-space ceiling. It checks the source/input manifest before and after replay. This reproduces scientific qualifications; it does not rerun AI construction or reproduce original latency.

The experiment used the available included-agent route and ordinary scientific Python. It was not a successful run of the HARNESS local-model mission endpoint and was not executed inside the smaller Capability Forge sandbox. No local-model identity or sandbox attestation was fabricated.

The original phase-code instrument and pinned historical artifact remain unchanged. The work is published through a draft branch and PR stacked on #520. Production integration, merge, release and operation of a released HARNESS remain separate statuses.

## Résultat en français

Deux capacités de recherche fonctionnent et ont été qualifiées indépendamment : la correction pour des codes quantiques valides aux espaces d'erreur plus généraux, puis la construction d'une expérience qui identifie le bruit accessible sans détruire l'information logique. Le second outil utilise réellement les opérateurs du premier.

Dans trois comparaisons appariées, la conservation du premier outil réduit le temps de construction observé dans deux cas; le troisième est pratiquement à égalité et favorise légèrement le témoin. Le gain moyen observé est de **50,6 secondes**. Il ne rembourse pas les **348,8 secondes** mesurées entre le lancement et le gel du premier outil, sans compter la recherche, la vérification et les coûts d'inférence inconnus.

Les outils et le transfert fonctionnel sont démontrés dans le domaine informatique déclaré. L'avantage économique complet, la rentabilité, l'exploitation d'un matériel quantique et l'amélioration de l'inventeur ne sont pas établis. Les résultats moins favorables et les solutions conventionnelles réussies sont conservés.
