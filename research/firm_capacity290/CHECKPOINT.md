## Productive arrangement and economic obstacle

A compute buyer could receive completed portable work earlier than otherwise possible by voluntarily contracting with an operator using conditional electricity access and a separately qualified firm-capacity provider. The new arrangement would sell accepted compute outcomes, not energy or capacity reservations it does not own. Source: [FERC June 5 2026 order ER26-1323, pp. 5, 7, 37](https://spp.org/documents/76880/20260605_order%20-%20revisions%20to%20add%20the%20conditional%20high%20impact%20large%20load%20service_er26-1323.pdf), effective July 1 subject to condition. The service is nonfirm; disconnection is possible, and the order describes exclusion from demand-response participation. Do not book demand-response payments or assume guaranteed supply. The [SPP November 17 planning update](https://www.spp.org/engineering/transmission-planning/integrated-transmission-planning/) and [FERC October cost-transparency guide](https://www.ferc.gov/cost-transparency-customer-protection) support the broad infrastructure constraint, not paid demand for our arrangement.

Strong alternatives: [Pearl Street Interconnect](https://pearlstreettechnologies.com/project-developers/) and [LBNL's 2,104-study dataset](https://eta.lbl.gov/publications/generator-interconnection-costs-0) for interconnection decision intelligence; [HiPO](https://openenergybenchmark.org/blog/hipo_study) and [TransitionZero's November 18 announced December 11 release](https://forum.openmod.org/t/webinar-invite-launching-11-dec-2026-conductor-and-tz-highs/6178) for solver acceleration; [EPRI Flex MOSAIC](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/03/23/3260463/0/en/EPRI-Launches-Flex-MOSAIC-to-Reduce-Time-to-Power-for-Data-Centers.html) and [Beyond Scalar Flexibility](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05406) for workload-dependent load-relief characterization. The latter already studies much stronger production traces and duration-dependent relief; no novelty or superiority over it is claimed. Non-entry remains available.
