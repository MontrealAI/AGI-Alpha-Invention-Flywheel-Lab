## Outcome and alternatives

The beneficiary proxy is one correct categorical decision among circle / square / triangle PETG shapes from **already recorded** experimental saline-tank electrical-impedance observations. This is not a new commercial buyer requirement, physical-property ground truth, independent fresh specimen, validated sensor or finished service. A 2026 SBrT study already reports roughly 97% shape test accuracy on experimental 16-electrode PETG measurements, with random forest **comparable** to its deep model (https://biblioteca.sbrt.org.br/articles/5249, DOI 10.14209/sbrt.2026.1571299951). That is a **strong existing incumbent**, not a result we can claim or assume under our different split.

Preserve four materially distinct choices: full-data conventional 16-pattern random forest and stronger unrestricted full-data extra trees; the same random forest restricted to **eight** fixed ordered injections; restricted to **four** fixed ordered injections; and no inference/no-deploy. A human direct visual shape check and professional EIT service are legitimate outside choices but have no cost/acceptance measurement in this public CSV and therefore prohibit any economic adoption claim. Do not portray a technical tie as a new invention. Choosing among these technical policies is not a negotiated commercial arrangement.

## Sources and prospective decision

Dataset: Thönes, Krukewitt, Spors, Zenodo 7192883 **v1**, CC BY 4.0, January 27 2023. The three original distinct files (all official upstream MD5, not hashes invented here):
- `circle_petg.csv`: `262a968766cd034914155ac2bfcd121e` (prior independently audited SHA256 `2e6b357e41b8b69bca8e23ce7a8b924677bae80ecab34da8830a4edada4ab62f`, 9,232,257 bytes).
- `square_petg.csv`: `bbdccac3f0ffb63cb0c1cd8b045fba9c`.
- `triangle_petg.csv`: `6d22c805a62e1017b841ab311a775156`.

Bound each HTTP download to <=20 MB, timeout 90 seconds; maximum 250,000 rows/file. Exactly one retrieval attempt per file, no substituted datasets. Source SHA256 of all files is computed/recorded after retrieval; only the circle SHA256 above was known independently before this freeze. Refuse changed MD5, malformed rows, duplicate/missing headers, nonfinite values, invalid injection pairs, missing/extra acquisition patterns, specimen-group collisions and absent class. No interpolation, synthesized voltages, imputation or missing truth; the 12 genuinely finite voltages per row are the only numerical features. Distinguish literal `NA` and blank from measurements. All 16 opposite-electrode ordered injection pairs must be present per group with one row each, else fail closed; this is a **data representation** condition, not proof of equipment timing or electrode grounding.

Experimental unit: a complete 16-row `(shape, material, r, phi, meas_num)` group, **not** each voltage row. Withhold all raw groups that share the same literal `(r, phi)` across all three shape files. Partition before fitting using first 64 bits of `SHA256("eit108|" + r + "|" + phi)`, modulo 5: group fold zero held out, all others training. No reseeding/splitting on results. The split protects exact stored location strings, not repeated correlated nearby positions, and cannot create independently acquired physical objects. If inadequate data in an arm or fewer than 20 unique position groups per class per side, fail and retain.

Fixed acquisition policies: 4-pair `00-08,02-10,04-12,06-14`; 8-pair `00-08,01-09,...,07-15`; full16 `00-08,...,15-07`. A selected pair's 12 measured E channels, in electrode order, form features. Excluded cells never become fabricated zero volts. Fewer selected archived rows are **not yet fewer physical injections**, reduced acquisition time or a saving on a real device.

Model: Python 3.12, scikit-learn 1.7.2, NumPy 2.3.5; random forest 200 trees, seed 108, leaf minimum 2, balanced subsample, one CPU job, other defaults. Stronger full-data `ExtraTreesClassifier`: 400 trees, seed 108, minimum leaf 2, balanced subsample, one CPU job. No tuning on holdout. Train each on identical training units, evaluate each on identical heldout units and exact same labels. Report accuracy, macro recall, per-class recall, all confusion cells, counts, failure cases, actual CPU/wall and both source and execution SHA. Never claim calibrated generalization or a 2026 paper's score was replicated.

**Precommitted screening only:** a sparse arm is a numerical candidate if it achieves heldout accuracy >=0.95 and is within 0.02 absolute of the **better of the two full-data incumbents** and per-class recall >=0.90.
