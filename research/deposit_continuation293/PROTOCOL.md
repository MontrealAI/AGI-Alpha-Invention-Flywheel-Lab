All cases are authored finite design cases, NOT measured DSpace executions. Model only one in-flight client attempt, no concurrent outside writers, no future delayed commit after a conclusive absence, exact authoritative query with payload binding, and eventual successful retried request or local link. Compare blind retry, fail closed, conventional manual authoritative reconciliation and a conventional atomic server idempotency key (same key/payload). Candidate is automated authoritative reconciliation; it must hold on unavailable, ambiguous, duplicate, or mismatched evidence. Local link is assumed idempotent, not implemented in VIVO/ArchivesSpace. Atomic server control requires an interface not established for inspected endpoint. No proof of novelty or advantage against manual/atomic incumbent.

Enumerate the seven fixed cases and five policies in probe.mjs, 35 results. For each: no added duplicate, no wrong link, completion only if exactly one remote deposit and correct local link. Require candidate safety on all seven and completion on the three determinate states (absent, present, known receipt). Require blind retry counterexample, no universally completing safe observation-blind policy for indistinguishable commit/no-commit, parity of candidate and manual decisions, report all results including atomic hypothetical control; no comparative advantage if tie. Stop after one complete execution; no seed tuning. No native ledger, paid calls or assumed buyer. Resource telemetry only; actual billing unknown.
