## Consequential missing means

The earlier IMPACT229 scopes which registered systems are affected when requirements change. It only accepts one systems inventory. If a system acquires a new AI decision-making tag or loses evidence while the directive is unchanged, the policy-only instrument correctly reports no requirement-driven reassessments. That is not permission to interpret the zero as general continuous governance clearance.

BIDIRECTIONAL230 compares both old and new requirement snapshots AND both old and new registered-system snapshots. A review is queued when either member of a system×requirement pair changes and the requirement applies on either side. Additions, retirements, changed applicability, control evidence removal and source-hash substitution are inspected. Both old/new evidence references are shown. Results make no compliance, legal or source-authentication claims.

The old tool remains unchanged as an upstream dependency. A competent exhaustive conventional O(requirements×systems) script is an independent correctness comparator and is allowed to tie. No new algorithmic superiority is claimed.
