## Commercial decision: NO ENTRY / NO DEPLOY

The productive hypothesis was economically bounded delegation of accepted outcomes across independently published agents. Visitor-funded composition alone shifts inference exposure rather than removing it. A budget-reserving inference gateway is already offered by [LiteLLM](https://docs.litellm.ai/docs/proxy/users), including documented pre-dispatch reservations, actual-cost reconciliation and optional fail-closed spend verification; [OpenRouter](https://github.com/OpenRouterTeam/docs/blob/main/api_reference/limits.mdx) documents per-key limits and in-flight estimates. These are documented competitors, not independently exercised or certified. A publisher-funded fixed-price accepted outcome would require actual independent acceptance, enforceable maximum charges across tool/model routes, reconciliation, correction, support and liability cost, and a voluntary buyer; none is established. No-entry is preferred to building a duplicate router or declaring a profitable arrangement. Existing public ecosystem directory lists scope enforcement, workflow composition and trust products; no residual advantage shown. No forced use of paid APIs, no new credentials or capital arrangements.
