## Additional mathematically decisive contradiction
[Public committed #0 contract](https://elgora.ai/bounties/0), commitment displayed 0x687631bda07cfbdb6558833aa8d46874e2cd4d5f6bfabbd85c6e5c363a087593 (platform reports match; exact Markdown bytes not independently hashed here). The strong Robin law uses outward flux q=k du/dn=r(u-s). Integration by parts gives bulk a(u,v) = bulk L(v) + integral q v, thus the strong Robin law requires subtracting the ruv term on the left and subtracting the rsv term on the right. The posted weak form instead adds both terms. Its implied flux is q=r(s-u). In V3 at either horizontal boundary midpoint the manufactured outward flux is -pi, the posted strong law gives -pi, and the posted weak law implies +pi. Strong and weak contracts cannot both accept that exact solution.

Counterexample to the blanket uniqueness condition: on the unit square take k=r=1, alpha=f=s=0, Dirichlet zero at x=0, Neumann zero on y=0 and y=1, Robin at x=1. Both u=0 and u=x satisfy the strong law; u=x has bulk and boundary residuals zero, yet against v=x the posted weak residual is 2 (correct sign yields zero). This does not show all well-posed strong-law instances fail, nor that this contradiction caused historical rejection. It does show a concrete source-specification inconsistency independent of any numerical model. Conventional mathematical review suffices to detect it. No general autonomous invention advantage.
