## Why this independent work

The 064 mixed-microcontact programme's model uncertainty defeats its prescribed static/dynamic target; even the 25%-of-original hypothetical calibration intervals cannot certify it. Rather than continue adjusting exposed geometries or claiming an imaginary exact measurement, inspect a **different physical operating mechanism**: the published collective magnetic-rotor system, which changes friction through internal magnet dynamics without a solid-solid contact.

This is a potential new **means** of a useful wear-resistant friction function, not evidence that a static holder and a dynamic slider already coexist or that a measured beneficiary values it. Prior art: Hongri Gu, Anton Lüders, Clemens Bechinger, *Non-monotonic magnetic friction from collective rotor dynamics*, Nature Materials 25, 767–774 (published 18 March 2026), DOI https://doi.org/10.1038/s41563-026-02538-1; authors' public data at https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18487116 (17 March 2026). This study **does not claim** the rotor route dominates those alternatives, conventional magnetic clutches, brakes, bearings, or no-purchase.
