(1) All impedance test data within the folder comprehensively covers the test conditions listed in Table S6. However, time-domain test data at 0.4 A/cm² under varying air humidity and coolant temperature conditions were not utilized for impedance spectrum prediction due to incomplete recording.

(2) The numerical identifiers (Num) in folder names correspond to the test numbers specified in Table S6.

(3) The folder naming convention follows these rules:

"400_RH_20a" denotes impedance spectrum testing at 20% air humidity and 400 mA/cm² current density;

"400_Air_1_9a" represents impedance measurements at an air stoichiometric ratio of 1.9 and 400 mA/cm² current density;

"800_cT_72c" indicates impedance testing at a coolant temperature of 72°C and 400 mA/cm² current density;

"400_pre_55a" refers to impedance spectrum measurements under an air inlet pressure of 55 kPa and 400 mA/cm² current density.
Other folder names follow an analogous convention.
