## 1. Flexible grid capacity without expensive new lines

**Consequential activity and users:** electricity distribution networks admitting more rooftop solar, batteries and flexible demand while preserving voltage, thermal, protection and service constraints. The causal possibility was to replace blanket fixed export caps with demonstrably safe time-varying access, then remove dependence on complete network models through smart-meter inference.

**Strong current route:** dynamic operating envelopes with network-aware controls and CSIP-AUS communication already have field deployment. ARENA's evolve project reports greater exports within network limits. Sandia's 2023 model-free hosting-capacity work used over 2,700 customer locations and reported less than 0.30 kW MAE against model-based estimates. These are stronger controls than a fixed cap or manual study.

**Opening falsifier:** a restriction to fixed caps, or a claim that no topology-free route exists, would withhold competent ordinary methods. More recent robust work also covers the full envelope under AC equations (de Carvalho et al. 2026), and partial coordination, fairness and bounded forecast uncertainty (Jalilian et al. 2026). A program that only implements those known features has not earned this campaign's enabling gap.

**Decision:** INVESTIGATED / NOT ADMITTED. No complete-service cost gap or important obligation left unmet by those methods was established. Transmission/distribution operating permissions, protection qualification, utility adoption and mandatory utility studies cannot be assumed away. No new grid optimization fixture was executed or relabeled as invention.

**Cheapest further discriminating action if revisited:** obtain an independently specified feeder obligation that the full nonlinear robust route cannot meet at its actual compute/instrumentation cost, then execute that exact method first. The IEEE 13-node reference feeder is accessible through the GitHub connector, but it is a reference engineering model, not lawful proof of utility operations or customer demand.

## 2. A pressure-robust thermal measurement for enhanced geothermal systems

**Consequential activity and users:** EGS developers and reservoir-test operators need to measure heat extraction and early thermal breakthrough to characterize effective flow-carrying area and reservoir lifetime. An economically useful first offering would be a qualified circulation-test design and interpretation service that produces a decision-grade thermal result and refuses non-identifiable cases.

**Genuine published defect:** the 2025 EGS Collab closeout still reports that thermal breakthrough was not conclusively established; pressure-dependent Joule–Thomson heating, changing flow pathways and inaccessible weep locations can mask cooling. This is a measurement failure, not an invitation to predict a better score. DOE separately identifies resource-characterization cost and uncertainty as barriers to project finance and development.

**Strong current route:** calibrated full thermohydraulic modelling including convection, conduction and JT effects (Wu et al. 2021), joint conservative/sorptive tracer assimilation (Wu et al. 2021), pressure/flow measurements, and better positioned temperature/pressure instruments. Fu et al. 2020 already transferred leakoff-dominated fracture-growth mathematics to infer flow-channel width from arrival times. Merely adding a JT correction, tracer inference or a hydraulic analogy is ordinary reuse.

**Proposed causal lever, supplied by this assistant role:** replace raw well-temperature trend interpretation with a pressure-conditioned differential thermal measurement. Explicitly certify whether thermal change is identifiable under measured local pressure-drop and heat-transfer uncertainty; if not, construct the missing measurement specification or orthogonal temperature-excitation protocol before a confirmation test. A future implementation could transfer nuisance rejection from differential calorimetry/lock-in measurement. That transfer is proposed, not demonstrated here.

**Executed construction:** `thermal_observability.py` builds a bounded local JT/thermal-change inversion and emits a pressure-precision requirement. Its criticism catches an unjustified tempting shortcut: applying the entire injection-to-production pressure difference to a local isenthalpic formula. The source's coupled scenario has a 2.8 MPa pressure difference and about 0.3 C production-well warming; the local relation would imply 0.60928 C if applied to the entire difference. This is not a validated calibration discrepancy: the domains differ and the substitution itself is refused. The repaired input contract requires the local pressure drop, thermometry error, water-property validity and bounds on mixing/conduction. Synthetic arithmetic witnesses remain explicitly labelled.

**Probe outcome:** the ordinary interval-arithmetic baseline receives the same relation and returns exactly the same intervals. Technical enabling advantage, invention novelty and complete-cost profitability are NOT ESTABLISHED. The computed conditional precision requirement is not a sensor procurement specification or field qualification. No thermal breakthrough or physical feasibility is claimed.

**Second construction and exhaustive experiment:** `excitation_probe.py` executes a source-informed, fixed-flow channel model with fluid advection, heat conduction into rock and a local JT pressure nuisance, then synthesizes inlet-temperature procedures from all 64 six-segment binary sequences. It independently evaluates raw separation, nuisance-projected worst-pair separation, ordinary D-optimal design and ordinary robust experiment design. `ALL64.json` retains every sequence and score. Raw search, repaired search and ordinary robust OED all choose the same continuously cold procedure (`111111`). The robust score is 1.9652133210 C in the declared vector-norm metric; D-optimal chooses `111000` with 1.4068109422 C under that metric. A direct robust OED baseline ties the candidate. Modulation has not earned a useful gap; no extra temperature/energy constraint was added to force it to win.

The criticism generates an executable `NUISANCE_IDENTIFIABILITY` constraint and tests an expanded-nuisance counterexample. If a nuisance is allowed to exactly reproduce the thermal response, its projected distinguishability collapses to numerical zero and the claim must be refused. The constraint changes the scoring call path but does not change the selected design in this source-informed setting. That is a guard and a scoped limitation, not evidence of a better invention strategy.

The reduced analytical kernel was checked against an independently implemented implicit fluid/rock finite-volume solution. Grid refinement gives RMSE 0.16452, 0.08224 and 0.04111 C at successively doubled spatial and temporal resolution; all original records are retained. This is numerical consistency of a rectangular-channel reduction, not field calibration. It does not reproduce Wu2021's full 3D fracture network or qualify TOUGH/STOMP. Those remain unexecuted stronger service comparators. Localized pressure drop, changing flow paths, mixing, sensor calibration and temperature-dependent fluid properties remain unvalidated. The total recorded physics-probe compute is below 35 CPU-seconds, within the parent's 180 CPU-second exploratory ceiling.

**Independent measurement and cost evidence:** the EGS Collab 2020 field account reports water ingress and swollen epoxy in the original thermistors, requiring redesign, recalibration and replacement. GDR1254 separately warns that some channel assignments changed. Consequently the arithmetic example's 0.05 C bound is an explicitly synthetic scenario, not an empirical error estimate. The manufacturer's DTS guidance also distinguishes resolution from accuracy and says both contribute to uncertainty. A current Omega exposed-bead thermistor listing starts at C$106.41 with ±0.2 C interchangeability. That is a commodity-component price, not a high-pressure geothermal probe, installation, service cost or buyer willingness to pay. No defensible total test-service price or calibration-error distribution was established.
