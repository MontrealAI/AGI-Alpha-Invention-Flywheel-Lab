# 🌌♾️ AGI ALPHA Invention Flywheel Lab

**SUPER IMPORTANT: BLUE SKY.**  
**IMPORTANT: Beyond-Human Foresight.**

## Mission

Build an invention-automation machine in which each verified invention becomes a tool for discovering, constructing and economically enabling the next.

Discover consequential productive possibilities that ordinary opportunity search overlooks. Work backward from those possibilities to the missing means. Construct and test those means. Retain demonstrated capabilities and the conditions under which they help. Connect useful deliveries to complete-cost economics and justified successor construction.

**Invent the possibility. Construct the missing means. Verify the transformation. Enable the next frontier.**

The laboratory is now hosted at https://github.com/MontrealAI/AGI-Alpha-Invention-Flywheel-Lab. Its initial executable screen is a source-input validation and evidence-gap tool—not an autonomous opportunity discovery engine. It has not demonstrated forecast superiority, economic transformation, or a commercially successful flywheel.

## Blue Sky is the selection rule

Begin with: **What important activity could become possible if a constraint people currently accept could be transformed?**

Do not begin with the application whose code happens to exist. Do not constrain invention to conventional software products, familiar industry categories, or the easiest benchmark. A missing means may be a representation, measurement procedure, experiment, algorithm, production process, coordination protocol, interface, or combination.

Compare at least three materially different possibilities before flagship commitment. Compare their consequential outcome, strongest complete alternative, binding restriction, enabling change, first decisive experiment, economic path, and successor possibilities. Three paraphrases of the same product do not count.

Early uncertainty is a reason to design a bounded experiment, not automatic rejection. A promising incomplete combination may receive a prospectively bounded exploratory allocation. It does not acquire a success label or operating authority merely by being promising.

Preserve useful old components and adverse evidence. Inherit neither the catalogue application nor any other application simply because it was previously implemented. Preserve genuinely binding authority and cumulative accounting constraints; a new repository is not a reset of obligations or statistical history.

## Beyond-Human Foresight: a research objective

Seek non-obvious, consequential opportunities arising from changes in what intelligence, science, technology and organization can accomplish. Investigate second- and later-order consequences, not only extrapolations of a familiar trend. Ask what becomes newly necessary when an old bottleneck disappears, which assumptions cease to hold, and which capabilities become valuable together.

Use two distinct questions:

1. **Anticipatory foresight:** What may become possible or necessary, on what horizon, and which observable signals would support or refute that expectation?
2. **Constructive foresight:** What could we build that changes that future, rather than merely predicts it?

Explore multiple plausible futures, including competitor adaptation, falling costs of ordinary tools, failed enabling technologies, low demand, high adoption costs and adverse outcomes. Distinguish observed facts, sourced projections, hypotheses, scenarios and deliberate interventions. Do not treat a persuasive scenario as a forecast or a forecast as a validated business.

When making an evaluable forecast, record its evidence cutoff, resolution condition, horizon, uncertainty and comparison before the outcome. Record the source and justification of probabilities; do not invent precision when the evidence does not support it. Preserve misses and unresolved forecasts.

“Beyond-Human Foresight” names the ambition, not an established performance level. Evaluate specific advantages against strong unrestricted frontier-model search, domain-specific methods and credible human-expert work when available. Missing expert comparison is UNMEASURED, not a win. Novelty, forecast quality, technical usefulness, economic feasibility and successor benefit are separate claims.

## Four mechanisms, one constructive loop

| Mechanism | Required work product |
| --- | --- |
| Cross-domain structural discovery | A donor-to-target mapping of causal relationships, constraints or invariants, with transfer conditions and a falsifying test. |
| Meta-strategic invention | An executable adaptation, instrument or workflow that changes how the authorized outcome can be achieved. |
| Adversarial verification | Source-grounded defects, reproducible counterexamples and outcome checks; criticism can become a construction target or a reason to change the formulation. |
| Retention | Reusable implementation, scoped evidence, costs, dependencies and failed-transfer conditions; actual successor use must be measured. |

**Future possibility → binding restriction → structural transfer → executable invention → decisive test → useful delivery → complete-cost economics → justified construction → fresh successor benefit.**

The order of investigative actions may change. These mechanisms are available capabilities, not a permanent committee or mandatory agent topology. The lab may invent a better method, simplify its workflow, or retain a strong conventional incumbent.

## What deserves the next experiment?

Choose the smallest decisive experiment on the largest consequential uncertainty that is tractable within the authorized resources. Small experiment does not mean trivial ambition.

Require a concrete explanation of how its result would change a build, stop, pivot or scale decision. Prefer evidence that reveals whether a missing means can change the attainable outcome over another demonstration that a familiar component works. Unknown costs, missing data or a weak purpose bridge must remain visible.

A rejected analogy is useful if it becomes a scoped constraint. A verified criticism is useful if it changes a construction or decision. More agents, more papers, more test cases and more code are not substitutes for either.

## Commercial comparison: fully autonomous AI first, human-delivered service comparator

The primary economic benchmark is a **competently configured, fully autonomous frontier-AI enterprise E versus a competent human-delivered enterprise H**, providing the *same accepted outcome and obligations*. E's agents themselves carry out sourcing/research, production, independent-style verification, corrections, sales/acquisition, delivery, support and development. Bill E's actual model/tool, compute, infrastructure, failed-attempt, acquisition-channel, payment and liability costs. **Do not assign mandatory hypothetical human review, sales, development or support hours to E.** Human participation, if genuinely necessary, belongs to a distinct hybrid arm or marks a failure of strict E autonomy.

Read [COMMERCIAL_COMPARISON.md](COMMERCIAL_COMPARISON.md) and its v2 seven-stage agent cost scenarios. The 20k-input/4k-output Terra production example costs approximately $0.04 in standard model inference, but the whole accepted article has additional autonomous processes and external charges. Keep sourced tariffs separate from assumed model usage, outcome quality, customer acquisition and adoption. Human+AI providers, competing AI services and free tools are **secondary market comparisons** rather than the prerequisite to demonstrate human-labor replacement. Economic viability and profitable invention-to-successor compounding still require accepted work, credible price/collections, complete costs and fresh causally beneficial invention.

## Independent commercial and invention flywheels

**A commercially successful autonomous service does not have to invent better capabilities.** The first flywheel is accepted autonomous delivery -> collected revenue -> full-cost profit and unrestricted cash -> financing further accepted profitable autonomous delivery, using **unchanged** frontier models and tools if they suffice. The second, distinct, flywheel is verified invention -> retained productive or inventive capability -> causally improved fresh successor construction. The latter can strengthen the former but is not a condition of the former's success.

The primary economic comparison remains the fully autonomous enterprise versus competent human-delivered work at realistic labor costs for the same outcome and obligations. Competitive AI services test sustainable pricing and buyer demand, not whether autonomous work can be profitable. A prior human service need not have been loss-making. Read [COMMERCIAL_COMPARISON.md](COMMERCIAL_COMPARISON.md) for separate qualification gates and the explicitly unvalidated global TAM hypothesis.


## Three achievements to establish separately

- **Useful invention:** an executed construction improves the authorized outcome, or makes it possible, against strong complete alternatives within declared resources.
- **Economic transformation:** the same prospectively admitted cohort crosses from negative to positive complete-cost economics against its strongest competent completed-service alternative. Ordinary software parity does not automatically rule out a viable service, but forbids claiming unique technical advantage from a tie.
- **Compounding:** an inherited capability causes a gain on fresh successor construction after learning, extraction, adaptation, verification and maintenance costs. Compare against no inheritance and equally available ordinary reuse.

Accepted delivery, accrued revenue, collected money, unrestricted earned funds and investment attractiveness are different records. In simulation they remain explicitly simulated. Early partial achievements do not replace the complete flywheel or reduce already authorized campaign-wide success criteria.

## Lean by design

Keep one active flagship construction and at most two bounded exploratory alternatives initially. This is a proposed work-in-progress limit, not an empirical optimum. Avoid a framework, website, token system, distributed network or publishing pipeline unless a measured experimental need requires it.

Use the existing AGI-Alpha-Agent-v0 repository as a source of selectively reused instruments and later integration. Import only a needed dependency closure, pin provenance, preserve licenses and relevant regressions, and test the actual boundary. Do not clone the entire platform into this lab or weaken its qualification gates. A genuinely standalone lab module may be developed independently without implying that an older blocked artifact is approved.

A local experiment does not need to become a production release before it can yield useful evidence. Production authority and integration remain separate. Do not use a new repository to bypass an existing permission, review or safety restriction.
