## Private Enterprise Supplier Assurance — Executive Mandate

**Confidential scoping document · Commercial proposal hypothesis, not a signed engagement**

### The result the CFO purchases

For one important private-sector outsourced IT, managed-services or professional-services supplier, **identify material billing discrepancies with a documented basis for buyer-led validation and potential recovery**, while identifying gaps that prevent reliable conclusions.

We compare the executed contract's approved fixed unit rates and billing conditions with supplier invoice lines, independent service/usage approval, payment history and posted credits. Each candidate is linked to the relevant source record, recomputed in exact cents and presented as a review-ready dossier.

**Our deliverable is not a declaration that the supplier owes money.** Qualified customer personnel confirm legal interpretation, factual delivery and rights before approving any follow-up.

### Initial private mandate · proposed CAD 25,000

Subject to written scope and contract:

- **Scope:** One major private company supplier and a defined historical period. Up to an agreed quantity of structured invoice lines and contract-rate terms; complex terms are expressly separately scoped.
- **Buyer onboarding:** Approved contract price/term map, original supplier invoices, independent billable-quantity records, AP payments and supplier credits, with sufficient authorization, authority and data-retention terms.
- **Investigation:** Locally execute Contract Value Ω; reconcile exact financial amounts; challenge apparent duplicates, unsupported charges and overlapping rate/quantity errors; reject weak findings.
- **Deliverables:** Buyer-owned private HTML review room, exact line-level CSV, evidence map, source/file hashes, supplier-specific review dossiers, qualified exception register, source-reconciliation verdict and prioritized corrective controls.
- **Decision:** Buyer validates each material candidate with its procurement controller and, where needed, legal or contract subject-matter experts.
- **Completion standard:** The evidence pack reproduces on the agreed customer inputs, rejects known planted/tampered cases, and distinguishes quantified candidates from uncertain claims and money actually received.
- **Excluded by default:** Signing contracts, collection on behalf of customer, supplier communications, tax returns, foreign exchange, formulaic tier/rebate terms, proof of service delivery not supplied by customer, legal claims and automation of original PDF interpretation.
- **Payment:** Proposed CAD 25,000 fixed price per agreed mandate; invoicing, milestones and taxes subject to signed commercial agreement. No recovery/savings guarantee.
