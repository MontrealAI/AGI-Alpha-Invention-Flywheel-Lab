# CONTRACT VALUE Ω — Private-sector commercial mandate

**Not government procurement, not synthetic revenue, not a validated price.**

## Who has a reason to pay

**Economic buyer:** CFO, VP Finance, Head of Procurement or Director of Internal Audit of a privately operated enterprise with substantial annual outsourced-IT, professional-services or managed-services spend.

**First screening threshold (hypothesis):** CAD 10m+ annual spend on 2–10 major outsourced suppliers, or one especially intricate supplier with CAD 5m+ annual billing. Ask whether the buyer has a signed audit-right clause, current AP data access, an approved service delivery ledger and an unresolved billing controversy. If not, do not pretend an extract-only engine can prove a refund.

**No government buyers.** No public-sector contract, grant, procurement programme or government-funded pilot is part of this offering.

All are **competitor's published cases**, not independently audited extrapolations or our results. They establish commercial stakes and a serious incumbent—not a free market or guaranteed customer savings.

## High-ticket offer for actual demand testing

**CONTRACT VALUE Ω · Executive Recovery Mandate**

**Proposed CAD 25,000 paid diagnostic** for one supplier and agreed historical period: buyer submits signed terms and amendments, original invoice exports, independent service usage and AP credit/payment extracts; MONTREAL.AI returns quantified candidates, document-level evidence, source mapping, exception review and a buyer-controlled vendor-review dossier. Acceptance: source-bound reproducible handoff, no unresolved calculation defects, separate high-confidence/requires-evidence categories and authorized buyer review. A finding is not the same as collected funds.

**Proposed CAD 120,000 annual programme**, negotiated separately after a successful paid diagnostic, for repeating monitoring across a **defined number of suppliers and work volume**, with review and exception-handling service levels. Additional variable outcome-based fee only on **verified incremental credits/cash actually received**, under a written contract. Pricing and exact SLAs remain open to qualification.

No unapproved payment initiation, supplier contact, debt collection or legal threats. Contracts, privacy commitments, liability, indemnities, audit rights and performance-fee rules need legal/professional review.

### Strong competitor comparison

Evaluate against a competent AP analyst using spreadsheets + ERP; an existing recovery-audit provider including PRGX/apexanalytix; enterprise audit software; and non-entry. Compare the **same accepted outcome**, including data extraction, expert interpretation, correction of false positives, supplier follow-up, debt/credit accounting, procurement relationships and long-run controls. A local demo that spots synthetic errors does not establish speed, price, recall or profitability superiority.

### Minimum factual demand test

Ask qualified private CFO/procurement buyers for an actual paid scoping agreement; an NDA alone is not demand. The first engagements should each specify: (a) buyer authority to use private data, (b) exact signed contract rights, (c) baseline audit and dispute process, (d) financial acceptance criterion, (e) historical spend and cost of full audit, (f) rate limits and exclusions, and (g) authorized handoff/retention terms.

A successful first pilot requires a signed agreement **and real collected payment**. Count identified dollars, validated claims, accepted credits, actual collections, fees, operating costs and unrestricted profit separately. Do not silently call any one another.

### Unit economics (explicit illustrative arithmetic, not forecast)

One hypothetical CAD 25k diagnostic with CAD 3k analyst/review support, CAD 2k selling and onboarding, CAD 1k compute/tool/security allocations, CAD 2k independent QA and CAD 2k support/insurance/overhead produces CAD 15k pre-tax operating contribution **if** fully collected and all costs have been counted. These costs are authored illustrative placeholders, not a sourced expense forecast or actual invoices.

If acquisition costs, domain review, data cleaning, legal support or disputes exceed the estimate, the result changes. Model actual quotes, all obligations, payment delays, taxes and unsuccessful sales; do not claim profit before receipt and cost reconciliation.
