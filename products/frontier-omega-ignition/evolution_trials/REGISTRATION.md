# A different archive decision from the same sky

The new question is whether a desk can identify a time-resolved change in positive difference light and state what the next independent-in-time visit shows. The old instrument answered whether g and r measurements lie close enough together to report a colour. These questions need different data.

## Comparison registered before computing the new answers

Use the same fixed archive bytes, roster, information clock, scope, tolerances and output contract for the newly built route and the conventional route. The numerical trigger, direction and validation rules are fixed by the companion [science registration](../invention/evolution/REGISTRATION.md). The units are individual target/passband decisions. Repeated targets, overlapping data or a reused baseline do not constitute independent population trials. A visit withheld by time is not necessarily statistically independent of earlier photometry.

1. **Retained source capability.** Run the producer from the product's normal entry point on its frozen public sources. Include reading, decision construction and delivery. Identify the particular component or source preparation actually reused.
2. **Conventional control with equal permissions.** Give a separate program the original class and object files, source references and the scientific rules. Reselect its population and epochs from source, use relational joins/window functions for the scientific decisions, and recover the published measurements and independent recipient verdict. Never import the production selector, decision builder or reference result to compute expected values. This route may win.
3. **Ordinary output reuse.** Attempt the same decision from the prior delivered g/r rows and their detection witnesses with the same event-time cutoff. Give the control its overlapping historical products where applicable. A missing witness is insufficient information. Do not convert the silence of a reduced output into a negative scientific observation. The cost of fetching original data and reconstructing missing information belongs to the other complete routes.
4. **No triage** is the desk's zero-delivery outside option. No fabricated participant, telescope allocation, saved time, classification, avoided exposure or sale.

Compare every science verdict, target/passband, signed quantity, timestamp, uncertainty, source observation identity and applicable follow-up disposition, including abstentions. Use separately constructed adverse cases to test future contamination, sign reversal, pseudo-confirmation and detection identity. For a recorded execution, measure new-process wall and CPU for comparable work in rotating order; report sample size and conditions, delivery bytes and separate recipient-check cost. These measurements exclude development, prior acquisition, human time, maintenance and bills. Preserve those historical measured inputs and the unknown categories explicitly. A functional tie does not establish a proprietary advantage. A successful cross-task transfer establishes neither cheaper full-programme construction nor recursively improving discovery.

**Next beneficiary test.** An archive or observatory operator may provide an actual consented shadow queue, review budget, decision SLA, acceptance rubric and incumbent. Predeclare each arm's access/allocation, cost horizon and adjudication; compare accepted actionable follow-ups per operator hour and per complete cost, plus false or missed priorities and future observations unavailable. Include a no-purchase/no-action decision. This registration authorizes no outreach, infrastructure charge, scientific observation or distribution on a third party's behalf.
