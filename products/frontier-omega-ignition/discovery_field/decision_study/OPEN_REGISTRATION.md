# Free discovery on a fresh facility-network mission

Register `open_prompt.json` before eliciting the response. Give one fresh included assistant only this prompt. It may propose zero to five capabilities, combine evidence, select a novel arrangement or abstain. It must not see the exhibition opportunities, answer key, other models’ outputs, or an objective secretly requiring the transient proposal to win. Ask for JSON, without tools beyond opening the given prompt and saving the answer.

Record the original output and available timing/model identity once. No semantic revision after assessment. The mechanical check counts real excerpts, source availability after withdrawal, required fields and complete proposals. Independently read each original answer for fidelity of the claim, material use of evidence in the proposed experiment, outcome comparability and an observation that could falsify the case. Mark unsupported capabilities as hypotheses, including cases that contain a genuine but immaterial citation. Distinguish a feasible future experiment from an executed physical result or demonstrated demand. Record the researcher-engineered possibilities, old Qwen open cohort, new bounded Qwen decisions and this free-generation trial as separate observations. No causal model comparison follows from their different missions and contexts.
