# Can an agent invent useful work for an instrument-sharing facility?

Registered before either open response. A distinct public scientific mission asks an agent to invent up to five capabilities, including possibilities unrepresented in the prepared opportunity field. The mission packet contains only the brief, authority, public source fragments, names and answer contract. It supplies no proposals, source interpretations, preferred slot assignment or expected result. Empty, partial and unsupported sets are admissible. Source choice may repeat; do not reward filling five slots.

The included integration assistant and the pinned local Qwen route each receive that packet once, with no feedback or repairs. Record which route ran, identities actually known, unrestricted proposal and failed cases. The assistant has previous project context; this is not a blinded or randomized head-to-head trial. The readers who prepared the packet may assess responses but may not write or repair them.

For each row, recompute the positive source premise directly from its registered fragment. Separately inspect its relevance to the proposed use: an alert-recovery statistic may motivate a follow-up-selection test, a CAD grading statistic an editability test, a proving measurement a computation-identity/resource test, an observation-initialization report a forecast-availability test, and a parallel batch recipe a numerical search/evaluation test. These are applicability boundaries for credited factual statements, not templates for a proposal. An unrelated proposed use, operational adoption from a published observation, a generic citation, negative polarity with positive evidence, and unsupported success must withhold credit. Derive the intended test domain from the agent's stated test and action, independently of its chosen evidence. If multiple or no domains can be resolved, leave materiality ungraded. A second scientific reader checks the mechanism in plain language and records a counterexample or acceptance reason. Report the two checks separately; substantive design quality and expected price are not machine-scored. Compare the typed source check with a simple eligible-ID lookup; inject a true but unrelated premise into one otherwise unchanged test. Keep all denominators and assess recovery after removing each cited source. No rankings of general invention ability. No physical demonstrations.
