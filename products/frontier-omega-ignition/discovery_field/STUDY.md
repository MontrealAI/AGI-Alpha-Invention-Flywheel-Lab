### What changed with prepared premises

The second included assistant returned five proposals in one pass. All five reported quantities and scopes checked. Four had a clear domain match in their proposed experiment. The fifth transferred observation-based weather initialization to instrument calibration after transport. The machine withheld a relevance verdict. A separate reader concluded that the weather case supplies an analogy only. It establishes no identifiability of gain, offset or alignment from a recipient’s reference readings. The design's discriminating test is to blind a handoff decision to paired faults that cancel at one reference level, then score separate unused levels and compare occupied bench minutes and false approvals with complete recalibration. This test has not been executed.

The four other proposed tests were technically specific. The adapter proposal tests missing dimensions and interference; it still requires a reason to introduce automated CAD generation. The cryptographic proposal withholds until the proof system can test output consistency for the specified private computation. Separate process evidence would be needed to identify the original execution. The observing-slot proposal counts displaced teaching requests alongside unresolved alerts, a material refinement of selection on labeled recoveries. The test-sequence proposal tests candidate search against an exact coverage and scheduling solver; it remains an unverified transfer of the cloud evaluation recipe. Domain matches measure legibility of the evidence-to-test connection. Neither arm establishes a better invention method or a facility willing to adopt its designs.

## Known costs and commercial decision

Five independent source responses qualified; one direct Google acquisition returned a content failure despite HTTP 200. The curated interpretations, version checks, included assistance, scientific assessment, unsuccessful retrievals, qualification and future revalidation have real costs. The included UI does not expose attributable billed tokens, call duration or a per-proposal price. Machine replay times measure the checker only. The local-run records include measured call time and usage. Prices, complete engineering and future maintenance costs, field demand, purchasing authority and realized benefit remain unknown. No denominator here supports profit per accepted discovery or comparison with a paid human team.

The concrete economic test is whether an operator accepts an independently reproducible worklist for a real observing window under the same data, rights and time costs as their own scheduler. A shadow test with a frozen issue time and no changes to existing station operations can establish technical fit before any consideration of voluntary adoption. No owner or buyer participation was observed.

The relevant OUT-THINK intervention turns a research assertion into a scoped, inspectable proposition; OUT-LEARN asks whether the observation in fact survives a changed source or interpretation. A new publisher version invalidates the old factual binding until source and scope are checked again.
