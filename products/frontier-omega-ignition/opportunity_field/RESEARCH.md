# Which capability earns the next experiment?

MONTREAL.AI’s immediate choice is where to commit its own research time. A public benchmark can justify investigating a service configuration. The lab still needs an authorized system and representative traffic to adopt one. The six [bilingual evidence cards](field.json) separate the published signal, the proposed use, the incumbent and the next test.

The search covered the five weeks ending November 17, 2026. All five primary publications fall within that window. They document newly available evidence, code or verification methods. None establishes that the underlying activity first became possible during those weeks.

## Why construct endpoint admission first?

The binding constraint is comparison of **complete measured runs**. System throughput, interactive speed and first-token tail latency can pull in different directions. Each value must come from the same observed run, model, system, benchmark and permissible review status. Combining the peak of each column gives an unobserved machine. Changing the beneficiary contract may legitimately reverse or remove the choice. An indexed load sweep permits an immediate independent check of these decisions without purchasing service access.

The practical transfer from [OUT-EXECUTE, pp. 4–18](../../../docs/harness/OUT_PAPERS.md) is to retain an operating point together with its witness and scope. A changed report or contract requires checking that witness again. Adversarial discovery adds a real source with a superficially attractive published result whose review was pending on November 17. A specialist and an independent SQL query should receive exactly the same admissible rows. Neither reproducing a published sweep nor winning on a single operator constraint establishes a new physical capability. [November 13 provisional submission](https://results.mlcommons.org/endpoints/report?source=phase2&submission=053005da-b0ad-495d-9721-e39e9b144bd8)

For capital allocation the relevant unit is **total cost per correctly accepted workload decision**, with erroneous approvals and missed viable alternatives reported separately. Count evidence acquisition, ingestion changes, verification, failed runs, operator time, service tests, maintenance and residual obligations. A peak throughput value provides neither a tariff nor a profit estimate. Human-only productivity, willingness to pay, access to those systems, realized savings and profit remain unmeasured. The selected construction is an internal research asset; any external engagement requires its own rights, scope and acceptance.
