# RR-H1 · Can a reader obtain a correct, citable result?

**Beneficiary and decision.** MONTREAL.AI needs to know which complete reading workflow to offer when another researcher must check a quantitative result or qualify a claim. Register before scoring any arm. Two different lab publications, OUT-ACCELERATE in English and CONTINUITÉ Ω in French, are fresh relative to RR-01 and RR-02. The original publisher HTML is a reading guide to the full PDF in both cases. Each arm retains the original reading guide, PDF, search, zoom, copy and the reader’s usual assistive technology. The repository owner has authorized work on these public editions. The reference file fixes both pairs of input hashes, all eight tasks, physical PDF pages and the answer rubrics. A physical page counts from the very first page, including the cover. Papers and languages differ; this design cannot isolate a causal language effect.

## Workflows and allocation

A supplies the original publication. B additionally supplies a complete conventional page-indexed Poppler layout export with ordinary browser Find and links to each physical page, alongside native PDF search. C adds the original, frozen Reading Room to A. D adds a local multi-term concordance to A, returning matching physical pages and verbatim line context, with citation links. D searches the full PDF text and receives no questions, answers or reference pages. Use the strongest workflow a reader prefers within the assigned arm. Existing author HTML and PDF remain usable even when an added instrument cannot resolve a task. Reuse publisher materials offline in the kit. External material linked by the author is outside the two-publication task.

Use a **between-reader**, stratified, block-randomized design. One participant gets one language, one paper and one arm: no participant views the same claim under different arms. Block by language and by regular screen-reader use versus ordinary reading. Enroll people fluent in the assigned language who routinely read research and have not read this paper or task key. Participants self-report eligibility; screen-reader users use their own technology. Balance four distinct task orders independently within each arm using a four-row Williams design. Allocate after consent with private seeded permutations in blocks of four. Coordinators safeguard the roster and do not condition recruitment on the next assignment. Pseudonymous codes are unique across the entire study. Never reenroll an individual after abandonment. An operator must screen duplicate people in the private consent roster.

## Procedure and measurements

One unscored orientation covers physical page numbers, use of the form, ordinary PDF functions and the participant’s own assistive technology. Reveal one task at a time without revealing answers or grading. The reader supplies an answer, a physical PDF page and a supporting quotation, or explicitly records unresolved/abandoned. Capture wall time through answer submission, interruptions, reader-reported access barriers and tool used. No countdown or forced time limit. An observer can enter the reader’s dictated answer and timestamps, logging the assistance. A participant may withdraw. Retain only deidentified observations explicitly authorized for retention. Store approval, informed consent and person identifiers privately outside Git. Participation, compensation, recruitment and third-party contact require separate approval. No public service or transmitting endpoint is part of the trial.

Two different reviewers independently inspect deidentified responses, the rubrics and actual PDFs, blind to arm, participant, time and each other. A correct result requires the right substantive answer, correct units or qualification and an excerpt that genuinely supports it on a valid page. The fixed key records verified target pages. A different genuinely supporting page or line-break variant may be credited only when both reviewers record that source check. A confident materially false numeric, units, source or support claim is a consequential error. An explicit explanation that the task’s claim lacks support can be correct. Abstention alone is unresolved. A third independent reviewer decides disagreements before unblinding. All four assigned tasks stay in the denominator for a retained enrolled participant; missing final forms are reported as incomplete rather than discarded. A withdrawn participant’s data follow their actual retention agreement.

## Decision and risk

The predeclared pilot endpoint is four complete randomization blocks in each of four strata (64 assigned participants). This is a finite design for estimation, not a power calculation, recruitment target imposed by software or a condition for ordinary technical use. Report recruitment, withdrawal, attrition, missing outcomes and partial blocks; no early winner selection. Primary metric is accepted answers with correct citations / assigned tasks; also report consequential errors / assigned tasks, time per attempted task and total observed task time per accepted outcome. Report each arm within language and screen-reader stratum, participant-level distributions and use/abandonment. The four task responses from one participant are correlated. Avoid pooled task-level significance tests.

A tool merits a larger confirmation only if it beats the best of A/B on citation-correct proportion by at least 0.15 **or** reduces observed time per accepted answer by at least 20% with no reduction in correctness, has no higher consequential-error proportion, and no subgroup has worse correctness. If incomplete groups, unknown timing/withdrawal, unresolved critical access barriers or material cost uncertainty prevent a comparison, leave the choice unresolved. Thresholds are an operator decision for this study. A tie favours the cheaper complete alternative when measured. No reader productivity or general accessibility claim follows from automated passage or browser checks.

Compute cost per accepted result only after recording common study/publishing setup, arm tooling/development including failed attempts and maintenance, preparation, consent and administration, support, participant time and adjudication; amortize or allocate shared costs under a declared rule fixed before collection. Separate paid amount from time, currency, measurement window and allocation assumptions; never count labor twice. Missing or unpriced inputs yield unknown total. Report the working recipient and technical failures independently of the human endpoint.

**Pre-enrollment qualification.** Check pinned source bytes, independent page counts and target snippets; verify that all arm packets open offline with their registered inputs, B and D cover all pages, the candidate shows ambiguity or no hits honestly, allocation balances and duplicate enrollment is refused. Exercise correct, wrong-unit, overstated and unresolved response paths only as labeled software controls. Never populate the human cohort with test records.
