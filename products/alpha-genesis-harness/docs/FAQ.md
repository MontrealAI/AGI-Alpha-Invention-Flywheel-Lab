# FAQ / Questions fréquentes

## What does CAD $299 buy?

The identified downloadable Official Edition: application source, launchers, examples, guides, integrity manifest and the qualification evidence supplied with that version. It is a one-time purchase. The current 0.3.0 artifact remains a release candidate while its stated qualification gates are open.

## Does it require a recurring subscription?

The HARNESS introduces no compulsory recurring service. Its offline/manual workflow needs a desktop browser. Local AI requires a separately installed inference server/model and suitable hardware. API and ChatGPT/Codex modes require their own eligible account access. Those providers' charges and limits are separate.

## Can I use ChatGPT Pro?

The product includes engineering for the official eligible sign-in pathway and an official Codex-compatible construction pathway. Availability depends on current OpenAI eligibility, account consent and the particular pathway. Paid-app authorization and a completed live Pro test must be recorded before the commercial edition claims operational plan inference. A Pro plan is not an unlimited API key. Manual copy-and-paste with your own account remains a distinct option.

## Is local-only really local?

The offline core makes no inference call. A local inference configuration must use approved loopback services and prohibit external requests. The model/server itself may have update or other network behavior outside the HARNESS. Disconnect networking or use OS-level controls when your privacy needs require independent enforcement. A scripted demonstration is not evidence of local LLM inference.

## Is this an autonomous business or a guaranteed invention engine?

No such outcome is established by the package. It executes bounded construction and verification workflows, retains usable artifacts and permits comparisons. A useful artifact, a successful transfer and improved discovery are separate claims. Scientific novelty, demand, revenue and profitability each require their own evidence.

## Can agents execute anything on my computer?

No. Default tools are constrained and project-scoped. Model output is treated as data. Consequential external actions are outside the default workflow authority. A separate Codex pathway has its own sandbox, consent and authentication boundary; inspect its actual configuration.

## Who owns my work?

You control your local project files and exports. Applicable rights in your inputs, model outputs, third-party content and generated artifacts still depend on their sources and terms. Apache 2.0 covers the eligible supplied product source; it does not relicense external models or your documents.

## Are future updates and support included?

The purchase covers the version identified at purchase. Future editions, updates, hosted monitoring, model compute, consulting and a guaranteed support service level are not included. Applicable consumer rights and the merchant's actual policies remain unchanged.

## Why pay if the code is Apache-licensed?

The package supplies a coherent identified release, onboarding, examples, guides and verifiable packaging. Buying it is not required to exercise the rights already granted by Apache 2.0.
