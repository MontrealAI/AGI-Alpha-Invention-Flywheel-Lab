# Ω Proof-to-Market / De la preuve au marché

Open **Invent the Economy → Ω Proof-to-Market** in the connected application.
Load the illustrative shared GENESIS verification plan, replace its assumptions,
and evaluate it. The planner creates no contracts, obligations or transactions.

The desired result and customer acceptance condition come first. Record the
missing capabilities, participants and their outside options, complete cost
categories, prospective funding with timing and terms, delivery milestones,
evidence references and existing alternatives. The example is hypothetical;
its amounts are neither a quotation nor evidence of willing customers.

The result recomputes conditional participant surplus, missing cost categories,
funding gaps by period and milestone status. Member contributions are not added
again to explicit funding. Financing proceeds are not revenue. Forecast,
interest, commitment, invoice, collection and earned-revenue records are kept
separate and must never be summed into sales. Operator-entered references do
not establish independent verification or actual commercial success.

This release distinguishes technical feasibility, conditional economic
feasibility and commercial validation. It does not yet independently authenticate
contracts, bank receipts or customer acceptance. Accordingly, verified earned
revenue, complete-cost profit and reinvestable surplus remain **unknown**, even
when someone enters amounts under those labels. Existing native enterprise
accounting remains separate; this tool is a planning projection, not a ledger.

The authenticated local API is `POST /api/proof-to-market/assess`. Its exact
input contract is implemented in `bridge/proof_to_market.py`; malformed records,
negative/noninteger amounts, unrecognized transaction fields and unsupported
states are rejected. Currency amounts are integer minor units. To compare
independent development, purchase, shared construction, commissioned development
and no-build, supply each as an explicit alternative with its feasibility,
known cost or `null` where unknown. An entered alternative remains an assumption.

## Français

Ouvrez **Inventer l'économie → Ω Proof-to-Market**. Chargez l'exemple de
développement partagé d'une capacité GENESIS, remplacez ses hypothèses, puis
évaluez le projet. Les montants de l'exemple sont fictifs.

Définissez le résultat, les critères d'acceptation, les capacités manquantes,
les participants, leurs autres options, les coûts complets, le financement
envisagé, les jalons et les preuves. Le résultat distingue faisabilité
technique, faisabilité économique conditionnelle et validation commerciale.

Les prévisions, marques d'intérêt, engagements, factures, encaissements et
revenus gagnés restent distincts. Une référence saisie par l'utilisateur n'est
pas une preuve authentifiée. Le système ne signe rien, ne déplace aucun fonds
et ne crée aucune obligation. Les profits vérifiés et fonds réinvestissables
demeurent inconnus tant qu'une vérification indépendante manque.
