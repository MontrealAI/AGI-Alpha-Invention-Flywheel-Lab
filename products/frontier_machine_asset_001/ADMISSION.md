## Honest outcome of the initial kernel
The current transformer and independent-candidate checker perform only copy/strip/upper/lower on authorized caller-supplied records and check output consistency. Free Python implementations and self-contained testing frameworks can do the same. SHA-256 hashes support local replay integrity, not authenticity, fresh source truth, trusted third-party certification, or payment. No new frontier enabler, novel productive activity, improved discovery, external sale, moat or superior result is established.

## Competing hypotheses to test next
- H1: Agent systems would pay for **independent acceptance of consequential work**, with source evidence acquisition, a frozen task-specific rubric, fresh outcome checks, and durable, tamper-evident records. Failure mode: ordinary tests or existing providers give the same guarantee at lower complete cost.
- H2: They would pay for an otherwise inaccessible **fresh, original data capability** with independently checked provenance and a legal right to resell. Failure mode: the data are accessible directly or cannot be resold lawfully.
- H3: They would pay for an **economically decisive specialist computation or instrument** that current general agents and conventional tools cannot economically reproduce. Failure mode: no verified capability threshold or real buyer demand.

These are research hypotheses, not product specifications to automatically build.

## Preregister the first choice
For each candidate, record the buyer's complete outcome and outside options, the latest frontier change alleged to unlock it, primary-source evidence, new required construction, honest full costs, machine-side discovery route and a minimum discriminating test. Include a do-nothing option. Freeze an evaluation before performing the candidate-specific construction. Compare competent agents with available general tools, strong free validators, specialized paid vendors and incumbent human+AI workflows with equal rights, evidence and full costs.

## Admission gates
1. **Actual productive advantage:** a consequential new result accepted on fresh independent tests, with a verified cost/quality advantage over the strongest complete alternative. An authored constraint or ordinary compiler output does not establish novelty.
2. **Defensible right to deliver:** rights to data, code, evaluation records, naming and any externally licensed resources; complete operator/security qualification.
3. **Automated purchase and delivery:** an authorized external buyer with a legitimate budget elects to pay, using a provider-approved merchant path. A 402 challenge, internal test charge, self-purchase or modeled willingness-to-pay is insufficient.
4. **Actual money and accounting:** independent settlement evidence, accepted delivery, refunds/reversals/retries, full economic cost and positive verified incremental contribution.
5. **Transfer and compounding:** repeat on another independent buyer, and later show a new task succeeds better due to retained discovery, against strong alternative reconstruction allowed comparable resources.

Until gates are passed, asset 001 remains a **supporting prototype**, with valuation impact and cash revenue both unestablished.
