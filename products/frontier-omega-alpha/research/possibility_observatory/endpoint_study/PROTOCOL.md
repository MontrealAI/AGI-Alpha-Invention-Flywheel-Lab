# Inference pilot evidence study · preregistration

Registered on 23 November 2026 before inspecting the selected operating-point values. Research authority covers the public MLCommons results and offline computation. It covers no endpoint traffic, purchase, credentials, supplier contact or production change.

## Decision

Give MONTREAL.AI a reproducible shortlist for an agentic inference pilot. Ask which measured configuration delivers the greatest aggregate output-token throughput while sustaining at least 40 end-to-end output tokens per second per user on the same benchmark replay. This is a study screening threshold. Operational adoption requires the actual workload, owner-approved service requirements, output-quality criteria, pricing and a real pilot.

The binding constraint in June's v0.7 result set was evidence for this particular multi-turn benchmark. The November v1.0 records add agentic operating points. A larger total token rate or an official label by itself cannot establish workload, metric or operating-point equivalence. The proposed instrument transforms admissible measurements into a source-bound local test environment and an exhaustive decision receipt, including every excluded row and the exact responsiveness breakpoints.

## Primary and control comparisons

- Use all rows exposed by the official Results Explorer public feed in the registered snapshot. Keep the v1.0 official, standardized, available, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash series on the published MLPerf agentic-inference dataset. Use workload identity from the source, not a substring in the system name. Enforce the as-of date on the publication record, and the online, complete, valid, non-test run state on the measurement record. A missing decisive field holds the point.
- The target metric is the submitted agentic end-to-end interactivity. The objective is published system output tokens per second. Preserve source precision. The eligible maximum is over observed points, including ties. Report the target load as submitted. No interpolation, power proxy, quality inference, uptime claim or recommendation to procure.
- Compare with direct SQLite over identical observations and thresholds. The SQL implementation independently evaluates eligibility, exclusions and argmax. Audit source cohort membership and point counts against the published report tabs where visible. Retain a discrepancy and withhold qualification when the two computation routes differ.
- Run the primary 40-token case, prespecified 20, 60 and 80-token sensitivity controls, and the exact measured breakpoints under the same contract. Mutations test a high-throughput wrong dataset, a provisional submission, an announcement without a measurement, a future publication, a missing metric, threshold equality and ties. Treat controls as engineering evidence. Public measurements are not fresh privately held-out operator workloads.
- Stop after one bounded snapshot and a separate parser/source review. Where a source changes, report its digest mismatch and register a new snapshot without silently substituting it. Record source collection and authorship, independent verification, CPU and wall time, scope, unknown work and cash costs. Identical decision quality is a tie. No speed or dollar advantage follows unless measured at the complete workflow level.
