# FRONTIER Ω ALPHA — Transformational Intelligence foundations

**Scope:** additive, source-grounded research integration for draft PR #541.  This is an engineering plan and one implemented evidence gate, **not** a claim that an autonomous inventor, customer, transaction, or generalized scientific verifier now exists.

## Why this matters

The current release-candidate radar primarily detects publisher-version changes; the original experiment compares an indexed parser with direct inspection of the *same* published Lean receipts. The next meaningful step is **Frontier Replay / the Missing Move**: revisit a valuable purpose that was previously blocked, determine whether a particular frontier change removes its binding constraint, construct an actual alternative where justified, and independently compare the complete outcome against competent approaches and no entry. Do not turn a release label into an assumed capability improvement.

The reviewed source manuscripts distinguish **purpose** from **formulation** from **evaluation**. Preserving the actual purpose is a hard boundary; changing its method and measurement can be legitimate invention. A benchmark score, a published proof receipt, and an economically accepted capability are different evidence objects.

## Implemented first increment: `research/reflective_gate.py`

This **read-only** tool challenges the existing *Lean receipt* experiment after rerunning its independent **receipt** checker. It records:

1. Whether the registered exact-source receipt replay passed, without claiming that Lean kernel verification was rerun.
2. How many **distinct upstream publisher hostnames** supplied the receipt files; under a conservative same-host fault grouping, it checks the necessary (not sufficient) `2t+1` group count for correcting `t` arbitrary source-group faults. It does **not** infer that different hostnames are independent.
3. Whether the retained parser was reloaded; it explicitly withholds any causal successor improvement claim without a matched no-reuse experiment.
4. Whether actual beneficiary acceptance, revenue and complete-cost economic advantage have been established (they have not in this registered experiment).
5. Actionable response alternatives: independent kernel replay, matched direct/no-reuse experiments, stronger source-fault instrumentation, authorized commercial acceptance, or defer/no build.

It emits a small versioned **challenge dossier** linking claims, source/version scope, materiality, discriminating tests, response alternatives, and non-granted authority. Nothing is encrypted or published automatically. The tool does not contact an LLM, Ethereum, an external service, or GitHub; and it cannot elevate the authority of research artifacts.

From `products/frontier-omega-alpha` after running the existing registered research experiment with its pinned inputs:

```sh
python3 -m research.reflective_gate \
  --protocol research/protocol.json \
  --inputs "$PRIVATE_REGISTERED_INPUTS" \
  --report "$PRIVATE_REGISTERED_RUN/research-receipt.json" \
  --output "$PRIVATE_REGISTERED_RUN/reflective-challenge.json"

python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -p 'test_reflective_gate.py' -v
```

The command refuses unregistered or unverified receipt inputs and writes its new report only under an operator-provided private output path. A test-only flag in the pure function is not a public operator approval mechanism; the CLI uses the existing source-bound verifier directly.

## Next research increments (not yet implemented)

### 1. Frontier Replay — make blocked valuable outcomes first-class

Record a source-pinned **blocked-purpose dossier** with a beneficiary, intended outcome, strong complete incumbent, original failure or prohibitive cost, permissions, full economic resources, and a *reopen predicate*. When the radar detects a material change, assess the predicate against a **reproducible task probe**, not publisher prose. Compare unchanged baseline, newly accessible method, a specialist, altered formulation, and no-build. Store negative replays; do not cherry-pick successful releases.

### 2. Constructive Missing Move — invent the missing means

Given a supported material obstruction, test at least these alternatives: **measure** (Out-Learn/Out-Reflect), **reframe** (Transformational Intelligence/Out-Think), **construct** (Out-Design/Out-Strategise/World as an Output), **compose/retain** (Out-Execute/Out-Evolve), **rearrange economic participation** (Invent the Economy), **adopt a strong existing route**, **defer**, and **stop**. A forced seven-stage ceremony would violate the source papers. Actions require explicit permissions and source-bound task semantics; proposal generation is never evidence of useful realization.

### 3. Structural transfer, not keyword analogies

Retain mechanistic structure, invariants, failure witnesses, applicability conditions, implementation code, and verified tests. Search across remote domains for comparable **causal constraints**, not just similar names. Where an analogy breaks, separately compare: reject it, construct the missing correspondence, or remove the supposed dependency. Add a random-analogy control and equal-budget unassisted frontier-agent and strong specialist controls.

### 4. Measure *better invention* causally

Require four distinct testable achievements: (A) useful S1, (B) S1 enabling a different accepted S2, (C) revised constructor beating an equal-content strong construction baseline, and (D) revised *learning rule* improving constructors across fresh development/confirmation campaigns. Pre-register full costs, source access, frozen tasks, information budgets and outside alternatives. Compare strategy invention alone vs + structural transfer vs + verified constructive challenge vs + retained experience; do not confuse more candidate trials with improved method.

### 5. Make the economic outcome real

Connect one authorized beneficiary request to a complete technical and commercial arrangement. Measure cost and acceptance before forecasting growth. Real customer decisions, actual collections, liabilities, finance timing, licensed rights, distribution and support have their own evidence. An attractive hypothetical buyer list is not consent; cash-in-the-future does not pay an earlier obligation. Make MONTREAL.AI the primary beneficiary without exposing confidential opportunities through the member reader.
