## What was actually built in this PR

- A bounded 25-configuration experiment constructor (plus no-entry and ordinary fee-free comparator).
- Two different, wholly authored synthetic task descriptions with separate development/holdout case IDs.
- A deploy-parameter constructor for an actual, immutable ERC-20 verifier-conditioned escrow reference.
- A working Solidity contract and local EVM adversarial checks (source and tests have separate identities).
- A local Ollama research adapter that can propose three materially different opportunities from operator-curated source signals. The included output is an **authored offline fixture**, not actual AI inference.
- A local hash-linked research register retaining failure cases without creating financial records.
- No mainnet deployment, accepted customer deliverable, real license agreement, fees or settled revenue.

The examples and experimental code were constructed contemporaneously; the frozen holdouts in the example files are **not independently sequestered** and are **not statistical evidence of generalization**. They check that the *program does not use holdout cases to choose the mechanism*. A future prospective study must register the cases and source evidence **before** constructing the candidate.

## Three materially different high-consequence frontier hypotheses (all HOLD)

| Candidate | Possible new productive outcome | Strong complete incumbent | Critical missing means / falsifier |
|---|---|---|---|
| Independently accepted agent deliverables | Agents buy consequential work and release funds only on independently testable acceptance | Existing escrow, ERC-8004 validators, ordinary CI, human/AI assurance, x402/MPP | Acquire original task requirements, execute meaningful tests, avoid collusive validation and demonstrate lower complete cost |

The core invention is **not** a generic ERC-20 escrow, new token, smart-contract fee, hash receipt, standard registry, generic AI agent, or subscription wrapper. These are already available mechanisms and strong controls. The research goal is a genuinely new **productive arrangement** that makes a currently blocked valuable outcome attainable.

## The decisive next experiment

**Prospective registration, not yet performed:**

1. Choose one beneficiary and a consequential outcome with authorized, primary evidence. Record the best existing complete alternative, including free forks and human-plus-AI completion. No winner specified.
2. Read source facts at a fixed cutoff. Have a pinned model/tool configuration generate at least three substantively different missing-means constructions. Treat all novelty claims as hypotheses and test applicability of already known tools.
3. Develop executable candidate(s) with the same cost, source access, tools, permissions, human intervention and safety gates as strong comparators. The experiment must be feasible even if the original beneficiary refuses or no asset qualifies.
4. Let a separate evaluator freeze independently held original cases and externally validated acceptance criteria before disclosure to the builder; preserve all refusals.
5. Record technical outcomes, measured resources, complete economic costs, actual voluntary counterparties and refunded/unsold work. Never infer true buyer value from a spreadsheet utility parameter.
6. For any candidate that wins, obtain a verifiable operating right and a real paying use. The fee must be attributable to *that service* on the *deployed asset*, not token issuance, wash transfers, simulated collections or inaccessible virtual valuations.
7. Freeze the retained capability. On a different fresh task, compare its reuse with a fully endowed alternative that may reconstruct or buy the same means. Include the cost of prior discovery and transfer; withhold a compounding claim if they tie or if the prior costs dominate.
