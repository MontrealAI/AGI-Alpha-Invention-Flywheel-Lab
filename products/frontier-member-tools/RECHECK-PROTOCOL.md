# Prospective experiment — rechecking a saved research decision

Registered on November 23, 2026, before building the challenger. This is a Member First capability comparison. Blue Sky admission, consequential member benefit, and profitable operation each require separate evidence.

The first beneficiary is the MONTREAL.AI research operator who needs to revisit a recommendation in a later evidence release. The immediate task is to reliably identify whether a saved decision needs renewed review, including a change to its strongest alternative, limitations or usable tool. Independent acceptance requires an exact answer on the released public evidence. Frequency of this task, value of avoided rework and willingness to pay are unmeasured.

| Candidate | Missing means or constraint | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Spectral recovery with a conventional photodiode | The reported enabling calibration needs a compatible instrument, reference spectra and validation across operating conditions ([author report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26532v1)). | Hold until rights-cleared observations and an applicable spectrometer comparator are available. |
| Automatically synthesize and verify quantum program transformations | Match the reported library and benchmark against explicit Lean proofs under the same correctness and resource constraints ([author report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09822v3)). | Hold until the release can reproduce the baseline on an independently applicable task. |
| Recheck a saved research decision | The current Atlas and member manifest expose versions and corrections, but a downloaded or remembered decision does not automatically flag stale advice. | Run the bounded comparison from the existing rights-cleared public Pages releases. |

The proposed operation applies OUT-EXECUTE’s scope-aware reuse and OUT-REFLECT’s fault-bounded evidence to retained public decisions. The candidate is a local saved objective with the selected decision’s ID, source version and fingerprint of its decision-relevant evidence. Reopening compares it to the current projection. The strongest available workflow here is direct keyed lookup and exact structural comparison of the same public decision manifests, using a small conventional program and the pinned sources. Give both arms the same public bytes and the same decision contract. Each arm may prepare its own mapping once. Use separate executable implementations.

- Check every earlier decision against the later release, then do the reverse for each later-only decision. Check each reviewed record’s oldest Git-pinned editorial version against its latest version using the later ledger. A version change requires renewed review even if the new recommendation is the same. Retain the original record and provide the current verdict or withdrawal.
- Run a same-version control for each of these changes: verdict, strongest alternative, scope or limitations, next action, and tool identity. Also test mismatched or absent record IDs and unchanged evidence with irrelevant metadata differences. These controls are deliberate perturbations of public inputs, not real research changes.
- Classify the saved evidence as current, revised or missing. An unavailable reference must not be described as current. Source-only findings must retain their original evidence tier. A digest detects disagreement with a pinned state; a member-provided file cannot authenticate a past release by itself.
- Record exact decisions and missed changes, provisioning/setup and per-task counts, execution time, input and retained bytes, failures and rework. Keep measurements of compute distinct from attention, accessibility, maintenance and complete cost. A tie on decisions or a compute-only win is insufficient to promote to flagship. Demonstrated improvement requires an independently accepted real task and a meaningful measured advantage against the strong complete route.

An authorized, consenting member and an independent evaluator are still needed for a human comparison. The experiment grants no permission under issues #550 or #565 and has no external commitments.

## Français

L’essai, enregistré avant la construction, compare la réouverture d’une décision conservée à une comparaison structurée des mêmes publications par identifiant. Les versions sont figées ci-dessus. Le contrôle porte sur toutes les décisions communes, les nouvelles fiches, les révisions Git et les modifications volontaires du verdict, de l’alternative, du périmètre, de l’action suivante et de l’outil. Une référence absente ou modifiée impose un réexamen. Aucun gain de productivité ni statut de capacité phare ne sera attribué sans tâche réelle consentie, validation indépendante et comparaison des coûts.
