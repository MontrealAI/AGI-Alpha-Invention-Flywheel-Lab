# AGI ALPHA Flywheel: developer instructions

## Active direction: Blue Sky only

**Blue Sky Super Alpha:** inventing what the world could not economically do before, with an extraordinary advantage that creates immense value and compounds into vast, sustainable profit. This is the ambition, not an achieved result.

New mission work must pursue consequential newly attainable productive possibilities. The object of invention is the complete arrangement: valuable outcome, missing productive means, acceptance, voluntary participation and funded expansion. Established mathematics, engineering and economic mechanisms are welcome components. A routine service, optimizer, dashboard, paper or renamed historical example is not an eligible flagship by itself.

Use the [064 charter](campaign/blue-sky-064/CHARTER.md), [constructive mechanisms](campaign/blue-sky-064/MECHANISMS.md) and [current candidate evidence](campaign/blue-sky-064/CANDIDATES.md). Compare strong complete alternatives with equal construction, coordination and financing permissions. A technical tie does not automatically disqualify a genuinely new enabling construction; an incumbent complete route that already delivers the same outcome within the same full budget does falsify an alleged residual. If no candidate earns admission, continue constructive research and report the failed or unresolved condition. Do not promote a conventional fallback, invent a victory or write the desired paper in advance.

Starting capital is a decision variable for new work, not a fixed US$100,000 requirement. Jointly choose capital, productive strategy and financing terms under realistic voluntary investor, lender, buyer and rival responses. US$100,000 is a reference comparison and historical initial guess, not the mandatory primary arm, a floor or a ceiling. Include US$1 billion as a possible modeled financing scale where relevant, not as secured funds or an upper bound. The US$3 billion trailing-365-day complete-cost profit milestone remains unachieved and is not a ceiling. Game-theoretic optimality must name the game, information, feasible actions, financing availability, incentive assumptions, objective and proof or gap; it is not a globally optimal enterprise or guaranteed profit claim.

For each prospective capital/financing comparison, preregister the feasible set, funding source and timing, operating and investor objectives, complete costs, downside and liquidity constraints. Compare non-entry, staging, smaller and larger capital where relevant; account for financing fees, interest, repayment, dilution/control and opportunity cost without confusing noncash charges with cash outflows or double counting. Assumed equity or debt is not revenue, earned profit or an executed funding agreement. A financing promise is not spendable cash; modeled received funding becomes available only through declared supported native accounting. Preserve frozen historical US$100,000 experiments and their results unchanged; a new capital arm is a separately registered fresh comparison, never a retrospective top-up or reset.

Use included ChatGPT/Codex capabilities. Do not access paid model APIs, obtain or use model API keys, activate billing, buy services, or enable paid runners/custom images/GPU capacity. Standard public-repository GitHub Actions compute is free under GitHub's policy and is separate from ChatGPT Pro; never describe it as part of the Pro subscription or claim an account-wide billing audit. Existing account storage/billing settings are not permission for new charges.

Remove discretionary engineering mission timeouts and universal business-scale ceilings. Use actual platform windows, streaming/batching and resumable work; a standard GitHub-hosted job has a non-removable six-hour boundary. Cash conservation, protected obligations, supplier contracts, security, acceptance, interruption recovery and finite registered experiment conditions remain binding. Preserve historical limits and outcomes in their original source: new authority does not retroactively turn an interrupted experiment into a pass. Old experiments and conventional controls may be reproduced for engineering evidence, but they are not the new Blue Sky flagship.

## Primary AI-versus-human benchmark

The primary productivity comparison is **competent frontier AI/agents versus competent 100% human workers using conventional software**, under the same complete outcome, acceptance, rights, deadlines and service obligations. This benchmark is distinct from Blue Sky admission and causal invention claims; it does not relax either.

- **AI arm:** competently use the strongest appropriate currently available frontier models, agents and tools across research, acquisition, onboarding, production, verification, corrections, delivery, support and development/maintenance. Maximize end-to-end autonomy. Count and disclose all actual or explicitly modeled human intervention; justify its necessity, minimize it, and target its reduction as capabilities improve; do not invent employees, mandatory human QA or human-speed AI labor. Label any nonzero human work inside the AI operation as a hybrid, separately from a strict 100% autonomous result; unmet autonomous acceptance remains unmet. Necessary minimal human help is permitted in a separately labeled and fully costed hybrid operating case; it does not by itself negate profit or mission progress, only a strict 100%-autonomous claim. Normal buyer participation, consent or approval is not internal human labor; disclose its real friction and costs separately.
- **Human arm:** all worker research, attention, decisions, execution and coordination are performed by competent humans at realistic human speed, using conventional software but no frontier AI/agents. Preserve conventional software's genuine automation and machine-speed computation; do not slow software to human speed or artificially weaken the human workflow. Include normal market wages, employer costs, software and all other complete delivery costs.
- **Cost and time:** model realistic inference, tools, compute, retries, failures and latency, plus acquisition, onboarding, verification, corrections, support, development and residual obligations. Use grounded estimates and ranges when simulating; state provenance, uncertainty and sensitivity. Included execution is not evidence of zero economic resource cost, and unknown actual bills remain unknown.
- **Separate alternatives:** human-plus-AI providers, other autonomous providers, self-service and no purchase remain separately labeled comparators and buyer outside options. They must inform price, demand and commercial robustness, but must not silently replace the primary 100%-human productivity comparator.

Use [the commercial comparison](COMMERCIAL_COMPARISON.md#primary-comparison-and-correction) for economic reporting and [the developer guide](docs/harness/DEVELOPER_GUIDE.md#run-the-right-comparison) for execution. Realistic simulation requires no purchase, API key or paid model call; existing spending and access prohibitions remain unchanged.

## Invent the complete productive arrangement

**Transformational intelligence changes the game.** The defining shift is **from optimizing an enterprise to constructing the conditions under which better enterprises become possible**. Discover the valuable outcome, the means of producing and verifying it, and the arrangement that makes each participant's involvement worthwhile—together. The object of invention is the complete productive arrangement, not a transaction counter, a catalogue of mechanisms or dependence on this machine.

Search jointly over beneficiary outcomes, production methods, measurement/verification, financing, incentives and allocation of work and risk. The missing means may be an interface, process, instrument, representation, contract or coordination arrangement rather than a new algorithm. Compare strong existing complete alternatives, including human-plus-AI, self-service, partners and no purchase. Participation must be voluntary and worthwhile under explicit outside options; never force demand, hide risks, manufacture dependency or infer consent from modeled surplus. A proposed contract or partner is not an executed agreement or spending authority.

Aim to expand the valuable outcomes that can be sustainably achieved through voluntary cooperation. Count construction, search, unsuccessful attempts, acquisition, production, checking, maintenance, financing and residual obligations. Use real available native cash for actions; uncertain future value is a planning input, not revenue or funding. Profit supports continuation, but maximizing transaction count or organizational complexity is not the objective.

Seek a connected sequence: cycle one creates a useful capability, information, relationship, productive asset or unrestricted earnings that makes a specific successor economically reachable. Demonstrate the successor's acceptance and full costs, and distinguish **financed by**, **enabled by**, and merely **later than**. For a new causal enabling claim, compare against the strongest feasible route without that cycle-created asset under matched starting resources. Better equipment is not by itself a better inventor: improved discovery of such sequences is a further claim requiring fresh equal-content and strong conventional controls. This mission is not a claim that the current finite mechanisms have already delivered a full transformational breakthrough.

### Keep the flagship ahead of supporting work

Select a consequential frontier problem, identify the missing means preventing a valuable activity, then construct and test those means and pursue profitable complete delivery with large consequences. Keep that flagship explicit: a small decisive test is appropriate when the consequence is large, and known methods remain eligible when they enable the construction.

For a Super Alpha claim, preregister the newly feasible activity, economic feasibility threshold and its supporting evidence, hypothesized extraordinary advantage and decisive acceptance test in the existing arrangement card. Measure the advantage against the strongest complete alternatives under matched outcomes, reliability, rights, resources and full costs. Quantify its magnitude, uncertainty and durability under rival adoption and price responses; a component speedup or ordinary-reuse tie does not establish extraordinary system-level advantage. Test the connected successor above before claiming compounding. Keep aspiration, hypothesis, executed evidence and commercial achievement distinct; unknown demand or complete costs remain unknown.

Link each supporting task—checkers, dashboards, routine optimization, papers or infrastructure—to a concrete flagship dependency and the next consequential test or delivery it enables. Supporting work must not substitute for the flagship. When it dominates, reprioritize toward removing the missing-means constraint rather than expanding support for its own sake. This is a selection and execution discipline, not another admission-checker project, a novelty requirement for every component, a guarantee of breakthrough or profit, or permission to weaken evidence and authority boundaries.

## Learn, consolidate, delete

- Report a compact **tried → outcome → why (or unknown) → what must change to succeed → next decisive test/avoid** lesson. Lead with what was out-learned and the next useful decision, not a chronology of negative findings. Retain registered-arm denominators, outcomes, complete costs and essential reproducible evidence; do not hide losses or uncertainty. Consolidate repeated lessons by failure class and improve the fix, regression or applicability rule rather than accumulating transcripts. Reopen a rejected path only when new evidence or changed means addresses its failed condition; otherwise redirect the ambitious search.
- Delete redundant terminal failure logs, ZIPs, dumps and intermediate output from the current Git tree after extracting the useful lesson. Keep useful code even when one experiment loses. Do not replace deleted bulk with another archive or an ever-growing failure catalogue.
- Keep high-value reusable instruments and inspiring artifacts, reproducible inputs/procedures, acceptance checks and essential proof for published claims. Preserve complete accounting: incurred costs including failed attempts, refunds, liabilities, reservations and uncertainty. Do not erase losses, cherry-pick seeds or relabel failure as success.
- Before deletion, check actual readers, manifests, source maps, recovery needs and tests. A historical hash mention alone is not a permanent storage requirement: point descriptive historical references to a pinned commit. Migrate genuine executable dependencies safely; never claim removed inputs remain self-contained.
- Preserve live/needed recovery state, keys and account authority. Use normal recoverable commits; do not rewrite Git history, purge remote artifacts/releases irreversibly or change access without the required authorization. Never export credentials or reopen accidentally scanned private records. Never publish private source documents, quotations, filenames or diagrams; public guidance uses authorized requirements in original wording, not private-source uploads.
- Keep [lean lessons](docs/packaging/LEAN_EXPERIMENTS.md) concise. Review retained material by usefulness, applicability and replacement value, pruning obsolete duplicates. Finite memory cannot retain infinite information: the goal is continued learning through compression and selective retention, not unlimited lossless memory. This policy is not a claim that automatic memory compaction is implemented.

## Use strong mechanisms; earn claims

Start at the frontier of relevant knowledge: consult current primary sources and the strongest proven methods, reuse established results and work on the actual gap rather than rediscovering basics. Keep research bounded and decision-relevant, not an endless literature-screening gate. Use competent strong comparators and disclose assumptions and unknowns.

Adopt strong mechanisms from established theory, economics, engineering and successful historical practice without demanding a new research proof for each adoption. Cite the basis, check assumptions/applicability and test correct implementation. Game-theoretic optimality is conditional on its model, information and incentives; historical success supports use, not universality. Ordinary profitable activity does not require a novel invention advantage. Reserve new research proofs for genuinely new claims beyond established knowledge and expected human foresight. Routine correctness, security, cash-conservation and truthful outcome checks still apply; they are not a new research project at every step.

Use structural mapping, executable strategy construction/composition, evidence-grounded criticism, independent verification, adjudicated repair and applicability-aware retained procedures when they improve a real decision or outcome. See the guide for actual call paths, optional policies and limitations. A named module, template, mock or developer-written repair is not demonstrated autonomous discovery. When claiming a new retained-method advantage, compare on fresh work against equal-content ordinary reuse and strong conventional controls; include acquisition and verification costs. Do not impose that research claim as a prerequisite for using an established mechanism. Keep the incumbent when a relevant comparison shows it wins. Do not force invention or fixed R&D spending into profitable commerce.

Report commercial success, invention, retained-method advantage and physical/scientific expansion separately. Transformational work must remove a consequential constraint and validate the enabling instrument; routine repair is not automatically transformation. Optimality claims apply only to the tested feasible set, information and assumptions, never to all strategies or markets.

### Practical invention and operating loop

1. When fresh selection is justified, compare at least three materially different consequential possibilities jointly across valuable outcomes, business arrangements, and production/verification means. Include a new outcome, capability or coordination arrangement rather than only variants of the current service. Poor economics can justify replacing the service, market, contract or production means. Preserve frozen campaigns and accounts as evidence; a separately registered independent campaign may search beyond their catalogue. Do not silently change an active comparison or repeat selection without cause. Then freeze the selected permissions and acceptance contract for execution; distinguish hard constraints from assumptions and prerequisites of the current method. Do not silently relax acceptance after seeing results. Start from the strongest applicable known complete alternative.
2. Identify the binding constraint. Compare direct execution, reuse, causal transfer, complementary composition, a missing connector/instrument, and changing the process so the constraint no longer matters. Change the means, not the required outcome.
3. Make the proposal executable: permitted inputs, current state, prerequisites, actions/branches, outputs, effects, complete costs and stopping conditions. Check interfaces and shared resources; a sequence of impressive names is not a working mechanism.
4. Let evidence-grounded criticism propose a checkable counterexample or discriminating test. Adjudicate before repair. Resolve important uncertainty, act acceptably despite it, remove the dependency, or stop; consider that all initial explanations may be wrong.
5. Prefer production that carries source/dependency evidence over repeatedly reconstructing it. Reuse compatible generic work, but establish current task acceptance and authority. Critic agreement and internally consistent references alone do not establish truth.
6. Keep the useful executable instrument, concise applicability/avoidance lesson and, when supported, its constructor. Prune redundant terminal files. A working tool, successful transfer and a better discovery procedure are separate achievements.
7. Choose the next action jointly across profitable delivery, acquisition, reuse, enabling research and retaining cash. Use actual available funds and obligations, not forecast profit. Do not force novelty, a fixed research share or a fixed mechanism sequence.

Established methods need applicability and implementation checks, not a fresh invention claim; new beyond-established-knowledge claims need evidence proportionate to that claim.

Build for open-ended profitable expansion: **no arbitrary ceiling on buyers, businesses, services, transactions or profit**. Prospective simulations jointly select starting capital, strategy and financing under the variable-capital guidance above and target US$3 billion trailing-365-day profit after complete costs, measured by the native observer at a preregistered fixed endpoint; this is a notification milestone, not a maximum. Report the chosen capital and financing terms beside every result and retain US$100,000 as a reference comparison, not a universal primary constraint. Do not pool accounts, silently top up, reset or substitute annualized short cohorts for that result. Any modeled financing requires explicit registered terms and supported native accounting before it can fund an action; this guidance does not implement financing or change account guards. Support US$100 billion annual profit as an engineering stress case, not an upper limit or an achieved result; larger supported cases must remain possible. Profit is not revenue, cash, valuation or cumulative multi-year profit. Separate native executed/audited simulation, conditional projections and actual commerce. State horizon, capital, cost scope, uncertainties and outstanding obligations; unknown actual model/host billing remains unknown rather than becoming zero. Use the guide’s closed-episode and cost boundaries. Simulation does not establish actual customers or revenue, and no scale outcome is guaranteed.

Remove arbitrary business-scale rejection limits rather than merely raising their constants. Use scalable state, streaming/batching or equivalent execution and validated aggregation, with meaningful workload and financial-conservation tests—not just large-integer acceptance. Finite experiment horizons and actual platform capacity, liquidity, obligations, permissions and security remain enforceable. A resource-bounded checkpoint is not a universal commercial ceiling. Do not weaken guards or alter historical cohorts to manufacture scale.

Completion requires source-matched tests, independent review, normal qualified merge, public release and verified operation from that release—not just a plan, local pass or successful workflow badge. Report blockers exactly and continue independent useful work. Report execution status, scientific verdict and authorized next action separately. Distinguish a handoff received, acknowledged and adopted on verified source; do not infer one from another. Never claim a message was delivered, a task is active, a merge happened or a result is qualified without a receipt. Update concise developer guidance when an implementation or its qualification changes; preserve historical facts without preserving unnecessary bulk.
