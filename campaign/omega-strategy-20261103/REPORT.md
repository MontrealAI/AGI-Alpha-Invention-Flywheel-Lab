# Omega: operative commerce, conventional ties, and the remaining scale gap

**Completed local evidence:** all 48 registered native reference accounts passed
independent replay over [0, 8760). The consequential Super Alpha thesis and the
US$3 billion objective remain unestablished. This is a finite commercial
diagnostic, not a qualified scale path or continuation of the original account.

## What changed

Corrected PR191 already supplied an operative causal seller and native
commerce. This edition extends that base with variable prices and channels,
rival response, independent whole-bundle choice, process-workflow delivery,
staged capability selection, and stronger source, acceptance and recovery
guards. The seller obtains voluntary simulated orders, delivers actual
executable software, independently checks acceptance, collects or refunds
payment, preserves obligations, and can spend available earnings on a later
selected action. It can also buy information, lower a price, stop, or never
enter. Costs follow those actions. The earlier disabled-sales/fixed-expense
design remains a failed historical experiment, not the implementation base or
an intelligent strategy.

This is useful operational progress. It does **not** establish a consequential
new capability unavailable from strong ordinary means. The finite water and
process workflows retain their conventional ties; the new shared-qualification
discovery also ties a competent ordinary arrangement. No Super Alpha flagship
is admitted, and the US$3 billion primary objective is not achieved.

All money in this edition is **USD**. Commercial currency remains undecided.
No real payment, purchase, paid model call, API Platform access, key
retrieval, prospect contact, event, or storefront modification was performed.

## The working buyer journey

The buyer supplies a precise ambition, authorized inputs, a model family,
possible observations and recipes, units, acceptance limits and a resource
cap. The constructor compares admissible contingent experiments and produces
an executable policy. A separate verifier checks coverage, cost and production
acceptance. The local session requests an observation, records its cost,
selects the justified next experiment or recipe, and accepts or rejects the
reported production outcome. The final state preserves costs and the next
permitted action.

The CLI example incurs 4 modeled cents for its supplied observation and 18 for
production: 22 on that path, with a 31-cent worst admitted policy path. These
are abstract experiment units, not measured industrial costs. A conventional
constructor ties. The changed successor has a 27-cent bound because its input
costs change; that reduction is not a learned advantage.

The tests exercise extraction and execution of the delivered software bytes,
unclear/invalid inputs, resource exhaustion, duplicate actions, changed
payloads, interrupt/reopen, rejected observations, rejected production, model
unavailability and a buyer declining further work. Existing unrelated
databases are refused. The local help command retrieves and hashes actual
product documentation; unknown problems remain unresolved. Linux/Python 3.12
is qualified; a polished consumer installer and Windows/macOS are not.

The implemented compute route is deterministic local computation. It does not
require a language model. A requested local LLM is explicitly unavailable; an
external route requires informed data and spending permission and then reports
its unimplemented provider adapter. There is no silent paid fallback. The
model separately assigns buyer hardware amortization/electricity, capability,
effort, trust and compute cost. The US$0.11/task compute allowance is an
authored input, not a universal measured rate. No private customer data or
intellectual property is reused.

## The working seller transaction

For each selected offer the model pays the chosen acquisition contract,
presents the whole truthful bundle and its outside alternatives, and lets the
independently implemented buyer choose or decline. A positive utility
calculation does not itself authorize a transaction: explicit modeled consent
is required.

The native adapter binds the exact price, consent receipt, request, cost
schedule and acceptance contract. It checks current cash before costs or
funding, creates the native order, produces the deliverable, verifies it
independently, prepares all selected items and only then releases their
payments. Failed payment cancels unfunded offers; failed acceptance
compensates/refunds the whole bundle. A refund of one companion invalidates
another item's release. A completed refund is not counted as useful accepted
delivery.

Durable native event IDs and the original account identity make repeated or
interrupted execution idempotent. Mid-release failures preserve breach and the
investment stop. Settlement is sequential and recoverable, not a distributed
atomic transaction. Reopening without the required native state fails closed;
the program cannot manufacture replacement custody.

Known nonpayment, acceptance rejection and compensation are automatic within
the bound transaction invocation. Interruption, immutable-byte corruption or an
unexpected native outcome/cash divergence can stop execution; supported recovery
requires an externally initiated reopen with the original sidecars and event
IDs. This evaluation deliberately used a checkpoint and three experimenter-
initiated disjoint continuation workers. It does not demonstrate an autonomous
supervisor for unknown exceptions. The late-claim regression retains a payable
and stops investment; the market loop does not receive or resolve real late
claims. These experimenter-controlled invocations were not measured as human
attention. Customer-support, security-incident and legal-exception intervention
frequency and duration remain unknown; this is not a measured zero-intervention
service. No real founder labor or human service occurred.

One continuation process later disappeared for an unknown reason after 201
durable native actions in `case2-joint`. After confirming that its writer lease
was free, the experiment explicitly reopened that same account and unchanged
source. The original receipt and action prefix were preserved. The interrupted
partial attempt lacks final CPU/wall telemetry; measured recovery time must not
be presented as the complete run time. This operational interruption was not a
new seed, a different market scenario or a favorable reroll.

Webinar attendance and private-session outcomes are explicit fictional
adapters, not actual Zoom/Eventbrite delivery. A private session yields scoped
review/access value and a decision note, not a deployed technical outcome. A
webinar can reduce one bounded learning burden; it cannot manufacture full
software value or negative adoption cost.

## Declared game and strategic comparison

The players are a one-person seller, voluntary buyers with complete outside
options, modeled payment/hosting suppliers, and a rival that may imitate after
observed sales and a declared delay. Buyers have budgets, effort, trust,
prerequisites, compute costs and a no-purchase option. Free core and ordinary
reuse retain the full capability; the paid product does not win by degrading
the free route.

The seller sees its current public state, previous observed choices and
settlements, available cash, paid information signals and an entered rival's
published terms. Its selector does not receive scenario labels, hidden buyer
values, seeds or future payment/delivery outcomes. Potential outcomes are
addressed by seed/round/prospect/event; policies do not get favorable draws by
making fewer random calls. Rival response to prior sales is an endogenous
consequence of action.

Prices US$49/99/149/299/499, three finite acquisition contracts, all three
product subsets, information gathering, staged capability packaging, reuse and
withdrawal compete. Qualification consumes simulated time before a buyer's
commitment window. Research is chosen only if its declared public-prior value
of changing the action exceeds its task cost. Forecast revenue never becomes
cash.

The market simulator first compiles a causal plan, evaluating the selector at
each public-information state. The native executor then performs and
reconciles the corresponding financial/delivery actions. It does not give that
selector the plan's future outcomes. An unexpected native divergence stops the
account; this edition does not claim an online controller that automatically
repairs every unanticipated provider failure. A compiled decision trace alone
is not revenue: the reported commerce requires actual native execution and
independent replay.

## Service mechanics versus strategic coverage

All three products pass native acceptance, refund, idempotence and replay
tests. The registered market comparison nevertheless offers software only. Its
public prototype buyers always have zero founder-access value and unmet
private-session prerequisites, and the information model only updates four
ordinary segments. The environment contains a small authored eligible-founder
niche, but the policy has no belief or observation path to discover it. **Zero
private-session uptake is therefore not a demand or price falsification.** It
is a limitation of this policy model. The older corrected strategy has a
founder-access regime, but its different buyer model is not substituted for
validation of this selector.

Webinars are available in the menu and independent buyer choice, yet none is
selected in the registered plans. The finite acquisition menu reaches at most
12 prospects in a round; that restricts the economics of a 330-minute session.
It does not test a 500-seat event or demonstrate demand at that capacity. A
separate exact arithmetic check gives 20 fully paid/accepted attendees for
standalone nonpartner after-tax break-even, or 23 with the current partner
commission, under the stated costs. Even 12/12 targeted prospects paying and
accepting leaves modeled economic contribution −US$529.97 after the owner-time
charge. These are optimistic standalone projections, not a full joint-bundle
optimum or collected money. Reaching 20 or 23 attendees would require an additional
costed acquisition route beyond the registered channels; that route has not
been demonstrated. US$99 software sales must not be relabeled webinar attendance.

The forced-action service tests establish engineering behavior and stated time
accounting, not policy uptake. Zero founder minutes in the software experiment

## Both flywheels, with causal limits

The prospectively designated reference is `case4-staged`; it was not selected
after finding the highest profit. Its native receipt determines whether the
process-package action was actually funded from available earnings after
earlier accepted sales. Current cash and protected obligations are checked at
