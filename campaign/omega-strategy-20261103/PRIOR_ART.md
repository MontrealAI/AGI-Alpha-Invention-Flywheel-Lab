## One new residual worth a small discovery step

**Hypothesis:** a cohort of small industrial operators may be unable to obtain a common qualified component before their adoption deadlines because qualification, buyer commitments, financing and scarce validation capacity must be coordinated together. An inspectable, automatically executed conditional schedule might make an otherwise uneconomic small cohort viable. The unresolved advantage is the **complete cost and feasibility of this particular arrangement**, not invention of crowdfunding, qualification reuse or coalition optimization.

DNV's current joint-industry-project description explicitly covers work too costly or complex for one party, shared cost/benefit and co-funding by two or more partners [S5]. Its welding guideline addresses reuse of previously qualified procedures through historical-data validation or supplementary testing [S6]. These sources defeat any broad claim that ordinary industry cannot coordinate or reuse qualifications. They do not establish costs or availability for the proposed small cohort. That narrower gap remains **unknown**, not proven.

The smallest discriminating discovery experiment is:

1. **Freeze one complete contract and three heterogeneous buyers.** Each has a required acceptance envelope, deadline, outside alternative, reusable-rights scope and right to decline. Declare whether a shared certificate actually covers each mission; no simulator may assume regulatory or purchaser acceptance.
2. **Couple real constraints in a finite authored test.** One qualification slot, a completion delay, a failure/refund branch, funding settlement delay and commitments that may expire. Price, conditional coalition, staged test and non-entry are available to both arms. Commitments are not native cash; a funding action must use existing supported native accounting or be reported unavailable.
3. **Use the strong control.** Give an ordinary specialist organizer the same complete contract, information, reusable content, coordination rights and exact solver. Separately compare individual commissioning and ordinary all-or-nothing funding. Do not invent slow computation or mandatory staff for the conventional route.
4. **Independently attack the claim.** Check buyer best responses, shared-information histories, each funded prefix, qualification capacity, deadline, failure obligations and actual accepted deliverables. Change one buyer's acceptance, a qualification result or a settlement time; the route must replan or stop. A common certificate cannot silently substitute for mission-specific acceptance.
5. **Decision:** if the ordinary complete route ties, reject an additional harness-method advantage and retain only useful implementation. If both routes fail, record the exact missing contract/observation rather than claiming impossibility. Only a plausible residual warrants acquiring a real public/authorized qualification specification and actual cost/capacity evidence; no outreach or real financing is authorized here.

The current market extension is a prerequisite investigation: it makes buyer choice, complete cost, information and native delivery consequences executable. The separate finite probe below implements an **authored feasibility model** of shared qualification; neither it nor the market extension executes a real shared-qualification contract, produces industrial qualification, or converts hypothetical value into demand. A technical tie could still leave ordinary voluntary commerce viable; a Super Alpha claim needs the consequential enabling outcome and strongest complete comparison together.

## Executed discovery result: shared qualification

**2026-11-03 result: 12/12 ordinary-control ties; four conditionally feasible positive-margin cohorts and eight non-entry outcomes. No additional harness-method advantage.** The discovery step above was executed in [qualification_pool.py](../../omega/qualification_pool.py), with [13 adverse/control tests](../../tests/test_omega_qualification_pool.py) passing. This is a finite feasibility instrument, not a new ledger or orchestration framework.

The purpose is joint qualification of an industrial sensor adapter covering three suppliers' declared interfaces and accuracy requirements. The candidate jointly chooses qualification scope, coalition, fee commitments and start date. The unrestricted conventional organizer enumerates the same complete menu and uses separately implemented algebraic cash-prefix checks; it does not call the candidate's eligibility or event checker. Both use identical information, rights, capacity, budgets and acceptance. Buyer participation is voluntary under explicit risk-neutral expected utility and outside alternatives; indifference declines.

These are **separate planning scenarios, not accounts or additive earnings**. Individual-only commissioning has no positive-margin route in all twelve; the unrestricted conventional organizer obtains every shared-route benefit. The comparison therefore demonstrates a familiar conditional cooperation benefit, not a newly invented capability or evidence that incumbent organizers cannot deliver it.

Declared design/qualification/delivery task costs are $9,000–$10,000. Each buyer's modeled accepted value is $10,000 and adoption cost is $500; selected contributions are $5,500/$4,000/$2,500. Qualification succeeds with authored probability 0.9. Refund promises range from 25% to 100%, plus $100 failure handling, fully reserved until acceptance. These values and supplier cash caps are assumptions, not quotations, fresh seed capital or observed demand. In the fully refunded funded case, success margin is $3,000 and failure margin is **−$9,100**; expected margin is not guaranteed profit. Actual industrial engineering, legal acceptance and complete enterprise costs remain unmeasured.

The decisive timing check rejects $3,099.99 of available cash when $3,000 is due before same-day collections and $100 must remain reserved; **$3,100 is sufficient**. Pledges and later cash cannot repair the earlier prefix. Tests also reject unavailable interior-day capacity, incompatible acceptance, unauthorized shared reuse, missing buyer funds, duplicate pledges and certain qualification failure. No native postings or funded accounts are created.
