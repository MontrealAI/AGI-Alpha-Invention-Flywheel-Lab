# Adaptive-012: executable enterprise design

This edition implements the [current mandate](MANDATE.md) and [owner steering](STEERING.md). Enterprise-011 remains a separate historical experiment with unchanged published conclusions. This document explains mechanisms and acceptance boundaries; it reports no fresh-confirmation outcome. The exact frozen source, policy and protocol in `FROZEN012.json` control a particular run.

## Complete offers govern production

`adaptive_enterprise/strategies.py` defines the complete offer: observable segment qualification, problem, promised scope, executable production and verification, price, payment, renewal, remedy, deadline, support, channel, authorized context, capacity, upfront resources, later obligations, applicability, provenance and falsifiers. Public pricing metadata is a closed interface; private reservation values, rival costs, future shocks, evaluation labels and generation seeds are excluded from policy observations.

An offer changes an actual procedure or promise. Canonical intake handles an admitted canonical source. Generated-schema intake constructs and executes a typed field mapping. A staged offer produces an explicitly narrower intake-readiness packet when supplied facts cannot support the original outcome. Choosing another business offer needs no claim of invention; claiming verified criticism or repair requires its evidence chain.

The initial primitive contracts are reconciliation, KPI variance and source-bound evidence matrices. They are the qualified executable scope of this increment, not a service-count ceiling. A new outcome requires an implemented producer, trusted acceptance contract, complete obligations, authorized input and new qualification. Catalogue entries alone cannot become accepted services.

The fourth contract is an operations bundle. It reexecutes and independently checks three existing primitive packets. Its three component `work_keys` are shared with the individual contracts; operating decisions consume those identities so the same work cannot generate both individual and bundle revenue. One buyer makes one joint bundle choice at a joint price computed through the existing bundle-price procedure. Three components require three production-capacity units. Eight bundle configurations are alternative packaging of existing work, not eight additional independent work units.

## Obstacle, construction, criticism and retention

`instruments.py` enumerates bounded column-role assignments and tests exact units, record identity, source-row conservation and customer/reference consistency. It receives source exports and unit declarations, not an expected mapping. Generated mappings execute against arbitrary fresh header names; unavailable monetary units are never guessed. The independently implemented checker reconstructs the adaptation and checks the resulting service outcome.

The executable chain is:

1. Preserve the actual raw source, attempted artifact and promised offer.
2. Recompute fixed-checker and promised-contract rejection. Reexecute the original method so a controller cannot invent an obstacle by merely choosing the wrong method.
3. Bind input, artifact, rejection receipt, offer and checker/producer/strategy source identities.
4. Construct the changed method or explicitly narrower scope, charge its construction allocation, execute it and independently check the repair.
5. Retain the recomputable adjudication and applicability constraints, then use them on fresh source exports.

`adjudicate_and_adapt` and `retain_failure` reject unsupported verification flags, accepted work relabeled as failure and changed evidence digests. Their classification and invariant-extraction rules are developer-authored. The bounded search generates executable mappings; it does not establish autonomous discovery of a novel algorithm.

Retention comparisons use the same source and tools for generated search, retained constraints, equal-content ordinary reuse and competent conventional pruning. Row visits count mapping-stage work only; CPU is measured separately. A tie with ordinary reuse or a conventional winner does not become an invention advantage. Current execution receipts distinguish the native delivered artifact from a rerun's search metadata and require identical normalized input and useful outcome before binding to the native delivery.

An accepted readiness packet completes only its stated readiness promise. A later full outcome requires a separate, provenance-bound customer-supplied source event. Selling readiness does not manufacture absent facts. Cohort generation changes amounts, identities, source facts and aliased headers under a registered rule; exposed development cases remain separate from confirmation sources.

## Operating decisions and financial authority

Persistent simulated buyers, rivals and suppliers have budgets, capacity, decision timing and histories. Rivals react to delayed observations; the seller learns from permitted delayed feedback. Pending payments remain pending until the modeled settlement rule resolves them. Forecasts are hypotheses, not current receipts.

The operating controller may change scope, packaging, price, channel, segment, capacity, supplier diversity, instruments or withdrawal. Service coverage belongs in qualification tests; production need not finish an inventory or every operating cycle. Refusing all work or preserving seed capital alone cannot demonstrate a commercial flywheel.

`Account` is the authority for the native ledger. Every earned-funded plan binds the current account-state digest and authorized action digest, reserves present resources and declared later exposure before execution, and reconciles completed and unused commitments. Single-writer ownership, replay, duplicate-event defenses and stale-state checks protect the adaptive namespace. Local POSIX ownership does not establish distributed consensus. Hypothetical future income cannot fund present commitments.

Expansion evidence records whether a paid construction is subsequently used on actual accepted and settled work. That use is distinct from its incremental causal profit against a matched control. Scale projections use separate declared capital, demand and action assumptions; their cash never enters the native operating account.

## Costs, comparison and limits

Actual artifact production, normalization, checking, CPU and decision traces execute. Service executors make no paid frontier-model calls. Host assistant billing is unavailable, so research, setup, failed attempts, learning, acquisition, onboarding, production, checking, corrections, support, maintenance, infrastructure, fees and obligations use disclosed conditional resource envelopes. No hypothetical human employee or CPU-to-human-hour conversion is inserted. Provider onboarding, buyer effort and external charges remain distinct; authorized customer-context onboarding is charged once.

The primary economic reference is competent human delivery of equivalent small, source-bound outcomes with checking, corrections, support and tools. Human task times, loaded-rate proxies, service waiting times and buyer values are authored scenario inputs, not measured labor receipts or observed selling prices. Relevant rival AI, self-service and no-purchase choices remain available. Exact costs, sensitivities, pricing boundaries and risk tolerances are frozen with each run.

| Constraint class | Treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Hard requirements | Authorization, truthful scope, actual acceptance, native conservation, restricted funds, state-bound commitments and hidden-information separation remain mandatory. |
| Environmental limits | Supplier capacity, latency, available source facts, reachable opportunities and imitation are declared scenario limits. Costed actions may change supported limits, with delays and obligations represented. |
| Revisable policy | Marketing allocation, cash buffers, prices, experiment cadence, offer mix, capacity investment and stopping choices may change within the frozen learning/action rules. Conservative settings are scenarios, not universal business laws. |

## Independent verdicts and qualification

| Verdict | Required evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Conditional simulated commercial flywheel | Repeated accepted and settled work, complete-cost surplus, available earned funds and source-traceable financing of further profitable delivery under the registered economic and risk criteria. |
| Competitive adaptation | Matched comparisons with strategic public priors, a strong fixed policy, an ordinary adaptive policy and targeted ablations; report action-space, information and cost differences. |
| Invention or retained-capability advantage | Executed construction and fresh-use benefit beyond equal-content ordinary reuse and competent conventional methods, including acquisition, adaptation and verification costs. |
| Productive expansion | A costed, reserved construction or capacity change actually used on subsequent accepted work; causal incremental benefit is separately compared. |
| Actual commercial operation | Actual authorized customers and receipts. Simulated buyers, settlements and scale projections do not establish this verdict. |

These verdicts are not a global conjunction. Confirmation freezes the generator, source, observation interface, learning algorithm, actions, budgets, scenarios, thresholds and stopping rules before protected outcomes are inspected. Online adaptation from allowed observations remains permitted. Published evidence retains failures, withdrawals, pending obligations, sensitivities, complete task coverage and recoverable checkpoints. Audit reconstructs native finance and report claims against the frozen package; package replay is required before qualification is reported.
