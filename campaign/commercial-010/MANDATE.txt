1. Implement the corrected success definitions

A fixed-capability autonomous commercial flywheel can succeed independently of recursive invention. Its essential loop is useful delivery, customer acceptance, collection, complete-cost surplus, available funds and further profitable delivery financed from those funds. Better algorithms, an improved successor, novel capabilities and superiority over every other AI operator are not prerequisites.

Implement separate machine-readable verdicts and explanations for:

• Profitable autonomous service: a complete accepted delivery has positive economics under the declared evidence scope.
• Simulated commercial flywheel: executed production and a strategic simulated commercial environment support repeat profitable delivery, settlement and earned-funded continuation under stated conditions.
• Observed commercial flywheel: actual accepted deliveries, actual collections, complete costs and available earned cash support repeated commercial operation.
• Capability-compounding flywheel: retained verified mechanisms improve fresh discovery or construction against appropriate controls.
• Blue Sky enablement: a reusable new means of discovery or production opens an important class of previously inaccessible work.

These are independent, potentially complementary conclusions. Fixed-capability success can fully satisfy the COMMERCIAL mission while the invention and Blue Sky lanes remain incomplete. Do not implement a global AND condition requiring all five. A successful simulated commercial loop deserves an explicit conditional success conclusion even without live customers, increasing capability or Blue Sky admission. Conversely, simulated success must never silently become observed commercial success.

Simulation is an evidence scope, not a universal HOLD. Empirical unknowns become explicit assumptions, bounded ranges and discriminating experiments. Report where success survives and where it fails. Negative findings are learning, not the requested positive mission outcome; use them to choose the next bounded action rather than stopping at a rejection report.

5. Implement a strategic simulated commercial environment

Build on existing executable paths rather than adding a decorative game-theory wrapper. Model buyers, competing providers and relevant suppliers as agents with explicit objectives, information, feasible actions and outside options. Their decisions must affect transactions and future states.

Buyer participation should depend on expected utility, price, quality, delivery risk, trust, switching costs and alternatives. Competitors must be able to change prices, quality, capacity or targeting under explicit response rules. Suppliers can alter terms or availability where relevant. Include imperfect information, reputation updates, disputes and payment incentives. Explain which actors are strategic, which use bounded heuristics and which are exogenous.

Specify payoffs and timing. Test participation constraints, profitable deviations or best-response consistency appropriate to the selected model. Do not claim equilibrium merely because a numerical loop converges. Report solution residuals, approximation limits and cases where strategic response destroys the margin.

Calibrate from credible sources where available, recording date, units and applicability. Otherwise use transparent ranges and alternative plausible models. Stress correlated conditions, not only one-parameter-at-a-time variations: lower demand alongside price competition, delayed settlement, increased revisions, tool-price changes and trust shocks. Distinguish structural uncertainty from random variation.

Do not force purchases, repeat orders or collection to produce success. Acquisition must consume resources and compete with outside options. Buyers may decline, switch, dispute or churn. Demand saturation and capacity constraints must be able to limit growth. Neither an enormous assumed market nor the owner’s scale ambition establishes demand.

Maintain strictly labeled simulated customers, orders and payments. No real outreach, spending, payment execution or credential changes are authorized by this brief beyond permissions already independently established for the executing team.
