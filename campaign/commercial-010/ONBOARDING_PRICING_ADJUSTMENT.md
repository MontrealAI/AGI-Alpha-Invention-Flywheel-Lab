# Owner adjustment autonomous onboarding and strategic pricing

The owner supplied this adjustment during the existing campaign on 30 October 2026. It continues PR16 and does not replenish the allocation or authorize spending, real commerce, outreach or access changes. Preserve the original zero-sales run and every prior failure.

AI agents perform onboarding, setup, data preparation, verification, corrections, acquisition and support. Charge their actual inference, tools, computation, infrastructure and other resources. Do not introduce hypothetical human staffing or conventional agency overhead. The expectation is that agent-operated onboarding can be very inexpensive; exact unmetered host bills remain unknown.

Separate agent-operated onboarding expenditure, customer switching/approval/integration/attention costs, and external fees or obligations. Record payer, evidence, uncertainty, timing and recurrence. Do not charge a one-time setup on repeat orders or amortize it over guaranteed future orders. Test first-order and repeat economics separately. Determine whether the original zero-sales result reflects a supported constraint, an error or an unmeasured assumption; explore defensible friction ranges including genuinely low-friction autonomous preparation.

Human-delivered complete cost remains the initial commercial anchor with a small discount. Optimize price, discount, offer and the resulting win probability jointly for expected complete-cost contribution and sustainable cash growth. Neither maximum win rate nor the highest advertised price is the objective. There is no predetermined optimal five-percent discount or guaranteed acceptance probability. AI costs remain a margin advantage for the flywheel.

Buyers retain private value/reservation information. Sellers observe only declared public or realized transaction information and may use an explicit uncertain prior. Include the human alternative, credible rival and DIY offers, quality, trust, turnaround, switching, finite capacity, acquisition failures, retries, disputes, refunds and delayed collections. Future customer value requires explicit retention and repeat assumptions. Scope any claimed optimum to the stated game, information structure, response rules and feasible candidate set; numerical convergence or a favorable authored world is not an equilibrium certificate.

Credit premium differentiation only when the workflow delivers it. If the premium offer fails, improve actual value or onboarding effort, target an appropriate segment, pivot service, or decline unprofitable work. Do not force purchases or automatically collapse prices toward AI costs.

Run a matched four-arm comparison under frozen policies and fresh common scenario seeds:

1. Original fixed-discount pricing with original onboarding assumptions.
2. Corrected autonomous onboarding with that fixed-discount pricing.
3. Strategic pricing with original onboarding assumptions.
4. Both corrections together.

Hold resource budgets and exogenous scenarios fixed; matched seed labels alone are insufficient if conditional random draws change shocks across arms. Report price distributions, win probabilities, accepted quality, complete-cost contribution, cash survival, repeat demand, reserves, reinvestment and sensitivity. Low customer friction remains a hypothesis where not measured; an invented original friction is not preserved as fact merely because it appeared first.

Execute actual source-bound service production and independent acceptance, then strategic simulated acquisition, settlement and repeat production financed from prior unrestricted simulated earnings. A fixed-capability simulated commercial flywheel can qualify independently of observed receipts, invention improvement and Blue Sky. All customers, orders and payments remain strictly simulated.
