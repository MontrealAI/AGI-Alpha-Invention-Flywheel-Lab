# Prespecified event-controller experiment

The machine-readable source/configuration/protocol freeze produced by `python run_growth012.py --output NEW --freeze-only` is authoritative. The registered allocation bounds work. `--resume` executes that unchanged freeze; source drift is refused. `--smoke` is exposed development engineering evidence, not confirmation.

First compare baseline, simple and joint policies under identical economic assumptions, starting capital, service tools, independent acceptance, rival/buyer behavior, quality and native permanent tail obligations. The baseline is explicitly reconstructed with fixed shares and a reporting-clock cadence; the original PR19 aggregate model is reproduced separately. The simpler policy has the same feasible economic choices. Paired exogenous draws are keyed by customer, period and event; policy execution does not consume a shared sequential RNG. Customer latent values, budgets and future draws remain in the environment. Declared segment/cost priors are assumptions, not observations.

The fixed protected design uses six previously uninspected primary seeds across ordinary, low-demand, price-war and correlated-shock conditions, with four primary seeds also used for separately costed business interventions in ordinary and low-demand conditions. No outcome-driven stopping or protected-result retuning is allowed. The exact task list and arms are frozen before evaluation. The sample targets material finite-design differences within the actual compute budget; it cannot support rare-risk or real-market confidence claims. Long-horizon and large-scale feasibility remains unresolved when the exact observed horizon cannot distinguish it.

Actual deterministic service work is executed after funded commitment and separately checked before simulated settlement. Repeated collections arise from buyer choice and fulfilled work. Qualified synthetic work stock is an experiment boundary, not a demand law. The event clock enforces positive production time and finite rate/capacity; acceptance, collection and reserve maturity have their specified event times. Reporting does not create an operational delay. Acquired capacity and every acquisition, including losses, consume cash. Later refunds, costs, taxes and outstanding reserves remain in native accounting; projected earnings never finance today's commitment.

The optional finite-liability contract is a separate negotiated hypothetical arrangement, with setup, fees and buyer friction. It preserves principal-plus-fee reserves until explicit maturity, then frees native eligible funds without creating cash/revenue/profit. It is not retroactively applied to existing contracts. Human service is the premium price anchor; rival AI, complete human-plus-AI, self-service with customer effort and no-purchase alternatives enter choice. Rivals react after observing public quotes on their declared delay; their own costs constrain their response.

Report all trajectories, failures, withdrawals and unreached targets. Report trailing365-day collected revenue separately from cumulative revenue, annualized run rate and profit. Conditional hitting-time distributions accompany attainment probabilities and unconditional nonreach. Larger milestones include $1M, $10M, $100M, $1B and $10B. No terminal enterprise value or inserted growth multiplier is permitted. An unvalidated aggregate projection cannot be called individually executed commerce.

Next action selection must preserve cash where marginal expansion loses money. Compare cost, payback, risk and acceleration explicitly; report conditional trade-offs. Inverse planning identifies only the smallest tested intervention meeting its stated criterion, never an unexecuted global minimum. Actual commerce, capability-compounding invention and physical expansion remain separate claims. The finite-contract implementation is developer-authored; generated operating choices do not establish autonomous invention of that contract.
