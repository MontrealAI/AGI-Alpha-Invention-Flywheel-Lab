# Public anchors and uncalibrated assumptions

Read November 4, 2026. These observations constrain a conditional decision model. They do not establish MONTREAL.AI demand, available financing, actual revenue or a guarantee.

## Observed anchors

1. **Firm population:** US Census SUSB 2022 national employer-firm total is 6,395,635: 93,341 firms with 100–499 employees and 21,041 with 500+. Subtracting disjoint bands gives 6,281,253 below 100. Released April 10, 2025. These are historical firms, not qualified buyers. [Primary data](https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/susb/tables/2022/us_state_naics_detailedsizes_2022.txt).
2. **Procurement:** Vertice's Q3 2026 observed means are 53.9 days for new purchases, 90.5 for renewals and 77.6 overall. The source describes over $75 billion of managed global spend. Internal procurement is not the full cold-contact-to-revenue journey. Two model periods and the three-period stress are illustrative discretizations, not fitted transaction distributions. [Primary report](https://www.vertice.one/insights/procurement-cycle-time).
3. **Collections:** Atradius North America 2026 reports 23% of receivables overdue and typical DSO around two months. Nearly half of suppliers report bad debt below 1%; fewer than one quarter report above 2%. The model's payment delays and revenue haircuts approximate these broad observations. Revenue-weighted loss is not an independent customer-default probability. [Primary survey](https://atradius.us/knowledge-and-research/reports/b2b-payment-practices-trends-in-north-america-2026).
4. **Human compensation:** BLS June 2026 loaded employer compensation is $74.42/hour for professional occupations, $38.27 for office/administrative support and $89.14 for management/business/financial occupations. Benefits are already included. The model uses $74.42 once; its 20/200/2,000 task-hour assumptions are not measured. [Primary table](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/ecec.t02.htm).
5. **AI resource valuation:** Official API pricing read November 4, 2026 lists GPT-6.1 Sol at $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens. The model hypothesizes 1/10/100 million input tokens and 10% as many output tokens, doubles these for verification/retries, then adds $150/$1,500/$15,000 per segment for tools, onboarding and support. Task tokens, error rates and actual resource bills are not measured. No API calls, keys, purchases or new charges occur. [Official pricing](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/pricing).

## Hypotheses, not measurements

The following remain authored: 10% eligibility; four opportunities per firm-year; buyer values and budgets; type weights; task hours; output quality and acceptance; referral/retention; acquisition conversion and prices; overlap; initial organic reach; $50,000 development; $2,000 monthly sales-platform cost; supplier-capacity pricing; 2% refunds; competitor behavior; 26.5% tax; and the 15% investor hurdle. Human-plus-AI and self-service workload and quality alternatives are also hypotheses. The delivered simulations must disclose these dependencies, not describe them as calibrated willingness to pay or measured autonomous productivity.

Actual industry-specific demand, autonomous acceptance, procurement distributions and complete costs remain missing. Public population and friction data improve structural realism but cannot identify those missing quantities.

## Adoption is not purchase demand

The Census May 2026 article reports 17–20% any-function AI use overall and 37% among firms with 250+ employees. A November 2025 wording change prevents naive comparison with earlier figures. The UK survey published October 29, 2026 reports 32% adoption, but 47% of respondents able to estimate spending reported no AI expenditure in 2025, and 81% applied human input or oversight. Neither source is converted into a sales probability.

[Census article](https://www.census.gov/library/stories/2026/05/ai-use-businesses.html) · [UK executive summary](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/ai-adoption-survey-2026/ai-adoption-survey-report-2026-executive-summary)

This is evidence-informed friction and stronger structural realism, **not a validated high-fidelity enterprise forecast**. It does not supersede the native paid-reach experiment's separate small-cohort result.
