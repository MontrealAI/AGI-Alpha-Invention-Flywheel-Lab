# Competitive commercial rerun: result and decision

**The stronger comparison does not support scaling this generic analytical-service arrangement.** It does not evaluate an existing company's finances, invalidate the earlier small paid-reach success, or establish an economy-wide limit.

All 1,440 registered five-year projections completed on unchanged model SHA256 `a458dffebef7467313db54ad984d5b35e1cbe2015f0a0d23731d8ca4eb9e52db`. No run has positive profit in its final 365-day period. Final-year outcomes range from **−$24,000 to $0**. Zero means stopped entry, not a thriving break-even enterprise. Cumulative five-year losses range from **$97,573.61 to $171,547.55**. No $3 billion or $10 billion annual-profit result, investor-hurdle success, new native operating qualification or actual revenue was established.

The non-entry comparator preserves capital and makes zero operating profit, beating every entered policy. It does not earn the investor opportunity return. Capital is not revenue: increasing initial equity from the $100,000 reference to $1 million, $100 million or $1 billion does not fix negative acquisition economics.

## What changed the decision

All tested policies declined paid acquisition. Competent rival responses, voluntary buyer alternatives and the declared contact/resource costs removed the attractive marginal investment. Organic opportunities then failed to cover the modeled fixed resource expense. Organic, staged and adaptive policies consequently coincide here. This is an informative policy tie, not evidence that ordinary commerce cannot succeed.

Independent engineering review caught a major false advantage before confirmation: the initial coarse price grid prevented a rival from profitably undercutting. Refinement removed that artifact. The final half-cent grid still has refinement sensitivity, so its zero enumeration gap is a **finite one-period commitment-game result**, not continuous-price or global enterprise optimality. The user-requested universal real-world optimality cannot be inferred from it.

The model now includes integer paid contact rights, strong buyer outside options with budgets, a profit-maximizing rival that may withdraw, funded dated capacity, correlated shocks, procurement and collection delays, production reserves, tax arrears and voluntary non-entry. It does not implement a globally optimized entry/exit policy, persistent identified buyer wallets, empirically measured willingness to pay or accepted native production at projected scale.

## Realism and limits

[Public sources](SOURCES.md) anchor firm counts, procurement friction, payment behavior, loaded human compensation and model-resource prices. Eligibility, values, task hours, autonomous acceptance, acquisition effectiveness, supplier congestion, overhead and the game remain hypotheses. This is a more disciplined stress test, **not a calibrated forecast of MONTREAL.AI**. The 100%-human task-cost reference is not a measured end-to-end productivity experiment. No contractor or employee was invented and charged as actual labor.

The historical US population is a declared market domain, not a universal demand ceiling. No high-volume scenario is created merely to meet a target. A different productive outcome, price/value relationship, actual installed reach or independently supported cost advantage requires its own registered comparison.

## Distilled lesson and next action

**Tried:** expand a generic service under explicit competitive and financial constraints. **Observed:** paid acquisition was not justified; non-entry wins. **Why:** the declared offer has insufficient defensible contribution after strong substitutes and contact/capacity costs. **Next:** construct and test a consequential complete arrangement, with a falsifiable accepted outcome and strong existing alternatives, rather than increase assumed demand or polish the losing arrangement.

Commercial success, enabling invention, better retained discovery, and physical qualification remain separate claims. The previously successful simulated paid-reach loop retains its original evidence and limitations.
