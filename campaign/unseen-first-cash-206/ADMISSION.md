## Prospective gates

1. **Integrity:** Published claims trace to a frozen technical report. Positive and negative evidence are both visible. No false endorsement, whole-reader speedup, forecasted ROI, artificial scarcity, or success counter. FR/EN order links use only the existing official shop URL.
2. **Buyer demand:** Count actual independently attributable outside customer purchases, not website traffic, test checkouts or internal/token transfers. A valid first sale requires authenticated merchant/order confirmation, settled funds, refund/dispute state and evidence of the user's voluntary agreement to the terms. Never publish customer details or financial credentials.
3. **Accepted result:** Deliver exactly the agreed 8–12-page personalized EN or FR PDF after purchaser supplies their public company website. Record buyer acceptance or documented delivery status, corrections and refunds. The generic public normalizer example is **not** the purchased custom dossier.
4. **Full economics:** Record the actual fixed and variable costs of research, discovery, production, independent verification, infrastructure, acquisition, payment fees, failures, support, commitments, tax/reserve treatment and human interventions, separating observed from unknown. Collected net of fees is not automatically unrestricted profit.
5. **Compounding:** After one accepted, collected and profitable sale, retain the traceable useful method, then attempt the second **different company** under a fresh requirement and strong incumbent comparator. Show reduced full costs or improved accepted result vs both no-inheritance and equally endowed ordinary reuse. Otherwise classify reuse/compounding as unproven.

## Decision rules

- **GO, first real dollar:** documented, attributable external cash collection (in the originally paid currency) and properly scoped delivery, with no known payment reversal; profit/scale remain separate verdicts.
- **GO, profitable repeated operation:** at least two distinct accepted and collected customer deliveries, positive complete-cost profit after obligations, and traceable reinvestment from genuinely unrestricted earned cash.
- **GO, invention flywheel:** fresh successor outperforms competent controls after accounting for transfer and requalification costs. This is a distinct, higher bar; never infer it from the first sale.
- **HOLD:** no independently verified buyer, settlement, acceptance, full cost or rights. A meaningful technical prototype can remain useful even when cash is absent.
- **STOP/PIVOT:** a strong complete incumbent already delivers the same result at comparable or better quality, cost and risk; or the claimed buyer outcome cannot be produced safely within the actual permissions and rights.
