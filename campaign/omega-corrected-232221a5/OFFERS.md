## Cost consequences

The new comparison retains the owner's human/hybrid resource proxy as a distinct
control. Autonomous low/central/high construction envelopes are $100/$1,000/
$10,000, allocated to selected product tasks, not an unconditional R&D invoice.
Information is an optional $1 machine-work action. Paid acquisition is $15 per
three-prospect attempt, including attempts reaching nobody. These are authored
ranges, not observed costs, demand or productivity evidence. Nonentry incurs no
new operating action expense; its historical sunk loss remains.

Native order invoices retain the declared fulfillment/verification/hosting and
exception envelopes and payment costs. The inherited marginal fulfillment
assumptions are $1/download, $0.25/seat and $1/private-session order; their small
size is an optimistic assumption, not a measured complete production bill.
Refunds retain incurred processing/fulfillment costs. Maturity releases reserves
without creating revenue. Residual claims remain protected; a late claim can stop
investment. No blanket free support or recurring personal-support salary is added.
Unknown security, fraud, mandatory redress, rights and actual infrastructure costs
are disclosed as unresolved commercial dependencies, not silently measured zeros.

## Buyer compute and rights

Implemented runtime is deterministic local computation; it makes no model calls.
The buyer supplies valid integer structured inputs, permissions, realistic physical
assumptions and installed pinned dependencies. Hardware, electricity, time, privacy
and domain validation are the buyer's costs. Physical and regulatory validation is
absent. Oversized requests can stop safely; local does not mean free or universally
capable. No actual language-model routing or local model-capability benchmark was
implemented. External proposals are manually supplied under the buyer's explicit
route choice; no key is requested, account called, data uploaded or bill incurred.

Dependencies are pinned in `omega/requirements.txt`; license review must include
the exact wheels and transitive/bundled notices before a real redistribution.
Installed metadata inspected for the tested versions identifies BSD-3-Clause core
terms for NumPy 2.3.5 and SciPy 1.17.0, and `Apache-2.0 OR BSD-3-Clause` for
cryptography 50.0.2. Binary bundles include additional notices; this is not a
completed distribution clearance. Those dependency grants do not fill the missing
root grant or establish contributor ownership. Contributions, privacy,
consumer obligations and platform eligibility remain unresolved launch checks.
They do not structurally disable conditional simulated commerce.

## Scale implication

At $299, even **zero costs** require more than 10 million accepted downloads in a
rolling year to reach $3 billion profit. Costs, tax, refunds and outside options
increase that volume. The executed finite buyer cohorts are not evidence of such
demand. Founder services within this registered time allowance cannot be the
scale engine. A modeled favorable small transaction, source audit or ordinary
reuse saving cannot establish a self-financing path to millions of buyers.
The next consequential gap is an accepted buyer outcome with an advantage robust
to capable rivals, together with a funded scalable acquisition/delivery path.
