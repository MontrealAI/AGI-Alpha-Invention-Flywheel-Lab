# Consequential opportunities and the selected next experiment

Research/access date: 2026-11-03. Three materially different complete businesses
were considered below. None has yet established an extraordinary advantage over
the strongest complete alternative. The selected immediate work is an **ordinary
operating seller that can fund further discovery**, with actual portable delivery
and qualified successor construction. It is not promoted to a Super Alpha
flagship. Unknown demand is modeled conditionally; it is neither assumed to be
zero nor treated as evidence of sales.

The consequential-new-possibility statements are therefore falsifiable
hypotheses, not achieved claims:

* **A:** A buyer can use FLYWHEEL OMEGA HARNESS to operate a complete, recoverable
  productive workflow by constructing executable transformations and independent
  acceptance, overcoming excessive setup/verification cost. The strongest
  alternative is a competent conventional engineer or unrestricted agent using
  the same SQL, orchestration and reusable content. The proposed advantage is a
  large reduction in complete cost per accepted unfamiliar workflow; the thesis
  fails if that complete alternative already delivers the outcome within the
  same budget with comparable effort and reliability.
* **B:** A buyer can use the harness to change a live process while preserving
  service and obligations by constructing a qualified transition, overcoming a
  demonstrated intermediate-state resource or semantic constraint. The strongest
  alternative is competent versioning, migration and rollback with ordinary
  tools. The proposed advantage is an accepted transition that becomes feasible
  within the same complete budget; the thesis fails when that alternative
  achieves it under the same conditions.
* **C:** Buyers can use the harness to obtain an individually accepted capability
  by operating a voluntary shared-construction arrangement, overcoming a fixed
  cost and funding/participation barrier. The strongest alternative is ordinary
  pooling or a conventionally financed reusable product. The proposed advantage
  is newly feasible participation and funded delivery with better complete
  economics; the thesis fails if conventional arrangements achieve the same
  outcome with equal information, permissions and financing opportunities.

## What the current construction actually selects

The registered [protocol](PROTOCOL.md) selects necessary operating work:
`omega/competitive.py` chooses among costed opportunities from public information;
`omega/primary_operator.py` executes them through existing native accounting and
recovers the same conditional account; `omega/successor.py` constructs a portable
batch/self-service artifact, checks changed authored requests and diagnoses
known failures. The [source-bound result](REPORT.md) now records 9,216 conditional
policy trajectories and one completed native counterpart. Ordinary reuse ties;
the conventional control resolves one additional oversized case. Three founder
sessions finance the qualified successor under authored assumptions. One explicit
cache repair was needed on the frozen account; its failed direct-resume verdict
is retained separately from corrected-source regressions. No Super Alpha admission
or improved discovery follows from the operating result.

This software successor deliberately uses the existing water workflow as an
ordinary useful content/control. It does **not** relabel water optimization or
finite observation selection as a new flagship. Its narrow question is whether
accepted content and collected earnings can causally produce a usable successor
while preserving obligations. Tests distinguish no predecessor, cash-only,
retained content, ordinary equivalent content and conventional construction.
Any content-reuse benefit must survive its full acquisition cost; an equal-content
tie is retained. A better discovery method requires a separate policy-at-fixed-
assets result on fresh work.

The three paid offers remain hypotheses in **USD for this model only**, without
deciding the real storefront currency: $299 for the defined GENESIS download,
$99 per optional webinar attendee (maximum 500, not assumed demand), and $20,000
for an optional private 30-minute founder session. The latter's proposed purpose
is a bounded review of a supplied ambition, alternatives and evidence dossier,
with a concise next-test/decision note; it is not implementation or continuing
advice. Preparation, administration, follow-up within the defined deliverable,
refunds and opportunity cost must be counted in addition to the 30 minutes.
Webinar preparation/presentation/administration and founder sessions are human
exceptions, separately labeled from autonomous software commerce. Current cost
provenance and sensitivity belong in the inherited
[COSTS.md](../omega-water-20261103/COSTS.md) and the new execution report, not in
invented staff or a mandatory research charge.

The business game permits positive, weak and adverse demand, paid uncertain
reach, imitation, rejection, nonpayment, refund and non-entry. No social audience
or attendee capacity becomes assumed sales. Discovery, delivery, research,
expansion and retaining cash compete. Only chosen actions incur their declared
costs. Expected future revenue cannot buy a present construction; accepted
successor capability must exist before it changes delivery economics.
