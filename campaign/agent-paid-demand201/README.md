The classifier excludes days averaging more than $1 per transfer; wallets are not identities.

This is a strong competing price, not our realized price or permission to reuse its data. [ChainReport's November 20 snapshot](https://chainreport.co/agent-economy/) reports 37,309 paid-listed endpoints but only 729 with >=10 paying wallets by its methodology. This is **not** additive to NoHumans' sample, and some activity may be testing or incentives. Observations do not establish global demand, reliable agent identity or profitable unit economics.
