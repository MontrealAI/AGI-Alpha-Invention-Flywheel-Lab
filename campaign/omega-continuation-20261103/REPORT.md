## What the buyer can actually do

Supply a finite set of plausible process mechanisms, allowed observations,
production recipes, a target and a budget. The program constructs a contingent
experiment policy, accepts observations, selects a production recipe, checks its
actual supplied output separately, calculates the declared observation/production
cost and records the next justified action. It persists source-bound events and
resumes them without repeating a completed observation.

The consequential ambition is affordable commissioning when passive observations
leave the useful production decision ambiguous. The complete real-world
alternative is competent engineering with existing experimental design,
identification and instrumentation. Pyomo.DoE and Ax already address substantial
experimental-design needs; a named harness is not an exclusive new capability.
The **executed** comparator here is an independently written exact conventional
contingent-policy constructor with identical models, information, costs, recipes,
budgets and ordinary reuse. It is not a measured deployment of those products or
a timed human-versus-AI study.

## Seller operation actually exercised

Six authored buyer journeys execute **45 replayed workflow commands**: six
explanations, one decline, five simulated captures/deliveries/onboardings, one
refund and one lost dispute. Three download journeys copy and verify the actual
1,324-byte research-policy artifact. Two service deliveries are explicit founder
test doubles. These are tests of mechanics, **not customers or demand**.

Capture is separate from settlement. Delivery is recognized only after actual
destination bytes are verified. Failed/unavailable payments cannot grant an
entitlement. Refunds revoke entitlement and preserve fee costs; disputes retain
their exposure and review burden. A seller-won dispute can resume delivery.
Interrupted file copy/commit, duplicate requests and conflicting destinations
are tested. Workflow records contain no seller cash balance or new financial
account; **native postings and new primary sales are both zero**.

The complete cost inventory names acquisition, processing, delivery, refunds,
fraud/disputes, documentation, maintenance, security, taxes, platform/hosting,
failed work and continuing obligations. Some have explicit authored envelopes;
actual full costs remain unknown. Consequently full-cost profit is null, not
the optimistic contribution figure relabeled as earnings.
