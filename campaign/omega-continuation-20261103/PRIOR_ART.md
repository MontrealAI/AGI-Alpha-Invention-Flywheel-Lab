## Selected discovery question

**A buyer could use FLYWHEEL Ω HARNESS to obtain an intervention-tested process
decision by constructing a discriminating experiment, overcoming insufficient
information in passive observations. The strongest alternative is competent
classical active experimental design and system identification with the same
instruments and assumptions. The proposed advantage would be a lower complete
burden for an equally reliable accepted decision. The superiority thesis fails
if the complete conventional route matches that outcome and burden.**

The local experiment addresses a narrower dependency: whether a specified
intervention supplies decision-relevant information under a finite authored model
family, and whether that information survives an independently checked successor.
It cannot measure physical commissioning, specialist human effort, actual model
inference quality, demand or complete industrial economics. A local tie therefore
rejects a computational-advantage claim in the tested contract; it does not prove
that affordable end-to-end commissioning is impossible.

This investigation is justified by the consequence of a wrong process decision
and by its ability to identify an exact missing observation or coverage condition.
It is not selected because a small prototype by itself earns commercial admission.
The other possibilities presently lack the executable specifications or external
contract evidence needed for an equally decisive complete test.

### Fair executable comparison

- Give the candidate and a competent conventional active-design method identical
  hypotheses, prior observations, permitted interventions, action costs, stopping
  rules and acceptance. If no prior probabilities are supported, use an explicit
  set-valued/worst-case contract rather than invented probabilities.
- Include ordinary reusable content and strong conventional reconstruction on the
  successor. Attribute a gain from an extra observation to that information; do
  not rename it an improved discovery procedure or assume it is exclusive.
- Keep acceptance separate from the constructor. Compare accepted decisions and
  the entire executed investigation/checking burden, not only solver CPU time.
  No numerical human-productivity claim is available without a human measurement
  or an explicitly sourced estimate.
- Challenge both inconsistent observations and a mechanism outside the declared
  family. A trace outside the family's predictions can be rejected. A mechanism
  that agrees on every permitted probe can remain undetectable; a successful
  finite test must not claim otherwise. State exact/noiseless authored assumptions
  wherever they apply, and do not silently extend them to sensor measurements.
- Allow refusal, unresolved ambiguity and resource exhaustion to remain distinct
  outcomes. A refusal may be the correct product behavior, but is not successful
  commissioning or evidence of buyer willingness to pay.

The next qualifying evidence would be an authorized real process with a supplied
observation/actuation contract, tolerances, independent useful-outcome acceptance,
complete integration costs and a matched competent baseline. Acquiring that
evidence is separate from running the finite probe; no physical access, outreach,
purchase or intervention is authorized by this experiment.

## Sources and limits

- **[S1] Pyomo.DoE:** [module documentation](https://pyomo.readthedocs.io/en/stable/explanation/analysis/doe/doe.html)
  and [overview](https://pyomo.readthedocs.io/en/6.10.1/explanation/analysis/doe/overview.html).
  Documents model-based design of experiments using science-based models, design
  criteria and information from earlier experiments. This makes experiment design
  a serious incumbent; it does not remove the need for a valid process model.
- **[S2] Ax:** [official project](https://ax.dev/index.html) and
  [repository](https://github.com/facebook/ax). Describes configurable adaptive
  experimentation and experiment management. It is prior art for an optimization
  loop, not proof that arbitrary buyer ambitions are already solved.
- **[S3] PySR:** [examples](https://pysr.ai/v2.7.1/examples) and
  [SymbolicRegression.jl API](https://julia.pysr.ai/dev/api). Documents symbolic
  regression, expression templates and dimensional constraints. An equation fit
  does not independently establish causal validity or out-of-family coverage.
- **[S4] PySINDy:** [official repository](https://github.com/dynamicslab/pysindy).
  Describes identification of governing dynamics from measurements, with model
  uses including prediction and control. No PySINDy performance result is claimed
  for this harness's fixture.
- **[S5] AutoSciLab:** [author paper, arXiv:2412.12347](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.12347).
  The authors describe generated experiments, active learning, latent-variable
  discovery and equation learning, and report scientific demonstrations. This
  review does not independently reproduce those results. It defeats a claim that
  combining active experimentation and interpretable discovery is inherently new.
- **[S6] CBMC:** [official project](https://www.cprover.org/cbmc/) and
  [documentation](https://diffblue.github.io/cbmc/). Documents bounded model
  checking and equivalence/property checks. Bounds, specifications and environment
  assumptions constrain what a successful check establishes.
- **[S7] OpenHands:** [official product](https://www.openhands.dev/) and
  [official custom-sandbox documentation source](https://github.com/OpenHands/docs/blob/main/openhands/usage/advanced/custom-sandbox-guide.mdx).
  Describes agent/model/runtime choices and extensible execution. These are
  product claims/features, not a measured cost or reliability advantage on this
  assignment. No paid service or model provider was used in this review.
- **[S8] Kickstarter:** [how it works](https://www.kickstarter.com/how-it-works).
  Describes all-or-nothing funding and a 5% platform fee. This is not a complete
  cost quotation, verified eligibility decision, escrow guarantee, proof of
  irreversible payment or an executed agreement for this business.
