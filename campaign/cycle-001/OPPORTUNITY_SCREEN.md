# Opportunity screen — 29 October 2026

Owner: opportunity/evidence role. These are separately investigated hypotheses, not admitted profitable cohorts. Primary sources were retrieved on 29 October 2026. Selection below precedes inspection of candidate benchmark outcomes. Sources S1–S9 are indexed in `sources.json`. Demand, prices, service-route profitability and available customer data remain unknown. Scale of a need is not evidence of willingness to pay.

## G — Faster trustworthy interconnection restudy

**Beneficiary and outcome.** Developers and utility study teams need a defensible assessment of network impacts after generator withdrawals, additions or network changes. The desired completed service is not merely a fast matrix solve: it includes validated topology, contingency coverage, AC voltage/reactive limits, relevant dynamics, study obligations and auditable change control.

**Evidence and recent change.** Berkeley Lab's June 2026 queue report uses end-2025 records: about 8,200 active projects; median request-to-operation time above five years among completed 2025 projects in regions with data [S1]. This demonstrates congestion of the process, not that numerical power-flow calculation causes the delay. DOE's roadmap separately identifies data access, automation, coordination and model validation [S2].

**Strongest competent alternative.** A qualified utility workflow, assisted by ordinary AI and established power-system libraries. PyPSA already supplies security-constrained linear OPF and outage distribution factors; its documentation explicitly handles topology-specific factors and warns that bridge outages make those factors singular [S3]. MATPOWER and PowerModels are available reference routes for PGLib AC OPF [S4]. Do not charge an artificially weak cold solver while giving only the candidate reusable factorizations.

**Residual constraint and missing means hypothesis.** A version-bound reusable sensitivity capability might reduce repeated construction cost without accepting stale topology or an unbalanced island. This is a developer-proposed transfer from incremental compilation/cache invalidation to network study artifacts, not an autonomously discovered mapping. The unmet operational bottleneck is unproven; contemporary tools already know the relevant numerical mechanism.

**Cheapest decisive experiment.** Use the public IEEE PES PGLib v23.07 IEEE14 network as predecessor and a separately declared IEEE30 successor, with immutable originals. Evaluate a declared DC approximation against fresh competent network solves and an ordinary equivalent-information reuse control. Explicitly include bridge outages, status changes, tap ratios and source mismatches. Report accuracy and complete construction/check costs; count the network family as the independent unit, not each outage as an independent trial. Neither network is a utility interconnection cohort.

**Falsifier and decision rule.** Any accepted wrong flow, undetected stale source or incorrect island balance rejects the instrument. If ordinary reuse matches construction quality and lifecycle cost, retain that incumbent and reject a causal experience-advantage claim. No speed threshold from this reduced service can establish a commercial threshold. Decision: **CONTINUE_BOUNDED_PROBE** for engineering discrimination only; **UNKNOWN** for commercial admission. Island handling itself is already known and cannot support a novelty claim.

**First commercial exercise and successor family.** Offer a version-bound study artifact only after access to a qualified complete utility model and independently measured acceptance requirements. Without contact or purchasing evidence, publish a price/participation frontier rather than assumed sales. Fresh successor work could construct a different network's contingency checker under equal lifecycle budgets; it cannot simply rerun IEEE14. Operational validation, utility acceptance, liability terms and buyer evidence are the next access requirements.

## B — Rapid qualification of heterogeneous retired batteries

**Beneficiary and outcome.** Battery repurposers need an application-specific decision about usable capacity, uncertainty, lifetime and safety before incurring disassembly, testing and integration costs. The cohort would be a fixed acquired lot, including rejected and damaged units, with chemistry, ownership and final disposal obligations frozen.

**Evidence and recent change.** A 2025 NREL/Smartville CRADA explicitly targets rapid state-of-health characterization and second-use life forecasts from sparse data [S5]. A 2025 original study reports generative transfer learning for capacity estimation across heterogeneous second-life batteries [S6]. These are capable predecessors; their existence contradicts any claim that sparse-data transfer is absent. The latter publisher page was not accessible in full in this run, so its published abstract cannot establish achieved reproducible performance.

**Strongest competent alternative.** The repurposer's validated capacity/impedance/pulse-test route, application safety checks, and competent existing sparse-data and transfer-learning methods with the same records. Standard laboratory discharge capacity alone does not establish pack safety or field remaining life.

**Residual constraint and missing means hypothesis.** A sequential measurement protocol could stop earlier for easily classified cells while explicitly escalating uncertain or anomalous units. Transfer from sequential diagnosis is plausible but developer supplied. Its value must be measured against existing adaptive diagnostic practice, including throughput, false acceptance and measurement energy.

**Cheapest decisive experiment.** Test partial-trace decisions against full independently measured reference outcomes, splitting by physical battery/lot rather than cycle. NASA's catalog describes public aging records with voltage, current, temperature, capacity and EIS, but the retrieved catalog currently says it has no attached resources [S7]. Such cell aging data would still not calibrate a heterogeneous retired-pack commercial cohort. The absence of accessible lot-level safety and cost records blocks a defensible immediate second-life admission.

**Falsifier and decision.** Failure to meet a prespecified safety/false-acceptance bound, indistinguishable latent capacities under permitted measurements, or no lifecycle advantage versus competent diagnostics rejects the claimed instrument. **DEFER / UNKNOWN** this cycle: stronger physical data and measurement access are needed before construction can decide the consequential claim.

**First commercial exercise and successor family.** A fixed-lot qualification service with independently observed buyer acceptance and terminal responsibility; successors could qualify a fresh chemistry/application. No battery purchase, physical testing, field performance or demand evidence is claimed here. A comparison with new certified batteries, direct sale and recycling is required before investment attractiveness can be assessed.

## H — Make low-grade industrial heat reusable at a specific site

**Beneficiary and outcome.** Industrial operators with a matched heat sink could substitute recovered heat for purchased fuel. A defined completed service must meet required temperature, timing, flow, contamination tolerance, uptime and maintenance obligations. The relevant cohort is a fixed site's streams and operating horizon, not total national waste heat.

**Evidence and recent change.** A 2025 California Energy Commission project reports measured reduction in gas use after recovery of low-pressure steam at a mineral-processing site [S8]. Another CEC project reports laboratory and installed trials of polymer heat exchangers [S9]. The 2026 IETS synthesis maps roughly fifty national projects and emphasizes process integration and industrial symbiosis [S10]. Existing physical construction therefore provides a serious alternative to a software-only claim.

**Strongest competent alternative.** A qualified process engineer using pinch/process integration, commercial exchangers or heat pumps, storage and validated site measurements, with ordinary AI/library assistance. Direct on-site reuse, lower process demand, fuel-price changes, postponement and non-entry remain alternatives.

**Residual constraint and missing means hypothesis.** The missing instrument might be a low-cost protocol for jointly measuring source/sink availability, fouling and uncertainty, enabling a robust design that cannot be justified from annual heat totals. An algebraic energy balance cannot create the physical heat-transfer effect or justify assumed recovery efficiency.

**Cheapest decisive experiment.** On a predeclared site, use measured synchronized source/sink traces and exchanger performance to compare the strongest complete design with the proposed adaptation. No such site data or hardware access is available in the recorded cycle. A generic optimizer on invented time series would not answer this question.

**Falsifier and decision.** Failure to meet temperature/availability/contamination limits, no difference from standard process integration, or complete-cost surplus that vanishes under joint adverse prices/downtime rejects the proposal. **DEFER / UNKNOWN**: do not replace physical evidence with a favorable simulated efficiency assumption.

**First commercial exercise and successor family.** Deliver a measured, qualified site retrofit decision or physical recovery service, then transfer the capability to a separately selected sink/site. Revenue capture requires an observed fee or contract; modeled fuel savings do not create an automatic royalty. Terminal duties, idle capacity and maintenance belong in the same-cohort ledger.

## Allocation recommendation and boundary

Select **G** as the sole bounded engineering flagship because its public network data and strong mathematical alternatives make a cheap, falsifiable probe executable without spending or outreach. Retain **B** and **H** as bounded alternatives at evidence-screen stage only. This choice is based on information value and access, not evidence that grid work has the largest commercial value. It does not admit a commercial cohort.

The investigation may retire the proposed shortcut while preserving the known incumbent. A public negative decision is useful if it prevents a stale/unsafe artifact from being misrepresented as productive capability. It does not complete the invention-automation mission. Escalation requires a materially different unmet constraint or new operational evidence; a favorable random seed does not reopen the same claim.
