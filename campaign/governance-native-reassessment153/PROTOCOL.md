## Actual beneficiary and missing means

Shared Services Canada's public [30 October 2026 AI governance challenge](https://www.ised-isde.canada.ca/site/innovative-solutions-canada/en/artificial-intelligence-ai-decision-making-process-visibility-traceability-and-compliance-governance) requires: continuous reassessment after AI governance requirement changes (Essential Outcome 4) **and** original data/model/output risk measurement integrated into those assessments (Essential Outcome 6). [CanadaBuys Amendment 001, 10 November 2026](https://canadabuys.canada.ca/en/tender-opportunities/tender-notice/cb-189-51083224) clarifies that imported third-party scores alone do not meet EO6. The government is an identified problem sponsor, **not** a confirmed customer of this product. Existing main has `products/governance_impact140/engine.py` (change impact with no authenticated approval) and `native_risk152.py` (exact first-party synthetic measurements) **but no source-bound compositional reassessment joining the two**. Test that missing means without claiming eligibility or external demand.

Full commercial alternatives: (A) competent human specialists with conventional scripts and manual evidence/reassessment, (B) existing enterprise GRC+AI-risk vendors with qualified integration, (C) source-contained first-party reassessment in this experiment, (D) no bid/no deployment. Imported scores alone are an intentionally invalid EO6 alternative, not the strongest comparator. No full-cost/time comparison, vendor quote or user acceptance is yet observed; retain no-bid.

## Fixed finite construction

Add only `products/governance_impact140/native_reassessment153.py`, `tests/test_governance_native_reassessment153.py`, this protocol and a narrow standard public-runner workflow. Do **not** mutate existing impact140 or native risk152 semantics. Standard library only, no live network/data, no logging of secrets. For one complete synthetic registry change, accept exactly: `schema`, an unchanged impact140 `governance` input, `observations` of raw binary cases keyed by system, plus distinct `before_policies` and `after_policies` for explicitly registered system/requirement/metric/version/threshold bindings. Every policy must be current for its stage, in scope and have the native152 exact threshold schema; reject mismatches rather than silently linking a risk assessment to the wrong system or old version.

Always compute every registered policy's value **from the same raw rows** with existing `native_risk152.evaluate` (never ingest submitted scores). Preserve the complete original impact140 finding, link its existing receipt and per-system raw-case content digest, show before and after native exact rational threshold signals and their difference. The before/after treatment holds observations fixed to isolate an authored requirement-threshold change, not real-world drift. Output the explicit list of current governance findings without any registered native risk binding; no silent positive coverage. Do not transform a favorable numerical signal, self-claimed review, hash or user-provided governance control into authenticated reviewer approval, official policy interpretation, legal compliance, government TRL, real model quality or financial revenue. Require external independently held digests for trusting report integrity.

## Frozen adversarial engineering tests — all required, no favorable-seed selection

T1. Authored three-row two-group case with two correct labels of three: when version changes and `min_accuracy` rises from `0.5` to `0.75`, the same raw observations yield prior within-threshold and current adverse-threshold, both exact. Impacts and risk reports are bound to the relevant system/requirement/version.
T2. Replacing a raw case changes source digest and resulting assessment; shuffled rows do not change semantic receipt; old version never counts as current.
T3. A known registered requirement with no risk policy is explicitly listed unmeasured, not given a pass; old-only policies never satisfy current coverage.
T4. Missing truth, repeat, group-positive support, or source labels correctly produce `UNABLE_TO_DETERMINE` through the native source evaluator, not zero risk.
T5. Reject duplicate system observations, duplicate per-stage bindings, wrong or retired version, unknown system/requirement, wrong scope, unsupported metric, invalid threshold and submitted precomputed-score fields.
T6. A self-declared reviewer approval in the input never authenticates evaluation or changes the unchanged impact140 pending/unable status; provenance and hash-only scope remain explicit.
T7. Changed control without version bump remains a changed requirement (impact140 semantics) and triggers a fresh current calculation rather than reusing prior approval.
T8. Reject malformed UTF-8, duplicate JSON keys, overwrite and nonobject payload at the standalone CLI boundary. Ensure no accounts, filesystem signing keys, private inputs or output paths from owners are accessed.
T9. Deterministic receipt hashes, external digest verification, mutation detection and reference to recomputed native152 source input; no self-signed compliance.
T10. Independent clean-source tests (normal and optimized Python), source SHA/manifest check, no secret-bearing artifact, exact-head CI. Failed cases must remain failed.
