# Constructed reserve capability, ordinary provision ties

## Result

A known prepositioning arrangement removes a modeled road-access dependency. On the public **1992 Anaheim research network**, three authored beneficiaries can receive their simultaneous stock requirements under all **797 registered directed-road-closure/intact scenarios**. Exact integer enumeration, full conventional HiGHS, and an independent feeder-cut lower bound agree: parent annual stock-slot cost is **$9,000**; doubled demand at one beneficiary costs **$12,000**. All three fixed deadlines (5,10,15 minutes) agree. Every accepted plan passes separate discrete assignment checks. These are authored facility/demand/cost assumptions, not existing customers or a current emergency plan.

**Strong ordinary provision ties. No superior complete enterprise or economically enabling successor is demonstrated.** The successor is possible with the same original $100k reference seed on slot cost alone, without parent earnings or inherited instrument. Ordinary equal-content reuse attains the same optimum. No actual transactions occurred. No native account, balance, signer, obligation, PR206 code or existing campaign was modified.

## Do not hide the failures

1. Frozen original endpoint selection used outward-only connector stubs, making every deadline infeasible even intact. 10,980 independently computed shortest-path distances agreed. `PROTOCOL.md`, `run.py`, `RESULT.json` preserve this rejected selection.
2. Separately registered zone interface corrected location semantics and restricted failures to physical through-node links rather than artificial centroid connectors. Intact 15-minute service was possible, but three fatal feeder cuts defeated every remote reserve capacity. All 9,564 independent distances agreed. `ZONE_PROTOCOL.md`/`ZONE_RESULT.json` preserve this second negative result.
3. `ONSITE_PROTOCOL.md` prospectively adds stock at beneficiary zones, removing the feeder-cut dependency without weakening the second study's acceptance. Direct conventional onsite stock is equally permitted and optimal. `ONSITE_RESULT.json` records the positive computational construction and absence of competitive advantage.

This is a finite constructive probe and a reusable rejection/dispatch instrument, not Blue Sky admission, autonomous invention, real disaster assurance or achievement of $3B trailing-year profit. In-memory retained reachability saves rebuilding content, but a stored-checksum match is not authenticated authority. Cache loader detects stale identities and uncoordinated payload changes; coordinated content/checksum replacement is not protected.

## Complete economics boundary

Stock-slot prices are explicit modeled assumptions. Construction, unsuccessful search, acquisition, onboarding, qualification, handling, fuel, congestion, maintenance, stock expiry, staffing, financing, taxes, liabilities and actual model/host bills are unmeasured. They are **unknown, not zero**. No profit or earned financing is posted. Against equally capable coordinated conventional procurement, incremental willingness to pay and seller margin are not demonstrated. The coalition payment model includes that grand-coalition outside option; a $500 markup rejection follows from this supplied model, not observed market behavior. The first two scenarios were infeasible so their payment allocations were never executed.

Road capacities/congestion are not simulated. One directed-road closure at a time is covered; correlated failures, depot destruction and centroid access failures are outside this contract. Before operational use, current routes, facilities, inventory, travel uncertainty and complete costs need independent validation. This software must not be presented as an emergency operating plan.

## Reproduce

Python3.11+, NumPy, SciPy (executed SciPy1.17.0); no model API, key or paid service.

Run `python fetch_input.py`, then `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 python run.py`, `python run_zone.py`, `python run_onsite.py`, `python audit.py`, and `python audit_zone.py`. Use a fresh directory to preserve historical receipts. Timings vary; compare objectives, feasibility, hashes and scenario counts. Enumeration timing includes Hall construction while MILP timing excludes that common construction; these are component measurements, not a whole-pipeline speed superiority result. All studies executed in about 20 process-CPU seconds combined, excluding research, reviews and unknown model work.

Public source: [TNTP Anaheim](https://github.com/bstabler/TransportationNetworks/tree/d1639b4ef218c17928ba573e806ddf8ba5e7ae6d/Anaheim). Metadata identifies 416 nodes,914 links,38 zones and minutes. `fetch_input.py` records pinned raw SHA256 and the explicit single terminal-newline materialization transformation. Raw data/caches are not published in this change; the small source, manifests, historical results and independent audit are sufficient to recreate them.

## Learning and next decision

Check transport endpoint direction and failure cuts before optimizing pooled stock. When demand is behind a fatal cut, better dispatch or more remote inventory cannot establish service. Known onsite placement can, but it earns no special supplier rent against the same conventional option. Do not invest or book sales on this evidence; retain the cut/dispatch checker and pursue a genuinely unresolved dependency elsewhere. This closes this finite comparison rather than tuning costs or selecting a less demanding failure set until a win appears.

Source qualification: local source-matched executed checks and independent review are recorded. Hosted qualification, integration, merge, released operation and real-world validation are separate and must not be presumed.
