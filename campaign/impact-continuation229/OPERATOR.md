## Beneficiary outcome and competing means

The [official 2026 ISC AI governance challenge](https://www.ised-isde.canada.ca/site/innovative-solutions-canada/en/artificial-intelligence-ai-decision-making-process-visibility-traceability-and-compliance-governance) requests continuous detection of relevant governance changes, potentially affected registered AI systems and reassessment (essential outcomes 1 and 4). It also requires **additional** outcomes: enterprise interoperability, authorized access, retention/data residency, human review, risk monitoring and complete assessment. CanadaBuys takes precedence if published solicitation sources conflict; the underlying tender documents were not independently obtained.

**One missing-means hypothesis:** turn two explicitly versioned structured requirement inventories and an authorized system inventory into a conservative reviewer queue preserving changed scope and retired requirements. The present instrument implements *only this offline subtask*. It does not interpret policy text, evaluate a model, authenticate evidence, hold access rights or assess legal compliance.

The method—an inverted tag index, union of old/new applicability and fail-closed source/version collision checks—is established software engineering. The strongest competing complete means include established governance suites, competent conventional scripts/human analysts, public government AIA tools, hybrid AI services and no purchase. None is shown inferior here. The source cannot establish procurement eligibility, demand or achievable revenue.

## Reproduce (Python standard library, no credentials/network)

From repo root:

~~~sh
python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -p 'test_impact_continuation229.py' -v
python3 tools/impact_continuation229.py campaign/impact-continuation229/AI_GOVERNANCE_SYNTHETIC.json
python3 tools/impact_continuation229.py campaign/impact-continuation229/SUPPLIER_QUALITY_SYNTHETIC.json
~~~

To save a local result, append --output /absolute/new/receipt.json. The output is create-only; overwriting an existing receipt fails. Inputs must provide schema impact-continuation229/v1, before, after, systems; requirements each have ID, version, source_sha256, nonempty applicability tags and controls; systems have ID, nonempty tags and evidence_refs. Asterisk is an explicit universal applicability marker. Duplicate JSON keys, malformed IDs/digests, duplicate records and modified content under a stable requirement ID/version are rejected.

Output reassessments is a **queue for an authorized reviewer**, never a compliance decision. linked_evidence is only a caller-supplied reference and is not authenticated or certified. unlinked_controls means no reference was supplied; even linked evidence may be stale, forged or inadequate. An empty queue means this structured change did not produce a reassessment trigger, **not** that anything is compliant. SHA-256 identifies content but does not confer authority.

## Independent qualification and first/second undertaking boundary

The independent exhaustive system-by-requirement oracle in tests/test_impact_continuation229.py tests every affected pair, including changed applicability, addition and retirement; fixed-seed adverse test contains 137 fictional systems, 57 old requirements and 12 additions. Both separate fixtures are **fully synthetic**.

- **Undertaking A:** assess scope of a fictional AI governance-policy revision across three fictional applications; two systems require review.
- **Undertaking B:** reuse the same instrument for an unrelated fictional industrial supplier-quality revision across three fictional suppliers; two require review.

This demonstrates executable portability, not a causal economic advantage for B: indexing and exhaustive alternatives are available to a competent conventional engineer. The experiment includes no real government AI registry, supplier record, independent human end-use acceptance or matched transfer costs. Actual benefit, time saved, integration cost, host/model billing, paid demand and inventor improvement are all **UNKNOWN**, not zero.
