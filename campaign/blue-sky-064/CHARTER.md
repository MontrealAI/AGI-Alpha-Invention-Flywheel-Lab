# Blue Sky 064: invent a larger productive frontier

The active campaign searches for consequential activities that become achievable only after constructing missing productive means. Its object of invention is the whole arrangement: an outcome worth delivering, an instrument or production method, a valid way to accept the result, voluntary participation, and a fundable delivery sequence. The potential consequence must extend beyond improving a routine service.

The flexibility063 experiment remains historical evidence with its original source, accounts, ordinary ties, losses and unsupported claims. It is not the new flagship and is not relabeled by a more ambitious description.

## Select the possibility and its means together

Compare at least three materially different productive possibilities before commitment. This is a minimum comparison requirement, not a cap on candidate generation. For each candidate, record:

1. **New accepted outcome.** State what a beneficiary could do after construction that the current installed means do not adequately permit. Identify the consequence beyond the first small prototype.
2. **Best complete alternative.** Execute or source the strongest accessible ordinary, specialist, self-service, human-plus-AI and included-agent routes that actually serve the same purpose. Include their available construction, coordination, procurement and financing options; do not cripple the control.
3. **Residual obstruction.** Name the remaining metric, acceptance threshold, achieved numerical value and evidence. Distinguish a proved limit, a measured failed comparison and an uncertain diagnosis. Repeated search failure is not an impossibility proof.
4. **Constructive invention target.** Specify the missing information channel, instrument, representation, process, productive operation or arrangement. It must have an executable realization and an acceptance bridge back to the original outcome.
5. **Decisive causal test.** Freeze the required outcome and compare with/without the construction under matched total resources. Test plausible competing explanations and the strongest complete route. Do not change acceptance after seeing the result.
6. **Complete commercial path.** Bind the accepted product, buyer choice, alternatives, resource consumption, construction/failures, contract timing, obligations and native funding. Model prices and payments are not observations of demand.
7. **Successor frontier.** Name a different fresh activity made reachable by the retained means, not simply another larger request for the same service. Compare an equally resourced successor that may build or buy its own means. Count construction, transfer, maintenance and validation.

If affordability is the enabling change, identify the supported budget threshold and the accepted activity on either side. An invented price or an arbitrarily expensive baseline cannot establish this transition. A potentially enormous market is not an admission result.

## Keep the constructive loop operational

Use evidence to diagnose the obstruction, derive a missing-means specification, generate complementary construction hypotheses, build the smallest discriminating realization, independently check the original outcome, and re-evaluate the complete productive arrangement. An experiment that reveals the diagnosis is wrong should redirect construction rather than trigger a new success narrative.

Search outcome, instrument, verification, participant terms and financing jointly. A cheaper single component can make the whole arrangement worse by reducing beneficiaries' outside-option costs or by moving payment after an unfundable construction stage. Admit complementary bundles and staged tests where individual components have no immediate return, but require an actual affordable path and a decisive joint test.

Use strong established mechanisms whenever applicable. New algorithms are not mandatory. Separate three claims: the installed capability stock changed, the constructor beat a strong ordinary discovery route, and the resulting offering is commercially viable. A conventional algorithm tie does not by itself veto a real enabling construction or profitable business. Conversely, if the strongest existing complete route already achieves the same outcome within the same full budget, the alleged residual obstruction must be revised rather than manufactured.

## Strict flagship status

The flagship requires a consequential enabling construction and evidence of a newly attainable outcome or an economically meaningful threshold. A routine optimizer, cosmetic rebranding, test runner, paper, dashboard or transaction counter cannot qualify by itself. Supporting actions must remove a named dependency of the consequential construction or its next decisive delivery.

Selection, a successful prototype, Blue Sky qualification, commercial qualification, investment affordability and successor benefit are distinct states. Unsupported fields remain unknown. If no candidate qualifies, keep the ambitious search open and report the exact unresolved condition; do not promote a conventional fallback as the flagship or write a paper claiming the desired outcome.

## Capital, scale and conditional optimality

The primary simulated challenge begins with exactly US$100,000 and measures US$3 billion trailing-365-day complete-cost profit at its registered endpoint. This is a milestone, not a ceiling. Do not pool accounts, reseed a continuing account, top up balances, annualize a short cohort or treat forecasts as available cash. Reuse the canonical `JournalAccount` and adaptive `Account`, with obligations and funding attribution intact.

Candidate generation, productive capabilities and economic scale have no arbitrary universal catalogue or profit limit. Actual resource capacity, finite declared experiments, current native cash, uncertainty and authority still constrain each action. A finite tested grammar is a disclosed experiment boundary, not a claim that all possible arrangements have been searched.

Any game-theoretic optimum must name its game, feasible actions, information, incentive assumptions, horizon and complete costs. Report the achieved value and an optimality gap, bound or regret certificate when available. An optimum for an authored finite game does not prove the globally best enterprise, truthful real participants or future profit. Buyer refusal, rival adoption, supplier changes and voluntary withdrawal remain valid outcomes.

Existing included reasoning, coding, tools and resources may construct and test candidates. No separately billable model APIs, purchases, outreach, real contracts, financial transfers, private-data access or new paid commitments are introduced. Physical, scientific, simulated and actual commercial evidence retain their separate scopes.
