## Passive mechanics: a real mechanism, an unresolved new function

[Fu and colleagues' Nature Physics paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41567-026-03503-0), published **30 October 2026**, supplies an experimentally grounded example of changing available contact laws through geometry. Its [public code version](https://zenodo.org/records/22142148) was published **28 August 2026** and modified **7 October**; the preprint identifier is `2605.19555`. The paper's publication is therefore not evidence that the algorithm became available only in the latest week.

The downloaded 11,919-byte code archive has SHA-256 `5bfb83a1e0c9f048e44bf1dfbfa35f75bd09ffd18b2286f6b471b597a8ee9671` and a CC-BY-4.0 record. It supplies proportional, power and bilinear constructors. Reproducing those cases is not a new AGI ALPHA capability claim. The stronger [released differentiable-physics route](https://github.com/JBil8/frictional_metasurfaces_inverse_design) must also be considered.

The full source already investigates fabrication errors and wear; generic robustness therefore supplies no demonstrated residual. A sharper construction hypothesis concerns independent static holding and dynamic sliding. The homogeneous source model fixes their force ratio for every height geometry. Separately calibrated contact families with different ratios and activation heights may remove that restriction. Exploratory fits suggest distinct ratios, but a common origin coefficient cannot fit all supplied per-row error boxes in three of six family/response combinations. Those boxes are not a joint confidence region and this does not reject the authors' physical findings. It does prevent treating nominal fits as guaranteed calibration. Transfer to a mixed surface remains unvalidated, so this is a new-function hypothesis rather than physical proof.

Neither the consequential end use nor its numerical thresholds is frozen by these cards. Strong ordinary constructors may construct the same new means under matched resources; a solver tie alone would not erase a validated new outcome. An already complete alternative supplying the same accepted outcome would defeat the alleged gap. A numerical blueprint remains distinct from a fabricated or physically qualified device.

## Interior observatory: change the information, not the confidence of the story

The [OpenAI elasticity scope note](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/372.md) describes a full-data uniqueness result for smooth three-dimensional elasticity and explicitly supplies **no reconstruction algorithm**. It cannot justify finite noisy sensing. The [8 October 2026 LLM-IDEA preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11253) already investigates identifiability-driven changes of experiment class. A generic agent that notices ambiguity is therefore an existing mechanism, not this candidate's enabling outcome.

Public [KIT4 data](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.1203914) and the [KTC dataset paper](https://www.aimsciences.org/article/doi/10.3934/ammc.2024009) provide physical conductivity measurements for a bounded test. They do not test the separate elasticity theorem. An unchanged observation null space cannot be overcome by additional reasoning. The candidate requires an accepted hidden-property result or an actual new information channel beyond strong complete alternatives; neither is established.

## Industrial closure: begin beyond the complete incumbent

[NASA's current lunar technology description](https://www.nasa.gov/lunar-surface-technology/) already includes Blue Alchemist's integrated regolith-to-material-and-solar-cell route. Renaming that chain cannot establish a missing means. A [2024 NASA plant model](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20240013999) identified a downstream metal-handling modeling gap, but this historical statement must be reassessed against present complete systems.

[NASA CEA](https://nasa.github.io/cea/) provides executable thermochemical foundations. Its [modernization bulletin](https://www.nasa.gov/centers-and-facilities/nesc/tb-26-04-updates-and-modernization-of-nasas-chemical-equilibrium-with-applications-cea-code/) is dated **21 August 2026**, rather than the latest month. Neither modern APIs nor an equilibrium calculation validates reaction kinetics, manufacturing, industrial closure or profit. Those unresolved conditions keep this candidate on HOLD.
