## Protected results and strongest completed alternatives

The frozen protocol requires at least 5% median paired relative RMSE gain against
**every** primary control, improved pooled RMSE, at most 10% tail relative harm,
and exchange invariance. Both fixed-cohort descriptive pilots fail that combined
criterion; no population-level statistical confirmation is claimed.

| Protected family | Candidate median pair RMSE | Full-feature kernel ridge median pair RMSE | Candidate pooled RMSE | Full-feature kernel ridge pooled RMSE |

Norman has some better pairwise outcomes but worse pooled error and unacceptable
tail harm. On Wessels, the inherited candidate beats additive prediction but
loses decisively to the complete conventional ridge control; Matching Mean also
has lower median and pooled error. Equivalent ordinary reuse produces the same
predictions as inheritance. A better starting capability set does not establish
an improved discovery policy or a lifecycle advantage after acquisition costs.

Raw reports, all arms, per-pair outcomes, frozen predictions, failures and
post-confirmation exposed replay inputs are included under
