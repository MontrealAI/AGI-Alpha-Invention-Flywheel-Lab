## What the game-theoretic claim means

Exact optimization is **only over the disclosed finite options and authored private-value assumptions**. The mechanism does not elicit values truthfully, guarantee incentive compatibility, find a coalition-proof core, certify partner willingness, guarantee legal enforceability, or imply real-world buyers. The Myerson–Satterthwaite theorem shows why full efficiency, individual rationality and budget balance cannot always be simultaneously achieved under private information. Market design and package allocation are established approaches to complementarities (Roth/Shapley 2012; Milgrom/Wilson 2020). Industrial symbiosis shows the possible physical-world importance of coordinated productive resources, **not** a physical validation of this synthetic digital proof.

Primary sources: [2012 Market Design Prize](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/economic-sciences/2012/press-release/), [2020 Auction Design Prize](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/economic-sciences/2020/press-release/), [1983 bilateral trade impossibility](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/0022053183900480), [UNIDO industrial symbiosis](https://www.unido.org/stories/eco-industrial-parks-resource-efficiency-and-industrial-symbiosis).
