## Question and falsifiers

A buyer can use the proposed harness workflow to construct and exercise a
portable behavioral replacement for a resettable, deterministic, finite-state
interface, by obtaining discriminating observations and constructing a checked
state machine. This can address missing source and missing behavioral
documentation. The strongest complete alternative is established active
automata learning plus ordinary adapter construction, alongside competent
migration products. The proposed residual is affordable complete delivery for
otherwise uneconomic cases. It fails if the ordinary complete route already
delivers the same accepted outcome within the same total budget. A finite local
test double is a construction test, not evidence of a real customer's difficulty
or willingness to pay. The extraordinary-advantage hypothesis starts unresolved.

The seller question is whether independently accepted software can be voluntarily
purchased, collected, delivered and expanded under actual native liquidity, with
costed reach and rival responses. Commercial profit, funded expansion, buyer
capability, invention advantage and the US$3 billion objective receive separate
verdicts. No founder sessions can substitute for this autonomous-software test.

## Declared game and information

Seller chooses non-entry, an affordable launch, paid acquisition size/channel,
price, pause/stop, additional qualification and capability investment where
offered. Buyers have independently specified budgets, use values, adoption costs,
deadlines and complete outside options. A positive value does not force consent;
no-purchase wins an exact zero-surplus tie. Suppliers charge only selected work.
Rivals can supply conventional solutions and change their public offers after
the declared delay. All market ranges and probability models are assumptions,
not calibrated demand or hidden facts supplied to the policy.

The policy receives current native cash/obligations, public supplier/rival offers
and previously observed decisions/outcomes. It cannot inspect private buyer
values, future draws, scenario names or evaluator answers. Qualification and
acceptance remain independent of seller assertions. Policies use the same
underlying opportunities and evaluation seeds. The proposed policy is a tested
heuristic, not a global optimum or proven equilibrium.

## Expandable reach and funding

An initial campaign is a policy choice, not a market ceiling. The interface must
permit larger campaigns and additional costed reach when current funds and
expected value justify them. Explicit uncertain audiences, overlap, expiry,
saturation and rival entry constrain a world; they are not universal business
limits. Production capacity alone creates no demand. Unprofitable acquisition
can be stopped or replaced. A dynamic content-addressed order contract removes
the previous need to preallocate one product job per possible customer; audit
must still authenticate each actual request, acceptance, cost and collection.

Reserve before incurring work. Fulfill independent buyers sequentially against
fresh cash snapshots; never reject a viable sequence merely because its entire
cohort cannot be prefunded. Available seed remains usable under the declared
policy, while earned-funded actions must identify genuinely available earnings.
Reuse JournalAccount, native obligations and independent replay; no new ledger.

## Acceptance, costs and successors

Software delivery requires actual portable files, integrity verification and
independent functional acceptance under the finite-machine contract. Refusal,
nonpayment, interrupted delivery, corruption, refunds, late claims, unavailable
oracles, insufficient resources and buyer withdrawal are required adverse tests.
Reset capability, determinism, alphabet and state bound are buyer-supplied
prerequisites, not facts inferred from a few successful examples. Local test
doubles and deterministic included execution remain labeled as such.

Every cost is either measured resource use with an explicit rate, a sourced
estimate, an assumed sensitivity range or unknown. No invented employees or
mandatory developer payroll. Included execution is not evidence of zero
commercial compute cost. The new tax model uses an explicitly hypothetical25%
immediate pay-as-earned schedule unless a separately tested deferred interface
is implemented; tax accrual and payment therefore coincide and the native tax
reserve is zero. It must not claim that generic deferred tax accounting exists.
Development is expensed when incurred; no debt, equity financing, capital asset
or depreciation is assumed. Unpriced costs remain visible.

Compare a fresh successor with no predecessor, cash only, retained productive
means, equal-content ordinary reuse and strong conventional construction. A
conventional tie rejects a special method claim while preserving useful ordinary
commerce. Only permissioned generic material can be reused. Forecast internal
utility is never recognized as revenue. Investment competes with doing nothing.

## Scale and stopping

Native execution, lower-scale equivalence checks and larger conditional
projections are distinct. An aggregate calculation cannot be called an executed
native US$3 billion year. Follow the registered operating year and outstanding
obligations; if an actual platform boundary interrupts work, checkpoint the same
identity and disclose the incomplete result. Do not insert a universal customer,
transaction, profit or machine-time cap. Stop an unattractive action by its
decision value, not by an unconditional research-spending schedule. After the
comparison, execute the highest-value permitted next test supported by evidence.

No paid APIs, keys, real purchases, outreach, events, transactions, live storefront
changes, deployment, merge or disclosure of private materials are authorized by
this experiment. Reviewable draft publication is separate from adoption.
