# Opportunity selection: a recoverable interface, not another water solver

Research date: 3 November 2026. This is prospective selection, before the new
confirmation comparison. The inherited water/MILP tie, observation-selector tie,
ordinary successor-reuse tie, and founder-service result remain unchanged. The
small historical cohort is an engineering fixture, not a limit on demand. This
experiment does not appropriate the separately owned software-growth campaign.

## Three materially different possibilities

| **A. Behavioral compatibility recovery** | Keep an orphaned stateful device or software service usable through a portable compatibility layer when source or a complete behavioral specification is unavailable. Replacement can be costly; whether existing recovery is actually uneconomic remains unmeasured. | Buyer-authorized probes, a constructed finite behavioral model, an executable adapter, and independent trace acceptance. Candidate A is selected for the immediate executable discovery step because it
can change the available interface and then face independent behavioral checks
without purchasing physical services. The important uncertainty is whether the
complete construction improves on established active learning and competent
adapter construction, not whether a transaction counter can run. B has a stronger
physical-observation dependency that this environment cannot settle. C remains a
credible alternative arrangement rather than an assumed monopoly. These are
judgments about useful next evidence, not precise probability-weighted market
rankings.

**Thesis.** A buyer can use FLYWHEEL OMEGA HARNESS to recover an executable,
locally operated interface to an otherwise undocumented stateful service by
constructing discriminating probes and a qualified behavioral adapter, overcoming
missing behavioral knowledge. The strongest alternative is competent active
model learning and conformance testing followed by ordinary adapter construction.
The proposed advantage is fewer full-cost queries, corrections and buyer steps
per accepted unfamiliar interface. The extraordinary-advantage thesis fails if
that alternative already delivers the same outcome within the same complete
budget. An authored finite-family prototype establishes neither recovery of
arbitrary software nor industrial safety.

## Current primary prior art and its consequence

The following primary sources were retrieved on the research date. They inform
selection and comparison; no paid service or physical experiment was used.

* [LearnLib](https://learnlib.de/) and its [official source](https://github.com/LearnLib/learnlib)
  already provide active/passive automata learning and approximate equivalence
  testing. [AutomataLib](https://learnlib.de/pages/automatalib) includes
  distinguishing sequences, state/transition covers and W-method variants.
  Therefore black-box finite-state learning, counterexample refinement and normal
  test reuse are available baseline capabilities, not harness inventions.
* [AWS Transform custom](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/transform/latest/userguide/custom.html)
  already describes reusable autonomous code/API/framework transformations and
  learning from executions. Its [product page](https://aws.amazon.com/transform/custom/)
  describes validation and repair. Source-available modernization is consequently
  a strong outside option. These claims do not establish performance on an
  orphaned black-box protocol; that narrower residual still requires a test.
* [BoTorch cost-aware optimization](https://botorch.org/docs/v0.17.1/tutorials/cost_aware_bayesian_optimization)
  and [multifidelity knowledge gradient](https://botorch.org/docs/v0.17.1/tutorials/multi_fidelity_bo)
  already jointly consider observations and evaluation costs under stated models.
  Rebranding that selection as new scientific instrumentation would be wrong.
* [The Open Reaction Database](https://docs.open-reaction-database.org/en/stable/overview.html)
  supplies a schema and data infrastructure for reaction research, including
  automated experimentation contexts. It does not supply the missing new physical
  observations or validate an arbitrary proposed reaction process.
* [Provision-point refund-bonus mechanisms](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.11695)
  have an established literature addressing free riding and delayed contributions
  under explicit assumptions. Ordinary coordination therefore receives comparable
  construction and financing permissions; a newly written pledge interface is not
  evidence of a new institution or incentive compatibility.

## Buyer and seller value must meet

For A, the buyer supplies permission to query, an input/output vocabulary, a safe
reset or equivalent test envelope, resource limits, a concrete workflow to retain,
and acceptance requirements. A tool cannot infer unlimited hidden behavior from
a finite transcript. It must refuse or report unresolved ambiguity outside its
contract. External queries or provider calls require separate informed buyer
permission; lab execution uses local authored systems only.

The proposed USD299 edition buys a defined local package and working workflow,
not continuing updates, personal implementation or unlimited inference. Free
core, specialist tooling and no purchase remain alternatives. Adoption effort,
query costs, hardware/electricity, failed traces, latency and requalification
reduce buyer net benefit. Seller value comes from a voluntarily preferred complete
experience, not mandatory fees on rights already granted. Root repository
redistribution rights remain unresolved; no new license grant is asserted.

Reach is an action-dependent resource. The commercial experiment must pay for
qualification and acquisition, exclude expired and duplicate identities, and
allow added channels or capacity when economically justified and funded. More
compute cannot create willingness to pay. Expansion is conditional on market
assumptions with finite reachable segments, overlap, deterioration and rival
response; no initial cohort becomes a universal market ceiling.

The private session and group event remain separately costed optional services.
They cannot establish that software delivery is autonomous. Every nonzero founder
operation, buyer participation cost and modeled human comparator must be labeled.
No real customers, sales, restored equipment, deployment or Super Alpha claim
follows from this prospective selection.
