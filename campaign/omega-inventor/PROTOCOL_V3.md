# Omega V3 — construct executable economic mechanisms, not just cheaper search

Prospective continuation from 98f0680517201a26a7bfd743bcbaaf2455f32eb3. Original experiments and accounts remain unchanged. This protocol does not predetermine the full-objective verdict.

## Construction

Generate deterministic allocation/payment programs from a finite public market specification: buyer type distribution, independently executable service outcomes, complete declared cost envelopes, hard budgets, outside utilities, and voluntary participation. The seller must not see a realized buyer's private type before posting the mechanism. The mechanism is a generated menu/program, not a choice among the predecessor's 32 developer-written policies or an assumed cost reduction. No new physical capability is inferred from a new contract.

Use established automated mechanism design and counterexample-guided constraint generation. An ordinary mixed-integer optimization solver is an eligible construction tool, not a claimed new algorithm. Generate and independently check participation, affordability, no-payment-for-no-service, and every supported unilateral misreport. Where different outcomes are assigned, require a declared integer utility margin; equal outcomes must have equal payment. This is a finite, deterministic, quasi-linear one-buyer model, not general collusion resistance or a universal equilibrium. Buyer refusal, unknown types, nonentry and competitive substitution remain possible.

## Learning

A failed incentive check supplies an executable missing constraint. Retain validated constraint relationships, with schema compatibility and provenance, for fresh mechanism construction. Never trust inheritance instead of rechecking all incentives. Compare full-constraint conventional solving, cold counterexample-guided solving, inherited-constraint solving, optimal separate pricing, optimal pure bundling, and a strong ordinary mixed-menu mechanism using the same feasible set. The strongest full solver may tie; do not conceal it. Distinguish constraint-set improvement, constructed-game improvement, and complete lifecycle improvement.

## Evaluation

Development/pilot data are exposed. Freeze implementation and retained memory before confirmation. Three registered fresh market families use seeds 391101,391102,391103: complementary values, substitute values, and additive values with outside competition. Common outcome interfaces have actually executed source-grounded work; valuations, population weights, prices and cost tariffs are authored hypotheses, not observed willingness to pay. Confirmation includes every declared type and all unilateral reports, plus exact mutation tests, changed budgets, stronger outside options, copycat response, missing productive inputs and high costs. No finite sample frequency is called a market probability.

Record solver-reported bounds/gaps separately from exact integer feasibility checks. A numerical optimization result is not a mathematical optimality proof. Count construction, failed solves, full incentive checking, production, refunds/rejection, acquisition, payment, support, maintenance, research and residual obligations. Report CPU time and structural operation counts separately; do not claim logical-work savings as measured financial profit. Research/setup tariffs are declared uncertainty envelopes; actual included-host/model bills remain unknown.

Where feasible, connect generated accepted outcomes through the existing JournalAccount and source-bound services. Use a fresh explicit simulation account rather than rewriting any existing account, with exactly US$100,000 for comparability. No forecasts, borrowed balances or hypothetical financing may pay for present actions. Exports must not contain account/recovery authority. Verify completed-account resume and negative scenarios. Compare matched complete financial trajectories, including search acquisition costs, against capable existing arrangements and nonentry.

## Decision

A functional finite-market engineering verdict requires automatic mechanism construction, independent incentive/outcome verification, strategic voluntary execution, current-cash financial continuation, inherited discovery changes, and successful fresh revalidation. Economic-superiority and full open-world objective claims require their own positive complete-cost evidence; a functional loop or finite solver tie does not supply that evidence. Report failure or unresolved conditions without changing the target or preserving a preferred architecture at the expense of the strongest alternative.
