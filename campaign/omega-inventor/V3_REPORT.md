# FLYWHEEL Ω V3 — executable economic-mechanism invention

**Achieved:** an end-to-end, automatically executed finite-market loop that constructs allocation/payment programs, checks strategic incentives, delivers actual source-grounded work, settles simulated payments, learns reusable constraint structure, and funds a checked successor from earned cash.

**Not established:** the broader open-world objective, independently measured complete-lifecycle superiority over strong complete alternatives, empirical demand, a durable competitive advantage, a production release, or US$3 billion in trailing-year profit. This report does not substitute the finite result for those objectives.

## What changed

V1 combined assumed cost reductions. V2 improved search over 32 supplied adapter procedures. V3 instead constructs new executable allocation/payment tables from a public finite market specification. The buyer's realized private values are not supplied to the constructor. Buyers subsequently choose among the posted offers or decline.

The generated object specifies which accepted outcome each report receives and its payment. The search uses ordinary mixed-integer optimization. A separate integer verifier checks every supported unilateral misreport, hard budget, outside alternative, voluntary participation and payment rule. Rejected candidates supply concrete incentive constraints; validated relationships are retained as a sparse graph and tested on fresh markets. Inherited constraints are hints, never approval: every new mechanism faces the complete verifier.

The native operating path reuses the existing JournalAccount and independently checked KPI/reconciliation services. There is no second ledger. Production is actually executed, not inferred from a claimed cost reduction. New scientific instruments or physical productive capabilities are not demonstrated by this contract construction.

The prospective protocol was published before confirmation at `a0ab160228defe939398b4f3dfacffc59e8081bd`, extending exact parent `98f0680517201a26a7bfd743bcbaaf2455f32eb3`. Original experiments, accounts, losses and source-specific results remain unchanged.

## Registered fresh comparisons

Three nine-type markets were registered: complementary values, substitute values, and additive values with outside competition. For each, compare inherited counterexample-guided construction, cold construction, full conventional optimization, optimal separate pricing and optimal pure bundling. All controls receive the same outcome definitions, budgets, costs and feasible participation requirements.

| Market | Generated expected contribution | Best standard offer | Full conventional optimizer |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Complementary | 55013/37 cents | 55013/37 cents | 55013/37 cents |
| Substitute | 22503/29 cents | 22503/29 cents | 22503/29 cents |
| Competitive | 6001/8 cents | 29377/40 cents | 6001/8 cents |

The competitive case improves expected contribution by **15.7 cents (about 2.14%)** over the best separate-or-bundled offer. The other two are ties. The full conventional optimizer ties all three. Its use is legitimate: an autonomous constructor does not need to invent a new optimization algorithm. However, a tie supplies no proprietary or unrestricted discovery-superiority claim.

All generated programs pass exact checks of the 72 possible unilateral type-to-report deviations per market. Numerical solver status and gaps apply only to the declared deterministic integer model; they are not formal mathematical certificates of global optimality.

### Learning is real but not universally better

| Inherited constraint structure | 1,140 | 576 | 0.560 s |

Inheritance reduces constructed rows by **113 (9.02%)** relative to the same cold method. This is a scoped causal transfer comparison, not a monetary saving or robust speed claim. The stronger full solver is faster overall and should remain an eligible, preferred operating method on these cases. The experiment does not force the more elaborate method to win.

## Five complete native simulated businesses

Each business begins with its own US$100,000 and executes a sparse 365-day schedule. Each of the 27 synthetic types receives one opportunity; this schedule is not a random sample from the prior type weights, an empirical demand forecast or short-run annualization.

The generated mechanism earns **$5.024 more modeled profit (3.94%)** than the standard-offer arm under these exact assumptions. It does not beat the full conventional construction. A copied menu is tested as a feasible buyer outside option, not asserted to be the globally optimal rival policy.

The positive arms each pay **$5 from unrestricted earnings** to construct a fresh successor mechanism, after the declared maintenance and tax provision. The successor executes and passes independent incentive verification. That expenditure is included in the reported expenses. Funding and successful execution do not establish that no equally funded ordinary route could construct the successor.

All five accounts passed source-matched native financial and operating audits. The generated run was interrupted by the execution tool after a planned order; exact-source continuation resumed the same account and completed without reseeding. A subsequent completed-account resume left the financial export byte-for-byte unchanged.

## What the cost claim does—and does not—cover

The ledger charges every declared setup, discovery, acquisition, productive delivery, verification/support envelope, payment fee, annual maintenance, tax provision and successor expense. Required costs are reconstructed independently; omitted costs cannot be excused by a self-consistent ledger.

These tariffs are **authored resource envelopes**, not measured commercial invoices. Actual developer, model and host lifecycle costs remain unknown. The study records method construction CPU separately, including unsuccessful solver attempts and checking; it does not manufacture dollar costs from those measurements. Consequently, these results are conditional modeled profitability, not validated real-world complete-cost profit. Actual revenue is **zero**.

## Scientific basis and remaining objective

Established automated mechanism design and constraint-generation methods are components, not evidence of unrestricted invention. Relevant primary sources are Mittelmann et al., *Automated Synthesis of Mechanisms*, IJCAI 2022; Zhang and Conitzer, *Automated Dynamic Mechanism Design*, NeurIPS 2021; and SciPy's official mixed-integer linear programming documentation. Links are retained in the prospective protocol. The implementation deliberately separates numerical optimization from exact finite incentive verification.

The connected engineering loop now works within this modeled domain: **construct a mechanism → expose strategic defects → revise → verify → deliver → collect → cover obligations → retain useful structure → fund and verify a successor**.

The full mission still requires the broader opportunity/production discovery and evidence that the resulting arrangements remain economically superior after lifecycle costs and capable competing responses. A working finite-market mechanism inventor is a substantive achieved capability; it is not permission to claim that those remaining requirements have been met.
