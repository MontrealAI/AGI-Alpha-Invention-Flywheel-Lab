# Complete alternatives before a flagship claim

Primary sources checked 2026-11-03 UTC. Bounded review, not exhaustive novelty
search, customer interviews or deployment proof. **HOLD unmet-need assertions;
STOP describing interval-robust water allocation or a certificate as Super Alpha.**

## Three consequential possibilities

1. **Industrial water reuse:** preserve useful production under freshwater
   constraints, uncertain contamination and voluntary multi-facility participation.
   Existing robust network methods, incentive mechanisms and engineering tools
   already address the core construction. Chosen as the cheapest decisive
   falsification probe, not as a demonstrated new commercial opportunity.
2. **Microgrid black-start resilience:** restore critical power after blackout.
   [DOE's project account](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/systems/articles/success-story-using-renewable-microgrids-keep-lights)
   describes simulated restart, four-microgrid resynchronization and incorporation
   into commercial products. [Associated research](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10045825/)
   includes 24 inverter resources. A small graph simulation cannot substitute for
   dynamic protection/hardware validation or establish a new restoration capability.
3. **Industrial feedstock closure:** convert qualified by-products into reliable
   substitute inputs. [International Synergies](https://international-synergies.com/)
   already provides facilitated symbiosis and a resource-matching platform with
   reusable solutions. [A 2024 research tool](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/chemical-engineering/articles/10.3389/fceng.2024.1363888/full)
   models liquid-waste properties, demand, prices and recovery costs. Matching
   is not qualified material, executed supply or closed-loop production.

## Water: serious complete controls

- [Aviso: robust water exchange networks](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0957582012001589)
  addresses independently operating plants, incomplete information and future
  scenarios. Its accessible abstract does not establish exact equivalence to
  this fixture, but defeats claims that robust inter-facility exchange is new.
- [Flexible water networks under uncertainty](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S095965262202978X)
  addresses flow/concentration uncertainty, multiple contaminants and globally
  solved nonlinear models. This probe's linear fixture is substantially narrower.
- [Aussel, Cao Van and Salas](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.09863) formulate a
  leader–follower exchange game and minimum improvement over stand-alone options.
  Their described validation excludes regeneration units; do not claim complete
  equivalence to every treatment-sharing problem.
- [WaterTAP](https://watertap.readthedocs.io/en/latest/index.html) supplies process,
  costing, flowsheet and UI infrastructure. [PyROS](https://pyomo.readthedocs.io/en/stable/explanation/solvers/pyros/index.html)
  supplies two-stage robust nonlinear optimization. Neither is automatically a
  turnkey deployment, but competent conventional engineers may combine them.
- [EPA industrial reuse resources](https://www.epa.gov/waterreuse/water-reuse-industrial-applications-resources)
  emphasize intended use, treatment and monitoring. This is illustrative U.S.
  guidance, not a local legal determination or approval of this synthetic model.

The implemented complete control uses SciPy/HiGHS MILP with the same integer
flows, interval upper endpoints, participant rights, source capacity, costs and
freshwater limits. Conventional software runs at machine speed. Human-plus-AI
providers and ordinary self-service can use the identical known formulation;
we have not run human subjects, measured their engineering time or established
an AI labor/productivity advantage. All may build/reuse the same tools.

Under nonnegative flow and monotone linear upper-quality constraints, maximum
concentrations at interval upper endpoints give the exact robust counterpart.
That is established elementary reasoning, not an invention. It does not apply
unchanged to nonlinear reactions or decision-dependent uncertainty.

## Residual and next decision

No reviewed source establishes that one existing self-service product delivers
all practical requirements. Equally, no evidence establishes that competent
people cannot economically assemble the full solution. Site data, treatment
validation, construction, agreements and total lifecycle economics remain
unknown. No private/customer data was gathered and no demand was inferred.

If the conventional control ties this exact model, STOP this flagship claim.
Retain the usable checker and explicit missing assumptions. Advance only after
finding an evidenced consequential residual and registering a new complete
comparison; do not rename packaging convenience as a new productive possibility.
