# FLYWHEEL Ω: coupled buyer/seller probe

Registered before first execution, 2026-11-03. Base main: `f8933a6`.
This is a fresh, independent finite authored experiment, not a migration of any
existing owner account. No model API, credentials, paid services, real customer,
outreach, purchase, deployment or actual transaction is part of this experiment.

## Consequential ambition and falsifier

Could cross-facility non-potable industrial water reuse preserve useful production
when the freshwater allocation is insufficient? The missing-means hypothesis is
an executable construction and checkable certificate combining source capacity,
interval-quality bounds, allocation, infrastructure and voluntary cost limits.
This is synthetic planning research, not drinking-water, physical, regulatory or
safety qualification. A complete existing route satisfying the same contract
falsifies a newly attainable-outcome claim. Existing water-network optimization
is a serious complete alternative, not a deliberately weak baseline.

Three candidate classes are screened in PRIOR_ART.md: water reuse, microgrid
resilience and feedstock closure. Water is chosen for a decisive local test, not
because an unresolved commercial or scientific gap has been proved. STOP/HOLD
is an acceptable outcome; packaging alone does not earn Blue Sky admission.

## Frozen comparisons

1. Candidate: integer dynamic programming across feasible source allocations.
2. Strong conventional control: independent exhaustive complete enumeration with
   the same information, capacities, costs, acceptance and construction rights.
   Its computation runs at machine speed. This is executable conventional
   software available to competent humans, not a timed human-subject study.
3. Fresh successor changes water quality/demand constraints. Reconstruct with
   the same general algorithm, remove inherited plan, and offer equal-content
   ordinary reuse. An old certificate never certifies a changed request.
4. Local/default model choice makes no model call. A buyer may independently use
   its own paid model account to propose a plan, save JSON, and run the same
   checker. This repository never requests or stores its credentials, invokes
   that account, or claims a model-quality benchmark it did not execute.

Acceptance: exact integer source/demand/capacity/quality accounting; source and
sink consent; each sink's delivered cost no greater than its freshwater outside
option; transfer compensation at each source's disclosed unit quote. Declining sources supply nothing; a declining sink makes its mandatory
production contract infeasible rather than silently dropping its demand. No unavailable supply, forecast income or unmeasured
water safety is assumed. All objective ties are reported.

Primary outcomes: accepted freshwater displacement under the constrained cap,
complete alternative result and cost, fresh-successor acceptance, equal-content
reuse difference, voluntary optional-offer choices and funded native expenses.
Blueprint acceptance is separate from installation and economic value.

## Exact preregistered implementation scope

Before first scientific execution, the source commit freezes 24 cases: primary,
changed-quality successor, known total-supply infeasibility, source withdrawal,
and 20 deterministic authored stress cases from seed 20261103 in runner.cases.
These are not hidden or fresh external data. The stronger conventional control
is SciPy/HiGHS MILP with integer flows and zero requested optimality gap, not the
initial proposed exhaustive enumerator. Both share only request validation and
receive exact independent acceptance. DP and MILP objective is lexicographic
freshwater then operating costs. No human or model speed advantage is inferred.

Two contaminants, nonnegative integer flow and monotone upper quality constraints
make upper interval endpoints exact for this mathematical model. Source unit
quotes abstract collection/transport; treatment efficacy, infrastructure capital,
biology, nonlinear reactions and real-world interval calibration are unvalidated
prerequisites, not omitted costs to be treated as zero. No physical model claim.

## Verification repair after initial execution

Initial candidate/MILP comparison tied on all 24 cases. Independent review found
resource pre-enumeration and report-tamper gaps; these are engineering failures,
not new scientific wins. A large-range regression initially exhausted memory,
then gained a cardinality check before Cartesian-product materialization. Corner
enumeration now refuses above 4096 points. Both return resource exhaustion, not
false infeasibility. Seller replay now rederives every field and rejects inflated
sales/profit, altered policy, boolean/integer substitutions and extra claims.

An added exhaustive integer oracle checks all possible allocations on the same
24 already-exposed small fixtures, eliminating dependence on a floating-point
optimality claim. This is post-result verification, not a new preregistered
scientific arm or held-out evidence. Original scientific acceptance and outcome
are unchanged. Re-run all checks after these repairs; do not relabel old evidence.

The first seller run was an authored fixed expense schedule with sales disabled.
Its US$24,800 loss was neither strategic optimization nor evidence of commercial
failure. In that closed zero-revenue model, non-entry would preserve US$100,000.
The US$8,000 construction item was not measured execution cost or an established
estimate. Preserve that run as a compact negative engineering lesson in
HISTORICAL_REPORT.md / HISTORICAL_RESULT.json, not as the current business model.

The corrected `omega.strategy` model is declared before its native execution,
but after seeing the old failure. It is not a historical preregistration or an
empirically calibrated demand model. It offers non-entry, paid information,
staged capability construction, stop, subsequent product choices and pivots.
Every observation leaf has one common executable continuation for every still
compatible hidden world. Information is paid for before revealing the signal.
The native `omega.product` adapter executes fictional but explicitly modeled
orders, actual software artifact construction/checking, modeled session
attendance/completion, payment, acceptance, refund, settlement and retained
liabilities. Live customers, actual sessions, legal rights and real settlement
are not prerequisites for the explicitly hypothetical simulation and are not
claimed to exist.

One independent native account per policy/world uses the US$100,000 reference
capital; results are comparisons, not pooled money or copied balances. There
are no top-ups, resets, loans, account migrations or changes to existing owners.
Each run spans [0,8760) hours. Funding requires current unrestricted liquidity;
forecast sales cannot finance their own costs. Accepted orders retain the full
price plus native collateral through the inclusive 720-hour claim window;
financial maturity at hour 721 releases collateral, not new revenue. A 1%
residual reserve remains, and later excess claims still stop investment.

Controls: exact expected-value finite policy, the same no-research policy class,
robust maximin policy, static-all-products with voluntary non-entry/stop, and
unconditional non-entry. Sixteen authored worlds combine four preference regimes,
a noisy research signal and normal/adverse fulfillment. Every buyer retains
no-purchase, free capable self-service and ordinary-provider alternatives. All

prospects per quarter are a bounded fixture, not a market-size forecast. Demand,

Costs and sources are recorded in COSTS.md. Packaging quantities range 20/80/160
hours rather than asserting a fixed US$8,000. Unknown model billing/platform base

## Consumed-case and correction lineage

The original 24 water fixtures and every seller world are authored and exposed.
No rerun is counted as a fresh holdout or an independent market replication.
The correction sequence was: fixed cost-only run; native product adapter and
causal policy integration; actual-paid-cost enforcement; fuller session workload
and private outside-choice observation repair; explicit free-core menu; separate
autonomous machine-cost sensitivity. These are engineering revisions to already
seen synthetic cases. Central priors and buyer premium remain authored inputs,
not customer estimates. There is no empirical confidence interval, discovery
claim, repeated-testing error guarantee or fresh confirmation inferred from these
reruns. Independent adversarial reviewers checked causal and financial code;
they did not supply independent market demand or physical water evidence.

The exact finite policy comparison is useful for checking the decision machinery
and financing arithmetic. It does not prove acceptable completion in every
residual world: that is a separate robust policy question, and this robust model
selects non-entry. Continuous-time research/delivery qualification, competition,
imitation and advantage-expiry/renewal races are not modeled. Existing ordinary
methods remain valid complete buyer alternatives, and ordinary equal-content
reuse wins the successor tie. No blanket paper-compliance claim is made.
