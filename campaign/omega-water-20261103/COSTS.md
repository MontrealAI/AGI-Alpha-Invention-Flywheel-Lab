# Costs: measurements, anchors and explicit sensitivities

All model amounts are **USD**, not a confirmed commercial currency. Native
accounting uses integer microUSD; policy planning uses integer cents converted
exactly. None of the historical US$24,800 or the corrected modeled operating
expenses is an actual bill paid by this lab. No API or paid service was invoked.
Local wall/CPU time is measurable; assistant inference, host allocation, existing
subscription and total energy costs are not available as a reliable invoice.
A Pro subscription is not assumed to provide free per-customer compute.

Primary source anchors checked 2026-11-03:

- [Stripe US standard pricing](https://stripe.com/pricing): illustrative domestic
  online card processing 2.9% + US$0.30. Round each transaction to nearest cent:
  US$299 → US$8.97; US$99 → US$3.17; US$20,000 → US$580.30. Original processing
  expense is retained after a refund. Country, manual entry, international cards,
  currency conversion, negotiated pricing and disputes can add costs. This is
  not the user's processor contract or a complete quote.
- [BLS software developers](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/software-developers.htm):
  May 2025 median annual pay US$135,980. An authored 2,080-hour denominator gives
  US$65.375/hour, not the user's measured wage or a contractor quote.
- [BLS employer compensation](https://www.bls.gov/charts/employer-costs-for-employee-compensation/costs-per-hour.htm):
  June 2026 private-industry wages US$32.82/hour, benefits US$14.07, total US$46.89.
  Applying the aggregate burden ratio to the developer median gives a rounded
  **US$93.40/hour proxy**. This cross-series approximation has not measured this
  enterprise's payroll, jurisdiction or marginal construction cost.
- [Zoom webinar starting price](https://www.zoom.com/en/small-business/webinars/):
  US$79/month starting anchor. [Zoom webinar licensing](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0064444)
  describes a licensed user plus webinar add-on and capacities beginning at 500.
  Three months at the starting anchor is US$237 per operating quarter, not a
  complete quote for the needed base plan, region or configuration.

Explicit authored quantities and missing costs:

- Packaging: total 20/80/160 hours (low/central/high), allocated 40% software,
  25% webinar and 35% private-session capability. These are sensitivity inputs,
  not measured construction hours. Each capability is paid only when selected.
- Optional research: 1.5 proxy labor hours. Reach/outreach: 0.25 proxy labor hours
  per active quarter, charged even when all three modeled prospects decline.
  The reach of three prospects is hypothetical and does not establish a CAC.
- Webinar: 330 total preparation/presentation/admin minutes at the labor proxy
  plus quarterly platform anchor, whenever offered. A 60-minute workload is
  retained only as a lower-bound sensitivity.
  Unknown required base-plan increment is tested at US$0/30/90 per quarter.
- Build inference bill: unknown, tested at US$0/100/1,000. The zero setting is an
  optimistic boundary, never an observation of free compute.
- Marginal fulfillment: authored US$1/software, US$0.25/webinar seat and
  US$1/private session, plus processing. These small per-order envelopes include
  modeled infrastructure/support/verification, not independently measured bills.
  A native artifact check is real computation but its dollar value is unknown.
- Founder: 30-minute conversation within 105 total review/preparation/admin
  minutes per attempted private session, one slot per quarter; authored
  opportunity rate US$200/hour, independent of the US$20,000 offer.
  The workload envelope is adapted from [PR193 economics](https://github.com/MontrealAI/AGI-Alpha-Invention-Flywheel-Lab/blob/973036c2f2059b3c82a54970dce02746f44098a0/omega/economics.py),
  itself an authored scenario, not observed times. Sensitivities include 30 total
  founder minutes, a US$2,000/hour opportunity rate, 12 exception minutes per
  attempted order and 20 dispute minutes per refund. These exception times are
  noncash owner opportunity costs, not invented salary payments. Opportunity
  rate and availability are unvalidated sensitivities.
- Simulated refunds preserve incurred expenses and remove collected revenue.
  A 1% residual reserve is an authored native risk-term parameter, not a loss
  estimate or proof that all legal liabilities end. Adverse worlds reject every
  attempted fulfillment. No stochastic empirical refund rate is claimed.
- Actual tax, legal/IP, deployment, regulatory and complete hosting/support costs
  remain unknown. Tax sensitivity must not replace actual tax accrual. The water
  blueprint does not include a physically installed or licensed customer system.

The original arbitrary US$8,000 construction item is retained only in the
historical failed design. It is not reused as an established cost. Positive
modeled profit is conditional on all these assumptions and is **not** complete
actual profit, viable demand, financing evidence or a Super Alpha finding.

## Autonomous cost sensitivity, not compulsory payroll

The human-assisted labor proxy is one comparison, not a claim that autonomous
construction requires paid developer hours. A separate arm sets packaging labor
and webinar facilitation labor to zero, disables the optional human founder
slot, and models a US$100/1,000 machine-build envelope, US$1 information action
and US$1 reach action. These are explicit authored machine-cost stress inputs,
not actual bills or measured acquisition effectiveness. Software construction
uses 40% of the build envelope (US$40/400). Marginal fulfillment, payment fees,
refunds and residual liabilities remain. Actual machine, hosting, acquisition,
support, tax and complete costs are still unknown. No model subscription is
treated as free scalable infrastructure. Optional-founder sensitivities keep
the required 30-minute conversation but do not impose unmeasured manual prep.
