## Finite registered comparisons and stopping rule
Exactly four paths are compared without synthetic buyers: **direct regulated-device supply**, **OEM/authorized-manufacturer companion**, **nonmedical industrial/research validation**, and **no-deploy/no-bid**. The first three can advance only with documented scope-specific independently accepted outcomes, current eligible buyer route, permission/regulatory/channel rights, verified complete cost and funding, and (where relevant) explicit voluntary partner agreement and real payable consideration. Missing evidence is a refusal, not zero cost or an assumed sale. For any expired official deadline, refusal is mandatory. For an unknown deadline, do not infer that it is open. Distinguish whole-bundle procurement values from EIT-only values.

Use read-only, deterministic standard-library checks against an explicit source-evidence manifest and adversarial fixtures, never proprietary procurement portal access or private customer information. No internet fetch by the evaluator, no spending, no API calls, new credentials, outreach, bid, offer, medical-device assertion, production, account mutation, or native ledger operation. Tests must preserve missing/false/expired/secondary-only/other-channel states, and force **NO_DEPLOY** when evidence is absent. No score is a measured commercial win. Publish failures, stop after one finite gate execution, and retain the normal protected integration/release boundaries.
