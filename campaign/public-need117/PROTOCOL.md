## Executed supporting construction

The concrete risk is interpreting AI-classified sampled perceptions as population opinion when independent labels, abstentions, denominators or sampling provenance are missing. Build an offline **sample-level** confusion/abstention instrument that explicitly refuses population and commercial claims.

Engineering acceptance is: exact five-column CSV, unique IDs and strict labels/predictions; source SHA-256 binding; visible unlabeled and abstained cases; per-group confusion, scored fraction, observed error and conditional Wilson 95% intervals; separate reviewer independently re-counts and rejects source tampering, receipt forgery, duplicated records and invented commerce; HTML dashboard is static and accessible. The role `EXPOSED_DEMO` is **UNQUALIFIED_DEMONSTRATION**. `FROZEN_HELDOUT` plus asserted independent review can only be `BOUNDED_HELDOUT_DIAGNOSTIC`, because the operator's declaration itself is not independent evidence. No representative-population estimate or bias certificate is output.
