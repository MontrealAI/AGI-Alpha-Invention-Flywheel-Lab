# Complete useful-offer selection: fixed economic comparison

This experiment corrects a specific controller restriction: previously a buyer's service index fixed one primitive offer before optimization. The new opt-in controller can select among the buyer's predeclared applicable admitted jobs, including a coherent receivables packet. Every packet executes and independently verifies reconciliation, source-derived receivables/collection metrics, and evidence-linked exceptions from the same entity, period and source. It consumes three production/supplier/support and capacity units. It is **not** full bookkeeping, a CFO service, collection outreach, tax preparation, account attestation or ledger writeback.

## Fixed before outcomes

Bundles are not offered by this request policy. Identical deterministic requested-job assignments, content cohort, capacity and paid channel menu apply in both arms. Neither a bigger pool nor generated inventory counts as acquired demand or sales.

The menu arm additionally incurs a $50 authored development envelope; both incur existing positive production/planning envelopes, all considered-offer qualification costs including no-bids, actual channel/capacity invoices, payment/support/refund/tax costs and native obligations. The reducer reports the additional unmodeled development cost that would exhaust each observed profit advantage (negative values mean no headroom). This is sensitivity arithmetic, not a measured development bill. Actual model/host/engineering billing remains unknown. The deterministic packet executors make no frontier-model calls; do not claim measured frontier-model autonomy or free inference.

## Independent values, strong alternatives and uncertainty

The exact runner declares joint buyer value/budget atoms independently of seller price and human wage anchors. Packet values $45/$120/$200 and budgets $180/$400/$800 have weights 30%/40%/30%; primitive values are separately specified. These are transparent uncertain market hypotheses, **not empirical willingness-to-pay estimates**. The permitted finite packet grid is $30/$50/$75, not borrowed CFO pricing. The finite controller may choose a primitive, a packet or no bid; buyers may select human, rival, self-service or refusal. The public prior is only truncated by observed spending, not full inference from choice history. No hidden budget/value draw enters the selling policy.

Human packet/reference prices ($120 packet,$45 primitive), autonomous alternatives ($85/$30), and software access plus buyer effort ($10+$20 packet,$3+$12 primitive) are explicitly authored **per-request all-in envelopes**, not advertisements for equivalent completed work. For the self-service-dominant control, self-service offers equal modeled quality and only $2 effort. The nonpayment case retains the positive-demand model but sets 25% nonpayment, 96-hour clearing and 80% retention. Positive cases retain the current declared nonzero failure/delay assumptions. Outside providers have independently available per-request contracts in this bounded market; no shared rival-capacity equilibrium is claimed.
