# Blue Sky admission record 063

This record separates opportunity selection from scientific and commercial qualification. A selected construction is permission to test a hypothesis, not a declaration of a breakthrough. Machine-readable requests, comparison arms and acceptance limits are frozen in [`flexibility063/REGISTRATION.json`](../../flexibility063/REGISTRATION.json).

## Three materially different possibilities

| Candidate | Consequential outcome | Residual limitation | Decisive inexpensive probe | Strong complete alternative | Selection consequence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pooled flexible-load contracts | Small resource owners collectively offer a useful dispatch quantity while preserving their own energy needs and outside options. | An individual resource is below the declared service threshold; aggregation also needs a realizable schedule and mutually acceptable compensation. | Construct and independently verify an integer-energy pooled schedule; retain infeasible and buyer-refused cases; compare isolated resources and unrestricted ordinary pooling. | Existing aggregators and an ordinary mixed-integer optimizer with the same resources and information. | Select for a complete local software experiment; scientific novelty remains unestablished. |
| Retrospective environmental measurement | A beneficiary reconstructs a decision-relevant past condition without having installed the desired instrument beforehand. | Distinct latent histories may be observationally indistinguishable; physical uncertainty and source coverage limit inference. | Construct an indistinguishable pair and ask whether the available observations identify the required quantity. | Existing archive analysis, uncertainty-aware remote sensing and installing a sensor for future measurements. | Do not sell identification that the available evidence cannot support. |
| Complementary manufacturing processes | Several small production capabilities jointly realize a part or process no participant can adequately deliver alone. | Interface tolerances, material history, quality transfer and physical acceptance can defeat apparent route compatibility. | Check the compatibility graph and identify the physical measurement needed to accept a composed process. | A qualified integrated manufacturer or known supply-chain planning system. | Defer physical/commercial claims without machine access and accepted manufactured output. |

The probes are intentionally bounded. A graph or identifiability counterexample is not an empirical market study. Their role is to avoid committing the flagship to an untestable or misleading first demonstration.

## Seven questions for the selected construction

1. **Useful possibility.** A coalition of subthreshold flexible resources can deliver a scheduled aggregate response while each participant retains its required energy and receives compensation above its declared displacement cost. The beneficiary receives a checkable dispatch plan, not an assertion that physical loads have moved.
2. **Remaining constraint.** The authored contract requires 100,000 Wh of response in a one-hour event, with a 5% derating allowance. Isolated resources may fail this quantity. Pooling must simultaneously satisfy each device's availability, power, energy, event-response and compensation constraints. Established aggregation already addresses this general class, so historical impossibility is not claimed.
3. **Why investigate now.** Recent publicly accessible computer-use and coding capabilities can lower the effort of assembling and maintaining such a complete digital workflow. That vendor-level change motivates a cost hypothesis. This campaign uses deterministic production and included development assistance; it does not measure a direct frontier model's production cost or establish that AI crossed a new market affordability threshold.
4. **Means constructed.** An executable request-to-schedule pipeline with exact integer-energy contracts, established HiGHS mixed-integer production, output disaggregation, participant-payment accounting, a separately implemented verifier, source-bound delivery artifacts and a connection to the canonical funded enterprise account.
5. **Discriminating experiment.** Hold the request and acceptance contract fixed. Run pooled production, isolated-resource ablation, unrestricted ordinary HiGHS pooling and fresh successor work whose equal-resource control may rebuild the same solver. Compare acceptance, quantities, participant surplus, costs and feasibility. Charge construction and retained-asset lifecycle costs; preserve ties and losing cases.
6. **Commercial dependence.** The simulated paid product is the accepted dispatch-planning artifact. Revenue cannot be earned from an unverified schedule or from the hypothetical physical value of the response. Buyer refusal, rival alternatives, native payment terms, refunds, incurred costs and obligations remain operative.
7. **Further frontier.** A retained request/verification interface can support a fresh two-event contract and additional resource combinations. Whether it adds value beyond ordinary rebuilding is a separate successor comparison. Physical dispatch, telemetry, calibrated uncertainty, actual market access, observed demand and lifecycle support are necessary further tests before any real energy-service claim.

## Admission decision

**Admit a bounded engineering and conditional commercial test. Keep Blue Sky Flagship qualification unresolved.** The construction can demonstrate a formulation-relative feasibility change from isolated to pooled resources. A conventional complete alternative can reach the same result, so this is not sufficient evidence of a new productive industry, superior discovery, scientific novelty or general superintelligence. Commercial and investment decisions must follow their own numerical evidence.
