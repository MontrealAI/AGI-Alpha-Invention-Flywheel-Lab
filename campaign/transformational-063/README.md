# Transformational 063: construct the productive arrangement

This edition tests a narrow, executable instance of a larger ambition: make a useful outcome attainable by changing the productive arrangement, deliver it, and measure whether the result supports its full costs and an economically justified successor.

The selected construction pools small flexible loads into a verifiable dispatch contract. A synthetic contract has a minimum deliverable quantity; individual resources cannot meet it, while a voluntary coalition may. The software constructs a schedule, disaggregates it to participants, and checks resource limits, participant outside options and contract delivery independently. **The experiment executes software on authored profiles. It does not operate electrical equipment or establish electricity-market qualification.**

The scientific question is deliberately stronger than whether pooling works. An unrestricted conventional optimizer can also pool resources, so crossing a threshold against an artificially isolated baseline does not establish a new industry or a new algorithm. The strongest complete comparator and an equally resourced successor remain in the comparison. Commercial, Blue Sky, investment and successor decisions are independent.

## Evidence and reproduction

The executable protocol, acceptance rules and source-bound outputs live with the `flexibility063` construction. Financial decisions are projections of the existing `JournalAccount` and mandatory adaptive `Account`; this edition introduces no alternative ledger. Current source, command receipts and independent audits determine the operative status. Neither this narrative nor a passing correctness test certifies a commercial result.

## Claims that remain separate

| Question | Required evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Does the construction deliver? | Executed schedule and independent acceptance against unchanged constraints. |
| Is the activity newly attainable relative to a stated restriction? | Accepted outcome with pooling, failure under the specified isolated formulation, and a named restriction. |
| Does AGI ALPHA outperform an established complete route? | Matched unrestricted conventional comparison with construction, verification and lifecycle costs. |
| Is the offering commercially viable? | Repeated voluntary choices, accepted delivery, native recognized profit and obligations covered under declared simulated assumptions. |
| Is an investment affordable? | Current unrestricted cash after obligations and reserves, with actual funding attribution. |
| Is retention worthwhile? | Fresh successor work with versus without inheritance under equivalent starting resources and complete costs. |
| Has the primary objective been reached? | US$100,000 primary seed and native fixed-endpoint trailing-365-day complete-cost profit of US$3 billion; short-cohort annualization is ineligible. |

All buyers, orders, payments, supplier terms and settlements in this campaign are simulated. Model/host billing that has not been measured remains unknown rather than zero. No real transactions, outreach, market registration, equipment control or separately billable model calls are authorized by these artifacts.
