| Replayed native money while trusting operating coverage metadata. | Independent review found that omitted attempts/coverage or altered choice summaries needed their own rejection. | Recompute registered request coverage and buyer choice from source-bound protocol and evidence; do not accept self-consistent producer summaries alone. |

| Pooled individually subthreshold synthetic loads and compared ordinary pooling. | Pooling accepts four requests; the ordinary complete route ties all four and a request-only generalist ties the fresh successor. | Keep the useful construction; do not advertise algorithmic or inherited superiority. A restricted-baseline win is not a new-industry result. |
| Bought a portable instrument under an authored positive-benefit forecast. | The matched fresh successor has no added accepted work and US$15 extra declared cost. | Distinguish ex-ante affordability/forecast from realized return; continue viable base delivery while declining unsupported retention. |
| Used immediate-expense accounting with idempotent native development charges. | Duplicate expense replay is prevented and an incompatible double-charge policy fails closed; a capitalization/amortization view is absent. | Retain the consistent implemented policy and disclose the unsupported mandatory variant. Do not count fail-closed rejection as implementation of a different accounting treatment. |

Earlier development versions are not retained as duplicate packages in the current tree. The single retained native record identifies its own source and audit scope. Declared development, failures, acquisition, operations, fees and tax costs remain in the experiment; this compression does not erase financial loss or rewrite historical outcomes.
