Trusted local source and custody, not a hostile-code security sandbox. No commands supplied by observations or requests are executable.

Finite experiment: one frozen canary, four fresh successor instances, three matched arms (retained bridge, ordinary identical-kernel reuse, independently authored direct method). Same input observations, context, tolerances and per-request safety envelope. These are authored exact synthetic cases, not physical measurements or independent external certification. All methods see the same input and calibration bounds; direct.py remains unmodified and may abstain conservatively.
