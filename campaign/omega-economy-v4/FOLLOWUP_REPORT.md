## What the operating decision changes

After the complete prepaid study, `cart_decision` independently verifies all
public candidate outcomes and selects the greatest exact modeled expected
contribution. Nonentry wins when the best contribution is nonpositive. On positive
ties it removes alternatives dominated in comparable rows/checks, preserves all
contribution ties and the remaining work frontier, then uses an explicit lexical
convention. Every study arm and failed attempt remains in sunk work and costs.
No counter is converted into wages, invoices or a claimed total-cost optimum.

The adaptive account actually chose equal-content reuse, equal-content reuse and
full conventional construction across the three exposed markets. It settles the
same nineteen buyers and earns the same modeled profit as generated/full
conventional accounts. No nonentry occurs in these positive-prior markets; the
separate high-cost regression proves refusal happens before private observation,
quote spending, wallets and orders. This is an implemented decision gate, not an
online demand-learning controller or an open-world model-discovery loop.
