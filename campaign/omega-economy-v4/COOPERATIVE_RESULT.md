## Proposed game and negative cases

A public capability may benefit nonpayers. Simply requesting every beneficiary's spare budget produces a profitable withdrawal deviation: the remaining contributors can fund production, so a payer prefers free riding. The constructor repairs this by selecting a coalition whose fixed contributions total the cost threshold exactly; every payer is pivotal. Independent checks verify individual budget, strictly better participation utility and each possible unilateral payer withdrawal.

Under the authored 900-cent cost envelope plus60-cent margin, the central three-beneficiary case admits a two-payer coalition, while no beneficiary can individually commission the same complete outcome within its650-cent budget. Removing one beneficiary still permits two-person cooperation. The ordinary exhaustive shared-commissioning control ties all eight cases. Weak values, zero demand, a free copied alternative and the cost overrun produce nonentry. Delayed commitments remain unreceived; failed verification blocks delivery.

These are public assumed values, not inferred private types. The model does not certify truthful elicitation, coalition-proofness, enforceable exclusivity, observed demand or real contracts. Restricting the alternative to separate commissions would manufacture the apparent enabling advantage; the full shared-commissioning incumbent defeats that claim.

## Finance boundary and next test

Contribution maps are proposed obligations, never received money. This module has no ledger and reports zero actual revenue. Existing unilateral native cart contracts cannot represent simultaneous multi-beneficiary assurance custody, refund and consent without a dedicated integration. A caller may pay a native earned-funded R&D expense and attach the verified productive proof as its successor output; that demonstrates funded construction, not cooperative sales or causal necessity of the prior earnings.

The next decisive implementation is a source-bound native assurance adapter with all-participant conditional custody, production funding, independent acceptance, refunds and unilateral-withdrawal traces. Its comparison must retain ordinary shared commissioning and complete actual acquisition/verification costs. Even a successful adapter would not by itself establish buyer demand or a $3B trailing-year profit result.
