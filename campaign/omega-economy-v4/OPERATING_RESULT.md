## Original primary: one complete year

The designated generated-central account began with exactly **US$100,000** and covers model days **[0,365)**; no short-period annualization. Nineteen of 27 offers settled; eight buyers declined. Collections equaled recognized revenue of $285.950. Expenses were $20 setup, $15 discovery, $2.700 quotes, $42.500 production/verification/support, $8.690 payment fees, $12 maintenance, $37.012 modeled tax and $5 expensed successor R&D. The tax was 20% of positive pretax income assessed before the final R&D charge. Development was expensed, not capitalized; there were no refunds or unpaid expenses. Closing cash was $100,143.048; $291.950 of tail obligations left **$99,851.098 available**, with no open investment reserve. The ordinary full-solver account tied. The $5.472 difference from the standard arm would cover only 3.93 minutes of additional annual attention at the $83.55/hour wage-and-benefits proxy, holding posted tax fixed (4.91 minutes if the 20% tax also falls). The [BLS hourly wage](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/math/data-scientists.htm) and [professional benefits ratio](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/ecec_09092026.htm) are sensitivity inputs; no human or hybrid operator was timed. A seed-funded incumbent could also perform the successor work.

The [original seven accounts](NATIVE_EVIDENCE.json), [eight corrected-source engineering accounts](CORRECTED_NATIVE_EVIDENCE.json), and [ten new paired accounts](../omega-paid-reach-20261104/PAIRED_EVIDENCE.json) have separate identities and histories. None of the latter eighteen accounts is a reset, extension or second year of the primary. A failed-acceptance stress refunded all nineteen would-be buyers; nonparticipation and failed collection were retained.

## What changed with distribution

In the central environment, sponsored access raised collections from $461.990 to $794.510 and settled baskets from 28 to 45. Annual profit rose from $262.792 to $467.904, an increase of **$205.112**. The seller paid $6.25 on day 90 and renewed for $6.25 on day 118. Seven first-tranche settlements outside the organic roster had collected $136.920. After $44.580 of paid-route variable costs and the $6.250 invoice, the observed contribution supporting renewal was $86.090. Higher protected obligations left the pilot with **$134.208 less available cash** than its organic counterpart at the close, despite the higher profit.

Under weak fresh demand, copycat competition and zero fresh demand, each paid pilot lost $7.160 relative to organic and gained zero settlements. Supplier activation failure cost $5.000 relative to organic after tax, with zero additional settlements. All four declined the second purchase. One of the five specified environments justified renewal. These settings have no assigned probabilities.

## Decision

Keep the composed-purchase checker, independent acceptance and native cash controls. Use the simpler demonstrated production or search alternative when it ties. Treat revenue or acquisition forecasts as unvalidated until an independent complete outcome and per-customer costs can be measured. The [next physical decision experiment](../omega-ktc-residual-20261103/NEXT_PHYSICAL_DECISION.md) specifies an exact sensor comparison and keeps existing valve/controller telemetry eligible. It is proposed, not executed: the available data label the commanded inlet state, and do not justify selling fault detection.
