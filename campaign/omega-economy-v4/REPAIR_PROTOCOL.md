# Post-review correction and operating-decision protocol

Registered after the first V4 experiments and before corrected-source account
confirmation. This is engineering requalification and retrospective application
of an operating rule, not fresh evidence of discovery superiority.

## Findings retained

1. Completed resume returned an old report after a legitimate additional native
   expense. Portable historical audit and live-account reconciliation must be
   distinct operations.
2. Public audit accepted altered successor construction-work and parent-memory
   metadata. Financial correctness alone did not establish those learning claims.
3. The operating arm was supplied by the caller. The operating process did not
   itself apply the campaign's preference for a capable conventional alternative.

The original findings, source, seven accounts and failed aggregate test remain
preserved. The original generated-central f1074b1 account remains the primary
challenge account. Corrected-source replicas and the adaptive account are
independent qualification comparisons: no balance is reset, transferred or pooled.

## Corrected behavior and checks

Completed resume must reopen the existing authoritative account under its native
ownership mechanism, compare its full export with the recorded export, and reject
divergence without overwriting evidence. Missing operating authority is not
permission to seed another account. A missing derived SQLite cache may be rebuilt
from intact authority. Pre-account bootstrap interruption remains recoverable.
Independent audit must reconstruct successor metadata and bind the funding work
to the retained parent content. Registered schema, arm and stress labels are checked.

After paying the existing setup and study costs, the adaptive arm may use only
the verified public study to choose a program. Select maximum exact modeled
expected contribution; choose nonentry when every contribution is nonpositive.
On positive ties, remove alternatives dominated in comparable rows/checks while
retaining the full contribution tie and remaining work frontier. A final lexical
choice is a convention, not a discovered economic advantage. All study costs and
failed candidates remain incurred. Work counts are not dollar costs; unknown
actual lifecycle costs remain unknown. The synthetic distribution is a public
model, not observed demand.

Nonentry must occur before private buyer observation, quote spending, orders and
production. Audit replays the decision and checks absence of those actions. This
is a bounded evidence-to-operation gate, not a claim of autonomous opportunity
discovery, optimal full lifecycle economics or online demand learning.

## Finite confirmation

Freeze the corrected runtime, run its native/decision/regression/recovery tests,
then execute and independently audit eight separate accounts: the original seven
registered arm/stress combinations plus adaptive-central. Each replica uses
exactly $100,000 starting capital. Compare completed resume with recorded exports;
retain ties, losses and all failed tests. Source-drift runs are rejected, not counted
as passes. Existing original accounts also receive an independent live-export
reconciliation without changing their authority or old reports.

The correction succeeds only if stale state and forged provenance are rejected,
valid recovery still works, the actual adaptive operation matches its public
decision, and finance independently reconciles. It does not erase the unresolved
first aggregate errors, establish demand, or validate scaling to $3 billion.
