**Selected immediate experiment:** cooperative commissioning, because the actual capability generation and independent output acceptance can run with the available repository and expose whether pooling, inheritance or ordinary reuse changes the decision. Its implementation belongs in `mechanism_harness/cooperative_construction.py`. It is a supporting economic-arrangement test; **no candidate in this record has yet earned the full Blue Sky flagship claim**. That admission condition is preserved rather than silently replaced by a routine adapter benchmark.

The scientific observatory is the next consequential alternative if the cooperative experiment ties or loses: preregister a particular property decision on physical held-out measurements, execute the strongest released reconstruction first, and test only the residual it leaves. No absent measurement is treated as proof of impossibility. No imagined market size ranks these options.

## Public evidence and its limits

- [KTC 2023 dataset paper](https://www.aimsciences.org/article/doi/10.3934/ammc.2024009): physical electrical-impedance data and algorithms across diminishing electrode coverage. This supports availability of a concrete evaluation substrate, not market demand or a new measurement channel. The paper's public results were inspected; raw data were not analyzed in this selection.
- [NASA lunar surface technology](https://www.nasa.gov/lunar-surface-technology/) and [project 146991](https://techport.nasa.gov/projects/146991): NASA describes Blue Alchemist as an integrated regolith-simulant-to-solar-cell/materials system. These primary descriptions require comparing against an existing complete industrial route. They do not supply this project's process costs or guarantee lunar operating economics.
- [Moulin and Shenker, strategyproof sharing of submodular access costs](https://public.econ.duke.edu/Papers/Abstracts96/abstract.96.31.html): cost sharing is established prior art with substantive assumptions and efficiency/budget-balance tradeoffs. The present public, copyable capability must explicitly test free riding; an excludable-good theorem cannot simply be imported.
- [Mittelmann et al., Automated Synthesis of Mechanisms, IJCAI 2022](https://www.ijcai.org/proceedings/2022/61): synthesis from logical specifications is existing work. Finite construction is legitimate machinery, not evidence of scientific novelty or unrestricted invention.

No wage, valuation, conversion probability, supplier quote or demand observation is inferred from these sources. Human workers may use conventional software at genuine machine speed. Human attention, AI inference, development, integration, verification, maintenance and supplier costs remain separate quantities; unknown costs stay unknown.

## Scope of the next decision

Run the registered finite experiment and retain every negative result. If ordinary shared commissioning or the conventional producer matches the result after complete costs, use it where commercially useful and reject the stronger inventor-superiority claim. If copying removes all willingness to pay, stop that offer rather than inventing exclusivity or sales. A follow-on claim requires a new natural task, independently accepted useful outcome and complete competent alternatives; cash-funded repetition alone does not establish this.
