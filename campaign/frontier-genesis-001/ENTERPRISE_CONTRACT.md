# ALPHA GENESIS FLYWHEEL Ω — Enterprise contract (research charter)

> **See the frontier. Discover the consequences. Improve the machinery that discovers consequences.**
>
> **Build the advantage that builds the next advantage.**

## Product, not a model leaderboard

**GENESIS offers an outcome:** a customer specifies an important activity it cannot accomplish at an acceptable complete cost, with permitted evidence, resources, rights, and acceptance requirements. GENESIS searches every available complete means: the strongest frontier model or agent directly; ordinary software and specialist firms; buying/licensing; partnership; combining proven capabilities; constructing a missing means; redesigning the problem or economic arrangement; a diagnostic experiment; and declining to enter.

**The purchaser obtains:** an independently inspectable capability or honest failure verdict, its executable artifacts, applicability and invalidation conditions, cost/resource record, the strongest complete alternative, and an authorized path for further use. Customer-owned information stays within declared permissions.

The first commercial wedge is a **frontier-triggered, evidence-backed discovery-to-delivery capability for enterprise engineering/research bottlenecks**. This is a *market hypothesis*, not a proven buyer segment or an authorization to contact customers. The current Blue Sky 064 charter requires comparing at least three materially different consequential opportunities before promoting a flagship; tomography is one qualification track, not the product identity.

## The three levels, as operating contracts

**I — Frontier Observatory.** Collect public primary sources, model/tool releases, scientific work and capability evidence at bounded intervals. Record publication versus actual availability, model interface, permissions, measured skill, operational failures, latency and full costs. Measure source coverage and missed relevant releases; never claim omniscience. The current `watch.py` implements only on-demand, bounded GitHub release metadata and marks access UNVERIFIED.

**II — Consequence Inventor.** Represent capabilities as typed operations with prerequisite sets, evidence, costs, rights and failure conditions. Generate *new candidate chains* from available means through intermediate instruments to possible buyer outcomes. Seek structural transfers and missing means, but also routes that eliminate the apparent need to build. For every candidate, ask what more becomes possible as a successor. The current `genesis.py` solves an exact **authored finite graph**, not an open-world opportunity generator. New outcomes beyond its grammar require accessible frontier-agent proposals and independent acceptance.

**III — Improvement of opportunity discovery.** Candidate improvements may alter the search policy, mechanism abstractions, information acquisition, opportunity ranking, instrument construction, or the planner itself. Freeze the challenger; compare it across fresh, independent campaigns against an unrestricted frontier agent with equal models, information, tools, permissions and complete resources. Measure whether discovery improves after separating stronger input models from inherited tools and the revised *method of invention*. A new archive or model upgrade is not evidence of a better inventor.

## Allocation: economic rationality, not invention vanity

At every decision, consider **use / buy / partner / compose / invent / experiment / defer / exit**. The objective is to maximize expected, risk-adjusted *collected* value of accepted work plus separately estimated, evidence-grounded successor option value **net of all search, construction, verification, failure, financing, distribution, migration, maintenance and opportunity costs**. Honor voluntary buyer/supplier participation, rival responses, cash timing, hard requirements and permissions. Uncertain option value is never booked as earned profit or unrestricted cash.

Use an existing frontier agent when it wins the complete comparison. Retire an inherited capability when marginal benefit no longer covers its carrying and integration costs. Do not make an invention merely because it is elegant. Buy information when the *expected improvement in subsequent decisions* justifies the experiment. A financial optimum is meaningful only for an explicit game, assumptions and feasible actions; global perfect foresight or universal optimality is not claimed.

No additional paid models, external customer contact, commerce, secrets, hardware purchase or real transactions are authorized by this document. Reuse the canonical `JournalAccount` and adaptive `Account` when actual authorized enterprise operation is later integrated; do not create a competing ledger.

## Proof gates for the enterprise

| Gate | Minimum evidence needed | Failure means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Observe** | Fresh, attributable source; operational access separately verified; measured input skill/cost | Stay unverified, never use restricted demonstrations as installed tools |
| **Construct** | Executable complete route and matched best alternative; new outcome or meaningful affordability threshold | Use incumbent or revise claimed obstacle |
| **Qualify** | Frozen candidate and original purpose; fresh independent acceptance; complete measured cost and failures | Reject or retain unknown, not a narrated win |
| **Commercialize** | Actual voluntary buyer, accepted outcome, collected payment, complete-cost surplus and protected obligations | No claim of real profit, regardless of synthetic attractive figures |
| **Compound** | Inherited means causally improve a genuinely different fresh successor against equal-content controls | One useful invention, not established recursive invention |
| **Improve invention** | Revised opportunity-discovery procedure wins across fresh full campaigns holding underlying model/initial archive fixed | Improved tool/model only, not a verified stronger inventor |

Do not promote the flagship on the basis of a paper, dashboard, conventional solver tie, simulated profit, or one finite decision-model proof. The target of US$3 billion trailing-365-day complete-cost profit remains an open objective, not a forecast or ceiling.

## The next decisive experiment

1. Register three consequential, materially different outcome hypotheses, each with a strongest complete route and an explicit unresolved dependency.
2. Give an accessible frontier agent and a GENESIS candidate generator the same new public capability, current source corpus, executable tools and complete resource budget. Require executable proposals **not restricted to the existing finite action grammar**. Register failed candidates.
3. Choose a single previously uneconomic or infeasible *buyer-valued* outcome for a source-grounded prototype and independent confirmation. Test non-entry, purchase and strongest specialists with equal permissions.
4. Run a genuinely different successor with and without inherited mechanisms, keeping initial model and resources matched.
5. Only when customer permission and spend authority exist: test real demand, acceptance, collected revenue, and self-funded repeat deliveries through the existing canonical enterprise.

**Current state:** finite, reproducible source-bound and allocation research only. No live global monitor, open-world model, commercial revenue, new physical observation, or measured meta-discovery gain. The operating enterprise is the next construction, not a present-tense achievement.
