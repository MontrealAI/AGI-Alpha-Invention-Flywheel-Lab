## The selection change

Evaluate the joint package: technical capability, actual prospective counterparties, responsibilities/transfers, finance and delivery timing, rights, and evidence. A useful technical candidate can remain an exploratory hypothesis even if unprofitable alone, provided a testable complementary arrangement might make the complete package viable. Do not fund the incomplete hypothesis as an accepted business. Do not force a coalition when a direct agent, existing supplier or bilateral transaction is better.

## Three frontier-linked possibilities

### A. Jointly commissioned computational inventions — first research priority

Several independently motivated users receive different accepted deliverables from a common newly constructed computational primitive. The object to invent is the reusable operation AND the arrangement that makes constructing and adapting it worthwhile.

Google's Computational Discovery added MCP integration on November 3, 2026 for approved users; its scientific-coding work supports investigating integrated construction. AlphaEvolve's earlier industrial examples are a precedent and strong alternative, not a new-this-week release or proof that those applications share one kernel.

A concrete search family is error-controlled scientific simulation/verification: identify a common parameterized numerical operation across independently documented workloads, then implement and validate each adaptation. The commonality must be demonstrated, not inferred from similar vocabulary or equations. Do not share calibration or acceptance evidence beyond its applicable domain.

Candidate arrangement: conditional commissioning of common development, separately priced and accepted adaptations, explicit nonexclusive rights, failure allocation, withdrawal conditions and financed timing. Actual participants, prices and funding are UNKNOWN. Compare direct frontier agents, scientific software vendors, accessible ERA/AlphaEvolve, specialists, buying an existing component and non-entry with equal freedom.

The first decisive construction is not a marketplace website: reproduce the strongest complete routes for distinct public workloads and determine whether a genuinely shared core plus ALL adapters can satisfy the original requirements with lower complete burden. Retain failed sharing attempts. Passing does not establish buyers. A fresh independent adoption must justify maintenance and transfer; an improved inventor requires another comparison.

### B. Jointly commissioned investigative instruments

OpenAI's November 3 physical-engineering report connects mechanical design, electronics and software but retains manufacturing corrections and review. The proposed market is access to purpose-built investigative capabilities that independent teams cannot economically commission separately.

Possible common means: compatible acquisition/control infrastructure, fixture-generation procedures and analysis software. Each buyer still needs its own sample model, calibration, measurements and acceptance. A single-material print does not realize multiple material properties. Shared qualification cost cannot be assumed for unrelated domains.

Compare existing instruments plus competent integration, specialist services and direct AI-assisted engineering. Reject the sharing hypothesis if incompatible requirements or per-user redevelopment consume the claimed fixed-cost saving. No hardware purchase or physical validation is authorized here.

### C. Jointly commissioned verifiable-AI infrastructure

Anthropic's November 2 FlashProve release provides executable and formal artifacts. Its reported large-model configuration incurs roughly 70–95 times ordinary-inference GPU time. That is configuration-specific, not a universal multiplier or a price quote.

A compatible group might share model-architecture proving infrastructure and integration work. Separate requests still incur their own proving costs; coordination cannot make those marginal costs vanish. Different model commitments, rights and threat models may limit sharing.

Compare direct provider delivery, signed records, hardware attestation and outcome-specific checkers. Model-origin assurance and output correctness are different requirements: do not substitute one for the other. If per-delivery contribution is nonpositive, adding buyers cannot rescue positive fixed development cost without changing something else. The existing PR240 proof work remains separate and its stored verification samples are not promoted to live-model assurance.

## Frontier Replay must update BOTH sides

A new AI capability may change setup cost, qualification probability, adaptation costs, access and timing. It can also improve buyer self-service and competitor offers, reducing the price a buyer will accept. Update the strongest outside option after every relevant advance. Technological progress does not monotonically improve a particular venture's economics.

Rank evidence-producing experiments before irreversible investment when commonality, demand or cash assumptions remain unknown. Public evidence justifies investigation; only an authorized counterparty establishes its acceptance. No fictional names, commitments, exclusivity rights, deposits or future revenue become available funds.

## The next achievement to earn

Frontier evidence → independently documented needs → tested shared construction → accepted voluntary terms → feasible cash path → independently accepted delivery and actual collection → a fresh adoption that benefits from retained means.

Preserve the paper's five separate gates: technical possibility, modeled viability, accepted arrangement, realized exchange, repeatable market. Compare technology-only and arrangement-only controls to diagnose complementarity, and also an unrestricted strong agent/entrepreneur allowed to find the same package. A useful market does not require a novel algorithm; a novel algorithm does not establish a market.
