# Earned-funded paid reach after a positive organic start

Register before executing these buyer, job or channel seeds. One prospective synthetic comparison asks whether a seller can use collected earnings to purchase access, serve additional voluntary buyers, and justify a second tranche using observations available at purchase time. This is a distribution experiment for the existing V4 cart service. Its finite cohort supplies no empirical market size, commercial demand or evidence of a human-time advantage.

## Accounts and observations

Run the Cartesian product of **organic, pilot** and **central, weak, copycat, no_demand, failed_activation**, ten fresh independent accounts. Each starts with exactly US$100,000 simulated capital and zero earned funds. Each accounts for the entire interval of model days [0,365). Original V4 balances, obligations and signers stay at their original accounts. Never pool results. The two arms share each environment, public market and menu, prospect IDs, jobs, qualification draws, operating rules and tariff. Freeze the source before confirmation. Run all ten cells including failed activation, missed purchases and unfavorable differences. There is one registered cohort, no reselection or replacement. Engineering tests use alternate seeds or explicit small fixtures.

Every entrant pays the V4 $20 setup, $5 for each of three market studies and $12 annual maintenance. Run the full charged cart study with its unsuccessful constructions, public finite verifier, and existing public adaptive selector. Use each original selected menu for its original warm contacts, then use the first complementary market for all fresh customers. A null selection skips private observation and individual quotes. Record construction work and CPU separately. The finite underlying generated menu sells distinct KPI variance and reconciliation outcomes, singly or as a set union. The buyer can refuse, take a component, compose affordable components, or buy a joint offer. Only a completely accepted union can collect. An outside score represents other providers and self-service in aggregate. This experiment does not independently establish those providers' real pricing, conversion or performance.

The common pre-period re-executes all 27 exposed V4 central adaptive opportunities in original case/type order. On day 2+3i, i from 1 through 27, quote buyer warm-001 through warm-027 respectively under the original type values, budget and outside option. The separate one-time wallets receive at most their original budgets. For every required service use the existing synthetic fixture with cohort seed 392900 and zero-based index 7+i. These jobs, types, prices and favorable underlying central environment were already exposed in the original V4 work. Their receipts, costs and earned liquidity must be produced anew in each native account. Preserve every refusal. Do not treat reproduction of this prefix as a fresh demand test. The prefix runs even if an expansion gate later refuses.

Use cohort seed 2026110401 and native ChannelBook seed 2026110403 across all fresh post-periods. The 24 persistent fresh prospects are reach-000 through reach-023, disjoint from warm buyers. For each n from 0 through 11, day 92+7n has exactly one organic contact for reach-nnn. This is the complete organic roster. Other days have zero fresh organic contacts. Post-start shocks never change prior receipts. Each new buyer draws one persistent type from the first market's declared weighted distribution. Its lifetime wallet is twice the type budget. On each visit, including a new job bought on a later day, the cart spend cap is the lesser of the original type budget and remaining lifetime wallet. No top-ups. A failed refunded purchase restores buyer funds under native rules.

For reproducible draws take the first eight SHA-256 digest bytes as an unsigned big-endian integer, hashing compact ASCII JSON of the list [cohort seed, purpose, buyer ID, day]. For type selection use purpose "type" and day 0, reduce modulo the sum of type weights and use the ordered cumulative weights. For qualification use purpose "qualify" and actual contact day, modulo 10000. The threshold is 7500 on contacts before any collected purchase and 5000 after at least one collected purchase. This is a retention and qualification hypothesis. Quote costs apply even if qualification or acceptance fails. Post-start central and failed_activation leave values unchanged. Weak halves each valuation with integer floor. No_demand sets every valuation to zero. Copycat matches the best attainable budget-feasible buyer surplus by increasing the outside utility by the nonnegative gain available from the public menu. Ties with nonparticipation decline. Wallet, qualification and selection are evaluated on a paid quote only. No private buyer attributes enter a channel decision.

## Exposure and production

Only the pilot arm may buy the existing default pilot contract, with its $6 price plus $0.25 support due before access. Its two-day lead, unchanged 21-day term, two slots/day, 95% activation, 75% realization, 25% shared-ranking draw, one prepaid activation retry, seven-day contact cooldown, three-contact fatigue limit, four excluded owned IDs and zero failure credit remain in effect. Failed_activation alone changes activation reliability to zero, testing both attempts with the invoice retained. Feed all 24 IDs to the native ChannelBook on each relevant day through expiry. Organic IDs 004–011 can also receive paid exposure. On a day where both sources reach a buyer, process exactly one organic contact, retaining the paid exposure as overlap in the channel record. Process any organic contact first, then remaining paid contacts in ChannelBook order. There are no automatically purchased orders or unlimited unduplicated leads.

Limit attempted production to two service units/day and 48 units per service/year including warm work, identical in both arms. Check the whole chosen cart against every required service and both capacities before committing production. Charge a $0.10 quote per deduplicated offered contact on an entered menu. On a supported, funded, capacity-admitted voluntary order, use and charge the complete declared production envelope once, including any subsequent failed acceptance. A single reconciliation job without the required input tables cannot fulfill the economic outcome. Native deliveries use the generated schema and existing independent acceptance plus equivalence to the admitted reference. Allocate 132 source-bound job slots per service. For a buyer's numeric ID n the index is 36+4n for organic work and 36+4n+k on its kth paid-only routed visit (k=1,2,3). These comparable new jobs prevent same-day partial fulfillment, duplicate components and repeat sale of the same job. Use a persistent native buyer wallet, unique native job/order identities, consent, fund, independent acceptance, and one whole-basket settlement or full refund. Fees follow existing market basis points on collected price. Preserve failures, consumed capacity, tail reserves, refunds and native liabilities.

## Purchases and decision evidence

There are two ex ante review days, 90 and 118, before that day's contacts. The first plan is reach-pilot-1 and the second is reach-pilot-2. Both arms evaluate the same public rule and may inspect only their own dated history. The organic arm declines the supplier. The pilot may purchase only after at least one warm delivery has collected and its declared trial estimate is strictly positive. Before seeing the fresh cohort, hypothesize that the 42-slot first contract will yield two incremental orders at the warm average realized contribution. Compute floor(2 times warm collections less twice all warm production and fee costs, divided by warm settled orders), then subtract the $6.25 invoice and all 42 potential quotes at $0.10. This explicit trial prior is a hypothesis about conversion and margins, not a market measurement. For both purchases, require native unrestricted earned cash of at least $6.25 and available cash of at least $18.25, leaving the fixed $12 annual maintenance liquid. Native protected reserves already excluded from available funds remain protected. The native reservation and spend must actually succeed with seed use disabled before recording a purchase. A plan or a supplier receivable has no spending authority.

Review day 118 follows the first contract's expiry. It adds a strict hurdle. Require the first paid invoice, at least one settled first-plan order from a buyer outside the entire organic roster, and strictly positive **observed attributable pretax contribution**. Compute this diagnostic from exclusive first-plan realized collections less the entire first invoice and *all* first-plan routed quote, incurred production and payment-fee costs. Include costs from refusals, unqualified contacts, failures and organic-roster buyers reached through a paid-only contact that day. Give no revenue credit for buyers ever eligible for organic contact, a same-day overlap, unpaid/refunded work, activation promises, projected repeats or future collections. The public evidence is the invoice, counts, realized receipts, paid costs and pre-decision native liquidity. If either gate fails, skip the tranche. For the second native plan's expected net, assume a repeat of the first observed attributable pretax amount. No retries at different purchase dates. This diagnostic can still miss opportunity cost from displaced organic work. Actual incremental *enterprise* profit is measured retrospectively by the separately operated matched organic arm. The decision function may not inspect it, the scenario label, future channel draws, unused private wallets, future valuation or held-out production results.

Recognize annual maintenance and the modeled 20% nonnegative pretax provision with the same accounting rule in both arms. Native channel, qualification, production, payment and maintenance expenses remain incurred. Reconcile every order, invoice, release, wallet and cost against native authority and report cash, restricted obligations and available funds. The tariffs are authored complete-work envelopes within this model. Actual operator development and research labor, infrastructure, model opportunity costs, support load, collection delay and market acquisition economics remain unmeasured. Do not claim fully measured real profit. No R&D or funding from the prior primary account, real spending or outreach.

## Decision and stopping rule

Report all five paired annual realized-profit deltas, source-bound order/collection totals, paid and deduplicated reach, overlap, repeat buyers, complete orders, invoices, both tranche decisions, per-service use and outstanding obligations. Report whether volume and after-cost profit increased together, where either fell, and where zero expansion or nonentry wins. Two invoices are the registered test of repeated investment. No extrapolation beyond this cohort/year or conversion of a small margin to the $3 billion milestone. Record a zero-entry comparator with zero new activity, revenue and costs, clearly identified as an arithmetic outside option, plus a unit-test cold gate in which the same seed cash with zero earned cash cannot finance either purchase. Test positive booked margin with insufficient available cash, exact break-even, overlapping attributed collections, and wrong-full-cart capacity. Use tamper/recovery checks against native evidence. A second tranche can be locally justified and still lose against organic control. Preserve that outcome. A failed funding or activation gate is an observed result, not a reason to change the seed. Any new treatment after viewing outcomes needs a separately registered question and evidence.
