# One next opportunity, native commerce and self-service

This is a fixed conditional native simulation, not actual business revenue or a billion-dollar scaling result. Two planning horizons operate in the **same** public finite buyer game. Only `lookahead_opportunities` differs within each pair. No inferred gain is promised: the exposed tiny engineering pair tied at two paid deliveries and −$20.176534 each.

The opt-in executed market includes human, rival, enterprise, self-service and refusal. Self-service has explicit fee, effort, quality, success probability, delay and switching effort. Its authored success/failure events are not independently verified outside-provider artifacts. Fees are spent on failures too. Public value/budget atom priors are declared assumptions; latent realized atoms are never seller inputs. Observed spending truncates the public prior, but full inference from historical choices is not implemented.

Two-opportunity planning uses an observable-outcome-conditioned next choice, not a multiplicative repeat bonus. Successor work is admitted and finite, its costs/clock/retention are included, and shared job/customer benefit IDs prevent unverified duplicate continuation claims. Continuation changes ranking only, never native expected profit, booked income or available cash. Native authorization uses current cash and obligations. Current-cash-only future feasibility, frozen observed rival quote/eligibility (not joint rival capacity depletion or weekly response), immediate nonnegative contribution and positive native expected-net constraints are restrictions of this edition, not universal business laws. Funded loss leaders and globally optimal multi-customer dynamic games are not implemented here.
