## Decision and frozen test

The central Commitment001 quote ties a complete outside option after buyer
friction. Test whether a public-price-only policy can avoid this known tie while
covering the complete declared cost envelope. Then test whether the resulting
settled unrestricted earnings actually cover the *whole* distinct successor.
Keep the US$100,000 primary requirement and US$3B exact trailing-year objective;
neither is established by this short experiment.

Freeze three arms: the original fixed quote, a competent direct competitive
quote, and the proposed exact quote constructor. The direct control gets the
same public prices, cost envelope, friction and tick. Independent exhaustive
enumeration on small integer grids must agree with the constructor. Additional
inspection with the same information is an equal-content control, not invented
new information. No AI method superiority or human productivity claim is made.

Policy inputs contain only comparable dated public alternatives, the firm's
declared friction and complete planned costs, price tick and margin requirement.
Scenario names, buyer value/budget, future shocks, future collections and supplier
consent are excluded. Missing, expired or incomparable complete quotes require
abstention. A proposed price is neither a sale nor cash nor permission to spend.

Predeclared mechanical cases: central tie; profitable undercut; outside price
below full cost; no usable quote; stale and incomparable quotes; unexpected
private/scenario field; Boolean/negative/noninteger money; non-divisible tick;
very large exact integers. Enumerate outside prices 1..40, friction 0..5,
cost 0..35 and ticks 1, 2, 3, 5. Every offered quote must be the largest tick
multiple strictly beating the cheapest comparable complete alternative after
friction, with positive complete planned contribution. Abstain otherwise.

Native test: use the existing BusinessAccount, JournalAccount and independent
SQLite acceptance with a fresh 17-record synthetic restore request fixed by
record index. No new ledger. Exactly US$100,000 opening per *disposable test arm*;
these are never pooled with or substituted for the primary enterprise. Central
public quote assumptions are unchanged. Tick is US$0.01. Charge the original
US$880 setup, US$100 acquisition, US$840 first-delivery cost, US$30 payment fee,
US$100 closing maintenance and the original 20% modeled profit tax. Production
follows a native prepayment. Buyer valuation and budget remain in the test-world
response only. Require independent content acceptance before collection.

At the predeclared successor decision, compare the entire US$990 budget against
both native available cash and unrestricted settled earnings. Insufficient
earnings must defer without an expense or deletion of recognized profit. Test
seed-funded affordability separately; do not call earned funding necessary.
Reject tampered delivered content independently. Retain all arm results and
costs; do not change price or cases after observing results.

## Interpretation and authority

The experiment can demonstrate a useful pricing correction and its financial
limit under authored assumptions. Public AWS restore orchestration/validation,
existing providers and nonentry remain strong outside options. No observed
price, demand, supplier agreement or real payment exists. Actual developer,
model, host, contracting and acquisition costs remain unknown. After-tax
simulated margin is conditional, not complete measured real profit.

No historical financial writer, private checkpoint, production access, outreach,
purchase, paid model call or source migration is used. Unit tests create only
private ephemeral harness accounts. The policy has no account-writing function.
Acceptance is local procedural independence, not independent institutions.
