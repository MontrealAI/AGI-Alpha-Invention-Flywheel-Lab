## Why this supporting dependency exists

A consequential accepted product cannot be chosen from imaginary buyer demand or vendor prices. The [Blue Sky 064 charter](../blue-sky-064/CHARTER.md) requires a strong complete alternative and a financially viable full arrangement; both remain open. Before committing to a newly constructed instrument, test whether **published federal procurement records contain even the weak public title-level signal** for materially different possible beneficiary outcomes. This is *market research only*, not demand calibration, buyer consent, accepted requirements, opportunity-to-bid, price evidence, proof of access or a new productive capability.

The actual source is the Government of Canada's [CanadaBuys procurement data](https://canadabuys.canada.ca/en/procurement-and-contracting-data), specifically the [award notices dataset](https://open.canada.ca/data/en/dataset/a1acb126-9ce8-40a9-b889-5da2b1dd20cb). The 2026-27 CSV download location to attempt (not yet a verified accessible snapshot) is `https://canadabuys.canada.ca/opendata/pub/2026-2027-awardNotice-avisAttribution.csv`. The source says its data were restructured March–April 2026. The actual current CSV schema, availability, number of records and SHA-256 are **UNKNOWN at registration**. An unreachable server, schema mismatch or empty source is a recorded failure, not permission to substitute third-party estimates.

## Competing productive possibilities (all remain HOLD)

1. **Accessible source-first publication and independently accepted document output.** Production means: semantic source, accessibility checks, expert/assistive-technology testing and maintenance. Strong alternatives: professional PDF remediation, accessible-by-design publishing, capable human-plus-AI suppliers and no purchase. PR332's narrow HTML verification does not qualify original-PDF remediation.
2. **Industrial measurement → accepted physical decision.** Production means: physical sensor acquisition/transfer calibration, an uncertainty contract and a verified downstream decision. Strong alternatives: existing NDT, established tomography, specialist lab work and ordinary inverse methods. EIT/other physical negative controls remain authoritative.
3. **Material recovery → qualified component or feedstock.** Production means: physical recovery, impurity/energy/metrology checks, accepted manufacturing and supply obligations. Strong alternatives: ordinary recycling/processing and already integrated industrial/NASA systems.

**No deploy** is the fourth eligible choice. No new price, demand rate, margin, outreach, purchasing or financing is authorized.

## Frozen evidence protocol

Input: exactly the federal FY2026-27 CanadaBuys award-notice CSV URL above, with SHA-256 of actual downloaded bytes. No personal names, contact details, addresses or private data may be retained in public outputs. No fallback datasets and no schema guesses concealed as facts.

Compute three predeclared **title-only** keyword families with Unicode case-folding, in one pass; terms are indicative, not exhaustive and not accepted scope:

- `accessible_publication`: `accessibility`, `accessible document`, `pdf remediation`, `wcag`, `aoda`, `document conversion`.
- `physical_measurement`: `non-destructive testing`, `nondestructive testing`, `tomography`, `metrology`, `industrial inspection`, `material testing`.
- `material_recovery`: `material recovery`, `recycling`, `waste valorization`, `electrolysis`, `resource recovery`, `waste processing`.

The program shall require one unambiguous title column and standard CSV parse, reject duplicate/blank headers and invalid UTF-8, inspect **all source rows**, count records matching each family and intersections, and report *source row count* separately from matching rows. A record may match multiple families; do not sum families into distinct buyers/awards. Disclose ambiguous titles and unknown field meanings; do not count records as opportunities available for us, independent paying buyers, accepted outcomes, executed contracts with us or prospective volume. Do not derive prices or unit economics from missing/uncalibrated values.

Output: compact JSON with source URI, byte length, SHA-256, parser version, manifest of title column used, row-level match counts and intersections, limitations and an explicit `NO_BID_NO_DEPLOY` decision. The artifact should be retained as an ephemeral GitHub Actions artifact; no full CSV or row-level personal/business data is committed or uploaded. Failure to retrieve a frozen source is a failed acquisition attempt, not a market-negative verdict.

## Engineering acceptance and stop

Predeclare at least six synthetic regression types: BOM/multiline RFC4180, Unicode case-fold, overlapping families, zero-match honest output, duplicate-column rejection, and invalid bytes/missing title rejection. Reject improperly formatted rows, unexpected fields and source-empty instead of fabricating default results. Only successful exact-head CI and authenticated source bytes support a claimed actual-data scan. Retain failed runs as failed; source and test repair must be prospective.

**Stop rule:** one frozen FY source attempt and one source-bound scan; publish observed result or explicit failure, no search-until-favorable, no hidden substitute source and no later broadening of terms to force positives. If the snapshot is available, the next independent decision is which *original solicitation and actual acceptance specifications* merit manual examination against strongest complete alternatives; zero successful bids or buyer agreements remain zero. If unavailable, report exactly what prevented access and keep all three candidate arrangements on HOLD.
