**Status:** one finite simulation-free public-data research experiment; not a business, sale, bid, integration or released runtime. Owner: independent commercial opportunity investigation, not native finance/PR40 and not UK notice PR510. The preregistered source is [PREREGISTRATION.md](PREREGISTRATION.md), frozen before construction at commit \`48727b0e7ef1c5be8790a9c064796023358eac98\`.

## Economic dependency

An existing firm investigating a new business needs evidence of *somebody buying work of that type*, not another AI idea list. Federal award notices are evidence of publicly reported contracting decisions, **not** settled vendor cash, realized margins, specific buyer willingness to pay for MONTREAL.AI's product, delivered fulfillment or continuing demand. A title-only AI keyword match is too weak to select a business; investigate the primary solicitation and strong free/in-house incumbents before any next commercial decision.

## Run without credentials, money, outreach or bids

From the repository root:

    python3 -B -m unittest -v tests.test_award_demand213
    python3 -B tools/award_demand213.py fetch --out /absolute/new/award213
    python3 -B tools/award_demand213.py verify --out /absolute/new/award213

The exact registered official URL is used once on fetch; do **not** repeat until an appealing sample appears. Fetch fails if output exists, on redirects, invalid CSV header, any oversized input, or errors. For the normal GitHub Actions run, use \`.github/workflows/award-demand213.yml\`; observed receipt, source hash, actual job URL, test status and source SHA must all agree before claiming even engineering observation. Download the resulting temporary artifact separately if independent source replay is needed. Negative/missing official records are a valid negative commercial result.

## Interpret outcome conservatively

- \`rows_total\` = full official file records actually parsed, not automatically distinct opportunities.
- \`keyword_matching_active_award_rows\` = rows with frozen narrow phrase matches and \`Active\` status; neither proof of market fit nor current open solicitation.
- \`distinct_matching_active_solicitation_refs\` = deduplicated reference tokens, **not independently paid customers** or bid opportunities.
- \`repeat_buyers_proven\`, \`delivered_contracts_proven\`, \`government_payments_proven\`, \`cash_collected\`, \`complete_cost_profit\` = unknown. No amount from an award is spendable or projected company revenue.
- \`operating_action=NO_BID_NO_DEPLOY_NO_FINANCIAL_AUTHORIZATION\` must remain unchanged in this experiment.

Strongest alternatives are free official CanadaBuys search, an experienced procurement specialist using conventional software, existing commercial procurement intelligence vendors, and no purchase. A high-signal awarded need could motivate a separate source-verified new-service construction and authentic buyer willingness-to-pay test, but not a real bid or outreach under this mandate.
