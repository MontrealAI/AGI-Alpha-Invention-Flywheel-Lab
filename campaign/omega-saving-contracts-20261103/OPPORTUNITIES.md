# Opportunity selection: construct a verified computational outcome

Selection date: 3 November 2026. Repository baseline:
`54c50a3f9a96e140f42e7cb50c810607a75a2231`.

The comparison below preceded the principal construction. It uses public primary
sources and explicit unresolved conditions. There is no numerical market ranking,
invented success probability or assumed willingness to pay. The chosen hypothesis
is a finite experiment; it does not permanently limit the enterprise's outcomes,
methods, channels or scale.

## Why select the third experiment

The principal uncertainty is whether a **complete, independently accepted
improvement contract** can create enough buyer benefit after competent
alternatives, buyer effort and verification cost to sustain an autonomous seller.
The linear solver is a tractable first production instrument with objective
output checks. It provides a real executable middle between a proposed saving
and a simulated payment.

The FinOps Foundation's 2026 survey describes organizations trying to fund AI
investment through optimization savings while facing smaller, more costly
remaining optimization opportunities [S7]. That is evidence that complete cost
and verification can matter; it is not evidence that these respondents want this
product, that the proposed price clears, or that a particular savings market
exists for our numerical workload.

The strongest existing numerical methods are admitted from the beginning.
SciPy separates matrix factorization from repeated solves. SciML explicitly
documents cached factorization and low-rank updates. Consequently, ordinary
software can match the algebraic construction. The experiment must find its
advantage, if any, in the whole accepted outcome, deployment burden, correct
selection, cost and voluntary arrangement. A benchmark against repeated naive
solves alone would be inadequate.

This is a consequential **candidate**, not yet a qualified Blue Sky flagship.
If an equally equipped conventional complete route delivers the same outcome
within the same full resource budget, it rejects the alleged enabling residual.
Ordinary profitable delivery may still be worth keeping. Neither a kernel
speedup nor a good simulated contract automatically demonstrates new economic
possibility, improved discovery or a US$3 billion enterprise.

## The commercial terms are part of the experiment

The fixed software hypothesis remains **US$299 once**. It carries no implicit
subscription, future updates or personal-support promise. The fixed-project and
outcome-contingent alternatives are separately stated finite offers. Their
prices, savings shares, verification obligations and payment dates must be
registered before confirmation; proposed terms do not establish demand.

For an outcome-contingent offer, distinguish:

- Measured elapsed-time or operation reduction.
- The buyer's avoided billable cost or demonstrated value of released scarce
  capacity.
- Buyer adoption, verification and continuing operating costs.
- Seller's contractually collectible price.
- Seller's recognized revenue, collected cash and complete-cost profit.

Those quantities cannot be substituted for one another. A faster run on an
already-owned machine does not automatically reduce a cash bill. A provider's
minimum charge, idle capacity, scaling policy or unrelated bottleneck can erase
the economic saving. A performance payment cannot be computed from an inflated
naive baseline when the buyer could freely use competent factorization reuse.
The same principles apply to simulated outcomes.

The buyer controls their data, local installation, accounts, compute and external
actions. This experiment invokes no paid model API, creates no API key, purchases
no computing or advertising, and performs no real customer transaction.
Customer participation and permission are separate from internal founder labor;
both must be measured or explicitly modeled. No seller employee, support team or
unpaid human rescue is introduced.

## Other proposed offers

The **US$99 online session**, limited by the assignment to 500 attendees per
session, could provide education if buyers voluntarily value it. It has distinct
preparation, delivery, administration, platform, refund and founder-time costs.
It does not substitute for autonomous software production. The **US$20,000
private 30-minute session** is likewise an optional founder service, with
preparation and follow-up counted. There is no observed demand for either offer
in this experiment, and neither is silently included in its scalable core.

Simple revenue arithmetic clarifies the burden without imposing a market cap:
even at zero cost, US$3 billion would require at least **10,033,445 purchases at
US$299**, **60,607 full 500-person sessions at US$99**, or **150,000 private
sessions at US$20,000**. The last requires **75,000 live founder hours**, before
preparation or follow-up. These are gross-revenue lower bounds, not profit
forecasts or estimates of obtainable demand. They motivate searching for a
higher-value autonomous arrangement; they do not justify inventing high prices
or buyers.

## Distinct ownership and nonduplication

- **PR191 and PR197:** corrected Omega offers, strategy and funded acquisition.
  This experiment reuses compatible native finance and commerce interfaces; it
  tests accepted computational improvement and performance-sensitive terms.
- **PR192:** reconstructed founder-service counterpart. This experiment opens a
  separately identified fresh US$100,000 primary account and does not reuse its
  balance, identity or private recovery authority.
- **Enterprise078:** SQL/CSV delivery and its associated whole-buyer commerce.
  This experiment's deliverable is a typed numerical response implementation
  with independent numerical acceptance, not another SQL/CSV implementation.
- **Prior compute/codec studies:** useful precedents and prior failures remain
  intact. This is not a renamed codec, claimed new linear-algebra method or
  repetition of a fixed authored scale projection.

No active branch or account is overwritten. The independently registered
counterfactual policies start with matched resources; their results are never
pooled as one enterprise.

## What would change the next action

Proceed from technical construction to commercial confirmation only when the
typed output and independent checker work and complete costs are explicit.
Retain the ordinary method where it ties or wins. Change the offer, terms or
workload if the buyer has no meaningful surplus after its strongest substitute.
Reduce or stop a losing acquisition channel based on permitted observed results.
Build additional reach or capacity only when an available, costed option has
decision value and current unrestricted cash can fund it.

A positive region should demonstrate actual simulated acceptance, collection,
obligations serviced and earned-funded continuation. A weak or parity region
should be allowed to produce refusal or non-entry. If the existing arrangement
cannot plausibly bridge the milestone, use the measured shortfall to specify
what larger productive capability or economic arrangement would be necessary;
do not multiply short-run margins by speculative demand.
