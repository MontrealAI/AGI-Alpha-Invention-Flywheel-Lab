# Economics, complete declared costs and uncertainty

All money is USD; records use integer microUSD. The financial experiment is
conditional on authored demand and equal-work-unit monetization. Actual full
workflow speed losses prevent interpreting its positive-demand world as evidence
of avoided cloud bills or a real profitable offer. No willingness to pay was
measured. The scenario grid is unweighted; no probabilities over worlds are
invented.

## Per-buyer seller cost

| Item | Ordinary-world USD per purchased buyer | Basis |

| Independent acceptance | 5.00 | Modeled; actual verifier runs separately measured |

| Storage and delivery | 1.00 | Modeled |

| One-time qualification | 4.00 | Modeled; actual independent diagnostics also reported |
| Failures, retries and correction | 2.00 | Nonrefundable envelope on every purchased buyer, including successful ones |
| Payment processing | 2.9% of quoted price +0.10 | Modeled; charged even on later rejection/default |
| Additional missing-cost sensitivity | 0,50 or200 | Separate registered complete-native runs |

The first eight items total USD31 per buyer; the adverse world multiplies them
by four (USD124). Payment processing is calculated separately. Homogeneous
cohorts multiply the same per-buyer costs and independently set budgets; grouping
never turns a per-buyer bill into a once-per-cohort charge. The single native
`complete_delivery` invoice is decomposed by these categories in the operating
record and final reconciliation.

Selected base packaging costs USD300; selecting all methods upfront costs800;
a later selected extension costs500. These are explicit estimates for a finite
known-method package, not measured development invoices or intellectual-property
valuations. Every selected campaign pays USD40 internal subscription,
infrastructure and administration envelope, plus the actual channel quote.
There is no construction charge on the no-entry account.

The actual experiment used included coding capabilities and local CPU. It made
no external purchase or model API call. The marginal invoice and allocated value
of included model/host resources are unknown and are not asserted economically
free. Wall/CPU telemetry is a measurement; modeled commercial cash cost is a
separate assumption. Founder time to prepare the initial assignment and install
a future real product was not measured. No fictitious reviewers, sales staff,
founder sessions or paid human workforce generate the simulation's results.

These budgets precede and are independent of the seller's USD299,20%-share and

multiplied into them. The value model explicitly assumes repeat business work

and a strict utility improvement over self-service, the rival and non-purchase.
The world's hidden preferences are unavailable to the seller policy.

Qualification is not conversion. Fixed owned hardware sets avoidable cash to
zero, so compute-work reductions alone cannot motivate purchase. Rival prices
deteriorate over calendar time; qualification also deteriorates with reach. The
base channel prices are USD6/search, USD3/community and USD4/partner per contact,
plus USD15 per campaign and increasing marginal saturation cost. Cross-channel
overlap is20% in positive/parity/fixed-hardware,40% in weak,50% in adverse. The
saturation scale is100,000 contacts or20,000 in weak/adverse: it changes marginal
cost and qualification; it is not a maximum market size. Rights activate after
24h and expire21days after purchase; expired/overlapping rights do not create
fresh buyers. The partner channel is a modeled paid supplier, not an executed
real partnership or employee.

Correction draws (4%,7%,3%,20%,4%) select a declared injected output fault and
actual checked reconstruction; they are not observed production-failure rates.
Late disputes recognize compensation of10% of price seven days after collection
and pay two days later. The numerical output remains accepted; compensation is a
separate cost. Native contingent reversal backing is price+USD0.20 per buyer,
then reduces after the inclusive720hour claim window to1% residual backing.
That residual remains a genuine protected obligation; neither protection level
is silently converted into profit. No insurer recovery is assumed.

Identical finite-package repeat purchase is zero. Fresh successor workloads are
tested for technical transfer, not counted as invented repeat orders. Larger
aggregation only duplicates identical artifacts/terms per separately budgeted
buyer; exact small-scale native comparisons validate integer arithmetic and
funding constraints. No billion-scale aggregation theorem is asserted.

## Accounting and missing costs

Revenue is recognized upon accepted native release; seller collections occur at
that event after the registered delay. Acquisition, production, failures and
construction are expensed when incurred and paid from current funds. No capital
asset is booked, so depreciation is zero. Claims are incurred before payment;
unresolved payables defer new resource commitments under the qualified native
interface. Tax is the explicitly modeled25% nonrefundable positive pretax
high-water levy, assessed and paid at collection/close with separate fields.
Year cash therefore differs from earnings and from unrestricted cash.

Sensitivity adds USD50 orUSD200 per purchased buyer and tests7/30day campaign
cadence against14days. It does not resolve unknown legal/compliance, distribution,
security, customer installation, actual compute tariffs or commercial demand.
Any of those may make the real offer unattractive; no finite envelope establishes
the absence of unmodeled costs. The appropriate next decision depends on useful
accepted buyer outcomes and strong alternatives, not merely a favorable modeled
seller margin.
