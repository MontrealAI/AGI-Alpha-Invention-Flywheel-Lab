# Ω saving contracts: prospective confirmation protocol

Owner: isolated experiment `omega-saving-contracts-20261103-665b`; source branch
`experiment/omega-saving-contracts-20261103-665b`. This is a fresh independent
challenge, never a continuation of PR191/192/197 or an inherited balance.

## Question and prior evidence

Can finite, locally delivered and independently checked numerical-work packages
support a complete autonomous commercial history under explicit conditional
demand, competing offers, full modeled costs and native cash constraints? Does
structural method selection improve on a competent equal-content incumbent?

Development found correct accepted output and lower declared arithmetic-work
counts, but **the complete workflow was slower than NumPy direct plus the same
verification**. A single bounded instrument iteration (directed-rounding interval
acceptance) also lost across n32/64/128 and RHS/low-rank families on seeds11/12.
These failures are retained. The financial model's equal-work-unit monetization
is a conditional assumption, not a supported conversion from measured time to
buyer cash. It does not establish a currently profitable real offer.

## Frozen comparisons

Before examining any confirmation outcome, freeze the complete runtime closure,
its per-file hashes, exact Git revision and each machine-readable registration.
The generated `SOURCE.json` and `REGISTRATION.json` are authoritative. Execute
only from their source directory. No source, world, price, seed, information set,
policy or cost change is allowed within a confirmation run. A failure remains
an outcome and is repaired only in a separately identified future experiment.

* Main: five unweighted worlds (`positive`, `weak`, `parity`, `adverse`,
  `fixed_hardware`), six policies (`adaptive`, `ordinary`, `fixed299`,
  `fixed_split`, `capability_only`, `no_entry`), seeds701/702: **60 accounts**.
* Primary challenge: `positive-adaptive-701`, exactly USD100,000 opening capital.
  Every control also starts exactly USD100,000, independently; never pool them.
* Sensitivity: positive/adverse worlds, adaptive policy, seeds701/702, separately
  named `cadence7`, `cadence30`, `cost50`, `cost200`: **16 additional accounts**.
  These change respectively decision cadence from14 to7/30days, or add USD50/
  USD200 per purchased buyer. They are robustness comparisons, not pooled wealth.
* Technical confirmation: seeds701/702/703, eight workload families and six
  policies, plus old/new method content crossed with discovery policies on fresh
  successor requests: **180 registered rows**. `experiment.py` records exact rows.
* The interval instrument is an optional qualified development artifact. The
  native service retains the conservative exact-Fraction acceptance path.

The fixed-split comparator uses a declared15% current-cash campaign allocation
at the final full supplier quote plus operating envelope for expansions beyond
the separately costed20-contact information pilot;
this is not a tuned global optimum. Adaptive and ordinary use the same competent
historical rule and method menu. Their expected tie tests unsupported claims of
special methodology. Fixed299 tests the user price hypothesis; the adaptive menu
also tests20% and35% of declared modeled savings. These are fixed quoted prices,
**not collections contingent on subsequently observed real utility bills**.

## Information, decisions and cash

Policies receive current native cash/protection, observed prior campaign outcomes,
current supplier quotes, current packaged capability and qualified requests.
They cannot receive world names, seeds, hidden values, future fates or evaluator
answers. Demand and future defaults/claims are generated outside policy code.
The scheduler selects the first currently cash-feasible buyers before consulting
their future settlement draws. Counts may be grouped only for identical terms,
costs, delivery and fate, with per-buyer rounding preserved.

Acquisition starts with20 contacts per exploratory campaign, not a market cap.
Cash-supported candidate sizes double without a count ceiling. Actual quoted
cost grows with saturation and cumulative reach; quotes must fit present cash.
For informed campaigns, the final quantity and quote determine expected net value
from historical contribution. Reduce or stop negative quoted opportunities.
Rights require payment, expire after21days, activate after24hours, overlap across
channels and are consumed once. More production capacity creates no buyers.

An actual development account exposed a native precondition: new earned-funded
resource resolution requires previously incurred invoices to be settled. The
policy now observes current native payables, defers new commitments when any
remain, and retries a deferred decision after the scheduled claim payment clears
them. The failed frozen account is preserved; this does not erase its reserved
intent or weaken native protection. No future receipt is brought forward.

Construction and expansion are selected actions. No-entry incurs no package or
campaign cost. Capability-only purchases a package but does not buy acquisition.
The joint policies stage base and extended known methods. No claim of global or
game-theoretic optimality is made; unknown better feasible policies remain.

## Operating contract and costs

Operate `[0,8760)` hours, then settle through hour9985: the maximum504hour
collection delay,720hour claim window and one inclusive-boundary hour beyond a
full year. Mark the year independently of settlement. All initial costs and
capital expenditure are expensed immediately; no invented depreciable asset.

Costs include paid reach, selected construction, per-campaign internal tools/
infrastructure/admin, production, acceptance, onboarding, local self-support,
storage, maintenance, one-time qualification, retries/correction, payment fees,
defaults/refunds, and postcollection compensation. The native contingent USD0.20
exposure is a protection requirement, not an automatic release expense.

Tax is an explicitly hypothetical nonrefundable25% levy on increases in positive
cumulative pretax-profit high-water mark. Assess expense and pay at each collection
and close; distinguish expense, cash payment and unpaid reserve. This is not a
jurisdictional tax model. Keep the1% residual claim protection after maturity;
releasing other eligible protection never creates revenue.

Late-claim cohorts collect for accepted delivery, then incur a10%-of-price
disputed compatibility compensation claim seven days later and pay two days
after that. The loss is an expense; it is not silently netted from revenue.
Refund/default cohorts do not produce seller collections. Repeat purchase of
the identical finite package is assumed zero; successor technical tasks are
fresh requests, not fictional repeat sales. No personal support, hidden renewal
or ongoing-update subscription is promised.

All commercial numbers are authored assumptions. Buyer budgets are independently
specified, never derived mechanically from price. The fixed-hardware world
sets avoidable compute cash to zero. Internal founder service minutes and sales
staff are zero because neither is executed; real customer preparation,
installation and founder administrative effort remain unmeasured, not proven
unnecessary. Buyer effort is a separate explicit utility cost.

## Acceptance, falsifiers and stopping

Acceptance requires independently checked numerical output and the declared
work-count promise. Contract/current input/output/source must bind exactly. A
producer's completion claim is insufficient. Optional interval validation is
tested against exact rational bounds and declines or falls back on unsupported
platforms. It has no privileged information or weaker buyer tolerance.

Commerce succeeds conditionally only if accepted deliveries collect and native
365-day complete-cost profit is positive. Funded expansion additionally needs
larger-than-initial campaigns and native EARNED-funded acquisition. Buyer economic
advantage requires a favorable full-workflow comparison and genuinely avoidable
cost; current development already fails the measured-speed part. Invention
advantage requires superiority over equal-content ordinary methods on fresh
tasks; ties reject that extra claim. USD3B requires one account's native trailing
year profit at that threshold, audited acceptance and executable scale. Do not
multiply a small margin by an assumed market or aggregate independent accounts.

Finish all76 commercial cases and180 technical rows, preserving exceptions,
losses, refusals and incomplete cases. Resume the same authenticated account;
never restart from a different balance or relabel a fresh account as recovery.
If a platform limit or genuine defect blocks execution, preserve exact source,
checkpoint and failure receipt and report qualification incomplete. Do not
continue searching for favorable seeds or tune against held-out results.

Decision after results: positive conditional finance with no measured buyer
advantage supports retaining the accounting mechanism and using the strong
incumbent, not launching a savings promise. A credible buyer advantage would
justify the next separately registered representative-workload test. No real
commerce, outreach, purchase, deployment, merge or release is part of this run.
