## Source and claim boundary

This review preceded principal construction. The base is
`54c50a3f9a96e140f42e7cb50c810607a75a2231`. Every PDF in the current website

Only the execution assignment was supplied as an attachment in this session.
No private research manuscript or customer document was provided or inferred to
have been reviewed. This record contains public-source analysis and original
implementation decisions; it reproduces no private document.

The first construction is a self-hosted, typed computational-improvement
workflow for repeated numerical linear-response problems. `kernel.construct`
emits an applicable executable route; `kernel.execute` runs it;
`verify.verify` checks the requested output independently. `world.profile_terms`,
`world.quote_price` and `world.ContactRights._exposure` implement hypothetical
voluntary purchase against self-service, a rival and non-purchase. Direct
solves, factorization reuse and low-rank updates are established methods. Their
correct use is useful production; it is not a new scientific method or a claim
of autonomous discovery.

**Implementation status after construction:** the original research decisions
below now have concrete bindings in the implementation table that follows.
Financial and commercial confirmation still require the exact frozen source,
registration and result receipts identified by the experiment report. Passing
source traceability or a unit fixture does not establish every paper's theorem,
optimal decision value or market demand. No mathematical guarantee is imported
merely by naming a paper.

## Two corrections that affect the actual implementation

### Sequential delivery is eligible for funding

*Invent the Economy* Proposition 4.1 assumes all development and all delivery
payments occur before any buyer collection. Its whole-cohort prefunding bound
does **not** apply to a severable sequence that collects after each accepted
delivery. The native adapter must permit a cash-feasible next delivery, then
reobserve actual settled cash. It must also reject a sequence that relies on an
uncollected invoice. Equivalent batched and individual execution must preserve
both final accounts and the funding constraints within the batch.

### Research is a choice with a cost, not an entry fee

The flagship Algorithm 1, OUT-STRATEGISE's construction/information comparison,
SUPER ALPHA's price window and FLYWHEEL 1.1 §10.4 all preserve an incumbent or
stop option. This experiment must not deduct a compulsory development bill
merely because the account has US$100,000. Existing delivery, a small paid test,
staged construction, alternative terms, expansion and abstention compete before
their respective costs occur. A retrospective profitable result does not prove
that the policy was optimal under the information then available.

## Deliberate nonapplications

The following omissions are substantive applicability decisions, not missing
citations to be filled with nominal calls:

- **OUT-LEARN:** the current task has exact supplied computational inputs, not a
  qualified sensor with sign reversal, polynomial drift and stable reference
  gain. Profiling is not the paper's minimax calibration construction.
- **OUT-THINK:** an exact matrix factorization is a useful computational asset,
  but this experiment has not constructed a minimum canonical encoder preserving
  all future operation-update semantics. No model-memory or alphabet-minimum
  claim follows.
- **OUT-DESIGN:** no resource-topology migration with continuous service is
  presently required. Increasing campaign size does not implement its qualified
  release and vacancy-routing theorem.
- **OUT-EXECUTE:** current-input reuse is sufficient initially. A low-rank
  linear-algebra update does not implement the paper's complete discrete boundary
  value-profile theorem. A genuinely needed profile implementation would require
  all boundary rows, factor ownership and current attaining witnesses.
- **OUT-REFLECT:** residual/reference checks are implemented as ordinary
  independent checks. They do not instantiate moment-code correction or certify
  an assumed number of independent faulty references. Shared wrong premises
  remain a possible failure mode.
- **The World as an Output:** no physical nuisance-elimination interface is
  installed. Numerical conditioning and method applicability must be tested;
  the manuscript's protected-operation guarantees are not imported.
- **Foundry v2:** its metrology failure motivates independent outcome checks,
  but no thermal foundry, sensor or industrial calibration is constructed here.
- **FLYWHEEL 1.0** is a preserved historical edition. **The 2026-11-02 report**
  is source-bound synthesis of earlier experiments. Neither contributes new
  confirmation outcomes or funds to this account.

## Required result and ablation record

The final report must bind each executed row to an existing code symbol, test,
source revision and receipt. At minimum, report:

1. Direct solve, cached factorization and applicable low-rank construction with
   identical useful-output acceptance; include rejection and ill-conditioning.
2. Proposed routing against competent ordinary routing with equal data and
   assets; retain ties.
3. No retained factors versus ordinary equal-content reuse on fresh successor
   workloads; distinguish equipment savings from improved discovery.
4. The implemented flat/package and declared-savings-share contracts with
   voluntary outside alternatives; no-entry remains a valid action. A distinct
   fixed-project arm and settlement against observed real cost savings remain
   unimplemented proposals unless later receipts demonstrate them.
5. Adaptive acquisition/allocation against tuned conventional fixed policies,
   commerce-only and capability-only controls where implemented.
6. Funding-prefix, positive-profit/low-cash, tax/reserve/payment, batch equivalence
   and interrupted-recovery regressions on the native account.

Scientific acceptance, commerce, funded expansion, buyer capability, invention
advantage and the US$3 billion trailing-year objective receive separate verdicts.
The hypotheses are finite experiments, not a permanent product catalogue or a
ceiling on ambition. Strong known methods should be retained when they win.
