The engineering fixture retains both an unresolved-help escalation (5 modeled
minutes) and a dispute escalation (10 minutes). These are explicit modeled human
exceptions, not actual observed Vincent interventions. Report counts and minutes
separately from CPU. Hourly founder-value sensitivity is $0/$200/$2,000, not a
market wage or a claim that founder time is free. Actual delivery performance and
customer usability have not been measured.

## Complete costs and financial meaning

The fixture has $120 setup/documentation/security readiness, and each funded
attempt incurs $5 acquisition, $9 processor envelope and $1 hosting/onboarding.
Refund administration costs $2; a released-order dispute adds $3 administration
plus an authored $0.20 dispute-processing cost (the native $0.20 reserve is not itself a paid fee). These deliberately exposed envelopes
are not verified platform prices or actual bills. Retained buyer custody, refund
exposure and payables are native obligations, not usable earnings. The fixture
report must derive recognized revenue, collections, costs, expense payments,
reserves, cash and available cash from the native account; it must not aggregate
its $1,000 seed or losses with the mission account.

Actual development/inference/host bills and applicable taxes remain unknown.
Security and maintenance readiness are inside the declared setup envelope only
for this finite engineering fixture, not unlimited lifetime coverage. No capex,
depreciation or financing is modeled in the fixture; real equipment costs,
capitalization rules and ongoing obligations require a separate documented model.
Do not translate modeled pre-tax result into complete actual profit.

## Buyer compute and permissions

The implemented buyer path is local deterministic code; it is not a local language
model. Local help reads the actual product documentation and admits unknowns.
External model proposals can be imported as files after the buyer independently
chooses/authorizes their provider; this code makes no provider call and stores no
key. A general model router remains unimplemented. Future routing must disclose
provider, data sent, estimated charge/limit, local hardware/electricity, latency,
capability/context and reliability before an authorized action. A choice of local
processing neither supplies a capable model nor makes computation free.

## Scale constraints, not forecasts

Even ignoring every cost and refund, $3 billion would require at least
10,033,445 downloads at $299, or 30,303,031 attendee purchases at $99 (60,607 full
500-seat sessions), or 150,000 private sessions at $20,000. The private sessions
alone need 75,000 presentation hours, exceeding 8,760 total hours in a year.
The proposed 60-minute group sessions similarly exceed a one-person year before
preparation/admin. These are gross-revenue necessary conditions, not sufficient
profit/demand evidence. Mixed offers still need a feasible shared founder-time
budget, not independent capacity sums. The download path needs measured
acquisition, conversion, refunds, imitation response, adoption value and affordable
cash timing. No such distribution advantage has been established.

Buyer gross value, net value after adoption/compute/fees, seller revenue,
contribution, full profit and unrestricted cash remain distinct. No observed
buyer-value figure exists here. No-buy and competent incumbent routes remain
available. A successful conditional receipt establishes neither willingness to
pay nor an attractive offer.
