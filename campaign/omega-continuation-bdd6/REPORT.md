## Buyer outcome and complete alternatives

A buyer wants to select an admissible productive action when existing records
alias process states requiring different actions. The new local instrument
constructs a minimum-cost fixed panel from a declared measurement menu, obtains
readings through an authorized callback, and independently checks the resulting
action. Initial bulk reading 10 cannot distinguish abstract states (2,8)/(8,2).
The declared component assay with error ±1 can distinguish them. This is a finite
mathematical dependency test with simulated instruments, not a water/chemical
safety certificate or a newly invented physical sensor.

[Prior art and business comparison](PRIOR_ART.md) examines four materially
different arrangements: measurement-to-decision editions; portable laboratory
procedures; voluntary industrial cooperation; and business-data assurance.
Current specialist products and existing organizations are credited. Physical
calibration, cross-equipment validation, counterparties and observed value remain
missing where required. No candidate has earned flagship admission. The
observation step was chosen to test a concrete missing-input dependency, not to
rename the rejected water optimizer.

The candidate uses interval partitions. The independent conventional control uses
incompatible-hypothesis subsets and exact minimum-cost hitting sets, with the
same observations, error model, cost menu, permissions and reuse. A strong
conventional workflow can use this ordinary decision-theoretic method; a claim
that the full existing market cannot do this is falsified for the tested scope.
The test compares fixed panels, not globally optimal adaptive science. It does
not measure human wages, model inference, development labor or end-to-end field
productivity.
