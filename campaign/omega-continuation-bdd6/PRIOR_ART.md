## Current primary evidence

- [Pyomo.DoE overview](https://pyomo.readthedocs.io/en/6.10.1/explanation/analysis/doe/overview.html)
  combines experiment selection, parameter estimation and uncertainty analysis.
  Its parameter-precision focus is not a license to deny a conventional engineer
  a decision-focused objective.
- [Wang et al. (2024), *Measure this, not that*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compchemeng.2024.108786)
  addresses costed measurement selection with mixed-integer optimization. Our
  finite costed panel construction is not claimed as a newly invented method.
- [Value-of-information sensor configuration](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0888327019305989)
  jointly considers information benefit and sensor cost. The observation-based
  decision principle is established prior art, not an Ω-exclusive discovery.
- [Opentrons Protocol Designer](https://docs.opentrons.com/protocol-designer/about/)
  supports browser-based design and export of executable protocols for supported
  robots. [SiLA specifications](https://sila-standard.com/downloads/) provide
  existing laboratory interoperability; neither alone verifies an arbitrary
  transferred physical procedure.
- [BoTorch cost-aware optimization](https://botorch.org/docs/tutorials/cost_aware_bayesian_optimization)
  and [constrained multiobjective optimization](https://botorch.org/docs/v0.14.0/tutorials/constrained_multi_objective_bo)
  are serious existing closed-loop experimental methods. Merely connecting an
  optimizer to an agent is not a newly attainable outcome.
- [Synergie Québec](https://synergiequebec.ca/a-propos/) already facilitates
  resource exchanges and reusable expertise. [RECYC-QUÉBEC's project description](https://www.recyc-quebec.gouv.qc.ca/communiques-de-presse/recyc-quebec-sengage-a-soutenir-synergie-quebec-pour-renforcer-le-reseau-des-symbioses-industrielles/)
  documents an established network and platform-modernization effort. These
  sources defeat an empty-competition assumption; they do not prove exact
  equivalence to every proposed arrangement.

## Selected dependency and exact failure boundary

A buyer can use this finite Ω prototype to determine which authored reuse action
is admissible by constructing and executing an observation panel, overcoming
ambiguity between declared process states consistent with the same aggregate
record. The strongest alternative is a competent conventional decision-focused
experiment-design workflow with the same observations, costs and reuse. The
proposed difference is accepted decisions per complete resource cost; a tie
defeats an extraordinary-advantage claim.

This test changes the **observations**, not the previously tied water optimizer.
It is justified because an optimal plan cannot identify which unobserved world
is real. `omega/observation.py` constructs a panel from a **supplied finite recipe
menu**, invokes a caller-supplied observation boundary, and produces an actual
conditional decision or refusal. It does not invent a sensor or obtain a
physical sample. `OBSERVATION_CASES.json` freezes fourteen authored cases and
controls; the candidate never receives their evaluator-only truth/readings.

The independent conventional selector receives the same finite hypotheses,
sensor menu, observation bounds, budget and acceptance. Comparison is on the
complete finite outcome, not against an intentionally weak midpoint estimate or
information-volume proxy. Computation runs at machine speed. Human productivity,
engineering effort and frontier-model quality are not measured here.

Recipe inheritance saves only its explicitly declared reconstruction cost.
Ordinary reuse receives identical content. No predecessor rebuilds at the same
declared price; cash-only receives equivalent extra spending capacity without
free construction; no-sensing removes the additional observation. Fresh action
semantics prevent reuse of a predecessor decision as successor acceptance.
Predecessor acquisition costs remain in lifecycle totals. These controls can
demonstrate ordinary reuse and conditional observation value, not an improved
discovery method or permission to learn from customer information.

Finite hypothesis closure, sensor calibration, error bounds, real availability,
hardware/electricity/latency, actual engineering costs and buyer demand remain
unknown. Bounded integer errors conservatively allow correlation. Out-of-model
readings can expose a wrong class, but an omitted world can also imitate an
in-class reading: no universal safety or completeness is claimed. With overlapping
error intervals or unaffordable/unauthorized measurement, the honest result is
unresolved. Failure within this finite menu is not physical impossibility.
