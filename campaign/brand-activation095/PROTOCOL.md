# Existing-brand activation 095: registered conditional sensitivity

## Question and isolation

Can activating a finite inherited awareness asset cover the costs of this particular generic analytical-service arrangement, without assuming a price premium? This is not a forecast of MONTREAL.AI or QUEBEC.AI, an estimate of their finances, or a native commercial execution. It complements the frozen 094 cold-start comparison rather than revising it. It is supporting sensitivity analysis, not a Blue Sky flagship or claimed invention.

The two brands share one synthetic deduplicated pool of organizations. Founder followers, company followers and claimed community membership are not added. Historical convening supports the existence of awareness, not active relevant buyers, sales, endorsements, exclusive rights or conversion. Founder technical judgment and new-offer construction may have additional value; this distribution-isolation experiment does not evaluate that value or presume it to be zero.

The exact 094 price game and cost/acceptance assumptions remain unchanged (model SHA256 a458dffebef7467313db54ad984d5b35e1cbe2015f0a0d23731d8ca4eb9e52db, PR221 source d99e695a181e576afd45bfd28f9ca55a9cb4477b). Strategies observe aggregate segment counts, not private buyer types or future draws. Buyers retain budget-feasible human, human-plus-AI, autonomous rival, self-service and no-purchase choices. Strong alternatives receive the same brand-access permissions; this tests inherited distribution, not AI-created brand value.

## Frozen finite matrix

Twelve operating months followed by six settlement-only months. Each synthetic organization has one persistent identifier, private value/budget type and at most one purchase opportunity. Uncontacted rights may carry forward within the year but expire at month12. No automatic referrals, audience renewal, repeat purchase or growth. Stated finite pools are experimental conditions, not business-scale ceilings.

Eight cells cross 1,000 or10,000 qualified unique organizations, all-small or90%small/9%medium/1%large organization mix, and lean/demanding activation envelopes. The10,000 qualified-organization case is explicitly optimistic and is not inferred from follower counts. Each is run with $100,000 hypothetical initial equity. The four10,000-organization central cells repeat with $1million equity. The mixed10,000 lean cell additionally faces the unchanged aggressive rival at both capital levels. Three policies and twelve paired seeds95100–95111 yield504 complete rows. Engineering uses95000–95001, plus a zero-audience case. No favorable seed selection or account pooling.

No-activation incurs no incremental costs or earnings. Full activation releases rights gradually over twelve months. Staged activation releases10% over the first three months, then makes one decision at month7: expand only if realized pilot collections exceed incurred production, capacity, activation cash, six platform payments, founder opportunity costs and outstanding production/tax commitments. Setup is sunk at that decision but remains charged in reported profit. This realized-pilot rule is conservative and is not a universal requirement to repay seed capital. Late uncollected invoices do not finance expansion. The remaining90% rights release over months7–12. The policy is a registered heuristic, not a globally optimal dynamic policy.

Each month the controller compares no production with10%,25%,50%,100% of currently available uncontacted IDs, under cash-before-commitment and full worst-case production reserves. It chooses maximum expected contribution after the unchanged capacity fee with pessimistic finite-grid rival response. The finite contact menu is a restriction, not an optimality claim over arbitrary customer selection or a universal limit. It cannot inspect future individual types/outcomes when choosing prices or quantities.

## Costs, funding and accounting

Lean activation assumes $1,000 cash plus8 founder hours; demanding assumes $10,000 plus40 hours. Founder time is valued at a hypothetical $150/hour, separately reconciled as noncash opportunity cost, not deducted twice or treated as a tax deduction. These are founder-assisted hybrid cases, not strictly autonomous activation. Staging initially charges10% and charges90% only when expanding. Historical brand-building is sunk and not spendable cash. No brand valuation is credited as income.

The primary isolation retains the full $50,000 service development expense and $2,000 monthly platform envelope. No reusable implementation saving is inferred from owning a brand. Production, compute/tool/support, failures/retries, acceptance, refunds/defaults, tax, capacity and payment delays retain094 assumptions. Actual resource bills, enterprise demand, rights, conversion and founder market-rate time are not measured. Costs are declared simulation envelopes, not verified complete industrial lifecycle economics.

Initial equity is hypothetical received seed capital, not revenue or obtained finance. No interest/debt/fees are introduced. Report non-entry alongside each capital choice and opportunity cost of unused capital qualitatively; no claimed investor-optimal financing result. No original accounts or signing authority are used.

Report twelve-month recognized accrual profit separately from total18-month settled episode profit, cash, reserves, receivables and tax liabilities. Founder opportunity costs give a separate economic-profit measure. Settlement drain is not silently added to the annual reporting window. Report contacts, orders, accepted orders, expansion decisions and all failed cases. No $3B annual milestone can be claimed from total episode profit.

## Validation and stopping

Freeze model and protocol publicly before confirmation. All504 rows must complete or have explicit failure status. Check unique IDs, single purchases, individual budgets, funded production, tax/receivable reconciliation, no future-funding, final settlement and noncash-cost reconciliation. Mutation tests must reject changed cash, duplicate contacts, budget violations and removed founder costs. Preserve useful correction lessons without rerunning confirmation to choose favorable assumptions. A source repair requires a new explicit edition, never retrospective relabeling.

Decision: identify profitable declared cells, failure regions and break-even requirements. A positive result is a conditional generic-service activation result, not evidence of real clients, MONTREAL.AI revenue, billions, native accepted artifacts, or invention advantage. A negative result rejects this arrangement under these assumptions, not the brands' wider commercial potential.
