## Decision and alternatives, frozen before implementation

Consequential possibility: a passive two-contact-family mechanical interface that safely supplies static hold and dynamic slide without an externally powered controller. Compare four complete arrangements: **A** reproduce/repeat only published pure-family microcontact rows; **B** pair new mixed-family static and dynamic conditions with same-apparatus pure controls and matched loads, then independent withheld load/velocity/wear qualification; **C** ordinary passive clutch/force limiter or adequately engineered active controller with their complete safety and operation costs; **D** no deployment. This test can rank *information necessity*, not economic viability or the true physical effectiveness of A/B/C. No actual costs, buyer participation, or hardware funding has been established, so D remains the only authorized real-world decision.

**Scientific obstruction:** single-material trials contain no observation of cross-contact transfer. The published article by Fu et al. (Nature Physics, 30 October 2026, DOI 10.1038/s41567-026-03503-0) optimizes metainterface friction laws; Zeka et al., arXiv:2604.08195, independently examine finite-size and microcontact interactions. Neither supplies measurements of this proposed mixed two-family static/dynamic surface. No author result is contradicted by an illustrative interaction coefficient.

## Frozen finite test model

Use a *hypothetical* phase-specific linear-plus-interaction law at one operating condition:
`F_s = k_As A + k_Bs B + gamma_s h(A,B)`;
`F_d = k_Ad A + k_Bd B + gamma_d h(A,B)`,
where `h(A,B)=AB/(A+B)` when `A,B>0`, otherwise zero. All coefficients and areas nonnegative. Do not mistake this grammar for an experimentally validated friction law, mixed-material calibration, or a physical confidence envelope.

Parameter ordering `[k_As,k_Bs,gamma_s,k_Ad,k_Bd,gamma_d]`. Construct exact rational design rows from *all* available static/dynamic force rows of selected families Batch1_R496 and Batch2_R1543. Pure rows have either A>0,B=0 or A=0,B>0 and h=0. Compute exact row-space rank over rationals (no floating-point threshold). With both source families and phases, expected pure rank **4/6**; adding one mixed-static at A=B=1 raises rank to **5/6**; one mixed-dynamic alone to **5/6**; both raises rank to **6/6**. Zero cross-contact feature gives no added rank. This is *structural rank only*: the pure source contains inconsistent force-error rectangles for a constant coefficient in three of six blocks (PR #390), so rank is not fitting validity.

Construct exactly two nonnegative **synthetic** worlds with `[k_As,k_Bs,k_Ad,k_Bd]=(2,4,1,2)` and A=B=1, and `gamma_d=0`. World0 `gamma_s=0`; World1 `gamma_s=1`. Each produces identical pure-family observations for every pure area, yet their mixed static/dynamic ratios are 2 and 13/6 respectively, yielding relative improvement 0 versus 1/12. The source registration's 0.05 target falls strictly between. These illustrative coefficients are NOT source-fitted estimates. The result is a counterexample to inferring mixed success from any amount of pure-only data **within the declared interaction model class**, not proof of interaction, physical benefit or inability to learn under a narrower physically justified model.
