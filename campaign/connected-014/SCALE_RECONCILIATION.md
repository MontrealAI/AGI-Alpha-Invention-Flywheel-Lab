# Scale reconciliation for connected enterprise 014

The published billion-dollar timings belong to two different authored aggregate models. Neither model operates the native small enterprise, and neither can authorize native account spending. The connected edition preserves both results and first measures what its exact event path supports.

Different milestone times do not identify one channel's causal advantage over the other. Starting cash, channel duration, allocation and action sets, forecast ranking, working buffers, randomization, and horizon differ. The apparently cheaper distribution contract in PR #23 therefore does not imply its enterprise should cross earlier than PR #22. A valid common comparison would freeze those factors, use equal starting capital, and change only the specific option or controller under test. Existing tests within each model remain valid within their own registered designs.

The two models share a USD 400 synthetic work price and USD 40 attempt cost. Those figures are not the canonical small enterprise's accepted service-price distribution or measured frontier-provider bills. Their `small_cohort_anchor` is null. No native account's retained profit is transferred into either scale allocation. Authored technical-success draws are not actual artifact acceptance receipts.

## Calendar and revenue definitions

Both historical scale studies qualify their milestones with a sum of 12 monthly collection bins after at least 12 completed monthly steps. They do not calculate a day-accurate trailing-365-day measure. Annualized current-month collections are reported separately and never qualify the milestone. Connected enterprise 014 uses an integer day event clock; its scale bridge reports `(day - 365, day]` collections and labels any annualization separately. In a run shorter than 365 days, the rolling amount contains only that run's observed days and is explicitly an incomplete-year observation.

Native settlement permanently protects the configured principal/fee tail until the native contract releases it. Aggregate forecast reserves that expire after three months cannot be silently substituted for that obligation. A billion-dollar conditional forecast with an expiring 10% reserve therefore does not establish billion-dollar feasibility under native permanent-tail capital requirements.

## Exact-to-aggregate qualification and growth boundary

The connected scale bridge aggregates native financial event deltas by day and independently verifies them against exact prefix summaries at overlapping delivery counts. This qualifies exact-record aggregation for collection, expense, cash, profit, liabilities and protected balances. It does **not** qualify a forward market simulator, extrapolate buyer demand, or render projected service outputs.

For USD 1 million, USD 10 million, USD 100 million, USD 1 billion and USD 10 billion, the bridge retains either an actual first crossing with its complete financial state, or an explicit unreached record. Revenue attainment and positive-profit, obligation-covered attainment are separate records. The finite admitted artifact inventory, audience, channel expiry and working funds are retained; there is no exponential multiplier or forecast-to-native-cash transfer.

Until a larger model is validated against the exact path under matched acquisition, production, acceptance, collection, reserve and cash timing, the connection to the two historical billion-dollar projections is `UNBRIDGED_NATIVE_SCALE`. That limitation does not invalidate a profitable native earned-funded loop. Its next scale action must be selected from the exact path's observed constraint and costed opportunity, rather than the fastest historical milestone number.
