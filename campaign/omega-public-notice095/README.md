# PublicNotice095 — can a real public procurement notice become an honest predecision input?

**Executable research, not an offer, bid, customer acquisition or account operation.** [Preregistered protocol](PROTOCOL.md) committed at `92f87dd82678a7ee946ec874bcbd230f74bdb1f8` before source implementation. Baseline main `4abbb85893c9c073ca092db4ad858f7bb156dc35`.

The challenge is concrete: older native paid-reach experiments and uncalibrated Bayesian prequote grids show **no validated customer-acquisition signal**. Instead of assuming buyers, PublicNotice095 constructs a small source-bound market-need observation from genuine public procurement processes. Three fixed UK official source processes—software reconciliation (`ocds-h6vhtk-05575d`), Connect to Work (`ocds-h6vhtk-05167a`), peatland restoration (`ocds-h6vhtk-050420`)—were selected before fetching, with no post-result replacements. The official [record package API](https://www.find-tender.service.gov.uk/apidocumentation/1.0/GET-ocdsRecordPackages) is an open data source under [OGL v3](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/open-contracting).

Run locally with Python stdlib: `python -m unittest discover -s tests -p 'test_public_notice095.py' -v`. On a network-enabled public GitHub Actions runner, `python -m research.public_notice095 --out /absolute/new/PUBLIC_NOTICE095.json` requests exactly three records and writes compact public receipts; it exits nonzero on any failed registered source retrieval. The workflow preserves its artifact even on failure. Do not rerun until favorable: diagnose before a prospective source repair. The program does not retain raw personal contacts, bids, supplier addresses, prices or source archives, only official identifiers, organization/title, category, SHA256, sizes, capture time, stages and explicit unknowns.

**Essential chronological rule:** A record's historical event date cannot prove its contents were accessible before a bidder's decision. The actual runtime capture must precede the decision, and award/post-award information is excluded from predecision features. This is only a host-clock engineering receipt, **not an independently trusted timestamp**. A now-retrieved 2025 award cannot be used as a 2025 predictive signal. Earlier tenders prove public organizations advertised work; neither purchase of our service nor our eligibility, bid success or accepted/completed paid delivery follows.

**Predeclared alternatives:** human conventional procurement services; established AI-assisted complete bid-provider services; a possible rights-cleared public-source evidence/dossier product; and no bid/nonentry. Complete comparative prices, wages, model costs, outside options, market reach, legal readiness and buyer willingness are unknown. We have not demonstrated that the new dossier is cheaper or better, or made any real contract. Any succeeding test is engineering only, not a qualified commercial flywheel.

**Decision:** `RESEARCH_ONLY_NO_BID`.
