# Economic admission and value-capture review

Public core-facility schedules establish real related services and posted price anchors. They do not establish willingness to pay for this candidate, unavoidable specialist hours, repeatable demand, or a loss-making cohort. Free software plus competent ordinary reuse must remain in the strongest completed-route baseline. Select a candidate first, freeze its useful obligation, then select comparables of matching scope; do not choose a high price and design the offering around it.

A defensible first modeled arrangement is a fixed fee per accepted, source-bound enabling-instrument delivery, with requirements frozen before purchase, a precise acceptance verifier, a refund/remedy obligation and separately priced support. The modeled buyer may choose non-entry, free self-service, a competent paid substitute, or the candidate. Trust, switching and integration costs enter the buyer's choice rather than disappearing from provider profit. No customer, commitment or enforceable real agreement has been observed.

For N prospectively admitted inputs, preserve every reject, failed experiment, unsold output and rework. Let q be participation, a the accepted fraction conditional on purchase, p the independently supported service price, r the refund/collection-loss fraction, C_all the cost of all admitted inputs (including failed/unsold), F the allocated acquisition/discovery cost and K all unpriced lifecycle costs. Conditional operating profit is N*q*a*p*(1-r) - C_all - F - K. This is a frontier, not a calibration of q or p. Minimum participation is (C_all+F+K)/(N*a*p*(1-r)) when the denominator is positive; values above one are infeasible. Include q=0. If K lacks a justified finite upper bound, a robust positive complete-cost claim is UNKNOWN.

Baseline and intervention use the same N, obligation, information and timing. The baseline receives ordinary retrieval, automation and the candidate's equally available reusable content when that is the causal question. Do not charge an invented consulting team to the baseline while pricing autonomous construction at CPU cost alone. Record deployment economics separately from total enterprise recovery; show acquisition allocations at explicit horizons rather than using hoped-for volume as current cash.

Investment compares discounted incremental net cash with non-entry and the strongest outside option. A positive accounting margin does not imply sufficient available cash. Escrow/deposits remain restricted, payables and remedies remain liabilities, and capital recovery is separate from gross collections. Do not double-charge a provision and its later payment. Reserve release creates availability, not new revenue.

The source-pinned Alpha ProtectedExchange is the required ledger. Its cost() recognizes expense even if liquidity permits only partial payment. snapshot() separately reports profit, cash, payables, custody restrictions, tail/working reserves and unrestrictedEarned. Only unrestrictedEarned may reserve a successor investment, with actual construction and matched fresh benefit required afterward. Opening capital, buyer deposits and refunds cannot earn-fund the successor. The ledger covers bounded provider/principal-plus-fee exposure; arbitrary external liabilities remain outside its qualified scope.

Current decision: no commercial cohort admitted; price and participation remain conditional; qualified simulated commercial receipts and earned funds are zero. This is a current gate, not a reason to stop constructing within the resource allocation.
