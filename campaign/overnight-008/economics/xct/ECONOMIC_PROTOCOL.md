# Sparse-XCT support-packet economic protocol

The modeled buyer is a CT research group or core facility needing a defensible interval for a prospectively fixed grayscale ROI feature from already acquired partial data. The first delivery is a source-matched admissible-support packet, not a material-density estimate, defect detector, scanner certification or assurance of physical truth. CC-BY walnut measurements can support a reproducible research calculation, with attribution; they do not establish an industrial production cohort or customer's willingness to pay.

The current repair uses all120 measured views and a dense reference reconstruction to calibrate its error envelope, then uses full-data LP duals to select a smaller packet. It is retrospective constraint compression. Charge the acquisition/calibration already consumed; do not count reduced packet size as an avoided scan. The new0.005 width is a prospectively authored development target, not an independent customer tolerance.

Freeze the complete technical cohort, feature, forward operator, box bounds, data/model error allowance, numerical enclosure rule, maximum useful interval width and rejection rule before final evaluation. Every selected source/task/failed packet belongs in cost accounting. If the interval does not meet the agreed useful-width/validity obligation, the proposed pay-on-acceptance service returns no earned fee. A correct broad refusal/support interval is useful evidence but cannot be counted as newly possible narrow measurement.

The strongest mathematical alternative receives direct HiGHS min/max, its available primal/dual values, ordinary retrieval/reuse and the same conservative checker. Imaging alternatives receive natural FBP/SIRT/CGLS/TV settings. Free Bayesian CT uncertainty tools and full commercial CT workflows remain alternatives, with their different prior and calibration contracts explicit. Do not price the baseline as an imaginary engineering staff when the solver and ordinary automation suffice.

Duke's current posted industrial prices are USD163.90/hour scanner and USD66.33/hour reconstruction workstation. They anchor related service-input sensitivities, not this service's contract price. A reduction in raw views or CPU time is not automatically billed savings: Cambridge specifies two-hour minimum bookings and Oregon State specifies a four-hour scanner block plus personnel minima. Acquisition savings require measured changes in billed obligations under the same quality requirements. No avoided scan/reconstruction hours are currently established. Do not import the high capital values of CT procurement into our recurring service revenue.

A plausible modeled value-capture arrangement is a fixed fee for an independently accepted source-bound packet with an immutable feature/error contract, transparent abstention and a refund for invalid delivery. The open implementation can still support paid execution/integration or checking services, but the buyer can run it or ordinary methods itself. No exclusive royalty, paid proprietary license or real contract is assumed. Support, procurement, attribution, integration, trust assessment and independent recalibration costs remain explicit.

For N fixed technical opportunities and modeled participation q, accepted fraction a, fee p and post-acceptance refund/loss r, net fee collections are N*q*a*p*(1-r). Let C include every admitted input's measured known monetary cost, F reusable acquisition recovery and K unpriced discovery/inference/verification/integration/support/terminal costs. Conditional complete profit is N*q*a*p*(1-r)-C-F-K. The unknown-cost ceiling is N*q*a*p*(1-r)-C-F; positive profit requires K below it. Demand evidence permits q=0, and K lacks a finite bound, so robust positive complete profit remains UNKNOWN regardless of optimistic frontier points. No baseline negative-to-positive crossing follows.

Participation is rational only if the best expected completed-outcome value after the buyer's trust discount exceeds p plus switching, validation, calibration and procurement costs, relative to free self-service, another qualified service, acquiring more data, postponement and non-entry. Include zero trust/participation and correlated failure: common geometry, beam-hardening/model mismatch or shared calibration invalidates many packets together. Packet hashes do not establish calibration or independence. There is no measured probability for those events; sensitivity assumptions are not empirical forecasts.

Use the existing source-pinned ProtectedExchange for any eventual transaction scenario. Bind the actual artifact, independent receipt, obligations and verifier identities to immutable terms. Exercise payment-gated release, rejection/refund, interrupted preparation/settlement, duplicate handling and correlated failure. Show bookkeeping and operational stress separately from economic calibration. Deposits are restricted cash, reserves are unavailable principal and releases are not new revenue. Opening funds cannot be relabeled earnings. Only post-obligation unrestricted simulated earned funds may reserve a successor; unknown complete costs or missing commercial admission keep the qualified commercial-funding gate closed.

Investment NPV uses incremental future net cash and the same time convention for both routes. Compare with non-entry and ordinary reinvestment; do not count conditional gross fees as achieved investment attractiveness. Even a profitable marginal deployment must recover acquisition and earlier failed search on a declared horizon, with that horizon separately sensitive to actual demand. No physical commercial operation, buyer commitment, received payment, real procurement or paid external access is authorized or claimed.

Next economic action after a useful technical result: bind the frozen cohort and independently accepted packet; report the conditional fee/participation/K frontier with measured cost receipts, and execute the existing ledger under explicitly labeled scenarios. A source-matched mathematical packet without useful accepted width or defensible complete costs remains an incomplete link.
