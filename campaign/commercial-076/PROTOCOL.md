## Source-backed buyer problem (not a sale)

The Government of Canada's Shared Services Canada (SSC) challenge "Artificial Intelligence (AI) decision-making process visibility, traceability and compliance governance" was posted October 30, 2026, closes December 22, 2026 14:00 ET, and has a **Phase 2 maximum of CAD 2,000,000 per contract**, estimated 1–2 contracts (not commitments). Official source:
https://www.ised-isde.canada.ca/site/innovative-solutions-canada/en/artificial-intelligence-ai-decision-making-process-visibility-traceability-and-compliance-governance

Its mandatory outcomes include continuous impact/reassessment from changed requirements, standards-based interoperability, restricted administration and information, verifiable mapping/evidence with authorized reviewers, and data/model/output-risk reassessment. Applicants must document TRL 5–9 and Canadian for-profit incorporation and other eligibility criteria; these are NOT presumed met. A solicitation is an *identified buyer problem*, not our sale, acceptance, award, price, voluntary agreement, collected demand or measured profit.

## Four predeclared arrangements and strong outside options

A. Integrate an established enterprise governance provider (e.g. IBM watsonx.governance/Credo AI), with licensing, integration, human oversight, and full support cost **unknown**. Evidence of their abilities is from their own product descriptions, not our verification.
B. Construct an open-standards, source-verifiable governance change/reassessment instrument, with integration and assurance, still at **unknown** TRL and cost.
C. A qualified human-plus-conventional-software advisory and audit service, with staff capacity, complete delivery cost and procurement qualifications **unknown**.
D. No bid / no deploy. Competing providers, self-service, and no purchase remain genuine outside options.

No exclusive buyer, price advantage, partner relationship, delivery capacity, human-hour estimate or winning probability is assumed.

## Frozen implementation and adversarial acceptance

Construct a **pure, standard-library, source-bound** opportunity screener and the fixed SSC fact input. It must not call any external service or financial writer, create native accounts, or mutate source/records during evaluation. Register source links, date, opportunity kind and currency explicitly. Require documented evidence for every mandatory eligibility/technical/delivery condition, independent verifiability of complete cost and working capital, voluntary terms, and dated real capacity before an arrangement can advance to `READY_FOR_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW`. Missing, stale, internally self-asserted, or expressly false evidence must result in `HOLD_NO_BID`. Even a fully evidenced synthetic pass grants no submission, financing or actual commercial authority.

Run and retain all cases, no seed shopping: (1) actual SSC record with all unverified eligibility and costs -> hold for A/B/C; (2) large advertised budget alone cannot make an arrangement ready; (3) official RFI/nonpurchase cannot count as awarded contract; (4) a source stale beyond its declared as-of cutoff -> hold; (5) purported positive profit with missing complete-cost or reserve elements -> hold; (6) a small fully specified **synthetic** condition set may become *review-only*, never bid; (7) explicitly false legal/TRL criterion blocks even with positive modeled margin; (8) the no-deploy alternative remains admissible; (9) exact deterministic JSON output in repeated evaluation.

Record tested behavior, repository+source hashes, incurred engineering resource scope and unknown real bills. No claim of state of the art, advantage over incumbents, real profit, 3B attainment or TRL qualification. This is a funding-safe **research admission gate**, NOT a Blue Sky flagship or native commercial policy integrated into the released enterprise.

## Decision boundary

If the SSC route lacks eligibility, TRL5 proof, verified compliance functionality, market-winning differentiated performance, rights/permissions, current capital or all complete cost receipts, retain **HOLD / NO BID / NO DEPLOY**. Next candidate construction can be considered only with independent full-stack comparisons and canonical source-bound financial operation. Historical transition failures remain unchanged.
