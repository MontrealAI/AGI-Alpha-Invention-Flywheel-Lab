## Decision and achievement

**Business target NOT ESTABLISHED.** A source-bound native 14-day enterprise was executed and audited, opening exactly two distinct simulated accounts with US$100,000 each and no top-up. A separate fixed 60-month conditional projection, using the repository's **existing** `enterprise.scale.simulate_scale`, tested five business hypotheses with four preregistered seeds each. The partner-distributed, high-value recurring-workflow hypothesis crosses **US$1,000,000,000 of projected trailing-365-day modeled profit AND cash contribution in 4/4 scenarios**, first in months **52, 53, 52, 53**. The matched no-referrals cases do not cross, nor does the direct-enterprise incumbent; channel disruption fails on cash in 4/4 cases.

**These four crossings are not an executed US$1B business, a calibrated market forecast, proof of customer value or a release qualification.** The native pilot only establishes the existing smaller service and canonical financial machinery; the 60-month cohort, enterprise-grade offer and partner supply are entirely authored hypotheses. A changed opt-in settlement model is exercised; no new ledger or active customer accounts are created by it. Source/exact-head public tests, independent review, and qualified normal release remain separate gates.

## Concrete business arrangement to construct

Sell a complete, inspectable **enterprise reconciliation and evidence workflow**, not a generic task: bounded source ingestion, reconciliation/variance and exception work, traceability, independent acceptance, corrected outputs, ongoing operational support and recurring refresh. Existing native CSV, KPI, evidence and verification engines are relevant starting components; **they do not already prove a fully autonomous production-quality $15,000/month enterprise function**. A full enterprise acceptance contract, integrations, compliance, support, warranties and buyer rights must be separately developed, implemented and priced. A customer may refuse or select human, hybrid, competing AI, self-service or no purchase.

The hypothesized partner route pays for qualified introductions and capacity out of available cash, offers a recurring checkable function to distributors with existing customer access, and shares verifiable savings with customers. A legal distributor, its consented market access, actual referral economics and license/settlement terms do **not** exist in this evidence. Nothing is purchased, marketed or contacted. A partner/acceptance protocol should only be deployed after separate rights and demand testing.

Economic alternatives include: direct acquisition (no partner network), ordinary low-ticket services, channel partnership without viral referrals, and a disrupted partner/channel market. Neither a novel invention nor spending an R&D percentage is a prerequisite for ordinary profitable operations. Invention becomes valuable when evidence shows a new mechanism resolves a real production, trust, distribution or liquidity constraint.

### Accounting and time boundaries

`enterprise.scale.simulate_scale` is a pre-existing conditional monthly engine, **not** the canonical live `JournalAccount` runtime. This edition changes only its opt-in treatment of payment processor fees and adds an optional receipt-based tax proxy. Default behavior and existing source-matched legacy output remain unchanged. Each 60-month row is reconciled to integer cents:

`cash = opening capital + cumulative collected cash - cumulative modeled expenses`;
`available cash = gross cash - explicit restricted balance`.

Profit is `cumulative recognized revenue - cumulative modeled expenses`. For the **TTM profit** observer, each recorded monthly recognized-profit change is uniformly distributed over the actual number of calendar days in that month, including leap days, and the latest 365 days are summed **without rounding away cents**. The 365-day **collected cash contribution** must separately exceed $1B, and unrestricted cash must be positive. The daily interpolation is a declared aggregation hypothesis, not native per-day evidence. The model charges all its *specified* costs; unmeasured economic costs remain limitations.

The native 14-day pilot and conditional 60-month projection are **not validated to equivalent customer/product capacity, demand, pricing or distribution semantics**. The native pilot demonstrates the exact $100k opening/finance/acceptance/replay boundary only. No multiplicative inference from native job counts is claimed.
