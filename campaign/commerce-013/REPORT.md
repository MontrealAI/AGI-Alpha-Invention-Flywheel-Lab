# Commerce 013 — Native-financed customer reach: a matched causal pilot

**Execution scope:** 30 October 2026, a four-day simulated native-account experiment on 96 prequalified public/synthetic service packets. This executes real deterministic service code and independent checks, the existing native simulated cash/earned-capital machinery, and the existing six-choice rival-responsive pricing environment. **No actual customers, paid model calls, booked real revenue, purchased outreach access, or large-scale service production occurred.**

## Why this is the experiment

The open-ended commercial mission needs more than the ability to imagine millions of orders. The prior aggregate scale studies indicate that customer access may dominate token economics, while the native event-clock runner already proves acceptance, cash conservation, contractual reserves and earned funding on bounded technical work. This edition deliberately **does not** replace either system. It executes the next small bridge: can a separately costed acquisition capacity option improve independently checked, settled repeat delivery when the cash-accounting and service constraints cannot be bypassed?

## Immutable comparisons

Five scenario families have explicit author-prior weights (25% ordinary; 20% AI price war; 20% differentiated; 15% correlated shock; 20% failed channel). The three declared strategic supply choices are (1) retain the existing single-contact/day channel; (2) purchase 2 added slots/day at $4 setup and $0.20/day; or (3) purchase 5 slots/day at $8 setup and $0.40/day. A failed-channel world keeps the expense while delivering zero added slots. All prices, failed acquisition, service attempts, onboarding, supplier effects, liabilities and simulated collections pass through the **unchanged** `enterprise.growth012.run_growth` and native `SingleWriterExchange`/`AuditedAllocation` path; no direct ledger writes are introduced.

The option affects **rate of access to six already declared potential synthetic buyers**. It does not create buyer identities, willingness to pay, order acceptance, private customer data, or additional qualified service inventory. The underlying 96-job prequalified source cohort and exact independent acceptance contracts are fixed. Seller pricing retains human-cost anchoring with human-plus-AI, rival autonomous AI, self-service, human delivery and no purchase alternatives; it is a finite authored buyer-choice game, **not** an empirically calibrated Nash equilibrium.

Development seeds 12001/12002 jointly select the preferred option by weighted simulated net profit subject to at least 95% modeled cash survival. The choice was frozen before fresh confirmation seeds 14013, 14017, 14021, 14027. The original combined development+confirmation process exceeded its execution window after durably saving development and the selected option. The protected seeds were then run in four bounded **separate** processes using the unchanged source and exactly the same source-bound manifest. The stopped attempt is not called completed. All 40 source-matched confirmation worlds (5 families × 4 seeds × the selected option and no-buy) were retained, including losses and unproductive channels.

## Confirmed within this simulated finite design

**Selected from development: `buy_large_channel`.** The weighted mean improvement in complete-cost **simulated** profit was **+$102.35 per four-day world** over no purchase. All 40 modeled worlds survived native cash obligations, but correlated-shock and failed-channel families lost money from buying additional reach. Family means of paired profit improvement:

| Market family | Paid channel minus no-buy, USD per four-day world |
| --- | ---: |
| Ordinary model | **+$180.54** |
| AI price war | **+$36.28** |
| Differentiated verified service | **+$264.69** |
| Correlated adverse shock | **−$9.60** |
| Paid channel fails to deliver additional contacts | **−$7.72** |

Source-confirmed every world had `native_replay.accepted = true`, and event-derived cash, earned-balance and independently accepted deterministic work originated from the original operating harness. The held-out results reflect *conditional* channel realization and customer-choice assumptions. Weights are authored exploration priors; the +$102.35 is **not a forecast of actual sales** and not the expected value under an empirically established distribution of market conditions.

**US$1B trajectory:** This narrow four-day native-account experiment cannot estimate time to the first billion in trailing-year collected revenue. The separate aggregate growth projections in the existing PRs do report favorable hypothetical milestone timings, but their large-scale jobs are not all individually executed and their contact-conversion/supplier assumptions are unobserved. Do not merge those projections into a false business claim. The current contribution validates a much smaller necessary link in the causal chain: costs of customer access versus accepted, settled, cash-backed service work.

## Reproduction and adversarial qualification

```sh
python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -p 'test_commerce013.py' -v
python3 -m commerce013.experiment --output NEW_NEVER_USED_DIRECTORY
```

The CLI preserves `FROZEN.json`, the development rows, selected policy and full matched confirmation rows. The long combined run can be resumed by source-identical seed-specific tasks as done in this edition; it should be updated to checkpoint confirmation per seed for operational use. Source identities, declared scenario families, cost assumptions and the stopped first attempt are preserved in the adjacent machine-readable files. Tests specifically forbid fractional contact slots, invented real revenue, missing controls, hidden additional buyers, unauthorized market variants and nonfinite costs. They test actual native accepted work, a failed supplier option and correlated adverse losses.

**Limits and next consequential construction:** No actual marketing supplier contract or authenticated distribution receipts have been obtained. Buyer willingness-to-pay, conversion, acquisition costs, customer service rights, model token bills and generalization across hundreds of services remain unverified. Next: validate a real accessible distribution channel (under separately approved outreach and provider access), bind a lawful rights/quality-qualified order to a complete service, observe actual acquisition cost and conversion, and repeat this native experiment with measured source data. Preserve PR24's interrupted longer controller tournament and its original experiment scope. Do not add fictional orders to make the target grow faster.

The market ambition is uncapped. This pilot's bounded workload, wall time and source rights are **experimental constraints**, not ceilings on service categories, revenue or future productive capacity. Fixed-capability commercial success is independent of capability-invention success. Positive simulated economics and real-world productive/scientific impacts remain independently scoped.
