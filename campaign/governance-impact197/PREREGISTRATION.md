## Frozen, falsifiable technical question

Can a bounded, self-contained, **standards-agnostic** governance-impact primitive transform explicitly human-curated, versioned requirement change records into (a) an exactly attributed affected-system queue, (b) evidence-state classifications with unknowns preserved, and (c) controlled reviewer decisions with append-only tamper-evident audit? This is one *missing instrument component*; it is NOT authoritative regulatory interpretation, continuous connectors, compliant government data residency/access control, secure deployed RBAC, a TRL 5 prototype, complete governance product, or new frontier AI capability. No LLM calls required.

**Fixed acceptance cases on synthetic inputs; execute exactly these, without reseeding:**
1. Changed applicable requirement selects the registered systems in tenant A; unaffected tenant-B systems never appear in its private queue.
2. Absent or unverified evidence classifies `UNABLE_TO_DETERMINE`, never `MET`.
3. A requirement changed from v1 to v2 invalidates v1 satisfaction, unless *independent v2-version-matched evidence* is present.
4. Retiring a requirement creates a traceable `REASSESSMENT_NEEDED` impact, not automatic proof of whole-system compliance.
5. An unassigned reviewer cannot approve/reject a finding; a matching tenant reviewer can annotate a finding without modifying the computed evidence verdict.
6. An append-only SHA-256 chain detects payload tampering and refuses a swapped/reordered audit event; no assertion of cryptographic actor authentication.
7. An ordinary, equal-information standard-library indexed join must produce the same affected set. A tie **rejects** an algorithmic-advantage claim.
8. Synthetic no-bid decision remains `NO_BID` when verified TRL/eligibility/mandatory-full-coverage/cost/buyer consent are absent. No synthetic financed account or award.

**Stopping:** one preregistered implementation and one fixed test suite; retain failures as failures. A later repaired source is a separately identified prospective revision, not a retroactive pass. Source-matched hosted CI and exact code are required for an engineering acceptance. Eligible commercial operation further requires full mandatory outcomes, actual data authorization, competition, measured cost/latency and lawful procurement/owner approval; no external outreach or submission here.
