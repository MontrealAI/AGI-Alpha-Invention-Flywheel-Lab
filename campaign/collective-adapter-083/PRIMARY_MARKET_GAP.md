## A documented real unmet outcome, not a committed sale

[CAN Health Network's Stratford Family Health Team (FHT) Data Exchange and Interoperability call](https://canhealthnetwork.ca/opportunities/data-exchange-and-interoperability/) opened **October 29, 2026** and closes **November 12, 2026**. Stratford FHT's transition toward **QHR Accuro** while collaborating practices use **TELUS Collaborative Health Record (CHR)** creates a concrete need to exchange clinical notes, care plans, medications and diagnostic results across the systems. The call specifically seeks near-real-time exchange within existing EMR workflows and measurable accuracy, clinical coordination and administrative-time improvements, supported by a **validated data-sharing strategy and procurement-quality evidence**. The buyer publicly reserves discretion not to proceed. This establishes a **source-grounded expressed institutional need**; it establishes neither purchase commitment, signed contract, price, volume, payment, available budget, consent for patient data nor a demonstrated gap against competent existing integrators.
