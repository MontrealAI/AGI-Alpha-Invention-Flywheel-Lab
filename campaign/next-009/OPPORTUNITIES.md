# Three consequential possibilities and selection

Source cutoff: 30 October 2026. These are development investigations; none is admitted as a scientifically novel flagship or a validated commercial cohort. The selected lead receives a bounded construction allocation, not automatic success.

## 1. Water-network observation planning

**Beneficiary and complete outcome.** Utility engineering teams and modeling consultancies need executable plans identifying which modeled source hypotheses their observations can distinguish, including uncertainty, failed sensors, and explicit abstention. The first offering is a source-bound model-analysis and measurement-plan package, not an operational emergency decision, site calibration, complete hydraulic master plan, or professional certification. A robust plan could reduce blind investigation and make experimental design reproducible across networks; field capacity gains remain to be measured.

**Constraint.** Sparse sensing and unknown constant sensor offsets can make different sources compatible with the same observations. No selection of existing observations can resolve an all-sensor ambiguity.

**Competent alternatives and human effort.** EPA EPANET/WNTR, Sandia Chama, WST source inversion, minimum test cover, robust sensor placement, conventional calibration and exact multicover are relevant. The actual cheap probe reproduces Chama's Net1 three-sensor objective exactly: 7600 seconds, sensors12/23/32. Matched competent-human package time is UNKNOWN; wages and broader contracts do not supply it.

**What can change now.** Available public executable physical models, current autonomous coding/research tools, exact interval arithmetic and independent certificate checks make a full uncertainty-aware planning package constructible here. This is integration opportunity, not evidence that the constituent mathematics is new.

**Alteration/falsifier.** Test whether a generated same-sensor reference-observation primitive removes a reproduced constant-offset obstruction while keeping the source set, noise bound, observation horizon and outage requirement fixed. Give the strongest conventional workflow exactly the same reference information. An unresolved all-measurement pair or conventional tie limits the claim; physical offset drift or a nonzero reference invalidates the calibration assumption.

**Buyer/price/volume.** York RFP3918-26 independently purchases model update/calibration/validation, but admits only six prequalified consultancies. University design/analysis rates support alternatives, not this product's willingness to pay. AGI-specific price and volume UNKNOWN. CAD250 in commercial scenarios is an authored sensitivity, not an order.

**Reusable capability.** Source-to-observability relations, ambiguity witnesses, reference-contrast programs, outage certificates and scoped failure memory can transfer to new model-based sensor/design jobs. Compare ordinary reuse with the same content and conventional solver; no special memory advantage assumed.

**Decision: CONSTRUCT_BOUNDED_MODEL_INSTRUMENT.** Retain the conventional tie as valid technical parity and preserve conditional autonomous-service economics separately.

Primary sources: [EPA WNTR examples](https://usepa.github.io/WNTR/examples.html); [Sandia executable example](https://github.com/sandialabs/chama/blob/main/examples/water_network_example.py); [minimum test-cover prior art](https://arxiv.org/abs/1507.07134); [robust placement](https://par.nsf.gov/servlets/purl/10350109); [source-identification benchmark](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/dataset-for-testing-contamination-source-identification-methods-for-water-distribution-net); [York procurement](https://york.bidsandtenders.ca/Module/Tenders/en/Tender/Detail/597d96c5-9a68-4907-a56d-afe5b1526bd4).

## 2. Metabolic flux measurement for bioprocess development

**Beneficiary/outcome.** Bioprocess teams need identifiable internal fluxes before changing production pathways. **Constraint:** uptake/secretion alone underdetermines internal fluxes and errors can amplify. **Alternatives:** flux variability analysis, isotope tracing, established optimal measurement design; exact measurement selection was studied in1992. Human tracer experimentation, review and fully loaded costs remain unmeasured here. **Changed opportunity:** public model availability and automated experiment generation could produce useful specifications. **Falsifier:** ordinary FVA/isotope design already meets the same obligation, or the missing physical tracing experiment cannot be performed. **Buyer/price/volume:** no independently grounded narrow-order evidence found. **Reusable capability:** rank/identifiability and additional-measurement planning. **Decision:** do not repeat the existing SBML lane as a new flagship; preserve this distinct screened alternative.

Primary sources: [Savinell and Palsson1992 PartI](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1453697/), [PartII](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1453698/).

## 3. Active spectral mixture identification

**Beneficiary/outcome.** Analytical laboratories need constituent identification from scarce or expensive measurements. **Constraint:** confounded spectra, library gaps and instrument mismatch. **Alternatives:** NIST AMDIS, calibrated FTIR methods and active wavelength selection experimentally studied in2016. Human instrument work and same-task time/cost are UNKNOWN. **Changed opportunity:** model-driven acquisition could generate next wavelength/measurement instructions. **Alteration/falsifier:** add independently calibrated discriminatory measurements; fail if an existing complete active method already resolves the target or calibrated mixture truth is unavailable. **Buyer/price/volume:** no independently grounded narrow offer evidence found. **Reusable capability:** active measurement and instrument-aware uncertainty. **Decision:** NOT_ADMITTED; no convenient synthetic mixture substituted for missing calibrated data.

Primary sources: [active wavelength selection2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27590540/), [NIST AMDIS](https://chemdata.nist.gov/dokuwiki/doku.php?id=chemdata%3Aamdisexplained), [NIOSH method guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/nmam/pdf/NMAM_5thEd_EBook-508-final.pdf).

## Bounded comparator search and residual uncertainty

The team inspected executable government/laboratory sources and primary method papers across all three fields, then ran the cheapest water baseline. This is not proof that every possible existing method fails. Relevant known calibration/test-cover methods receive explicit strong-control status. No beyond-human foresight or scientific novelty is established by this search. Scientific model usefulness, human-service economics, AI-native competition and investment attractiveness will receive separate verdicts.
