## What the business actually compares

The candidates are enterprise AI operations, a sector AI consortium, and verified compute enablement. All three are selected somewhere in the main matrix:572,684 and398 episodes respectively, with overlap between offers. These are executed financial/game simulations of hypothesized complete arrangements. Their technical acceptance is sampled under stated assumptions, not independently produced industrial artifacts or delivered customer systems. NHS and EU programme sources establish consequential market context, not our prices, costs or demand.

The policies include non-entry, a100%-human production benchmark, a strong equal-permissions conventional control, founder-led AI-enabled operation, autonomous research and combined research. All eligible modes may choose paid research, a second sample, human/AI/repair methods and warm-network/convening channels. A policy named autonomous research is labeled hybrid whenever it chooses human work. Founder/conventional rules are identical and all480 matched pairs tie: **this does not identify the actual founder's incremental judgment, technical construction or business-design value**. It is a declared modeling limitation, not evidence that those capabilities have no value. Any difference from more information or lower assumed research cost must be labeled accordingly.

Inherited MONTREAL.AI/QUEBEC.AI assets are not added follower lists or a guaranteed premium. Their hypothetical channel-cost/reach effect generally helps at high capital, but not every matched seed. This is an asset-effect sensitivity, not an estimate of actual brand value. New offer construction and a cheaper $100k-feasible partnership/pilot route require a distinct successor experiment, not retuning these held-out results.

Year-five profit is recognized net revenue minus declared incurred expenses/tax in quarters17–20. Costs cover setup, research, human work, channels, platform, production/rework, failures,3% invoice loss, tax and financing fees where applicable. Reserves protect commitments; they are not double-counted expenses. Real model/host bills, long-tail industrial liabilities, rights and empirical customer acceptance remain unknown. No verified complete real-world lifecycle-cost claim follows.
