# Consequential AI business 096: prospective conditional scenario

## Purpose and status

The user asks to simulate an AI business using MONTREAL.AI, QUEBEC.AI, international relationships and credibility, and strong technical/business judgment. This experiment treats those as inherited assets and tests specified operating decisions. It does not infer actual company revenue, cash, client lists, contract rights, endorsements, conversion or proprietary technical performance. No number of followers is a buyer count. Brand overlap is not counted twice.

This is a finite conditional business game, not a calibrated forecast or globally optimal enterprise. It is not a native Account execution, actual useful customer delivery or a replacement for issue219's native commercial work. The separate 095 finite-audience component remains development-only and distribution-isolating; its old development outputs are not confirmation evidence. Frozen094 is unchanged. Grid/work construction PR222 is separate engineering evidence, not acceptance of the economic offers below.

## Executed policies and equal permissions

Compare non-entry, an exclusively human production benchmark, conventional expert research with equal productive choices, founder-led AI-enabled research, autonomous research and combined research. Except for the explicitly all-human benchmark, all productive modes can choose conventional human delivery, AI delivery or AI with contingent repair. Founder-led is not forced into all-human production. All modes receive the same offers, assets, rival responses, repair and channel options. The conventional control is a strong equal-information control, not a deliberately weak legacy service.

Each quarter, research purchases a noisy benefit observation, a technical acceptance probe and channel eligibility probes. All policies can buy a second independent benefit observation and larger technical sample when current research funds permit. A Beta(17,3) prior is updated with eight or sixteen sampled technical outcomes. The probe's relevance to full delivery is a declared hypothesis, not proof of industrial acceptance. Founder-guided priors are not assigned privileged correctness. There is no automatic founder quality or profit multiplier.

Research costs are offer-specific. Conventional/founder expert analysis costs the full declared research envelope; autonomous/combined analysis costs20% in the base hypothesis. Founder/conventional/combined oversight additionally uses20 hours per researched offer at a hypothetical loaded $150/hour. Purchased expert delivery hours are separately counted; they are not all the founder's own labor. Founder oversight has a500-hour quarterly constraint. Combined is an information/cost configuration, not evidence of superior judgment. Report decisions and human work explicitly; nonzero human work is hybrid, not strictly autonomous. Even the policy named autonomous research can select purchased human delivery; those resulting episodes are labeled hybrid. The separate human-only arm, not the conventional equal-permissions policy, supplies the100%-human benchmark.

Technical repair attempts to recover failed AI delivery, with an additional30% cost envelope fully reserved and charged. Its conditional success is60%, reduced to30% in the unreliable-capability world. Every mode can select it. Repair outcomes are executed Bernoulli draws inside this economic simulation, not an actually repaired physical system.

Stakeholder assembly chooses a paid warm-introduction channel or a costed convening channel. Warm access costs $5,000 per attempted organization, with80% eligibility and a scenario-specific prospect flow. Convening costs $100,000 plus $2,000 per attempt, has four times that flow and40% eligibility. Eight-trial channel probes inform a shared eligibility belief. Research envelopes include probe costs. These numbers are uncertain hypotheses, not measured effects of either brand. The no-inherited-assets sensitivity reduces available flow to25% and raises warm access to $25,000; it is explicitly a hypothetical asset effect, not measured brand value.

The controller chooses offer, productive variant, channel, price and10/25/50/100% of currently affordable contact capacity, with non-entry always available. Setup is paid once per activated offer. Research is capped at2% of current cash and must fit free cash. Every candidate is re-evaluated after all research has been paid before setup, acquisition or production commitments. Quantity staging respects current free cash; future collections never fund present commitments. Fixed menus and research heuristics are not a proof of globally optimal policy or optimal experimentation.

## Buyers, rivals and information

Buyer benefit types are0.5/1/2 times the offer reference with weights50/35/15%; budgets are65% of those benefits. Buyers compare the offered expected accepted outcome with human, hybrid, self-service, a competent autonomous rival and no purchase. Actual autonomous acceptance is uncertain. Economic resource costs, acceptance, demand and probe accuracy remain hypotheses.

Prices are a1,001-point leader grid from0 to reference benefit. The rival optimizes its response on the declared integer-cent money/utility grid using every budget/utility demand breakpoint and its one-cent undercut. No floating epsilon or unattained supremum is called an optimum. Independent development comparison against the nested201-point grid found material sensitivity (up to64% in one medium-offer stage and larger relative changes near zero), so the finer grid is frozen before confirmation. This does not prove continuous leader optimality. Ties use the buyer outside option first and split seller ties; follower ties choose the response least favorable to the leader. Withdrawal is allowed. A zero gap is only within the leader's finite grid and the stated follower/buyer game.

Instant rival response, public price visibility and substitutability are explicit competitive assumptions, not universal laws. The tested model omits negotiated exclusivity, many forms of differentiation, scarce rival capacity and long strategic reputation formation. Do not call a loss under this game a universal business ceiling. Nominal expected production cost is multiplied by1.025, matching the mean of its Uniform(0.8,1.25) realized cost envelope. Maximum1.25 cost is reserved before commitment.

Policies use observed signals, not hidden realized benefit or future delivery draws. The environment uses latent realized benefit to evaluate voluntary demand after the chosen price; the controller does not reprice clairvoyantly. Paired policies share seeded market shocks. All prospects in each finite quarterly cohort are distinct; there is no automatic repeat-sales claim or imported customer database.

## Prospective finance and cost accounting

Own-equity arms assume the stated initial cash only for their counterfactual comparison. They do not assert the user has that capital. A separate prospective external-equity screen uses twelve disjoint calibration seeds96040–96051 before held-out execution. Investor preferences are hypothetical:15% annual expected return over6.5 years and worst simulated terminal proceeds at least75% of capital. The prior weights central at50% and the other eight worlds equally over50%; these are not market probabilities.

External equity pays a1% upfront closing fee. Investor receives returned capital plus80% of positive residual profit, or absorbs enterprise losses; founder retains20% of positive residual. No guaranteed principal repayment, debt or hidden top-up. A pair is approved only when the fixed preflight screen passes; no held-out profit can retroactively approve it. Only approved pairs receive separate external-finance runs. Declined pairs remain clearly marked own-equity numerical counterfactuals, not funded businesses. The funding receipt is a simulated agreement at time0, before any commitment, never an actual commitment or obtained finance.

Costs include declared development, research, channel acquisition, platform overhead ($50k per active operating quarter), human work, production/rework envelope, failed deliveries, net refunds/defaults (3% gross), tax26.5% with loss carryforward, and financing fees where applicable. Delay is one or three quarters plus an extra quarter on23% of receipts. All invoice losses are recognized before receivables; no free bad-debt receivable is counted. Tax arrears remain liabilities and stop new entry. Reserves are commitments, not expenses; actual incurred production is expensed when delivered. Unknown host/model bills and real long-tail industrial liabilities are not measured; no verified complete-cost after-tax real-world profitability claim follows.
