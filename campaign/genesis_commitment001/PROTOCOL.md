# GENESIS Commitment 001: a capacity-backed operating turn

## Registered question

Can one source-bound local simulated enterprise reserve shared delivery capacity and cash before promising work, execute and independently verify a restored synthetic application, collect payment, and use available earnings for a different accepted schema-migration delivery?

The primary fresh account starts with exactly **US$100,000**. It is neither a migration nor a continuation of any historical account. Other cases and the conventional comparator have separate counterfactual accounts; never add their earnings together. The long-run objective remains US$3 billion complete-cost profit in an exact trailing 365-day window. This short decisive experiment cannot establish that objective.

## Frozen acceptance and decisions

`PROTOCOL.json` freezes explicit money, costs, buyer budgets, outside options, timing, supplier capacity, scenario conditions and the successor budget before the campaign runs. Deliverable input records and checker source are bound before offers. Buyer participation may be refused. Supplier participation is an authored model assumption, never actual consent. Prices, costs and response delays are hypothetical inputs, not market observations.

1. Restore: actually execute restoration of a destroyed synthetic SQLite application. Independent acceptance must reconstruct the required records and invariants from source, inspect the delivered database content, and reject lost, altered or fabricated data.
2. Shared resources: use one native account owner and write capacity interval/quantity into its native resource-spend commitment before issuing the promise. Independently replay overlapping claims; a rejected or unaffordable request creates no promise. Retries must not buy capacity or charge cash twice. No distributed, cross-seller supplier registry is claimed.
3. Economics: enumerate research, failed acquisition, onboarding, development, production, tools/inference envelope, infrastructure, supervision/review envelope, reservations, verification, maintenance/support, failures, fees, refunds and modeled tax. Nonapplicable financing has an explicit zero-debt assumption. Actual unmeasured development/model/host/labor bills remain unknown, separately from all modeled costs. No borrowed money or future collections fund actions.
4. Successor: a fresh schema-migration task has a different output and acceptance contract. Its budget must be recorded before the first sale, then funded only from collected unrestricted earnings. Acceptance, invoicing, collection and full costs apply to this second delivery as well. This proves funding sufficiency under the policy if it passes, not necessity: an unrestricted seed-funded conventional alternative remains eligible.
5. Comparators: an independently executed direct conventional route receives the same data, tools, ordinary reuse, financing rights and acceptance. Count preparation and checking for both. A tie establishes no retained-method or intelligence advantage. A modeled labor comparison is not an observed AI-versus-human productivity study.

## Registered cases and stopping

Run the declared central, no-demand, delayed-payment, bad-output, shared-supplier-shock and higher-cost cases. Retain every case, decline and loss; no probability is assigned to this scenario set. A central loss is not permission to edit prices. Source repairs require preserving the failure and fresh prospective confirmation inputs. Do not silently alter the original account or labels. Each case stops at its declared endpoint with receivables, custody, reserves and obligations still visible.

At least one attempted capacity collision must be refused before a promise. At least one delayed collection must prevent an otherwise unaffordable earned-only successor. Deliberate malformed inputs, altered delivered records, duplicate ids, concurrent callers, interrupted commits and idempotent retries challenge both production and independent audit. Exact implemented coverage and untested conditions are reported separately.

## Threat model and qualification

Named environment: disposable synthetic data, Python on a supported local POSIX filesystem, one owned account writer, trusted source and honest operating system. Source hashes prove byte identity, not supplier consent or code safety. Reject malformed input, ambiguous identifiers, traversal and symlinks within the supported boundary. No production accounts, external services, paid model calls, customers, suppliers or physical devices are accessed.

Process-crash and retry checks do not establish protection against malicious filesystem custody, arbitrary disk corruption, power loss, multi-host concurrency or real application recovery. No production-grade or security-certification claim follows. Rollback means stop the opt-in campaign and retain its complete original source, journal and authority; never rewrite committed account history to make a newer source resume it.

Publish compact public source, reproducible inputs, key-free native exports and qualified receipts. Exclude private simulation keys and original user documents. Exact-head tests, normal review and integration, released bytes and operation of those bytes are separate qualification stages. Local or candidate-download success must never be called a main release.
