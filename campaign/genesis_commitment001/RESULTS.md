# Commitment 001: executed result and claim boundaries

The primary case **did not sell** and lost US$1,080 in modeled expenses. The machinery completed a restore → collection → earnings-funded schema-migration sequence in three authored positive cases. Both conventional production routes produced the same accepted outputs and financial outcomes. This is an executed conditional simulation, not demonstrated demand, actual commerce, autonomous discovery or the US$3 billion objective.

## Exact experiment

Initial execution source (before the later F03/F04 repairs): [`ffcc65221ffaac28533c5f759d166b44ecc8b734`](https://github.com/MontrealAI/AGI-Alpha-Invention-Flywheel-Lab/tree/ffcc65221ffaac28533c5f759d166b44ecc8b734). The horizon is integer hours **0 through 120 inclusive**. Accounting uses integer micro-USD and the native `JournalAccount`/adaptive `Account`: development and prepaid delivery resources are expensed when incurred; buyer custody stays restricted until released; maturity alone is not income. No debt, top-up or account migration occurs. Actual revenue is zero. Actual developer time, ChatGPT resource allocation, host compute, acquisition, legal contracting, supplier capacity and tax liability remain unknown, not zero. Explicit human supervision and expert-review envelopes make the economic model hybrid, not a strict autonomous enterprise.

The four compared opportunities, alternatives, rejection experiments and primary-source provenance are in [OPPORTUNITIES.md](OPPORTUNITIES.md). Shared supplier capacity was selected for the decision value of testing simultaneous commitments. This selection does not establish market superiority, invention novelty or admission as a transformational business.

## What actually executed

The first output is an actual restored synthetic SQLite application after deliberate corruption. The second is a fresh invoice schema migration with different input records and acceptance requirements. The separately authored checker reads the delivered database bytes, validates schemas, integrity, relationships and exact record invariants, and does not accept a producer's success flag. This verifies those synthetic deliverables, not real application recovery, an incident history or a production recovery-time commitment.

Both deliveries reserve native cash and a shared supplier interval before a promise; an overlapping claim is refused. Native records bind the order, buyer, terms, source, request, actual output, acceptance and payment transitions. A modeled supplier's consent remains an authored assumption. One account's inventory cannot stop a real supplier promising the same capacity to another organization.

For the favorable case, the modeled costs are US$880 setup, US$100 first acquisition, US$740 first delivery, US$60 combined payment fees, US$400 successor development, US$80 successor acquisition, US$480 successor delivery, US$100 closing maintenance and US$672 tax: US$3,512 in total. Delivery costs include onboarding, reserved capacity, production, inference, infrastructure, verification, expert review, support and failure/retry capacity. Prepaid slots are expensed even if not used.

The preregistered US$990 successor budget includes development, acquisition, delivery and its payment fee. Native unrestricted collected earnings fund the successor's development, acquisition and delivery. Later enterprise maintenance and tax are still incurred and reported. This is not a lockbox for every possible future obligation. The registered natural cases do not demonstrate positive accounting profit with an unaffordable successor: the favorable pre-maturity checkpoint has US$1,800 of unrestricted earnings and demand-only has US$1,000 after maturity. Both can cover US$990; seed equity backs their reserves. The separate no-earnings and insufficient-cash refusal checks remain narrower evidence. Because the original seed could also finance the successor, the evidence establishes **earnings-funded**, not **causally enabled only by those earnings**.

`direct` uses competent ordinary database copy/SQL with the same information, resources, reuse and acceptance. It ties every outcome. No retained-method advantage, end-to-end AI-versus-human productivity advantage or improved discovery is established.
