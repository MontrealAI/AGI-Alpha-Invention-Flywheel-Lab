# Native Opportunity Braid 040 — registered exposed synthetic reproduction

**Objective.** Demonstrate one **machine-selected and native-executed** sequence: discover an affordable complete-service opportunity, use observed accepted collections to identify an unmet distribution/capacity bottleneck, choose and pay for the best feasible means, enter another requested and independently accepted service opportunity, then retain applicability information for the next choice. This is **not** a billion-dollar experiment or a novel invention result.

## Exact current experiment

`REGISTRATION.json` is the single immutable complete native input: 28-day horizon; seed **341014** (already exposed in historical engineering); two separate arms baseline/growth; 104 source-derived service jobs; **$100,000 initial simulated capital per arm**, zero external deposits or borrowing. Positive central synthetic case only, explicitly labeled `reproduction` (not fresh holdout). Buyer budgets/value, arrival, channel terms, reliability, retention, rivals, production capacity, payment deadline, nonpayment and all fees are declared in the registration. No live customers, paid provider or real money.

The growth-only `opportunity_policy` is admitted by `engine.validate_config`, frozen in `FROZEN.json`, then used from **inside** `_prepare_plan` at each daily plan. Each decision consumes only paid *prior* collections, available/unrestricted-earned cash after native obligations, known current customer needs, accepted source job types, visible declines and declared channel terms. It enumerates qualified direct service opportunities plus feasible channel and capacity additions. Its discounted distribution benefit is a **declared hypothesis**, not observed conversion. It may choose no additional investment. The incumbent baseline uses exactly the same frozen configuration but does not activate the new decision rule. The search never supplies or alters a buyer's private willingness-to-pay draw.

The native engine and `JournalAccount.spend(...,earned_only=True)` still execute acquisition and capacity commitments. Buyer choice, producer artifact, independent checker, collection/refund, taxes and reserve accounting remain the canonical path. Source-frozen verifier additionally checks one opportunity decision per day, prior-settlement values, hash-bound emitted decisions, selected finite feasible alternatives, actual investment type and earned-funding identity. The post-audit `connected_sequence` requires a past settled service, native earned-funded investment, a source-attributed later settled service with different type and greater per-order modeled contribution. Its result is a bounded claim, not a new native financial authority.

## The actual constraint and search

An order may be executable and profitable yet the enterprise cannot reach new voluntary buyers without a paid contact channel; after access grows, one working delivery slot can limit orders. Search compares **continue qualifying the current requested service**, **fund a time-limited pilot contact contract**, **fund one native capacity unit** and **retain cash**. A service target is selected from *accepted source capabilities* using observed contribution plus a disclosed 50%-of-offer-surplus public-catalog prior. No unrestricted new program is invented. Channel exposure is un-targeted by service and does not guarantee the targeted service; only later accepted and paid order evidence can close that link.

Source identities, command receipts, account state and actual service outputs must match for a claim. A matched baseline receiving the same exogenous buyer values, shocks, service inventory and funding assumptions is the comparison; it may win.

## Stopping and claims

One immutable seed/case, exact 28 days + scheduled clearance, 2 arms, no additional seeds or horizon changes; max 1100 seconds cumulative script operation and 25min hosted job limit. Preserve explicit incomplete status if timeout, never re-seed or fill absent records. CI publishes only a compact public `SUMMARY.json`; all original financial recovery state remains private in its own runner. A development success is not causal channel attribution, confirmed general policy advantage, real demand or recursive invention-method improvement. More positive and adverse **fresh** arms must be frozen separately before advancement.
