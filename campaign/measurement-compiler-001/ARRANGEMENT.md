# From a reusable measurement method to a possible enterprise

The candidate outcome is a useful measurement of an otherwise opaque industrial
process. A possible offering combines application-specific electrodes and fixtures,
an accepted inference method, calibration, commissioning and continued support.
Several customers could jointly fund a common acquisition/processing core while
paying for their distinct adaptations. This is a hypothesis, not an accepted offer.

The current executable construction removes some sequential acquisition work in a
particular laboratory dataset. It does not yet measure a customer-selected hidden
property with accepted uncertainty. Different geometries, frequencies, contact
conditions, motion and materials can require new calibration and invalidate reuse.
Signal recovery is a dependency; it is not the customer outcome by itself.

The commercial alternatives include existing tomography vendors and research
instruments, conventional experimental-design and low-rank methods, engineering
firms using AI, laboratories doing the work themselves, and no purchase. The
same-algebra ordinary control ties this prototype, so proprietary algorithmic
advantage has not been established. A meaningful residual might concern an unmet
measurement specification, reliable low-volume adaptation or lower complete
deployment cost. None is demonstrated by the laboratory result alone.

Before treating this as a market, the construction needs distinct real requirements,
the best complete incumbent offers, physical repeatability and drift evidence,
customer acceptance and voluntary terms covering development failure and support.
No current quote, price, willingness to pay, procurement requirement, accepted
buyer or revenue is supplied here. The earlier synthetic shared-invention dollar
figures are not imported into this instrument case.

The retained asset is a runnable compiler, data adapter, applicability boundary and
failure evidence. Applying it to another specimen reuses a method; it does not
inherit the first specimen's answer or acceptance. A genuinely different successor
must demonstrate a fresh useful property or application, including adaptation and
validation costs, against an equally resourced route that may construct or buy its
own means. No such economic successor has been completed here.

This experiment runs no native account, creates no escrow, rights, royalty or
funding obligation, and makes no token-value claim. Commercial funding, if later
authorized and modeled, must use the canonical accounts and supported terms.
The primary human-versus-AI productivity benchmark remains unmeasured; no human
hours, wages or imaginary business receipts are inferred from arithmetic timings.
