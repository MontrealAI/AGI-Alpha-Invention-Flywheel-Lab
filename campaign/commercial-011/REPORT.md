# Conditional simulated commercial results and portfolio growth

**A conditional simulated commercial flywheel succeeded under the following tested conditions.** Four narrow technical service categories were actually produced and independently checked. The source-gated growth simulation then sustained profitable, earned-funded repeated operation under both the favorable and central conditions with a guarded allocation policy. This is a conditional simulated enterprise, not observed customers, collections, or individually rendered work at the projected scale.

The screened portfolio contains all25 PR17 categories. The executed subset is receivables reconciliation, one actual supervisor-reporting software repair, a source-bound operating guide with a checked example, and a12-question evidence-supported proposal packet. The proposal packet is not a full enterprise RFP; the repair is not a general claim to solve arbitrary bugs. Their matched cost intervals replace the much larger reference-job proxies.

## Guarded growth on fresh matched scenarios

The one predeclared allocation repair used30 fresh seeds,10 per scenario, and compared three controls on the same scenarios: aggressive reinvestment, fixed capacity, and guarded reinvestment. Each run covered up to120 monthly steps with USD5,000 initial capital. All90 full native-accounted event histories were retained and independently replayed. The guarded policy reserves pending fee/tax/support exposure and two settlement windows before expansion, uses public historical contribution and observed backlog, and never uses future buyer reservation values.

## The USD1billion annual-revenue objective

No tested world reached USD1billion in trailing12-month collected revenue within120 months:0/200 original matched-policy worlds and0/90 fresh repair worlds. Time to target is therefore right-censored beyond the declared horizon. This is a simulated growth result, not a customer-count equation and not an estimate of real-world target probability. Equal one-third scenario weights are declared reporting conventions, not calibrated probabilities. Binding limits include reachable leads and activation, churn, buyer and competing offers, capacity, settlement timing, working capital and provider liability terms. The seed-capital level was tested, not optimized; the minimum capital required remains unestablished.

## Pricing and risk assumptions

The seller searches a human-anchored finite price grid including small discounts, larger discounts, premiums and NoBid, extended to0.05–4 times the matched human benchmark to expose boundary effects. It maximizes modeled expected collected contribution per quote using public information and a declared prior; win probability is endogenous. The result is the best tested policy in that class, not a universal optimum or a Nash-equilibrium certificate. Human first and repeat costs include human-operated onboarding, preparation, QA, correction and support; AI operating and one-time onboarding costs contain no hypothetical human staffing. Actual host token bills remain unknown.

Initial public reputation uses a common Beta prior and one correlated technical qualification observation for our service, followed by delivered-history updates. Rival prices and service reputation can respond on a stated cadence. Buyers compare delivered human service, a responding rival, self-service with customer effort, and no purchase. Demand and acceptance at scale, collection performance, permissions, customer effort, cost intervals and repeat behavior remain explicit scenario assumptions. The simulation does not prove that one narrow technical example generalizes to every future job.

**Financial-terms limitation:** favorable and central growth scenarios assume2- and3-month expiry of full-principal exposure. The adverse case retains the native original forever-reserved principal. Expiry is an explicit versioned contract assumption checked against its frozen scenario; it is not an established feature of the original protected exchange or an observed provider. Aggregate growth settlement events are projections, not fabricated signed provider receipts. The native ProtectedExchange still supplies cash, restriction, earned-fund and investment-admission semantics.

## Source and verification

The first100 fixed seeds were assigned round-robin across three scenarios and matched across two controls. An independent review found that the generic projection settlement interface could accept unbacked collections. The original source and matrix remain marked initially unqualified. A guard-only repair rejected unsupported collections, overcollection and unqualified expiry before mutation. All200 worlds were reexecuted with unchanged policies and seeds; every financial, price, target and funding summary was byte-identical. Every full event history was rebuilt through the native accounting adapter. The subsequent capital repair used a separately frozen, untouched30-seed block and three matched controls.

Thirteen growth and independent-review regression tests pass, including invoice-versus-cash separation, no seed-funded earned investment, overcollection, duplicate settlement, scenario-bound expiry, summary mutation, one-time onboarding, deterministic replay, price search, NoBid and the guarded liquidity buffer.
