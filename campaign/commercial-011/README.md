# Versioned portfolio and growth experiment 011

This successor screens all25 PR17 services and admits only the narrower source-bound technical subset actually produced and independently checked: receivables reconciliation, a supervisor reporting-defect repair, an operating guide, and a12-question evidence packet. These qualifications do not establish arbitrary future bug-fixing, full enterprise RFP capability, observed customers or exact model bills. The source, artifacts and acceptance limits are in `services/EXECUTED_SERVICES.json`; full PR17 reference-job hours/prices are not substituted for the smaller delivered scopes.

The monthly simulator projects recurring opportunities, channel reach and saturation, acquisition expenditure, approval, buyer choice, supplier outages, capacity, churn, quality stress, rework, deferred settlement and noncollection. Opportunity arrival differs from winning; one-time provider onboarding is charged to a newly acquired customer, while repeat jobs omit it. Human first and repeat costs remain human-delivered, with corresponding onboarding/preparation/QA/support in the declared ranges. Seller pricing receives public reputation, rival offers and the repeat mix, without private buyer reservation values. Price search includes the human anchor, small and larger discounts, premiums and NoBid. It reports the best tested finite-price policy, never a universal optimum or an equilibrium certificate.

The existing ProtectedExchange defines cash, profit, restrictions, unrestricted earnings and investment admission. `NativeProjection` supplies aggregate scenario journal entries to that native accounting definition; it does not manufacture individual signed funding/clearing receipts. An actual protected service-loop demonstration remains a separate PR16 result. Scaled production and acceptance are explicitly conditional extrapolations from the small technical qualification, not millions of actually rendered or independently checked artifacts. The lossless representative event tapes are independently reconstructed through the same native accounting code.

Provider liability terms are an important changed assumption. The favorable and central successor cases declare2- and3-month expiry of full-principal exposure; the adverse case preserves the original forever-reserved principal. Expiry is checked against the frozen scenario and recorded as its own projection event. No expired-tail assumption is presented as an established feature of PR16 or an observed payment provider. Seed funds still backing protection are reported separately; capital replacement is an additional metric, not a hidden commercial-success gate.

The experiment uses100 fresh fixed seeds distributed round-robin across favorable, central and adverse cases, with matched earned-reinvestment and fixed-capacity controls. Equal one-third reporting weights are a transparent convention, not measured world probabilities. No case weights or seeds are selected for favorable outputs. Representative journals are retained for each case/control. The120-month target is USD1billion of trailing12-month collected revenue; annualized monthly run rate, trailing-year collections, cumulative collections and profit remain distinct. Nonattainment is right-censored and reported, even when a smaller positive recurring business works.

Complete resource cost envelopes include failures, acquisition without sales, production, verification, corrections, delivery, support, maintenance, payment charges, modeled taxes and setup. Actual host token bills remain unknown. The development liquidity failure and original source are preserved in `development/`: accrued-tax cash needs were initially not provisioned before production. The repaired controller keeps a tax liquidity buffer before accepting production commitments. Subsequent negative scenarios and all earlier interrupted PR16 records remain unchanged.

Reproduce with Python3.12 and the pinned commercial dependencies. Obtain the literal hashes from the published source freeze, then run:

```sh
python -m unittest discover -s tests -p 'test_portfolio_growth*011.py' -v
python -m commercial.portfolio_growth --services campaign/commercial-011/services/EXECUTED_SERVICES.json --policy campaign/commercial-011/POLICY.json --expected-policy-sha256 POLICY_SHA --expected-services-sha256 SERVICES_SHA --output NEW_DIRECTORY
```

The final `SOURCE_FREEZE.json` supplies literal hashes and the exact runnable command; placeholders above explain the interface only. A source-matched rerun is engineering reproduction, not a new independent market observation. No spending, customer contact, private data, financial provisioning or real payment occurs.
