## Useful work and acceptance
A merchant-month bank/ledger/payment packet becomes source-linked settlement groups, a cent-exact balance bridge and explicit duplicate/missing/ambiguous/invalid queues. No posting, payment, audit opinion or tax advice. Independent generated truth includes grouped/split settlements, fees, conflicting IDs, malformed dates/currency and unresolved references. Producer sees public input only. Development and held-out seeds are disjoint. Every actual held-out packet must have no false match, no missing required exception and an exact bridge; corrupt-output negative controls must fail. A simple competent reference-based matching baseline is retained. Passing establishes this bounded synthetic task, not unconstrained real-accounting competence.

## Three materially different possibilities considered
Reconciliation (objective cross-record acceptance, recurring paid class), software bug repair (valuable but heterogeneous environments and independent tests), and policy customer support (large paid class, harder outcome verification and account authority). The supplied 25-family screen ranks reconciliation first on explicit analyst priors. Choose it for this first implemented growth kernel, not as a global optimum.

## Representation and native finance
Actual accepted sample packets are separate from modeled bulk fulfillment quantities. Large quantities are **homogeneous SIM bulk contracts**, not individually rendered outputs, independent observed customers or secured distribution partners. Each batch binds accepted quality evidence, quantity, source, modeled choice and capacity state, price and full unit costs. Partition different acceptance/collection/maturity outcomes. Use canonical JournalAccount/adaptive Account and exact-prospective-v2 only. No direct journal writes, loose balance copy or projection ledger can authorize spending. Native small unrolled/bulk equivalence tests reconcile cash, profit, reserves and obligations. Independent operating audit recomputes quantities/choice/costs from committed observations and random stream before accepting final native result.

Initial financing is modeled equity at the chosen opening amount. Current native schema has no dynamic debt/equity injection; report that exact limitation, never disguise loans as customer wallets/revenue. Separate capital financing/opportunity cost from seed, revenue, profit and cash. Do not require seed repayment before profit. The primary capital-available policy may use available seed working capital throughout; native receipts identify SEED versus EARNED funding. A separate earned-only expansion control allows seed at launch but subsequent growth only from collected unrestricted earnings. Neither labels seed as earned.
