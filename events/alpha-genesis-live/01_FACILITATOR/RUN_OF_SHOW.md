# RUN OF SHOW — ALPHA GENESIS FLYWHEEL Ω — LIVE

**Format:** 120-minute live online flagship professional laboratory. **Teaching mechanism:** witness an original ambition becoming a working artifact, then practice a reusable method. **Room size:** aim for 12–30 interactive pilot participants; capacity and price are unverified commercial hypotheses.

| Minutes | Live experience | Audience action | Evidence / transition |
|---|---|---|---|
| 00–08 | Cinematic opening: “What if?” / “Look what we made possible.” State concrete outcomes and honest claim boundaries. | Write a meaningful professional ambition. | Make the outcome—not the software UI—the promise. |

## Live pacing and contingency

- A complete `codex-pro` run is the target. It may take an unpredictable amount of time and requires the presenter’s authorized CLI session. Do a full-account rehearsal; do not depend on first-time model installation live.
- If a step runs long, show a **previously qualified actual Codex receipt** with its timestamp/source identity; label it as a replay, then conduct an actual fresh focus-choice strategy call if operationally possible. Never label scripted preview as a fresh AI run.
- If credentials/network are unavailable, disclose the fallback as `SCRIPTED_PREVIEW`, run the interactive evidence desk and deliver the method. Do not advertise that as equivalent to a completed live-agent demonstration.
- Only synthetic data are entered on screen. Never let unscreened audience inputs become external tool instructions, permissions, secrets or production actions.

## Three moments to engineer for impact

1. **The surprise:** “Six holds” looks like the answer. Then ask “What if the real task is preserving the launch safely?”
2. **The invention:** Three strategies lead to a constructed browser instrument—not another slide deck.
3. **The insight:** An 8/8 hypothetical result under clear assumptions becomes a *better question*, not an unsupported success announcement.
