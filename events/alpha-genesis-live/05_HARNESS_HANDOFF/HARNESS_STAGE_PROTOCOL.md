# HARNESS is the stage; the transformation is the show

Open the official HARNESS from https://github.com/MontrealAI/AGI-Alpha-Invention-Flywheel-Lab/tree/main/products/alpha-genesis-harness and follow its own installer/launcher instructions. Its existing manual mission flow lets an operator enter an ambition, define sources, compare alternatives, inspect evidence, and retain a project. The model bridge's commercial and authenticated operation has separate qualification boundaries; this event package **does not embed or impersonate the official HARNESS**.

## Choreography

1. **Audience ambition:** On the HARNESS Mission page enter “What if we could rescue a threatened launch without authorizing unsafe purchases?”
2. **Mission contract:** Use the eight-order synthetic Northstar case; protected constraints are no unsafe release, exact policy compliance, traceable sources, and human-only authorization.
3. **Possible paths:** Ask the room to nominate at least two ways forward; compare with the three prequalified synthetic alternatives rather than automatically assuming the grandest is best.
4. **Live companion:** Run the officially authorized Codex multi-step tool-using agent and separate verifier (`../00_STAGE/OPERATOR_GUIDE.md`). Then inspect `ALPHA_GENESIS_MISSION_STUDIO.html` as the working instrument the process built.
5. **Reintroduce to HARNESS:** Open the generated `HARNESS_HANDOFF.md`. Record the actual source-bound outcome, the model/preview label, the selected strategy, the independently checked baseline, and what remains unproven in the HARNESS manual workbench.
6. **New move:** Tell the room exactly which new supplier evidence would change the result. Invite attendees to state what an equivalent test would be in their own profession.

The new HTML decision desk is a companion artifact generated from a model-checked dossier plus trusted scenario rules. The HARNESS *research program* and this *event package's constructed tool* have a shared methodology; this is not a claimed automatic interoperability protocol.

## Minimum acceptance criteria

- The visible multi-step investigation used a genuinely authorized model; a preview must be clearly marked.
- The original dossier passes a separate verifier under fixed exact input sources.
- At least three alternatives are compared and the room can choose a branch.
- The resulting HTML works offline, changes its output on audience choice, shows each assumption, and exports a record.
- The next move is an evidence request or test, never an unsupported claim of vendor improvement or legal authority.
