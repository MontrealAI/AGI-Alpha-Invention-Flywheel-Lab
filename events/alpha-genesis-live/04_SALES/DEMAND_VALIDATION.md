# Commercial demand test — observed purchases beat optimistic assumptions

**Hypothesis:** a live, outcome-led AI invention laboratory sells better than a generic AI introduction to professionals who already use models. The company can initially validate willingness to pay through one public cohort, then expand to private-team delivery.

## Minimum viable commercial test

- **Offer:** 120-minute live ALPHA GENESIS FLYWHEEL Ω — LIVE, interactive workbook and standalone instrument, real demonstrable agent, CAD 195 (pricing hypothesis, one transparent price for the first test).
- **Target:** operations/innovation leaders, founders and consultants (more focused positioning may win even if the total audience is smaller).
- **Capacity:** initial 24 interactive seats, subject to facilitation qualification. Do not advertise “sold out” unless true.
- **Single conversion event:** completed *paid, nonrefunded* transaction, not event-page click, RSVP or survey interest.
- **Recommended primary thresholds (decision assumptions, not market facts):** 12 paid tickets for an initial viability signal; 18 for a stronger repeat signal; 24 sold out for capacity-pressure evidence. Compare gross ticket revenue with actual costs and presenter effort before deciding repeatability.

| Tickets sold | Gross at CAD 195 (before fees/taxes/costs) | Interpretation (proposed) |

## How to test the message

Use three credible promotional angles from `OUTREACH.md`, with distinct tracked channels/UTM tags but an identical actual ticket offer. Observe unique landing visits, checkout starts, paid conversions, cancellations, buyer role and acquisition source. Avoid comparing unrepresentative channels as if randomized samples.

**After the first cohort**, ask attendees whether they completed the activity, which specific workplace use they plan to test, and whether a private/team lab is a genuine near-term need. Permission to follow up must be explicit.

## What large demand would require

The quality of the single workshop is only one factor. Scale depends on repeatable access to qualified audiences, conversion economics, support capacity, event satisfaction, referrals and a product ladder. No GitHub repo, copy variant or synthetic benchmark alone demonstrates large paid demand.

## Pricing evolution

Keep one public price for this pilot. To test CAD 249–295 later, use a later cohort or a genuinely differentiated additional service (e.g. 30-minute small-group application review), with clear differences and consistent disclosure—not arbitrary hidden different prices for identical seats.
