# Paid event production & Zoom / Eventbrite operations

## Before publication

- Confirm the event owner, billing/payout/tax treatment, support inbox, refund policy and actual event date in **America/Toronto**.
- Create one clear professional access ticket at the approved price and actual supportable capacity. Disclose platform fees and applicable taxes at checkout.
- Enter the long listing from `EVENTBRITE_COPY.md`. Do not imply named sponsors, institutional endorsements, accredited certification, live enterprise deployments or guaranteed outcomes.
- Configure the webinar/video connection through the organizer account. Test joining from a separate guest account, including confirmation, reminder, display timezone, captions, chat/poll, screen share, cohost roles and audio.
- Confirm whether the session will be recorded; obtain appropriate notice/consent, and do not reveal participant names, screens or private tabs without authorization.
- Host the participant workbook and browser studio on a reliable attendee-accessible file delivery path; never publish credentials, account sign-in files or the presenter's actual live access receipts.
- Complete `01_FACILITATOR/GO_NO_GO.md`. In particular, full live Codex authorization and scenario workflow must work on the **presenter's** machine.

## Show day

- Presenter and optional cohost join early enough to check shared screens and audio. Keep a pinned browser tab, HARNESS workspace and terminal view ready; ensure notifications and private accounts are hidden.
- Ask the room to choose time/cost/confidence through the platform's own polling/chat mechanisms; don't rely on an invented integration.
- Share the attendee lab resource only after the facilitator explains the fictional/synthetic nature and non-sensitive-input boundary.
- Read the claim boundary before showing the combined 8/8 hypothetical scenario.
- End with a single follow-up: participant resources and a voluntary private-team inquiry route. Do not add attendees to unrelated marketing lists without consent.

## Afterward

- Deliver a working participant kit plus replayable evidence **only with data the attendees are entitled to receive**. Keep presenter-only model/account receipts private.
- Use a short feedback form: usefulness, completed artifact, next practical challenge, and optional private-lab interest.
- Track real paid orders, refunds, attendance, kit completion and inquiries in `CAMPAIGN_SCORECARD.csv` and the event platform's own records.
- Close the event only when refund/support obligations and any promised recording delivery are fulfilled.
