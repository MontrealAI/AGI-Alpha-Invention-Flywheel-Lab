# Sales launch kit — compelling but evidence-grounded

## The commercial hook

**Most AI events give you answers. This one builds something.**

The strongest emotional moment is when the audience watches a real agent complete an investigation, construct a working instrument, and pass an independent check. Make that moment the first 15 seconds of your promotional video and the first sentence of the event listing. Do not lead with repository names, protocols, tokenomics, or a catalogue of eight mechanisms.

## Targeting hierarchy

1. Existing MONTREAL.AI event attendees and community members who have consented to announcements.
2. AI-interested operations, innovation, product, consulting and founder communities; approach hosts for voluntary newsletter distribution.
3. Professional societies, tech associations, entrepreneurship and innovation groups with genuinely relevant members.
4. Specific enterprise teams for private lab proposals **after** the public experience is qualified.

No implied institutional endorsements, consent to email old contact lists, or unsupported financial outcome claims.

## Launch sequence

**Phase 1 — Qualify:** complete actual Codex run, audience first-user test, Eventbrite/Zoom checkout, delivery rehearsal, legal/recording notice.

**Phase 2 — Publish one consistent public offer:** sell a clearly priced seat with exact date, language, audience, prerequisites, concrete artifacts and delivery terms. Make the browser result demo visible, labeled as a scripted preview until the authentic live run is available.

**Phase 3 — Run three creative tests:** use comparable audiences and the same ticket price, schedule, landing page and attribution. Track unique qualified visitors, checkout starts, paid transactions and refunds by source, not vanity impressions.

**Phase 4 — Respond to evidence:** retain the best-performing message only after sufficient real observations; refine the session if participants fail to produce the advertised take-home result.

## Private-lab extension

After the public event, offer a private team version centered on a single **approved** organizational challenge, with prespecified sources, safe tool permissions, independent acceptance and a handoff plan. Quote after requirements discovery; do not promise a production deployment or monetary savings merely because the demonstration worked.
