# Private enterprise extension — from a vivid experience to one qualified workflow

**Product direction:** An invite-only team session that turns one approved business challenge into a bounded, reusable AI-assisted work instrument with independent acceptance criteria and human authority limits.

**Qualify first:** identify decision owner, data sensitivity, allowed model/tool access, non-negotiable constraints, current ordinary baseline, success criteria, reviewer, scope and change-control policy. Do not connect to the client's systems before authorization.

**Suggested format:** half-day workshop plus a follow-on requirements/review session. **Indicative offer testing range:** CAD 3,500–5,500 for a well-bounded private team engagement, subject to actual scope and market response. Pricing is not a verified customer quote, a fixed public tariff or a promise of economic benefit.

**Delivery:** one co-defined ambition, sources, competing approaches, a minimum useful demonstrator, independent checks, a review dossier and next-move roadmap. A production system is a separately scoped project.

**Best conversion question at the public event:** “Which recurring decision in your organization deserves a working proof, rather than another AI presentation?”
