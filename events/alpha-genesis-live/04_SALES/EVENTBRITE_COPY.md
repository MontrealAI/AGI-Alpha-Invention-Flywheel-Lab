## Long description

### What if the next capability you need could be built—and tested—in front of you?

Most AI events show you what an AI can say. **ALPHA GENESIS FLYWHEEL Ω — LIVE** shows you what becomes possible when a capable agent is given a consequential challenge, real tools, a rigorous test, and the freedom to explore a better approach.

**From “What if?” to “Look what we made possible.”**

In this live MONTREAL.AI experience, we begin with an ambitious professional question: *Could an AI agent help rescue an at-risk product launch without hiding uncertainty or bypassing human approval?*

Then the work begins—on screen, in front of you.

A genuine tool-using AI agent investigates four source files, checks eight proposed purchase orders, and builds an evidence-backed decision dossier. We push further: what if the first answer is not the best possible outcome? A second AI stage compares three recovery approaches. The results become a **working, interactive decision instrument**. An independent verifier challenges the original conclusions against frozen rules. The audience then chooses which hypothetical recovery scenario to explore.

This is not a lecture about agents. **It is a front-row seat at the transformation of an idea into a constructed and tested capability.**

### What you will actually see

- **An ambition becomes a mission:** an objective, constraints and a precise definition of success.
- **Real agentic investigation:** an authorized AI model makes multi-step decisions and requests actual read-only tools. The tool-use record is visible.
- **The first answer becomes a better question:** the room compares supply recovery, commercial/quality recovery, and a combined approach.
- **A useful tool gets built:** a standalone browser instrument with interactive scenarios and downloadable evidence.
- **A serious test:** a separate program recomputes source-bound decisions; assumptions remain visible, and human authority remains explicit.

### And then you learn to do it yourself

You'll use the same five-move framework to formulate a meaningful challenge from your own profession: **Ambition → Investigation → Alternatives → Build & test → Next move.** You'll work with the interactive instrument and leave with a structured mission blueprint, the workbook, and an evidence-based method you can reuse.

**Your take-home resources:** a standalone interactive Mission Studio, a participant workbook, a working synthetic case and evidence export, a transferable mission brief, and a practical independent-check protocol.

**For whom:** founders, managers, innovation and transformation leaders, consultants, product and operations professionals, analysts and builders who want AI to accomplish meaningful work—not just produce impressive sentences. No coding experience is required for the guided attendee exercise.

### What to bring

A laptop with an up-to-date browser; one professional “What if?” that you can discuss without disclosing confidential information; and a willingness to challenge results. No participant ChatGPT Pro subscription, API key, cryptocurrency wallet, or software installation is required for the take-home browser experience. The presenter operates the model-backed investigation.

**The standard:** a constructed result is valuable when you can inspect how it works and what its evidence actually establishes. Our Northstar case and recovery scenarios are fictional; the simulated improvements are conditional, not claims about actual vendors, realized savings, or production approvals.
