# Participant mission card — bring one consequential “What if?”

### The promise
You will see an agent investigate the Northstar Launch challenge, compare alternatives, construct a working scenario instrument and subject its baseline to independent checks. Then you'll use the same method to define an experiment on a task from your own profession.

**Your challenge, in one sentence:** What if we could _____________________________ ?

**For whom:** Who benefits from a successful result?

**One non-negotiable constraint:** What must never be violated?

**One observable output:** What would exist at the end besides a convincing paragraph?

**The acceptance test:** How would someone other than the agent check it?

**The ordinary baseline:** How would a capable professional already do this?

**The next move:** What would be worth attempting if the first result were useful?

**Privacy:** submit no confidential business details, personal data, credentials or account files. The public case and interactive instrument use synthetic data.
