\section*{\color{accent}1. Question, contribution and evidence boundary}
Can an executable experiment compiler recognize that a measurement family cannot distinguish a consequential model class, construct a different observable, and retain the verified construction for another circuit? This study executes that question on two public ngspice passive-network designs. The first output is an experiment-design report, not a manufactured circuit, measured specimen, or customer order. The broader objective is to turn verified instruments into means for further discovery and production; the present evidence supports only part of that chain.\par
The implemented capability parses a bounded linear passive netlist, derives a scaling transformation from typed constitutive laws, produces exact responses and ambiguity witnesses, extends a voltage-only strategy grammar with input-admittance measurements, and selects a finite separating plan. A separate checker recomputes circuit behavior without importing candidate arithmetic. The retained instrument is tested on a different RLC topology under the same explicitly limited model semantics.\par
The primary cohort has 48 authored parameter hypotheses and 1,128 unordered pairs. The voltage-only arm separates 1,071; the constructed arm separates all 1,128. The second topology has 24 hypotheses and 276 pairs, with voltage-only separation of 252 and constructed separation of 276. Ordinary exhaustive experiment design attains the same complete coverage in each case. Its selected design is smaller than the initial greedy design. The added information matters; superior invention or search performance is not established.\par
All numerical cases are exposed DEVELOPMENT. Exact finite engineering checks are not statistical confirmation, and no protected scientific holdout or additional campaign error allocation is claimed. Candidate and verifier roles share a host, model family, tools and libraries. Their separation is procedural, not independent external certification. Public netlist provenance does not turn simulated responses into measured physical data.\par
A separate provider-identified current-frontier construction arm and a timed competent-human engineering study were not executed. Program reset and equal-content reuse are matched software controls; they are not new frontier API calls or measured expert labor. The study therefore does not establish superiority over independently operating frontier-AI construction, human scientific discovery or their best complete workflows.\par

\section*{\color{accent}3. Constructed means: symmetry to a measurement extension}
For a passive branch at positive angular frequency omega, the admittances are 1/R, j omega C and 1/(j omega L). The developer supplies these constitutive laws and their units. The program solves the exponent condition that every branch admittance scale by the same factor 1/a. It therefore emits R->aR, L->aL and C->C/a, together with coverage of the parsed branches and applicability exclusions. The algebraic derivation is executed; neither the physical laws nor the source selection are claimed to have been discovered autonomously.\par
\[ R\mapsto aR,\qquad L\mapsto aL,\qquad C\mapsto C/a\quad\Longrightarrow\quad y_e\mapsto y_e/a. \]
At fixed normalized source voltage, the complete nodal matrix and forcing vector both scale by 1/a. When the nodal system is nonsingular, its voltage solution is unchanged at every node and every frequency. Consequently, more voltage samples from this same model cannot identify global impedance scale. Input current, and hence input admittance, scales by 1/a. An admitted current observation changes the available information rather than merely searching harder over voltage reconstructions.\par
\[ \begin{gathered}Yv=b\;\longmapsto\;(Y/a)v=b/a,\\ V_n/V_{\rm in}\;\text{unchanged},\qquad I_{\rm in}/V_{\rm in}\mapsto(I_{\rm in}/V_{\rm in})/a.\end{gathered} \]
The implementation uses exact rational complex arithmetic and Gaussian elimination, avoiding floating-point equality as evidence of structural non-identifiability. The initial voltage-only toolbox cannot express an input-admittance action. A generated extension adds that observable with its physical unit, frequency, acquisition-unit weight, derived symmetry and ancestry. A greedy finite strategy builder then chooses actions that separate unresolved pairs. The selected plan retains exact response tables, uncertainty policies, unresolved witnesses and source identities.\par
