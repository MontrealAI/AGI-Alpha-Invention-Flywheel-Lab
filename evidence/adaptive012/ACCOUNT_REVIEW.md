# Adaptive enterprise 012: account and investment review

A conditional simulated commercial flywheel succeeded under the registered conditions: the full adaptive policy passed all 28 central trajectories, with cash survival in all 28. This is a separate procedural financial review of completed confirmation edition 002. The review uses raw financial events and completed service records rather than accepting reported summaries as evidence of themselves.

The full adaptive policy was profitable across all 32 primary worlds, but it was not the highest-profit tested policy on average. The migrated strategic public-prior baseline and strong fixed policy remain valid, stronger overall alternatives. This finding does not negate the independently qualified simulated-commercial result.

## Controls and applicability

| Primary policy, 32 matched worlds each | Mean complete simulated profit |
| --- | ---: |
| Full adaptive | $889.485866 |
| Migrated strategic 011 public-prior adapter | $1,064.927777 |
| Strong fixed | $980.617602 |
| Ordinary adaptive | $29.616567 |

Full adaptation improves mean profit by $859.869299 against ordinary adaptive, while trailing the strategic adapter by $175.441910 and strong fixed by $91.131736 overall. Its advantages against those stronger controls are region-specific, including competent-imitation and joint-adverse cases; they are not general superiority. The stronger overall alternatives should remain available in the next operating decision.

On four matched ablation cases, capital adaptation adds mean $383.448657, while adding the instrument has mean −$31.519305 versus `no_instrument` (range −$103.345407 to +$34.593234). This does not establish a generally profitable invention advantage. The retained instrument changes executable mapping and is used, but ordinary equal-content reuse remains a strong relevant comparison.

The $350 setup sensitivity passes both tested cases; $1,500 setup fails both, with profits −$958.208484 and −$893.271142. Higher operating-cost scenarios happen to pass in their small tested samples partly because the frozen controller changes decisions; do not infer that higher costs cause greater profit. The stated profitable region is conditional on cost and market assumptions. Actual commerce, unrestricted invention superiority and scientific/physical productive expansion remain separate, unestablished claims.

## Reproduce the account evidence

From the exact extracted source package:

```sh
python3 -m adaptive_enterprise audit --output ..
```

The default performs native and full operating replay. `--financial-only` is an explicitly narrower check with zero operating replays; it is not a substitute for full package qualification. Live account recovery additionally requires the retained local simulation key, which is intentionally omitted from public packages. Public financial/state/action verification does not require that private key.
