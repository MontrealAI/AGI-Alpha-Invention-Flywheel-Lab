# Adaptive-012 market and commercial review

**A conditional simulated commercial flywheel succeeded under the registered conditions. General superiority of the full adaptive controller was not demonstrated.**

Edition 002 completed all 160 registered trajectories: 128 primary, 20 ablation and 12 cost/obligation sensitivities. The seven-family central region passed 28/28 trajectories, with mean complete-cost profit $928.53, observed range $392.19–$1,366.28, and mean available earned funds $928.53. Cash survival was 100%; the mean of the lowest 20% of profits was $640.37.

The preregistered requirements were at least 75% central flywheel success, 90% cash survival and a lower-tail mean of at least −$750. These are declared risk policies, not universal business laws. The observed 100% result does not become a requirement for future businesses. No all-cycle or service-inventory completion gate was applied.

## Strong controls and competitive adaptation

All comparisons below pair the same seed and family across 32 primary trajectories. A positive delta favors the full controller.

| Comparator | Comparator mean profit | Full minus comparator | Full wins / losses | Comparator flywheel passes |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `strategic_011_public_priors` | $1,064.93 | $-175.44 | 9 / 23 | 32/32 |
| `strong_fixed` | $980.62 | $-91.13 | 11 / 21 | 32/32 |
| `ordinary_adaptive` | $29.62 | $859.87 | 32 / 0 | 2/32 |

**The retained strategic solver and strong fixed policy both outperform the full controller on overall mean profit.** The full controller is commercially successful under its registered simulation, but it is not the best tested general operating policy. Preserve these stronger baselines.

The `ordinary_adaptive` comparator is a limited contextual Beta-bandit with separate price-bin counts, flat conversion priors and no pooled demand model or opponent pattern model. Untried expensive price bins can remain attractive. Beating this implementation is not evidence of superiority to every competent adaptive operator. The `no_opponent` result shares part of that limitation.

Against the retained solver, the full controller has positive family means only in competent imitation (+$23.89; 3/4 pair wins) and joint adversity (+$100.13; 4/4 wins). Against the fixed policy it gains $332.58 in imitation (4/4 wins) and $99.21 in joint adversity (3/4 wins). These are evaluated family-conditioned effects. Evaluator labels are unavailable to the operating controller, so these findings do not implement an omniscient router. A prospective public-observation meta-policy comparison would need a new frozen evaluation.

## Actual constructions, reuse and expansion

Across the 32 full-controller counterfactual trajectories, there were 454 costed diagnostic records and 454 terminal adjudications; 13 trajectories recorded change detections. The 32 source-bound instrument executions are repeated constructions across counterfactuals, not 32 independent inventions. Retained and equal-content ordinary reuse produced identical useful-outcome hashes in 32/32 instrument comparisons. No invention-superiority or Blue Sky-enablement verdict follows.

| Paid growth action | Executions across full trajectories | Source-linked later settled-use instances |
|---|---:|---:|
| acquisition | 128 | 581 |
| instrument | 32 | 26 |
| capacity | 32 | 241 |
| retention | 192 | 205 |
| supplier_diversity | 8 | 0 |

These use links show actual subsequent work associated with a paid resource. Complementary resources can support the same job, so link counts must not be summed as extra deliveries or revenue. Native revenues count each accepted order once. Supplier diversity was purchased eight times but produced no source-linked settled fallback delivery in this horizon; its value is not established. The current controller proposes capital options after selecting an executable offer, which can postpone investment during complete withdrawal. This is a controller limitation to investigate, not an empirical finding that diversification cannot help.

Only 1 joint bundle settled across the 32 full trajectories. Joint pricing and three-component execution were exercised, with shared work keys preventing component resale, but repeatable bundle demand was not established. The 1,174 full-controller settled orders are a total across separate counterfactual episodes, not a single enterprise's customer cohort or realized business revenue.

Two fresh seeds per case; the same recurring-service world was rerun with one registered policy change.

The $75 default setup and all per-attempt charges are authored envelopes, not measured model bills or established conservative upper bounds. $350 setup still passes in these two cases; $1,500 setup loses money in both and yields no profitable earned-funded continuation. The profitability region is conditional on complete costs. The $8 production result exceeds the $3 result because adaptive decisions and their downstream trajectories change; it does not show that raising costs improves a fixed business.

The 14-day payment-deadline sensitivity uses the normal recurring-service payer timing. It is not the cross-test of a 14-day deadline against the 23-day delayed-payment family. Existing pending-payment and deadline regressions remain supporting engineering evidence. No untested joint-cost or timing robustness is inferred.
