## What the paired result does not establish

A stronger factorial necessity claim would require separate evidence (Section 9.2); none is needed for the existing narrower claim.

Theorem 5.1 requires a feasible bootstrap and, throughout a declared finite scale range, an admitted fixed-duration schedule that expands **every essential capacity** while preserving obligations, paying costs and obtaining material/external flows. Neither the five-route replay nor the paired experiment supplies that certificate. The manuscript's supplied nineteen-module simulation cannot fill this gap (Sections 4-5).
