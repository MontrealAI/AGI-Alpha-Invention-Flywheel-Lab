## Material obligations still open

1. **Advantage and compounding remain unestablished.** Sections 6.7.5–6.7.6 (pp. 59–60), 24 (pp. 156–161) and 27.10 (pp. 165–166) require relevant strong baselines, equal resource constraints, cost-adjusted improvement and reproducibility before stronger promotion. The ordinary same-information tie is therefore decisive: numerical acceptance supports the bounded acquisition contract, not a superior invention procedure, coordination advantage or breakthrough. Repeated future-task improvement and measured benefits remain untested. The manuscript's longer experimental program is not evidence that those experiments occurred.

2. **Computational acceptance does not establish physical or commercial consequence.** Sections 6.11.4 (p. 69), 13.4.2 (pp. 98–99), 13.13.3–13.13.5 (p. 132) and 30 (p. 180) require grounding appropriate to the claim. Actual paired measurements, justified drift/error bounds, geometry and spectral calibration, exposure/dose/time budgets, delayed outcomes, buyer value and complete episode costs remain open. The current HOLD/refusal decisions and unknown upstream model, host and labor costs must remain visible; hypothetical cash and runtime alone cannot establish profit or productivity.

3. **Replay is not external attestation or full institutional qualification.** The evidence ladder (pp. 36–37), ECI distinction (pp. 58–59) and reviewer protocol/honesty box (pp. 191–192) separate reproducible local evidence from external review. Same-host peer review, CI and downloaded-release replay do not supply an independent laboratory result, external reviewer attestation, real-world safety certification or measured agent-scaling advantage. Preserve those distinctions in release wording.

4. **Future operational promotion needs additional evidence and authority.** Settlement-grade requirements (pp. 25–27), persistence controls (pp. 57–60), action traces (p. 70) and cost/evidence records (pp. 159–160) extend beyond this account-free study. It implements neither escrow nor validator commit-reveal/quorum, calibrated work units, complete settlement metering or a deployed recursive institution. The manuscript grants no additional authority to add those operations. Retain failed cases, source lineage, refusal reasons and the original arrangement rejection; do not reclassify them through later successes.

The appropriate current disposition is **retain and release reproducible conditional evidence with its ordinary ties and counterexamples; leave physical, commercial and architecture-wide promotion unresolved**.
