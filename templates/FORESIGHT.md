## Non-obvious implication
What could become possible or necessary that ordinary search may overlook? What was not supplied in the assignment?

## Evidence and causal chain
Dated observations and primary sources; change in capabilities/cost/coordination; first-order consequence; changed bottleneck; further consequence. Mark every unsupported link as a hypothesis.

## Horizon and resolution
Specific observable outcome; date or event that resolves it; source of resolution. An experiment can resolve a technical assumption without resolving a market forecast.

## Uncertainty
Probability/range only with a stated basis, or UNKNOWN; base-rate/comparator when available; sources of model uncertainty; correlated signals; earliest falsifier.

## Rival futures
At least one plausible alternative, competitor response and adverse condition. Which observations distinguish the futures?

## Constructive move
What can AGI ALPHA build to alter the attainable outcome? What depends on the outside world, and what can be influenced through authorized construction?

## First discriminating experiment
Lowest-cost permitted test that changes the next decision. State permissions, budget, expected interpretations and stop rule.

## Comparison and hindsight control
Strong ordinary foresight/search baseline, expert evidence when available, frozen information set and budgets, all forecasts retained. No hindsight relabeling of a miss as the original prediction.

## Later resolution
Outcome, date, source, score if the preregistered forecast supports one, and effect on the investment decision. Unresolved stays unresolved.
