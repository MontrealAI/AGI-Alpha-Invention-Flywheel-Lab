## Defect and prospective repair

The incumbent `intent.evaluate` can recommend two substitute services because
individual utility and a shared wallet do not establish voluntary joint utility.
Its realized-collection ranking is an authored retrospective diagnostic, not a
causal decision rule. Do not use future payment/acceptance observations to choose
a supposedly prospective action.

1. Require explicit evidence for every buyer's exact subset in a candidate bundle:
   joint value, complete labeled outside utilities, and whole-bundle consent.
   Do not sum individual values or infer consent. Unknown evidence refuses.
2. Compare exact joint value less total quote to human/conventional software,
   competitor AI, self-service and no-purchase alternatives. A tie refuses.
   Joint complementarity is allowed; individual utility is not a veto on a
   separately consented, worthwhile complete package.
3. Keep realized collections only in an explicitly retrospective report.
   Add a separate prospective maximin comparison over a fixed, declared finite
   set of predecision scenarios. It accepts collection lower bounds and complete
   scenario costs, never realized payment or future output acceptance fields.
   Unknown bounds/costs refuse; current funds cover the whole cost before receipts.
4. Candidate packages are mutually exclusive offered arrangements, not a
   simultaneous menu or incentive-compatible mechanism. Each buyer may decline
   the exact offered package. Partner participation must be explicitly supplied.
   No-entry pays the same sunk acquisition cost and wins economic ties.
