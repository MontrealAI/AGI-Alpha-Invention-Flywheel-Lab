## Constructed physical envelope and successor boundary

For1,000L at2g/L feed and0.5g/L product, increasing recovery0.5→0.95 reduces brine500→50L while increasing brine concentration3.5→30.5g/L. Salt remains in both product and concentrate; disposal burden does not disappear. Ideal stack energy is not total plant energy. No membrane limiting-current, fouling, chemical or disposal evidence qualifies these recovery settings.
