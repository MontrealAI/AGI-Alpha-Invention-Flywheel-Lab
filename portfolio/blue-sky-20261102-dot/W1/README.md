## Physical meaning
`separate` imposes ideal monovalent salt conservation and Faraday charge. It excludes leakage, water transport, concentration-dependent resistance, membrane selectivity and fouling. Current efficiency0.4 is the paper's reported observation;0.9 is an explicitly unqualified marketed alternative.150cell pairs follows the paper. Stack voltage50V is an illustrative scenario, not optimized or measured site voltage. Solar and pump totals are recorded, not enough to infer a new controller's counterfactual power performance. Faraday's law is a necessary bound, not full process qualification.

`construct` creates storage capacity and starting inventory for the recorded hourly production and a fixed hourly obligation. Spills count. Supply increments become available at the later measured timestamp, not interpolated. Known stock is conserved and terminal stock cannot finance the first day's delivery. Initial fill is a real prerequisite, not free construction water. It must be procured or produced before service, with costs/qualification unknown. A daily replay's feasible design is not a seasonal reliability claim.

## Successor boundary
Higher recovery can reduce concentrate volume while increasing concentration. It does not eliminate salt. K2 waste-disposal acceptance needs scaling/fouling/chemistry and actual disposal conditions that this dataset does not provide. A valid arithmetic envelope remains unqualified for deployment. Ordinary specialists may use the same equations and storage control and are expected to tie.
