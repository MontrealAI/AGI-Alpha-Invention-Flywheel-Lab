# Economic adversary review — first construction stage

Independent economic reviewer: test_autonomous_business_economics. Review is read-only; it does not open accounts, accept contracts or certify demand.

**Decision: proceed with finite engineering evidence; no commercial collection trigger is qualified.** This is not rejection solely because an ordinary mathematical method ties.

## I1 optical instrument
Acceptance of a reconstructed authored field is not acceptance of an industrial defect inspection. A viable offering needs a specific inspected feature/defect and independent reference truth, false-negative and abstention requirements, liability/remedy terms, a physically realizable and calibrated reflector interface, and useful SNR/throughput under drift. Compare delivered cost with existing reflector-stack methods and any independently qualified implementations, coherent bundles, rigid endoscopes and distal calibration wherever access is feasible. Count fabrication, switching/dispersion calibration, operator intervention, maintenance and requalification. All are presently unknown. Conventional reuse saves the same acquisition content; no human labor differential or AI-specific feasibility threshold is measured.

## W1 water infrastructure
Hourly quantity arithmetic cannot certify potable, continuous or seasonal delivery. Product-channel mapping and source-water envelope, a physical bill of quantities, actual train scaling, storage/initial fill, pretreatment, membrane replacement, maintenance, energy, brine/site obligations and delivery penalties must be established. Repeating one measured trace across parallel trains is not independent weather diversification or demonstrated scale-up. Salt mass balance does not establish disposal acceptance. No price or buyer willingness to pay is inferred from the research dataset.

## C1 heat/power infrastructure
Public industrial programmes show significant need and existing competing complete routes. Award totals, plant capex and incumbent revenue cannot be treated as labor removable by autonomous software. A source-backed site-interface cost/qualification bottleneck is required before constructing a profitable-agent deployment claim.
