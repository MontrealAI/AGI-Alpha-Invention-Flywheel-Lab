# Interface I1: proximal-only reciprocal optical calibration
Status: preregistration; no confirmatory outcomes. Owner portfolio_interface; coordinator owns publication/account.

## Selection
1. Selected: proximal-only calibration of a flexible optical fibre, followed by phase-sensitive inaccessible industrial inspection. Designed reflectors substitute for distal optical access; preserves transmitted specimen information. Strong alternative: established transmission-matrix reflector-stack methods, coherent bundle/rigid endoscope, direct distal calibration. Commercial residual NOT yet established.
2. Four-terminal electrochemical interface eliminates contact voltage nuisance, enabling impedance/material characterization. Mature Kelvin/impedance instruments already supply this; no compelling residual or independent new successor selected.
3. Differential magnetic array rejects ambient common-mode fields for internal current mapping. Interesting battery/industrial monitoring, but inverse nonuniqueness and spatially nonuniform ambient fields can defeat the claimed elimination; public paired internal-current truth not established here.

## Model and constructed artifact
Forward field y=T x; reciprocal return T^T, NOT T*. Calibration data C_j=T^T R_j T. T invertible complex n-by-n passive operator, spectral norm <=1. Designed symmetric passive reflectors: R0=0.3 I; R1 diagonal distinct between .1 and .7; R2=.3 I+.08 adjacency(path). All have norm<1. Same T during all three exposures; calibration reflector selection and fixed-wavelength operation are modeled engineering assumptions, NOT proven fabrication. The realization may require switches or spectral correction; those costs and qualified physical behavior remain unknown.
K1 constructor consumes C0,C1 and known reflectors, no T or object truth. Generalized eigensystem and congruence normalization yield T_hat up to row signs. This enables intensity focus commands for individual distal coordinates without distal calibration access. Returns residual/condition diagnostics; numerical failure abstains.
Freeze K1 code and outputs before K2 evaluation. K2 adds C2, resolves relative signs along connected reflector graph, and reconstructs a complex object field from y=T^T object. This is a phase-sensitive measurement purpose distinct from energy delivery; no assertion it is more economically consequential without independent buyer evidence.

Constructor never receives T or object. n in {4,8,16}; condition numbers 1,3,10; repeated independently changed T between sessions with full recalibration; stale calibration control. Noise relative Frobenius norm levels 0,1e-6,1e-4,1e-2; within-session drift levels 0,1e-6,1e-3,.05. Noise/drift regions are authored stress conditions, not empirical distributions. No cross-seed tuning.
Noiseless accepted K1 focus fraction versus true matched-filter optimum >=1-1e-8; K2 relative complex-field error after ONE global sign <=1e-7. General losses may prevent unit focus fraction; compare attainable matched-filter intensity, not impossible perfect focus. Noisy cases report full error/refusal curves; no predeclared claim of physical accuracy. Rank deficiency, repeated reflector eigenvalues, disconnected sign graph must fail or expose unresolved ambiguity, never claim unique phase reconstruction.
Controls: no calibration; one reflector orthogonal-gauge ambiguity; K1-only independent row-sign ambiguity; retained K1+new C2; fresh three-response construction; equal-content classical reuse (same eigensystem/calibration); strongest direct distal T calibration (requires distal access but permitted full alternative); independent SciPy generalized eigensolver and analytic diagonal special cases.
