# N3B economic and claim-boundary review

Read-only economic review of the registered construction and reported results; not a second physical replay or certification.

**Verdict:** useful source-bound engineering instrument and negative comparative evidence; no commercial collection, manufacturing acceptance, or new-business profit claim is supported yet.

The full 300-patch comparison favors the ordinary single-blur model over the constructed multiscale model (20.075 versus 20.897 µm mean active-region RMSE). The random-mask subgroup does not replace the registered denominator. Strong archived neural-model outputs were retained, but saved-output comparison is not reproduction of their training. One physical test slide does not establish independent-batch yield or deployment reliability. These limits do not rule out a profitable autonomous workflow; its comparative lifecycle cost has not been measured.

Historical recipe selection has measured physical outcomes, but no prospectively specified manufacturing tolerance. New PNG/SVG masks are real computational artifacts, not manufactured parts: five remain MODEL ONLY and four COMPUTATIONAL HOLD. Forward prediction accuracy and internal numerical replay cannot certify the new masks or their functional wall/ridge continuity. No payment trigger should treat them as accepted physical production.

A subsequent commercial test must distinguish a process-design dossier from a qualified printed-part contract. Before new outcomes, specify the purchaser's functional acceptance, strongest conventional calibration/model/recipe alternative, defect and abstention consequences, rework, warranty and revalidation obligations. Preserve conventional software's genuine automation in the primary competent human-only comparator; autonomous and human-plus-AI providers remain additional outside options. Neither invented human hours nor assumed wage savings are warranted here.

Full costs would include source acquisition and unsuccessful development, inference and tools, resin and coupons, light-source/printer calibration, metrology, operator intervention, failed prints, qualification, acquisition/onboarding, maintenance, support and residual liability. Historical NIST experiments are not free future production. The reported 41.46-second confirmation runtime excludes these stages; unknown included-agent and host billing is not zero cost. No task price, willingness to pay, financing availability or removable industrial cost has been established.

The consequential next discriminator is blinded physical production of frozen new masks under independently fixed functional tolerances, compared with incumbent recipes across multiple batches. That requires separate physical authorization and evidence; further fitting to this same slide cannot substitute. Offline work can retain the tested instrument and exact failure boundaries without claiming that this physical step occurred.

No account, collection or financing receipt is required to document this learning. Any later economic simulation must prospectively bind its paid scope to actual acceptance, fully cost failures and obligations, separate capital from revenue/profit, and retain refusal/non-deployment. The $3B trailing-year objective remains untested for this arrangement; scaling patches or masks into hypothetical customers would not establish it.
