# Strategic reset after N3B

The user's objective is a consequential, previously uneconomic productive outcome with a credible route to very large earned profit—not improved benchmark prediction. N3B is supporting engineering work and does not qualify as that destination. Further regression tuning is not the next flagship experiment.

## Change the search unit
Start with a complete productive arrangement: beneficiary and scarce output; present delivered-cost or capacity constraint; physical production and acceptance chain; ownership, competitors and contracting; construction/qualification/ramp time; full lifecycle liabilities and capital. Candidate selection must be justified before looking for convenient datasets. An ordinary algorithm is admissible if autonomous execution and the whole arrangement materially change delivered feasibility.

Before the next experiment, freeze one conditional economic/engineering model that includes the strongest build/buy/outsource alternative and a no-deploy branch. State which missing capability makes the whole outcome uneconomic today and exactly how a first constructed instrument would remove it. A different K2 outcome must use that instrument; same-task prediction improvements do not count. Acquisition, qualification and integration work must not disappear from the human baseline or the autonomous lifecycle.

Capital is endogenous: explicitly compare financing-available scenarios, including large initial equity where justified, with delay, dilution/terms, construction risk and finite funding. Hypothetical financing is not cash, revenue or proof that investors will provide it. No extrapolation from tiny software margins to $3B trailing-year profit.

## Admission gate for a new construction
- Independently supported output need and a concrete paid/contractable acceptance unit.
- A binding engineering or coordination constraint, not merely an available dataset.
- A realistic full-system baseline and a finite experiment whose result could reverse deployment choice.
- Explicit separation of measured input, validated engineering model, authored scenario and unvalidated extrapolation.
- Independent verifier and economic adversary before outcome access.
- A capital/risk envelope and scale bottleneck that could accommodate the ambition without presuming market capture.

If these are not yet available, report the unresolved arrangement honestly and perform only the targeted evidence acquisition needed to distinguish alternatives. Do not rename a conventional scientific test as a new industry, or require novel ML when the actual business mechanism is cheaper reliable execution.

N3B teaches a narrow lesson: source-bound interfaces can be built and checked, but forward-model accuracy, generated artifacts and economically accepted physical outputs are different achievements. Its preserved negative result prevents spending the next cycle polishing the wrong proxy.
