## Why this selection is not a convenience-only claim
Photopolymer construction offers independent manufactured geometry, calibrated input channels and an executable physical acceptance bridge now, rather than an ideal-matrix generator or a model fit used as its own truth. This justifies the first discriminating test, NOT admission as a new industry. Capital, demand and human-versus-agent lifecycle cost are not yet measured. No source establishes that cheap autonomous intelligence already crosses a profitable commercial threshold; hardware, qualification and conventional incumbents remain in the comparison.

Potential capital needs differ substantially: optical metrology/printer fixtures/resin qualification for photopolymer work; acquisition, testing, disassembly, BMS, pack assembly and liability for batteries; well rights, integrity, insulated tubing, surface equipment, installation, cooling and depletion for geothermal. Exact totals are UNKNOWN. Neither100k nor1B is a universal budget boundary. A prospective deployment/finance scenario must price these complements rather than calling source data free industrial capacity.

## Prospective physical falsifiers for the selected route
1. A correct radiant-energy total is insufficient if irradiance/time, spectrum, oxygen inhibition or other kinetics change cure. Restrict the operating envelope or model those effects explicitly.
2. A measured beam intensity profile is not automatically a full lateral optical point-spread function or a cure/shrinkage model.
3. Fit/calibration and held-out manufactured geometry must be distinct. Preserve sample/recipe identity, units, preprocessing, alignment and measurement uncertainty.
4. A new inverse mask cannot be physically accepted solely by the same forward model that generated it. Historical exact-recipe replay can test a selection policy; genuinely new masks need a separately labeled model-only result until manufactured truth exists.
5. K1 metrology and K2 design correction are different roles, but a changed geometry alone is not proof of a more consequential successor or commercial feasibility.
