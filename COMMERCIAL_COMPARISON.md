# Current commercial comparison: enterprise 011

The primary benchmark is a super-competently configured autonomous frontier-AI operation versus competent human delivery at realistic speed and complete human service cost for the same accepted outcome and obligations. Appropriate validated deterministic tools may perform production. Record which tools actually executed; no live frontier-model call or metered token charge may be implied by deterministic code.

## Matched outcomes and costs

The human comparator itemizes preparation, onboarding, research, production, verification, correction, acquisition/ordering, support and necessary external resources. Separate sourced wages and benefit loading from assumed task hours, complete service cost, observed selling prices and buyer willingness to pay. Do not double-load compensation or equate program runtime with human work. When task timings are unmeasured, use declared ranges and matched-scope sensitivity cases.

The autonomous enterprise incurs agent research, intake, production, verification, repairs, acquisition, delivery, support, development and maintenance, plus tools, compute, storage, fees, failed work and obligations. Include unsuccessful bids, rejected/unpaid/refunded output and shared setup. Charge onboarding once per acquired authorized customer context; do not amortize setup over guaranteed future purchases. Do not insert hypothetical human review or agency staffing. Actual required human intervention is separately measured and labeled.

Actual model configuration, tokens, tools and provider billing are recorded when exposed. Otherwise complete-cost envelopes and break-even ranges support explicitly conditional conclusions. Unknown actual usage is not zero cost. The historic tariff/wage examples are preserved in `historical/enterprise011-prior-policy/COMMERCIAL_COMPARISON.md`; they are not current measured receipts or calibrated service prices.

## Demand and strategic pricing

Begin near comparable total human service costs and search a defensible price range. Optimize price, discount, conditional win probability, expected lifetime contribution and liquidity together, including no-bid. Refine price boundaries. No fixed five-percent discount, narrow premium interval, near-AI-cost pricing or requirement to win every job controls the current policy.

Buyers compare complete service outcomes from us, competent humans, relevant AI-assisted/rival services, self-service, postponement and no purchase. Include buyer execution, integration, approval and verification effort; software subscription price is not a delivered-service comparator. Keep reachable population, arrival, qualification, exposure, choice, activation, settlement, repeat purchase and churn distinct. Seller policy cannot access private realized values, future random draws or buyer reservations.

Test ordinary competition, AI price wars, differentiation supported by executed evidence and correlated trust/demand/cost shocks. Use overlapping quality and reputation ranges, responsive and lagged rivals, and multiple plausible choice models. Record supplier rate limits, capacity, latency, costs and outages. Finite-grid best response or heuristic pricing is not a proven market equilibrium.

## Connected commercial acceptance

Predeclare a useful cohort, repeated cycles, service acceptance, settled jobs, complete-cost profit, unrestricted earned funds, earned-funded continuation and cash survival before confirmation. Evaluate all admitted work and failures. Event-derived finance must conserve cash and distinguish seed, deposits, earned revenue, collections, expenses, taxes, liabilities, reserves, profit and unrestricted earned funds. Initial capital may still support reserves; seed payback and reserve replacement are separately reported metrics.

Trace prior eligible earnings into additional profitable acquisition and actual delivery. Future LTV is a decision estimate, not revenue to add again to realized events. Bundle sales replace overlapping component sales and share customer identities. Expansion represents marginal setup, support, saturation, concentration, correlated failures and supplier limits. Scale milestones are conditional time-to-target simulations, with failures and unreached trajectories retained.

## Five separate conclusions

| Verdict | Evidence needed |
| --- | --- |
| Executed service capability | Authorized input, actual output and independent artifact acceptance tied to source identity. |
| Conditional simulated commercial flywheel | Preregistered repeated settled simulated service, complete-cost surplus, liquid unrestricted earned funding and further profitable delivery under disclosed tested conditions. |
| Actual commercial operation | Separately authorized observed customers, delivery, real collections, costs and obligations. Simulation cannot establish it. |
| Capability-compounding invention | Causal fresh successor benefit after full acquisition/adaptation/verification costs against no-inheritance and equal-content ordinary reuse. |
| Blue Sky enablement | Decisive evidence of important newly accessible productive work and the relevant conventional limits. |

A passing simulated commercial verdict is independent of fixed capability, actual commerce and invention. Some failed stress scenarios do not invalidate a clearly declared profitable region. Conversely, favorable token budgets, a catalogue, passing tests or customer-count arithmetic do not establish the loop.
