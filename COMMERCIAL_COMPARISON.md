# Current commercial comparison

Adaptive-012 compares complete accepted outcomes at realistic, disclosed human work and waiting-time assumptions with autonomous production, independent checking, acquisition, correction, delivery and support. No hypothetical human staffing is inserted into the autonomous operation. Host billing is unavailable; all-in cost envelopes, ranges and break-even boundaries remain conditional assumptions.

The operating comparator set includes the existing finite strategic-pricing algorithm adapted to common observable priors, a strong fixed policy, competent ordinary adaptation and targeted ablations. Every arm starts with equal tools, information, cash and shocks. The prior published enterprise-011 result remains unchanged; new public-information controls do not retroactively reproduce or relabel its original observation assumptions.

Seller policies cannot read private reservations, competitor private costs, future shocks or evaluator labels. Rejections are censored; pending collections mature before default conclusions. Competitors and suppliers react to actual delayed histories. Offer changes must alter actual executable plans, terms, capacity or delivered attributes.

Revenue, settlement, cash, expenses, liabilities, protected reserves, seed and earned funds remain separate native records. Available earned funds—not projected return—finance new commitments. Later liabilities remain protected until source-bound resolution. Local ownership and state/action proofs prevent cooperating-worker stale reservations; unsupported distributed custody is not claimed.

A qualifying conditional flywheel has enough accepted settled deliveries and repeat customers, positive full-cost surplus and source-traceable profitable earned-funded continuation. No all-cycle, inventory-completion, perfect-survival or invention AND requirement applies. Risk tolerance is declared separately before new confirmation. Protected funds and actual obligations remain hard.

Cash-preserving withdrawal may be good adaptation but cannot alone qualify commerce. Expanded capability, competitive gain, invention benefit and realized physical productivity require their own evidence. Aggregate scale projections remain hypotheses and cannot fund the native ledger.
